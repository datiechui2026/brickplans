# Qiji Brick Unboxing Review: Is This Budget Brand Worth It?

## Unboxing Experience

The set arrived direct from Qiji in a rigid corrugated outer carton, with reinforcing ribs running along all four corners. After a long transit the box was still square and crisp, with no visible crushing — a detail that already puts Qiji ahead of many domestic brands.

Lifting the lid reveals parts sorted into several resealable bags, grouped by colour family and size, with clear numbering on each bag. The instructions are printed on colour coated paper, and the step diagrams are sharp-lined with very little colour drift, so even a first-time builder can follow them at a glance. The box also includes a small brick separator and a sorting tray. They look like minor extras, but they make the build noticeably more convenient, and it is clear Qiji has put real thought into the unboxing details.

## Part Quality and Building Feel

Qiji parts are moulded in ABS with rich, saturated colour, and the transparent elements transmit light well. Surfaces are almost entirely free of sprue marks and flash.

Clutch power runs on the tight side. Parts seat with a crisp click, and finished models hold together firmly without coming apart. The trade-off is that you need to press with some force, so your hands will feel tired during a long continuous build. The parts are compatible with mainstream brick systems and the tolerances are well controlled — mixing them into an existing collection caused no binding or stiffness.

The pleasant surprise is that some sets now use printed elements instead of stickers. That gives sharper detail and makes the finished model photograph much better in a review shoot.

## Set Value Analysis

At a comparable part count, Qiji typically costs only around 60% of what an international brand charges, which makes the value proposition stand out. The part count printed on the instructions matches what is actually in the box, and there were almost no extra or missing pieces — genuinely rare for a domestic set under the 1000-yuan mark.

Combined with the companion building instructions available on BrickPlans, builders can push the official set further and get several different models out of a single box, which multiplies the real value on the shelf.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

On the plus side: Qiji is affordable, part quality is consistent, printed elements are plentiful, and the unboxing details are thoughtful. It is a high-value pick for beginners and for anyone buying in bulk.

The shortcomings are just as clear. Some set designs play it safe and are less inventive than the top-tier brands. The instructions occasionally split a step across a page turn, or place a position marker less precisely than it should be. A few of the more obscure parts have limited reusability.

On top of that, the tight clutch is not friendly to younger children — these sets suit builders with some experience under their belt.

## Who Should Buy It

If your budget is limited and you are chasing value, or you want to top up your MOC stockpile with a large batch of parts, a Qiji unboxing is well worth trying. Experienced builders chasing uncompromising original design and complex mechanism structures may want to modify a Qiji base rather than build it stock.

Overall, solid manufacturing and approachable pricing have earned Qiji a stable place among domestic brick brands. It is the safe choice when you want to protect both your wallet and your enjoyment.
