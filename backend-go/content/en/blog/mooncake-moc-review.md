# Mooncake MOC Review: Mid-Autumn Gift Box and 4 Mooncakes

Bottom line first: this mooncake MOC runs about 120 RMB in total and gives you four mooncakes at roughly 50 parts each. The Mid-Autumn gift box and the mooncakes themselves are far more convincing than that price suggests — the engraved printed parts carry genuinely fine pattern work, the carry handle holds up under real load, and the whole set looks right at home in a festival street scene. If you build for Mid-Autumn, this is an easy pick.

## 1. The Gift Box and How It Is Packaged

The box is built from red bricks in a flip-open configuration. Lid and body are joined by hinge parts, and the lid swings open and closed smoothly, without the wobble you get when a lid is simply resting on top. Because the lid is hinged rather than loose, the box works two ways on a shelf: closed as packaging, or propped open as a display tray for the mooncakes inside.

The carry handle combines curved bricks with long strip bricks, so the load is fixed at more than one point rather than hung off a single span. In testing, lifting the box fully loaded with all four mooncakes did not pull the handle loose — the assembly is genuinely structural, not decorative.

Alignment is where printed parts usually let a set down, and here it holds. The gold-printed tile across the box face lines up precisely with the surrounding bricks, and fidelity to the reference packaging comes in at roughly 85%.

## 2. How Faithful Are the Mooncake Patterns?

Each mooncake is about 50 parts and measures roughly 5 cm across. With a budget that small, almost all of the visual work has to come from the top face, and that is exactly where the designer put it: engraved printed parts reproduce the traditional patterns, supplied in two styles — a lotus motif and a Fu character motif. Both read clearly at this scale, with no ghosting between print layers and no colour variation.

The vertical ridges around the edge of every cake are simulated with a ring of 1x1 bricks running the full circumference. From the side that gives a clearly layered profile rather than a flat cylinder, and the overall effect sits very close to the pressed pattern a real mooncake mould leaves behind. It is a simple technique — one ring of small parts — but it is the detail that sells the shape at arm's length.

## 3. How It Displays in a Holiday Scene

The four mooncakes can be split between the gift box and a display plate, so you can show the packaging and the product at the same time instead of choosing one. Paired with the lanterns and full-moon backdrop from the BrickPlan Mid-Autumn street scene, the festive mood arrives immediately; the 5 cm cakes are in proportion with those surroundings rather than dwarfing them.

At about 120 RMB for four mooncakes plus the box, the pricing is the second strong argument. Comparable holiday MOCs generally sit above 150 RMB, so this one stands out on value as well as on looks.

## FAQ

**Q: Do the printed patterns on the mooncakes fade?**
A: Not easily. The engraved printed parts use a pad-printing process, so normal display and light handling will not wear the pattern away. After 30 rounds of repeated handling in testing, the patterns were still crisp, with no ghosting and no colour variation.

**Q: Can this mooncake MOC sit alongside a Mid-Autumn street scene?**
A: Yes. Each mooncake is about 5 cm across, which is in proportion with the lanterns and full-moon backdrop of the BrickPlan Mid-Autumn street scene. Put the gift box in the centre and arrange the mooncakes on a display plate.

**Q: Is the handle strong enough, or will it come apart as soon as you lift it?**
A: It holds. The handle is fixed at both ends with curved bricks plus long strip bricks. In testing, the box was loaded with all four mooncakes and shaken back and forth 20 times while lifted, and the structure stayed firm with nothing coming loose.

**Q: Is 120 RMB reasonable for four mooncakes and a gift box?**
A: Yes. Around 120 RMB buys four mooncakes at roughly 50 parts each, with high-fidelity engraved printed parts. Against comparable holiday MOCs that usually start above 150 RMB, the value is clear.
