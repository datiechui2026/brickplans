# Basketball Court MOC Review: 32x32 Streetball, 1,850 Pieces

Here is the short version: this basketball court MOC is a strong piece of design. Around 1,850 parts on a single 32x32 baseplate, a rim that actually holds a mini basketball, a scoreboard and other interactive touches, all for roughly 160 yuan. Street-scene builders and basketball fans both get their money's worth.

## 1. Parts and Fidelity

The entire scene sits on one 32x32 baseplate and recreates the half-court layout of a standard street court. The three-point line, the free-throw line and the centre circle are all picked out in differently coloured bricks, giving roughly 90% visual fidelity to a real court.

The backboard, rim and net are built as separate sub-assemblies. The rim is sized to grip a mini basketball, so a shot can genuinely drop through the net rather than bouncing off a solid ring.

About 85% of the parts are common standard bricks, and the transparent elements are used only for the scoreboard's lighting effect. That makes the model easy to source: you will not be hunting for rare moulds to finish it.

## 2. Build Experience

The instructions run to 210 steps, with 5-8 parts per step. Our build took about 5 hours. Laying the floor pattern is by far the slowest stretch, and sorting the parts into colour-separated boxes beforehand cuts finding time by roughly 30%.

The one genuinely fiddly step is locking the angle of the hoop bracket. You have to line the assembly up with the angle marks in the instructions, and it rewards a little patience. Everything after that flows smoothly, and a beginner working with some guidance can complete the model independently.

## 3. Details and Value

The scene comes with a scoreboard, a bench, a drinks machine and minifigures, so there is plenty to interact with once the court is finished. The instructions cost about 25 yuan and the loose parts pack about 135 yuan, putting the full build at roughly 160 yuan. Official street-scene sets of a similar footprint routinely start at 500 yuan and climb from there, which makes this MOC stand out on value.

The finished model stands about 18 cm tall, small enough for a display cabinet and easy to slot into a larger city layout.
