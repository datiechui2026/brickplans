# What Age Is Bloks For? A Guide by Brick Size

Short answer first: Bloks spans ages 1.5 to 12, and what actually decides whether a child takes to a set is brick size, not the age band printed on the box. Pick the large-brick enlightenment series for 1.5-3, scene-based theme sets for 3-6, and small-brick mechanical sets from 6 upward. Choosing the wrong brick size is the real reason a child stops playing.

**Step one: ages 1.5-3, large bricks from the enlightenment series**

Children at this stage are still mouthing objects and still developing their grip, so bricks need to be at least 3 cm across to rule out any swallowing risk. A single brick in the Bloks enlightenment series weighs roughly 6 g, with rounded corners and no rough flashing, sized for an 18-month-old's hand. Sets include instructions for 10 or more basic builds, and 15 minutes of adult help is enough to finish one together.

**Step two: ages 3-6, scene and character sets**

After three, children move into pretend play, so look for sets with figures, animals and locations — a fire station or zoo theme, for example. These run 100-300 pieces and are easy to assemble. The value is in scenario imagination and language, and a child can complete about 80% of the build independently.

**Step three: ages 6-9, small bricks and simple mechanisms**

By six, fine motor control is mature enough for the switch to small bricks. Choose simple mechanism sets with gears and axles, 300-600 pieces, where the manual is broken into clear steps of no more than eight parts each. A child can then follow the instructions without help, which is a real confidence payoff.

**Step four: ages 9-12, complex mechanisms and remote control**

At this stage the options open up to 600-1500 piece remote-control cars and excavators, including motors and gearboxes. A Bloks 9+ set takes roughly 8-12 hours to build, and can be modified afterwards together with an adult — far more play value than a static display model.

**Step five: choose the theme by the child's interest, not the adult's**

Age band is the floor; interest is the ceiling. A dinosaur-obsessed child will not build an expensive castle just because you like it. Ask what theme the child wants first, then pick a piece count that suits their age band. That is the best answer to the question of what age Bloks is for.

## FAQ

**Q: Bloks or LEGO Duplo for a toddler — which is the better first brick?**

A: Both have bricks that are large enough and safe enough. Bloks wins on value for money and on themes drawn from everyday life that feel familiar to a child here. For a 1.5-3 introduction, large Bloks bricks are entirely sufficient; once the child passes four you can start mixing in small bricks, which makes the transition smoother.

**Q: Can a three-year-old play with small bricks?**

A: Not recommended. A three-year-old is at high risk of swallowing, and small bricks are under 1 cm across, so they are easy to swallow by accident. Small-brick building also demands fine motor control a three-year-old usually does not have, which leads to frustration. Wait until at least five and a half before trying small bricks.

**Q: Are Bloks parts compatible with other brands?**

A: Large bricks are broadly compatible with mainstream large-brick systems; with small bricks it depends on the specific connection. Bloks small bricks use a standard stud design and are more than 90% compatible with mainstream brands on the market. Before mixing a whole collection, buy one small pack of loose parts and test-build with it.

**Q: My child only takes models apart and never builds — what now?**

A: This is normal, and taking apart is exploration too. Try a turn-taking game — "you dismantle, I build" — or deliberately assemble one part incorrectly and let the child find the mistake. Turning dismantling into a game with a job attached works better than forcing the child to follow the instructions.

**Q: How many years will one set stay in use?**

A: Large-brick sets are typically outgrown in 1-2 years as the child gets older, while small-brick mechanical sets can stay in play for 3-4 years. Buy for the current age band rather than stockpiling — children's interests shift faster than we expect.
