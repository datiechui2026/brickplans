## Build Difficulty

**Difficulty rating: ★★★☆☆ (intermediate)**

GaoZhuan MOC instructions span a wide range, from 300–500 piece sets that suit newcomers to 2000+ piece builds aimed at advanced builders. If this is your first time working with GaoZhuan parts, start with a mid-size plan in the 500–800 piece range and get used to how the elements seat and pull apart. Some Technic designs assume a working knowledge of gear trains, so it is worth keeping the official documentation on hand as a reference while you build.

## Part Review

GaoZhuan parts stand out in four areas:

- **Mould precision: ★★★★★** GaoZhuan's injection moulding sits at the front of the domestic brands. Edges are smooth with no flash, and sprue marks are cleanly finished.
- **Clutch power: ★★★★☆** The fit is moderate — parts do not fall off on their own, and they are not so tight that pressing them hurts your fingers.
- **Colour consistency: ★★★★☆** Batch-to-batch colour variation is well controlled, and dark colours are particularly strong.
- **Specialist part range: ★★★★☆** Gears, liftarms, Technic panels and transparent elements are all available, which covers the needs of the vast majority of MOC designs.

As for where to buy GaoZhuan parts, the most convenient routes are the official GaoZhuan store and authorised Taobao shops. Individual parts are priced by weight, which makes them very good value. Paired with the free instructions on [brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn), you can buy exactly the parts a model calls for and avoid ending up with a pile of spares.

## Build Experience

We picked a GaoZhuan MOC instruction set of roughly 1,200 pieces for a hands-on test. The steps were clearly laid out and the parts were easy to sort. From the chassis through to the exterior detailing, the whole build took about six hours. The clutch feel of the GaoZhuan elements was the memorable part: on large structures the connections between sections stayed very solid, and the finished model had no looseness when handled.

## Finished Model

The finished build delivered satisfying detail. GaoZhuan's transparent elements transmit light well, which makes them a good fit for lighting kits, and the Technic parts run smoothly with no catching in the gear train. Overall the look and feel comes close to original LEGO quality, at roughly one third of the cost.
