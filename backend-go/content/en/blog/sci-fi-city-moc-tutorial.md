# Sci-Fi City MOC Tutorial: Build a Cyberpunk LEGO City

Towering future skyscrapers, glaring neon and impossible flying machines — a sci-fi city is the dream project for a lot of brick builders. This sci-fi city MOC tutorial walks you through building a cyberpunk future metropolis from zero, so you can let your imagination run across the baseplate.

## Sci-Fi City MOC Tutorial: Theme and Planning

### Lock In the City Style

Before you place a single brick, decide what kind of city you are building. This tutorial suggests picking one baseline: cyberpunk, space colony, or near-future metropolis. Each style comes with its own colour palette and architectural language, so nailing the theme first keeps every later decision pointed in the same direction.

### Sketch the Layout

Draw the city plan on paper before you build: where the main buildings sit, where the streets run, where the overpasses and sky corridors connect. Sensible zoning makes the whole build process smoother and gives the finished model far more credibility and story.

## Sci-Fi City MOC Tutorial: Future Building Structure

### Towering Skyscrapers

Skyscrapers are the stars of any sci-fi city. Build them in modular layers, one storey at a time, combining transparent glass bricks with metallic-coloured bricks. Offset the layers or add sloped sections to create a silhouette that reads as futuristic rather than generic. Rooftops are a great place to mount antennas and satellite dishes to push the tech feel further.

### Floating and Suspended Structures

Suspended structures and sky corridors are signature elements of the genre. Use transparent supports or anti-gravity style structural pieces to make buildings appear to hover — this is the advanced layer of the tutorial. Walkways linking separate towers add depth and make the whole city feel three-dimensional instead of a flat row of facades.

## Sci-Fi City MOC Tutorial: Neon and Lighting Effects

### Making Neon Signs

Cyberpunk does not work without blazing neon. Use coloured transparent bricks and light-up elements to build neon signage, then add printed parts or stickers for lettering and symbols to instantly sell the cyberpunk mood. Layer several colours of light so they cross and overlap, and the city glows once the room goes dark.

### Using a Lighting System

If you want the model to really hit, wire in a lighting system. Hide LED elements inside the buildings so cool-toned light leaks out through the gaps in the structure, mimicking a future city at night. Pair that with a dark backdrop and a sensible camera angle, and the finished result is genuinely striking.

## Sci-Fi City MOC Tutorial: Details and Atmosphere

### Future Vehicles

Sprinkle flying craft, maglev trains and robotic cars along the streets and through the air and the city comes alive. These vehicles are small and structurally simple, which makes them perfect accent pieces in a sci-fi city MOC — they add motion and energy to the scene without eating your whole parts budget.

### Minifigures and Street Detail

A rich cast of minifigures and street-level detail is the soul of the scene. Populate the city with a mix of characters — mechanically augmented cyborgs, hackers, street vendors — and add neon awnings, holographic projections and piles of junk and clutter. That is what turns a clean architectural study into a place with stories and grit.

## Closing Thoughts

By now you have the full workflow: theme and planning, structural building, then lighting and atmosphere. There is no single correct answer in sci-fi city building — push your own ideas hard, and your future metropolis is already waiting to appear on the baseplate.
