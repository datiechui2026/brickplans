# Brick Clinic MOC Tutorial: 2,800-Piece Medical Scene Build

The short version: this clinic MOC uses about 2,800 pieces, measures roughly 54×42 cm when finished, takes around 9 hours to build at 2 hours a day, and costs about 520 yuan in parts. The design is an open cross-section build, with three functional zones lined up side by side: reception desk, exam room and pharmacy. White walls are accented with light green and light blue for a clean, bright look. The instructions come from the brickplan platform, and the parts list is split by zone. It is a classic companion build for a city street scene.

## Step 1: Lay the Base and Build the Zone Walls

Start with a 32×24 stud baseplate. Following the instructions, use white plates to divide it into three zones: reception desk on the left as you enter, exam room in the middle, pharmacy on the right. Run all partition walls to a uniform height of 14 brick courses and leave connection points along the top for a ceiling later. The walls are predominantly white, with a light green wainscot on the exam room side and light blue on the pharmacy side. This step uses roughly 900 pieces.

## Step 2: Complete the Reception Desk and Waiting Area

The reception desk is an L-shaped counter. On the countertop go a computer, a telephone and a registration book. In front of it, set a red cross tile into the floor. The waiting area gets two rows of benches, with a height chart and a green potted plant standing alongside. Hang a price list on the inside of the counter, built from 1×2 printed tiles. This module uses about 500 pieces.

## Step 3: Assemble the Exam Room Equipment

The exam room is the visual centre of the whole build: an adjustable exam bed, a cart with an instrument tray, a floor-standing exam lamp, plus a sink and a desk. The bed is built from white plates, and the head end can be raised. The exam lamp is made from a silver bent tube with a shade on the end, and its angle can be adjusted. Put an anatomy chart sticker on the wall. This module uses about 800 pieces.

## Step 4: Pharmacy and Finishing Details

The pharmacy gets a row of medicine cabinets, with the compartments built from small transparent drawer pieces holding coloured medicine bottle pieces inside; a cash register sits in front of the counter. Finish up with the red cross sign over the entrance, the eave lights and the tactile paving tiles on the ground outside. Once everything is done, check your parts against the zone-by-zone list on the brickplan instruction page. White bricks are used in large quantities, so it is worth buying enough in one go.
