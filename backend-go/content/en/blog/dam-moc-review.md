# Dam MOC Review: 1,680 Pieces for a Working Spillway

The short answer first: this dam MOC is a very complete design. At 1,680 pieces and roughly six hours of building, it suits builders aged 12 and up; the one weak spot is a spillway gate linkage that is a little involved and asks for patience from newcomers. Overall score: 8.5/10, and worth the download.

### Drawing Structure and Readability

The instructions use colour step images with part numbers called out, spread across 86 steps, each held to 3-5 pieces. The logic is clear and easy to follow, and the three main zones — spillway gates, powerhouse and dam body — are cleanly separated so you always know where you are in the build. The PDF runs about 42 MB and you can zoom in to check fine details. Search "dam MOC" on BrickPlan to find the listing, which also includes a 3D rotating preview of the finished model.

### Parts List and Cost

The parts list totals 1,680 pieces. Standard bricks make up 72% of that; the special parts — curved dam-face elements and transparent water-splash pieces — account for about 180 pieces. At an average of ¥0.12 per domestic part, sourcing everything from your own stock or a parts order comes to roughly ¥200. Buying the dedicated parts pack instead lands at about ¥268 and includes 12 transparent blue splash-effect pieces. That puts the value in the upper-middle range: you are not saving a fortune, but you are getting a lot of model for the money.

### Build Experience and Accuracy

Total build time is around six hours, split into the dam body (3 hours), the gate linkage (1.5 hours) and the powerhouse plus decoration (1.5 hours). The gates raise and lower through a knob-driven linkage, and the finished model recreates the real thing to about 85% — no small achievement at this scale. It measures 52 cm long and 24 cm tall, so it makes a genuinely imposing desk piece.

The only real complaint is steps 47-52, where the gear train needs repeated adjustment to mesh cleanly. The practical fix is to pre-assemble the gears exactly as drawn, test that they turn without binding, and only then fix them into the frame — rather than forcing them once they are already buried in the dam body.

### Value for Money

Comparable official water-engineering sets routinely run past ¥500. Here the drawing plus loose parts comes in around ¥268, and because you are sourcing your own bricks you can recolour the model freely. It is a good fit for builders who like engineering and machinery subjects and already have some large-model experience behind them.
