# Auldey Instructions Lost? Find PDFs and Replacement Parts

It is a familiar frustration for Auldey builders: the box is still on the shelf, but the instruction booklet has disappeared. The problem is especially annoying with discontinued mech and dinosaur sets. Stopping halfway through a build because the plans are missing can feel like the only option. In practice, losing Auldey instructions is a well-worn problem with a clear set of solutions. Work through the channels below in order, and most sets can be matched with an electronic copy.

## Official channels: WeChat account and website first

Auldey runs an official WeChat account and an official website in China. For many recent releases, the instruction booklets are posted as PDFs in the 'Service Support' or 'Download Center' sections. The key is the product number printed on the packaging, which is usually a run of digits beginning with AU. Enter that number and look for the matching plan. If the website does not have the file, contact official customer service by email. Include the product number and the channel where you bought the set; support can usually send an electronic copy.

## Communities and secondhand platforms: the old builders' database

For sets that have been discontinued, the official route often comes up empty. That is when brick communities and secondhand platforms become useful. Many experienced builders scan their manuals and share the files. Search for 'product number + instructions' on Baidu Tieba, in Bilibili articles, and on sites dedicated to brick plans; the hit rate is often high. Check the version number before you build. The same product number can cover different production batches, and small details may differ between them.

## Missing parts? Know the replacement process

If the plans turn up but parts are missing, start by checking the model against its parts list before assembly. Confirm what is actually short, then begin the replacement request. Official replacement service usually asks for proof of purchase and the product number, and the process commonly takes one to two weeks. If you are impatient to keep building, borrow common parts from your own stock; many car body and weapon sections can be built with substitutes. Photograph the missing parts before you make contact, so the conversation is easier.

## Prevent it happening again: one habit that fixes it

After this problem is solved, take one preventive step: photograph or scan the manual and save it in your phone's photo album, with the file named by product number. If the manual goes missing again, you can pull it up in five minutes. For the paper copy, use a clear document bag and keep it with the set's box so it does not end up mixed into a pile of parts from other sets.

## Final notes

The practical priority for recovering plans is: official WeChat account > official website download area > official customer service > communities and secondhand platforms. For missing parts, follow the order of counting, documenting, and submitting. A little patience can bring an old set back to full strength.
