# Brick Mushroom MOC Review: A Cozy Forest Scene for Your Desk

The verdict up front: this mushroom MOC is the forest-scene build I have enjoyed most in recent months. Roughly 260 parts turn into three mushrooms of different sizes standing on a moss-covered base, the whole thing goes together in about an hour, and value for money comes in at 9/10. On a desk, or parked beside a tree house build, it is a calm, cheering little model — one worth buying.

## Look and Accuracy to the Real Thing

The caps are the heart of the model, built from curved slope bricks stacked layer by layer until the dome closes. The red-and-white scheme is the classic one and it holds up: the base colour is even, and the spots are scattered at irregular intervals rather than in a neat grid, which is what gives the model the character of a real fly agaric.

Height variation does a lot of work here. The three mushrooms sit at different levels instead of lining up, and together with the moss elements scattered across the green baseplate they sell the damp, quiet feeling of a forest floor. There is not much to it — baseplate, stems, caps, grass — but the composition reads instantly, and looking at it is genuinely relaxing.

## The Build

Construction follows a logical order: moss on the baseplate first, then the stems and caps of the three mushrooms, then the small grass tufts as the final dressing. The step breakdown in the instructions is sensible and never fussy. The one part that asks for patience is stacking the curved sections of the caps, where each ring has to seat properly before the next goes on — but the result justifies the care.

The whole mushroom MOC takes about an hour. That makes it a light, low-pressure project, the kind you can pick up after work, and also a friendly one to build with a child while talking about how a forest floor actually works.

## Part Quality

Moulding precision is good and the clutch sits right in the middle — not so loose that things shift, not so tight that you fight the parts. Red and white are clean, with no colour mixing or shade difference between pieces of the same colour. The baseplate is flat and stays flat once it is on the table, with no curling at the edges, and the finished model feels solid in the hand.

The greens are nicely separated too: the moss elements and the grass tufts sit in clearly different tones, so the ground does not read as one flat mass. The only real complaint is quantity — there are relatively few white spot pieces in the box, so building a whole cluster of mushrooms means ordering extra parts.

## Value for Money

At about 69 yuan for 260 parts, the maths works out to roughly 0.27 yuan per piece, which is friendly pricing by any standard. More importantly, the design gives you something budget sets at the same price usually do not: a scene with actual composition rather than a pile of bricks. It lifts the mood of a shelf on its own, and that is why I score it 9/10 and recommend it without much hesitation.

**Q: Can the mushroom MOC be displayed with a tree house set?**
