# Brick Piano MOC Review: A Desktop Piano You Can Play

The short version: this brick piano MOC is one of the most popular instrument instructions on brickplan. It uses roughly 1,450 parts and measures 26 cm long by 18 cm tall when finished. The black and white keys genuinely press down and produce sound, the model is about 85% faithful to a real grand piano, and it takes around 6 hours to build. Sourcing the loose parts costs about 300-450 CNY, which makes it one of the few desk pieces you can actually play with rather than just look at.

## 1. Structural Accuracy

The designer uses smooth tiles to shape the curved body of a grand piano. The lid opens and closes, and the music stand, pedals and legs are all present. The real highlight is the keyboard: the black and white keys connect through lever elements to metal tone bars underneath, so pressing a key strikes a bar and produces a clear note. Only about 15 keys actually sound, but that playable action puts it far ahead of purely decorative builds. Overall accuracy is around 85% — in photos it can pass for the real thing.

## 2. Parts and Color Palette

The model uses roughly 1,450 parts, dominated by gloss black and white, with small amounts of dark grey and gold as accents inside the body. The color scheme is restrained and looks premium. The keyboard area separates black and white keys cleanly, and there are no stickers anywhere on the body — everything is done with printed elements and smooth tiles, so it holds up to close inspection. The one small drawback is that solid black parts lose detail in low light, so display it somewhere with decent lighting.

## 3. Build Experience

Working from the PDF instructions on brickplan.cn, our test build took about 6 hours. The steps are split into 6 major stages, and the keyboard linkage mechanism sits in stage 3 — the most interesting part of the whole build. The instructions are clearly labeled, with 10-25 parts per step, which keeps a comfortable rhythm. The one thing to watch: the tone bars need to be aligned precisely with their slots during installation. Tweezers help here, and they stop you from bending the bars.

## 4. Value for Money

Sourcing the loose parts costs about 300-450 CNY, and you can gather everything from the parts list on brickplan.cn. That is roughly 30% cheaper than a finished set of comparable size. Given the playable sound mechanism and the high proportion of printed elements, the price is excellent value. It suits music fans, collectors of desktop models, and makes a fun gift for a child learning piano. Anyone aged 12 and up can complete it independently.
