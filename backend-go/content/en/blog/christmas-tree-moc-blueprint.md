# LEGO Christmas Tree MOC: Blueprint Guide and Build Tutorial

Short version first: beginners should pick a Christmas tree MOC with 800 to 1,200 pieces and a blueprint that numbers each layer. Budget 150 to 300 CNY, aim for a finished height of 35 to 45 cm, and you get something that looks good without burning you out. The rest breaks down into three parts.

## 1. How to Choose a Blueprint

Christmas tree MOC blueprints come in three types: mods of official sets, independent designer blueprints, and free starter blueprints. Official-set mods are built on a green set of roughly 1,000 pieces and produce a tree around 40 cm tall. Independent designer blueprints commonly run past 2,000 pieces and cost around 400 CNY. Free starter blueprints are for practice.

Judge a blueprint on three points: does it state the part count for each layer, does it include step-by-step rendered images, and does it come with a parts list. A good list goes down to color and quantity, for example 48 dark green 2x4 bricks, so you can buy straight from it without guessing.

## 2. How to Prepare the Parts

Step one: total up the quantities against the list. A Christmas tree MOC needs about 900 to 1,500 pieces, with green making up more than 60 percent.

Step two: sort by color. The canopy uses a gradient of dark green and light green, the trunk is brown, and the decorations are red and gold. The rough ratio is 6 to 2 to 2.

Step three: control the budget. Domestic compatible parts run about 0.15 CNY per piece, so 900 pieces comes to roughly 150 CNY. Original-brand parts run about 0.5 CNY per piece, or 450 CNY for the same count. For a first attempt, domestic parts are the sensible choice.

## 3. Step-by-Step Build Tutorial

Step one: build the base. Start from a 24x24 baseplate and run a border of 2x4 bricks around the edge. That is about 80 pieces and 20 minutes.

Step two: build the trunk and the first layer of canopy. The trunk is six 2x2 bricks stacked up. The canopy spirals inward from the outside, losing one ring per layer to form the cone shape.

Step three: work upward layer by layer. Check four-way symmetry at every level so the tree does not lean. A 1,200-piece blueprint breaks into 12 to 15 steps, each taking 30 to 60 minutes.

Step four: add decorations. Place red round plates and gold star-shaped elements along a spiral line, then finish the top with a large star element. The finished tree stands about 40 cm tall.

## FAQ

**Q: Where can I find Christmas tree MOC blueprints?**
A: Check the blueprint library on the brickplan site and independent designer platforms. Favor blueprints that include a parts list and step-by-step renders. Beginners should start with something in the 800 to 1,200 piece range.

**Q: How much does a 900-piece Christmas tree MOC cost?**
A: Domestic compatible parts are about 0.15 CNY per piece, so 900 pieces is roughly 150 CNY. Original-brand parts are about 0.5 CNY per piece, or 450 CNY. Add the baseplate and decorations and the total budget lands between 200 and 500 CNY.

**Q: Can a beginner jump straight to a 2,000-piece-plus Christmas tree MOC?**
A: It is not recommended. Blueprints above 2,000 pieces have many more steps and messier part sorting, and beginners tend to give up. Finish an 800 to 1,200 piece blueprint first, then take on a larger scale once you are comfortable.

**Q: What is the difference between a Christmas tree MOC and an official Christmas set?**
A: An official set has a fixed design and sells as parts plus instructions in one package. A MOC blueprint only provides the design and the parts list, so you are free to swap colors. Choose MOC if you want to customize, choose an official set if you want it simple.

**Q: Is a finished Christmas tree worth leaving on display long term?**
A: Yes. A 40 cm tree takes up less than 0.1 square meters, the structure is stable and does not fall apart easily, and you can break it down for storage after the holidays and rebuild it next year. The parts last for years.
