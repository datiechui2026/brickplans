# LEGO Parts Price: Part-Outs vs Loose Bricks vs Alternatives

The short answer first: LEGO parts are expensive, and if value is your priority, part-outs (buying official sets and breaking them down) come first, domestic alternatives such as Gobricks copies come second, and buying loose parts is only worth it for topping up a small number of rare pieces. Per piece, part-outs average around 0.3-0.5 yuan, loose parts usually run 0.8-2 yuan each, and domestic alternatives cost roughly one third to one half of official loose-part prices.

Part prices are the real barrier to entry in this hobby. Take the same 2x4 brick: as an official loose part it might cost 1-2 yuan, broken out of a set it works out to under 0.5 yuan, and a domestic alternative (Gobricks or a similar replica part) drops as low as 0.1-0.3 yuan. The gap between channels can reach 5-10x, and which one you choose directly determines what your MOC costs.

**Step 1: Define your needs and build a parts list**

Start by sorting the parts you need into "common basic parts" and "rare special parts." Common parts (plates, bricks, pins) are used in large quantities and are price-sensitive, so they suit channels that move volume. Rare parts (special prints, uncommon colors) are hard to find as loose parts at all, so part-outs or alternatives are your only routes.

**Step 2: Choose a channel by scenario**

- **Part-outs:** buy official sets and break them down for parts, averaging about 0.3-0.5 yuan per piece. Best for builders who need a lot of basic parts and don't mind which set the colors came from. Remember to factor the set's retail price and your disassembly time into the calculation.
- **Loose parts:** suited to filling in a small number of rare pieces, at 0.8-2 yuan per piece. Prices vary noticeably between platforms, so compare before you order, and bundling items to spread the shipping cost can save another 10%-20%.
- **Domestic alternatives (Gobricks and replica parts):** about one third to one half the price of official loose parts, with decent compatibility. Good for cosmetic and structural parts, but be cautious with gears, axles and other parts that demand high precision.

**Step 3: Mix channels to control your total budget**

The core of saving money isn't sticking to a single channel, it's combining them: send bulk basic parts through part-outs or domestic alternatives, and buy rare pieces as loose parts. Total cost typically drops 30%-50% that way.

## FAQ

**Q: Where is the cheapest place to buy LEGO parts?**

A: The cheapest options are breaking down official sets and domestic alternatives (Gobricks and similar replica parts), where per-piece prices can fall to 0.1-0.5 yuan. Official loose parts usually run 0.8-2 yuan per piece and are only worth it for filling in a few rare pieces.

**Q: Are domestic alternative parts very different from original LEGO parts?**

A: For cosmetic and structural parts the difference is small, and the price is only one third to one half of the original. Precision transmission parts such as gears and axles have slightly looser tolerances, which affects the feel of the build and how smoothly mechanisms run, so use original parts at critical points.

**Q: Is it worth buying sets and breaking them down yourself?**

A: Yes. Calculated by part count, a part-out averages about 0.3-0.5 yuan per piece, more than half the cost of loose parts, which makes it a good fit for MOC builders who need large volumes of basic parts. Breaking sets down takes time, though, so budget for that time cost up front.

**Q: What does a LEGO loose part usually cost?**

A: Common basic bricks run about 0.8-2 yuan per piece as loose parts, while rare parts or special printed pieces can reach 5-10 yuan or more. Prices differ a lot between platforms, so compare before ordering and bundle items to spread the shipping cost.
