# J-20 Stealth Fighter MOC Blueprint Review: 1:48 Scale Build

## Product Overview

The J-20 Wei Long is a fifth-generation stealth fighter, and its sci-fi silhouette has made it a dream subject for aviation fans. This **brick aircraft blueprint** — a J-20 stealth fighter MOC — comes from BrickPlans community-certified designer "BlueSky Craftsman" and recreates the aircraft's aerodynamic layout and detail features at 1:48 scale. The blueprint package includes a complete parts list and a PDF building guide, and you can grab the latest version at [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn).

## Build Difficulty

**Difficulty rating**: ★★★☆☆

Medium difficulty, best suited to builders with some MOC experience. The main challenges are the smooth, streamlined transition across the wings and the deflection mechanism for the canards. Estimated build time: 4–6 hours. If this is your first aircraft MOC, it's worth reading other builders' notes on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) first.

## Parts Review

The blueprint calls for roughly **1,280 parts**, putting it in the 1,000–1,500 pcs range. Recommended brands: **Gobricks** (high part precision, which gives a smoother streamlined exterior) or **SEMBO** (wide color range, good for custom liveries).

Key parts breakdown:

- **Fuselage frame**: heavy use of technic bricks and connectors. Gobricks dark gray parts are recommended here for consistent color tone.
- **Wing panels**: requires large tiles. SEMBO's molding process gives these an excellent surface gloss.
- **Canopy**: needs transparent parts. Buying extra LEGO-compatible transparent canopy elements is advised.
- **Engine nozzles**: round tiles and ring parts recreate the serrated nozzle shape — the result looks outstanding.

## Build Experience

The blueprint is a high-resolution color PDF with 156 steps, and no step uses more than 5 parts, so the logic stays clear throughout. You start with the internal fuselage frame using a "frame plus skin" approach: build the load-bearing structure first, then gradually cover it with the shell. This design makes the finished model extremely rigid.

The wing section is the highlight of the whole build. The designer makes clever use of wedge plates and slope bricks, using SNOT technique to achieve the J-20's signature diamond wing and leading-edge root extensions. The canards are movable and deflect in sync through a gear linkage. The weapons bay holds 4 micro missiles inside, so there's detail everywhere. During the build, it's worth noting any problems you hit in the blueprint's comment section on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) — community members respond very quickly.

## Finished Model

The finished model measures about 38cm × 26cm × 12cm and has serious visual impact. The dark gray livery captures the texture of the J-20's stealth coating, and the cockpit even includes an ejection seat and instrument panel details. Working features include: canard deflection, folding vertical tails, opening and closing weapons bay, and retractable landing gear. Pair it with a transparent display stand to show it in a flight attitude — the effect is striking.

## Recommendation

**Overall rating**: ★★★★★

This J-20 MOC blueprint is a must-have for aviation-themed brick fans. The design is refined, accuracy is high, and the parts are easy to source. Download the high-resolution original blueprint at [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn); it looks best built with Gobricks parts. If you enjoy military aircraft MOCs, this blueprint is absolutely worth a try.
