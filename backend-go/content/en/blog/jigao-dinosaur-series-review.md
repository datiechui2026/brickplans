# JiGao Dinosaur Series Review: Five Popular Dinosaurs Compared

Bottom line first: the JiGao dinosaur line is finished to a higher standard than most domestic sets at the same price point. The Tyrannosaurus and the Triceratops are the two worth buying first. Each set runs 600-1000 pieces at 89-159 yuan, and both accuracy and articulation set the benchmark for the range.

## 1. Model Accuracy

The JiGao dinosaur series covers five core species: Tyrannosaurus, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Spinosaurus and Pteranodon, all modelled from museum reconstruction illustrations. The T-Rex scores high on head proportions and tooth detail, while the Triceratops gets careful colour separation on the scales of its neck frill. Displayed in a cabinet, the finished models have real presence, and from a distance they come close to imported-brand standards.

## 2. Articulation

Articulation is where the JiGao dinosaur series earns extra credit. The Tyrannosaurus has 8 articulated joints across the head, lower jaw, four limbs and tail, and the tail swings up and down as well. The Triceratops has a rotating neck, and the Spinosaurus sail is a separately removable piece. In hands-on testing the joint damping feels about right, and posed stances hold without sagging, which makes them more fun than the static display pieces at the same price.

## 3. Part Quality

JiGao dinosaur sets use ABS, with clean sprue mark finishing and a tight clutch between parts. Nothing during the build showed obvious flash or tolerance problems, and the finished model can be lifted as a whole without falling apart. The instructions are clearly stepped out, and a single set takes roughly 2-4 hours to build, which is friendly to newcomers. Quality control sits in the upper-middle band for domestic production.

## 4. Value for Money

On value, the average cost per piece across the JiGao dinosaur series is about 0.15 yuan, below comparable LEGO themes. Prioritise the Tyrannosaurus (159 yuan) and the Triceratops (129 yuan); the Stegosaurus and Pteranodon start at 89 yuan and make good entry points. Buying all five at once also earns a set discount, saving roughly 60 yuan, which suits dinosaur fans collecting the whole range.

## FAQ

**Q: Which JiGao dinosaur is the best buy?**
A: The Tyrannosaurus and Triceratops are the strongest recommendations. With 600-1000 pieces and prices of 89-159 yuan, they deliver the best model accuracy and the 8 articulated joints in the line.

**Q: What age are JiGao dinosaur sets suitable for?**
A: The official rating is 6 and up. The Stegosaurus and Pteranodon have simpler structures and suit ages 6-8, while the Tyrannosaurus uses slightly finer parts and is better for ages 8 and up, or for building with a parent present.

**Q: Can JiGao dinosaurs be mixed with other bricks?**
A: Yes. JiGao dinosaurs use standard stud connections and are compatible with mainstream domestic and imported brick parts, so you can also modify and combine the dinosaur designs yourself.

**Q: Are JiGao dinosaur sets hard to build?**
A: Not really. A single set takes about 2-4 hours, and the instructions are clearly stepped out. For some sets you can also find companion plans and builder troubleshooting tips on BrickPlan.
