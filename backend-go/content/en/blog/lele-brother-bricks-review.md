# Lele Brothers Bricks Review: Quality and Value Tested

The short version: Lele Brothers is a value-focused Chinese brick brand. Parts are acceptable, clutch power is good, most sets land between 20 and 150 yuan, and the average works out to roughly 15–20 yuan per 100 pieces. It suits beginners and collectors alike. Overall score: 8/10 — worth a try.

## 1. How Faithful Are the Models?

Lele Brothers focuses on street scenes, military subjects and car models, and its accuracy sits in the upper-middle range of domestic brands. Street scene sets use harmonious colour schemes and pack in plenty of detail, while car models have clean lines and sharply printed liveries. Some military sets are well executed, though a handful of individual parts are simplified — impressive from a distance, with a small gap up close. Overall, the models are fair for the price.

## 2. Build Experience

Instructions are clearly printed and the steps are sensibly divided, averaging 3–5 parts per step, which makes the sets beginner-friendly. Most builds take between 1 and 3 hours; a street scene shop of around 800 pieces, for example, runs about 2 hours. Parts come in numbered bags, so finding pieces is easy. A small number of sets arrive with missing parts, but the official replacement service responds quickly, and the experience is smooth on the whole.

## 3. Part Quality

Parts are made from ABS, and the feel is close to that of mainstream big brands. Clutch power is moderate — the occasional part is very tight or very loose, but it does not affect the overall structure. Brick surfaces have good gloss with no obvious burrs, and printed elements are crisp and resist fading. All told, Lele Brothers sits in the stable, dependable tier of domestic brick brands when it comes to part quality.

## 4. Value for Money

This is where Lele Brothers wins. At the same piece count, its prices are usually only a quarter to a third of what international brands charge. A large 1500-piece scene set, for instance, sells for about 180 yuan — roughly 0.12 yuan per piece. That makes it ideal for builders on a tight budget who want to try a wide range of subjects. Value score: 9/10.

## FAQ

**Q: How good is the quality of Lele Brothers bricks?**
A: Quality is stable and reliable. Parts are ABS with moderate clutch power and clear printing. You may occasionally hit a part that is too tight or a set with a missing piece, but the official replacement service is prompt, and the overall product is worth the price.

**Q: Are Lele Brothers bricks compatible with LEGO?**
A: Fully compatible. Lele Brothers uses standard brick dimensions, so its parts mix and connect seamlessly with LEGO and other mainstream brands — handy when you want to combine sets into a larger scene or do your own creative modifications.

**Q: How much does a Lele Brothers set usually cost?**
A: Prices are friendly: most sets fall between 20 and 150 yuan, averaging about 15–20 yuan per 100 pieces. There is plenty of choice across street scenes, military and car models, which suits beginners on a limited budget.

**Q: What is the difference between Lele Brothers and LEGO?**
A: The main differences are price and some fine detail. Lele Brothers costs roughly a quarter to a third of LEGO, and its clutch and hand feel are slightly behind, but overall performance is acceptable. Value is the biggest highlight.

**Q: Are Lele Brothers sets good gifts for children?**
A: Yes. Most sets have a moderate difficulty level and clear instructions, and the parts meet safety standards. As a gift for a child it builds hands-on skills, and at these prices you won't mind if a build gets broken.
