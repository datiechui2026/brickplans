# Where to Find StarBricks Instructions: Official and Fan Sources

Lose the manual from a StarBricks (XINGBAO) set, or buy a loose batch of parts that never came with one, and the first instinct for most builders is to go online and buy a replacement copy. In practice there are faster and cheaper routes. Here are three paths — official, fan-run and platform-based — plus what to do when the instruction file you find is incomplete.

## 1. Identify the Box and Item Number First — Versions Differ

StarBricks has released sets across many themes, including modular street scenes, military subjects and architecture. The same model may exist in two production batches, an older version and a newer one, with parts and building steps that are not identical. Before searching, check the side of the box or the cover of the manual for the item number (usually an X followed by digits) and note which theme series the set belongs to. Searching with the item number prevents you from downloading the wrong version and building yourself into a corner. This is the key precondition for finding StarBricks instructions — get the version right and everything after it goes smoothly.

## 2. Official Channels: WeChat Account and Customer Service

StarBricks runs an instruction replacement service. Follow the brand's official WeChat account; the menu usually includes entries such as "Manuals" or "Instruction Downloads", where you enter the item number to view or download the matching PDF. If your set is not listed there, leave a message on the account or contact official customer service directly, stating the item number and exactly what is missing. Customer service will generally send you the electronic file. This channel is legitimate and free, and it is the first option to try — nearly every genuine boxed set can be covered this way.

## 3. Player Communities: Tieba, QQ Groups and Forum Archive Packs

When official channels come up empty, player communities are the richest supplement. The Tieba boards where StarBricks fans gather, various brick-building QQ groups and forums all have members who have compiled instruction collections for past sets. Searching "item number + instructions" frequently turns up scanned or re-photographed copies shared by helpful builders. After joining a group, check the group files and pinned announcements first — plenty of groups keep instructions in a shared folder. If you still cannot find it, post a request stating the item number, the page numbers you are missing and how clear a copy you need; a helpful reply usually arrives quickly.

## 4. Instruction Platforms and Scan Sites: Keyword Search Tactics

The third category is instruction platforms and scan sites. Global MOC databases such as Rebrickable include some player-made instructions for StarBricks subjects. In a search engine, try keyword patterns like "item number + manual" and "item number + instructions", and pair them with image search to surface scans of decent quality. When searching, remember to distinguish official instructions from player MOC instructions: official files have complete steps but may be low in resolution, while player versions are often sharper but may have substituted parts, so check that the steps are complete after downloading.

## 5. Missing Pages and Replacement Parts: Check the List Before You Order

If the manual you end up with is missing pages or too blurry to read, do not throw it out. First check the parts list against the quantities in front of you — often only a handful of common bricks are short, and you can substitute bricks of the same size you already own (for example, two 1x1 bricks in place of one 1x2 dark gray brick). If pages are missing, ask the community: someone with a complete copy can photograph the missing pages, or you can work backwards from a photo of the finished model to reconstruct those steps. Only go to the official replacement service or a parts shop when you genuinely need bricks, and describe them using the brick types printed on the list — stud dimensions such as 2x4 or 1x8 — rather than vague wording like "long dark gray strip".

The priority order is simple: try the official WeChat account and customer service first, then fan community archive packs, and only then platform scans. Missing pages are best solved by checking the parts list and substituting what you already have.
