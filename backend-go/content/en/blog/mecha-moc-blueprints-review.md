## 4. Mecha MOC Building Tips

Whichever blueprint you choose, these techniques will improve your build:

1. **Sort by color first:** mecha MOCs usually involve large part counts, so sort everything by color before you start and you will cut part-hunting time by 80%
2. **Frame first, armor later:** finish the inner frame and test the joints before attaching armor, so you know articulation meets your standard
3. **Counterweight matters:** large mecha tend to end up top-heavy, so add counterweight parts at the feet and waist
4. **Make good use of SNOT:** mecha armor relies heavily on side building, and solid SNOT (Studs Not On Top) skills will make your mecha much more refined
