# LEGO City Buying Guide: Fire, Police, Trains and Arctic

The short answer first: the two classic themes, fire and police, are the safest buys in LEGO City. If you want the best value, aim at mid-size sets in the 600-800 piece range. If you want a display centerpiece, go straight to 60292 City Center. Train fans should look at 60336 Freight Train, which works out to roughly CNY 0.55 per piece, the best cost-per-part ratio in the entire line.

LEGO City is the first set in many households, but the range is huge and the set numbers are confusing: small vehicles cost a little over CNY 100, while flagship models run close to CNY 2000. Beginners can easily lose track. This review compares the themes side by side so your money goes where it counts.

## Why LEGO City Is Worth Buying

City is positioned as an entry line for ages 4-8, but it is a mature one. The parts are mostly basic bricks, the building logic is clear, the play scenarios are strong, and it is fully compatible with Technic, Creator and every other standard-brick system. Against adult lines that start at four figures, City averages CNY 0.6-1.0 per piece, which makes it the value choice for beginners and for gifts.

## Set-by-Set Reviews

**60320 Fire Station:** a two-storey building plus a fire truck and a helicopter. The 779 pieces take about two hours to build, the three-level structure is sturdy, and the "call-out" scenario is what kids play with most. At CNY 599 it scores highest overall and is the set that anchors the whole range.

**60316 Police Station:** 674 pieces with a jail and a police car. The storytelling is strong, and it suits two children playing against each other. The downside is a heavy sticker sheet; if that bothers you, choose a CNY 100-class small set such as 60312 Police Patrol Car instead.

**60292 City Center:** the 1517-piece display piece, containing five modules including a bakery, a pizza shop and a pet shop. Official price is CNY 1199, about CNY 0.79 per piece. It makes a good birthday or holiday gift and looks good on a shelf too.

**60336 Freight Train:** 1153 pieces including a motorized locomotive, and the ceiling for playability. At roughly CNY 0.55 per piece it is the best value in the line. The downside is that track layouts need about 2 square meters of floor space, so measure first if you live in a small apartment.

## Choosing by Budget and Age

1. Under CNY 200, pick small sets such as police cars and fire trucks. At 91-200 pieces they build in about 20 minutes and suit a 4-year-old starting out.
2. With CNY 400-600, put 60320 Fire Station or 60316 Police Station first. This is where value peaks.
3. Above CNY 1000, look at 60336 Freight Train or 60292 City Center and buy once rather than working up in steps.
4. If your child is 6 or older and likes exploration themes, then consider 60368 Icebreaker.

## FAQ

**Q: Which LEGO City set is the best buy?**
A: On overall value, 60320 Fire Station: 779 pieces for about CNY 599, with buildings, vehicles and minifigure scenarios all included. On a tighter budget, choose 60316 Police Station: 674 pieces for about CNY 499, with stronger storytelling.

**Q: What age is LEGO City aimed at?**
A: The official marking is 4+. In practice a 4-year-old can build the small sets, and a 6-7-year-old can finish the 600-800 piece mid-size sets independently. From 8 up, children tend to find it too simple and can move to Technic or the adult lines.

**Q: How is City different from the Friends line?**
A: City focuses on realistic scenarios such as police, fire, trains and construction vehicles, and appeals to both boys and girls. Friends centers on friendship stories and pastel colors, with more detailed minidolls, and tends to be better received by girls.

**Q: What price counts as a good deal on a City set?**
A: Wait for 60-80% of retail on standard sets. For example, 60316 Police Station dropping from CNY 499 to under CNY 350 is a good price. For retired and hot sets, buy at full retail instead; the longer you wait, the more expensive they get.

**Q: Can LEGO City sets be mixed with other lines?**
A: Yes. Every City set uses standard bricks, so it mixes with Technic, Creator and even compatible third-party brands. Once the model is built, rebuild it from instructions at https://brickplan.cn and the play value doubles.
