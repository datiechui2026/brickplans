# One Piece MOC Plans Review: Luffy and the Straw Hats in Bricks

One Piece has been running for more than twenty years, and Luffy and his Straw Hat crew have fans in every corner of the world. One Piece replica plans let brick builders recreate the adventures of the Grand Line out of real bricks. This review walks through several of the most popular plans available.

## 1. Popular One Piece MOC Plans

### 1. Luffy Gear 4 Form
- **Plan name**: Monkey D. Luffy Gear 4 Snake Man form
- **Piece count**: about 1,000
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Recommended rating**: ★★★★★

This One Piece replica plan captures the muscle lines of Luffy's Gear 4 Snake Man form and the black Armament Haki coating. The red cape is built from large curved slope pieces, and the elastic joints on the arms are designed to suggest the stretching effect of the Gum-Gum Fruit.

### 2. Zoro's Three-Sword Style
- **Plan name**: Roronoa Zoro three-sword combat pose
- **Piece count**: about 750
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Recommended rating**: ★★★★★

Zoro's three swords — Wado Ichimonji, Sandai Kitetsu and Shusui — each get their own distinct treatment. The curled green hair is achieved by stacking slope bricks, and the scar across his chest is reproduced with a sticker.

### 3. Thousand Sunny
- **Plan name**: Thousand Sunny 1:100 scale model
- **Piece count**: about 2,500
- **Difficulty**: High
- **Recommended rating**: ★★★★★

The Thousand Sunny is the Straw Hats' flagship and one of the most demanding builds among One Piece replica plans. The lion-head bow, the grassy deck and the Gaon Cannon launcher are all rendered in detail. Expect a build time of roughly 20–30 hours.

### 4. Sanji
- **Plan name**: Sanji Black Leg combat form
- **Piece count**: about 600
- **Difficulty**: Low to medium
- **Recommended rating**: ★★★★☆

Sanji's blond hair and suit are recreated with dark-colored bricks and printed pieces. The flame effect on his right leg is built from transparent orange bricks and looks striking on display.

## 2. Key Building Points

### 1. Recreating Devil Fruit Powers

Devil Fruit abilities such as the Gum-Gum Fruit and the Flame-Flame Fruit are one of the biggest challenges in these builds. The usual approach is to combine transparent effect pieces with flexible connectors to simulate the ability in action.

### 2. The Straw Hat

Luffy's straw hat is the signature element of any figure. It is built from large round plates and curved slopes, with the brim tilted slightly upward.

### 3. Ship Structure

The Thousand Sunny's hull calls for large quantities of reddish-brown and blue bricks, and the interior structure is complex. Building it step by step from a digital plan is strongly recommended.
