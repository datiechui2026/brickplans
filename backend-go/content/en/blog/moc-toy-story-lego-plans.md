# Toy Story LEGO Plans Review: Building Andy's Room in Bricks

Toy Story was Pixar's first feature film, and the friendship between Woody and Buzz Lightyear still lands with audiences of every age. Toy Story LEGO plans let those characters reunite in brick form, and this review covers the most popular builds out there — what each plan contains, how hard it is to assemble, and which details earn it a spot on the shelf.

## Popular Toy Story MOC Plans

### 1. Sheriff Woody
- **Plan name**: Sheriff Woody, standard minifigure scale
- **Part count**: about 400
- **Difficulty**: Low
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

This Toy Story LEGO plan recreates Woody's cowboy outfit. The brown cowboy hat is built from wedge bricks, the red bandana hangs around the neck, and the pull string on his back is made with a flexible hose piece. The Andy signature on the right hand is the finishing touch.

### 2. Buzz Lightyear
- **Plan name**: Buzz Lightyear, Space Ranger build
- **Part count**: about 550
- **Difficulty**: Low to medium
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

The space suit is the center of the build here. The purple and green color scheme relies on bricks in special colors, and the helmet visor uses a transparent blue curved piece. The wings fold out and retract, and the chest buttons are printed elements.

### 3. Andy's Room
- **Plan name**: Andy's Room diorama set
- **Part count**: about 1,200
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

Cloud wallpaper, the letter-shaped bed frame and the toy storage bin — Andy's room is the most nostalgic scene in the whole Toy Story LEGO plan lineup. The cloud pattern above the bed is laid out in white bricks, and the lamp on the nightstand uses a light brick.

### 4. Little Green Men
- **Plan name**: Little Green Men, set of three
- **Part count**: about 200 per figure
- **Difficulty**: Low
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆

The three green aliens each have a different expression, and the three eyes are made with transparent green 1x1 round bricks. Bought as a trio they are genuinely charming and make an easy desk display.

## Build Notes

### 1. Head proportions
Woody and Buzz are both big-head designs, with the head taking up roughly half the total height. Ball joints connect the head to the body, which adds a useful amount of movement.

### 2. Costume details
Woody's checked shirt is built by alternating red and yellow bricks, while Buzz's space suit uses slope and curved bricks to create the look of armor plating.

### 3. Scene construction
Andy's bed frame is built from Technic bricks so the structure can carry weight. The cloud wallpaper is assembled freely from white bricks, which makes it the most forgiving part of the model to customize.
