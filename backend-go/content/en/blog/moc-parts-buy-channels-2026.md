# Where to Buy MOC Parts in 2026: Gobricks vs Taobao vs BrickLink

The short answer first: for 2026 MOC part shopping, **Gobricks parts are the default choice for ordinary builders**, Taobao replica shops are for plugging rare gaps, and BrickLink is only worth the trouble on large bulk orders. Don't follow the crowd that assumes "buying overseas = better quality." Judge a channel on four dimensions: unit price, shipping, delivery time and quality. Here is the breakdown.

## 1. Unit Price Comparison

Gobricks pricing is transparent and sold by weight: basic bricks run about CNY 0.03–0.05 per piece, with special parts a little higher. Taobao replica shops have the lowest unit price, around CNY 0.02 for common bricks, but quality is inconsistent. BrickLink looks expensive at roughly CNY 0.2–0.5 per brick, yet once you assemble an order of a thousand-plus pieces in one go, the average price becomes competitive.

## 2. Shipping and Minimum Order

- **Gobricks parts**: shipping is charged by weight, about CNY 10–15 for anything under one kilogram, with no minimum order. Very friendly for scattered top-up orders.
- **Taobao replica shops**: most offer free shipping on single orders over CNY 50; a small top-up order costing CNY 5–8 in postage is not worth it.
- **BrickLink**: international shipping is the heavy cost. A 2 kg parcel runs about CNY 120–200, so small orders make no sense at all through this route.

## 3. Delivery Time

Gobricks ships from domestic warehouses and arrives in 2–4 days, making it the fastest channel. Taobao replica shops take 1–3 days. BrickLink takes 7–15 days, and a customs hold can stretch that to 3 weeks — never pick this route when you are racing a deadline on an MOC.

## 4. Part Quality

Gobricks parts come close to genuine LEGO in feel and clutch power, with good color consistency, so they suit display-shelf builds. Taobao replica shops occasionally miss on color and clutch, which makes them a fit for models you will never take apart. What you buy on BrickLink is genuine LEGO, giving it the highest quality ceiling, but mixed new and used parts and batch-to-batch color variation are the norm.

## FAQ

**Q: How should I choose an MOC parts channel?**
A: Let your needs decide. For routine domestic top-ups, Gobricks is the pick — transparent pricing and 2–4 day delivery. If you are short a few rare parts, a Taobao replica shop can help. Only when you are assembling a large bulk order and want factory-original quality is BrickLink worth it.

**Q: Is buying from BrickLink worth it?**
A: Unit prices are low but international shipping is high — roughly CNY 120–200 for a 2 kg parcel. It pays off when you buy 3,000 pieces or more; a small order of a few dozen or hundred pieces is a total loss, and you still wait 7–15 days.

**Q: What is the difference between Gobricks parts and genuine LEGO?**
A: Gobricks parts are domestically made compatible bricks whose clutch and feel are close to the original, at only CNY 0.03–0.05 per piece. Genuine LEGO costs 3–10 times more but offers stable material quality. For ordinary MOCs, Gobricks parts deliver the best value.

**Q: Can Taobao replica shops supply every MOC part?**
A: No. Replica stock covers common bricks and popular special parts, while rare printed pieces and limited-run parts are often out of stock. Those have to be custom-ordered from Gobricks or sourced overseas, so confirm stock before you place an order.
