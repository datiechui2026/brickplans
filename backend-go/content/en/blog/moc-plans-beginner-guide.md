# MOC Instructions for Beginners: From Zero to First Build

MOC stands for My Own Creation — a model a builder designs and constructs from their own idea rather than from an official set. For a newcomer, learning to work from MOC instructions is the key first step. This guide walks you through the whole process from zero: what these documents are, how to pick one, what to prepare, and how to build.

## 1. What Are MOC Instructions?

MOC instructions are documents that record how a model is put together. They usually contain:

- A parts list (which parts you need and how many of each)
- Step-by-step build diagrams (how each stage goes together)
- Photos of the finished model

Getting comfortable with beginner-level MOC instructions simply means you can read and execute these three elements, and end up with a finished model of your own.

## 2. Types of MOC Instructions

### By difficulty

- **Beginner** (100–500 pieces): for anyone building a MOC for the first time
- **Intermediate** (500–1500 pieces): for builders with some experience
- **Master** (1500+ pieces): a challenge for veteran builders

### By theme

- Vehicles (sports cars, jeeps, trucks)
- Architecture (street scenes, castles, historical buildings)
- Mecha (Gundam-style, Transformers-style)
- Military (tanks, fighter jets, warships)

## 3. How to Choose Your First MOC Instructions

For a first MOC, judge a candidate on these four points:

1. **Piece count**: aim for a model in the 300–500 piece range
2. **Part commonality**: favor designs that use widely available parts
3. **Diagram clarity**: pick instructions with high-resolution step images
4. **Subject interest**: choose a theme you actually care about

On brickplan.cn, every set of instructions is labeled with a difficulty level and piece count, so beginners can filter for something that fits without guesswork.

## 4. Tools and Parts You Need

Before your first MOC build, have these ready:

- **A basic parts supply**: 2x4 bricks, 1x2 bricks and plates in a range of colors
- **A brick separator**: for undoing parts you seated incorrectly
- **Sorting boxes**: so you can find a part without emptying the whole pile

## 5. The MOC Building Process

1. **Read the parts list**: check that you have every required part before you start
2. **Build step by step**: follow the numbered steps exactly — do not skip ahead
3. **Check the structure**: after each step, confirm the assembly is solid
4. **Final adjustments**: once complete, fine-tune the details

## 6. From Instructions to Original Design

Once you have the basics of working from MOC instructions down, you can try designing your own model. A good route is to start by modifying existing instructions — swap a color scheme, change a wheel set, extend a section — and build your design instincts gradually from there.

The [MOC builder community](/en/blog/moc-player-community-guide) on brickplan.cn has plenty of builders sharing their own models and experience; joining in is one of the fastest ways to improve.

## Final Thoughts

Starting with MOC instructions is not difficult — the important thing is taking the first step. Pick a set of instructions that suits your level, gather the parts, and you can begin your MOC journey. Visit brickplan.cn to explore a large library of MOC instruction resources.
