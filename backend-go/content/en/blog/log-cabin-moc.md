# Log Cabin MOC: 2,600-Piece Mountain Cabin Build Guide

The short version: this log cabin MOC uses about 2,600 pieces, measures roughly 46 × 40 cm when finished, and takes around 9 hours if you build 2 hours a day. Total budget is about 500 yuan. The design is a two-story timber cabin with dark brown log-style walls, a grey pitched roof, a small front yard and a firewood stack, so it reads as a proper countryside scene. The instructions come from the brickplan platform, and the parts list is split by floor, which makes it a good match for builders who like natural, rural settings.

## Step 1: Baseplate and timber walls

Start on a 28 × 22 stud baseplate. Build the outer walls course by course in dark brown bricks up to 16 layers high, and work light brown spacer pieces between them to suggest wood grain. Leave openings for the door and windows at the front, and frame the windows with white pieces. The ground floor holds two bays, a living room and a kitchen, with a partition wall made from plates. This step uses roughly 900 pieces.

## Step 2: Pitched roof and chimney

The roof is a symmetrical double-pitch design: lay dark grey plates across the slopes and finish the ridge with ridge cap pieces. Cut two skylights into the front of the roof and glaze them with transparent pieces. The chimney stands on one side of the roof, built from reddish-brown bricks with a rain cap on top. The roof locks to the walls with clip pieces, so the whole assembly stays solid. This module takes about 700 pieces.

## Step 3: Interior and fireplace

The ground-floor living room gets a fireplace: build the body from black pieces, add an orange-red transparent piece at the opening to suggest flames, and run the flue straight up into the roof chimney. The kitchen takes a stove and a dining table, and the upper floor becomes a bedroom with a bed that has a headboard, plus a wardrobe. The staircase uses a switchback layout, running from the living room up to the second floor. This module uses around 500 pieces.

## Step 4: Front yard and finishing touches

Lay a stone-slab path in front of the door, plant greenery and fencing along both sides, and stack firewood in one corner. Hang a warm yellow light brick under the eaves — once it is powered up, the cabin looks like a mountain hut on a winter evening. When everything is done, check your parts against the per-floor lists on the brickplan instruction page. Dark brown bricks are used heavily here, so buy them all in one go.

**Q: Is this MOC suitable for beginners?**

A: Yes. The structure is mostly standard bricks and sloped roof pieces, with no complicated mechanical parts. New builders can work floor by floor from the instructions and finish in about 9 hours — it is a good way to practice countryside scenes.

**Q: How much does it cost to buy all the parts?**

**Q: How do you get the glowing fire effect in the fireplace?**

A: Put an orange transparent piece in the fireplace opening with a small light brick behind it — the light shines through and looks like a burning fire. If you do not want to wire anything up, a plain orange transparent piece still reads well in daylight.
