# LEGO Mine MOC Tutorial: Build a Mining Scene Step by Step

## Introduction

Mining scenes have a hold on brick builders for good reason: rough industrial shapes, stacked and terraced terrain, and busy equipment all combine into a picture that feels like it is moving. This **mine MOC tutorial** takes you from the underground workings up to the surface, one step at a time, so you can build a striking mining world of your own.

## 1. Planning the Mine Scene

### 1.1 Pick Your Theme and Style

Before you place a single brick, decide which kind of mine you are building:

- **Open-pit mine**: built around stepped, terraced extraction faces; ideal for showing off large equipment
- **Underground mine**: tunnels and caverns; ideal for showing miners at work
- **Mixed mine**: an open pit plus a tunnel entrance into the hillside

For a first build, start with an **open-pit mine**. The terrain is simpler to shape and the finished result reads more dramatically.

### 1.2 Lay Out the Scene

A complete brick mine scene usually contains these zones:
1. Mine entrance or extraction face
2. Rail transport system
3. Ore stockyard
4. Office and living area
5. Equipment parking area

Two 48x48 baseplates side by side give you a generous working area of roughly 96x48 studs.

## 2. Building the Terrain

### 2.1 Base Terrain

Terrain is the foundation of the whole build. Use a layered stacking method to create the rises and falls:

1. **Bottom layer**: dark gray and brown bricks form the structural skeleton, with varying brick heights to create elevation changes
2. **Middle layer**: brown and reddish-brown bricks imitate soil and rock strata
3. **Top layer**: green plates and bricks mark the areas with vegetation cover

### 2.2 The Mine Entrance

The mine entrance is the visual focal point of any MOC scene. How to build it:

- Build the arch with curved bricks; aim for a height of about 6-8 bricks
- Line the inside with dark bricks (black, dark gray) to suggest depth
- Add a timber support frame above the opening, using brown bricks as wooden posts
- Pile loose ore outside, mixing dark gray and black 1x1 round plates

### 2.3 The Extraction Face

For an open-pit mine, the extraction face is the key element. Techniques:

- Use a stepped structure, with each level set back by one stud
- Alternate colors between levels: dark gray, brown, ochre
- Scatter mining tools and ore across the working faces
- Cap the top edge with green bricks to show untouched vegetation

## 3. Building the Mining Equipment

### 3.1 Excavators

A large excavator is the centerpiece of the site. Options to build:

- **Cable shovel**: Technic parts for the boom, with curved bricks forming the bucket
- **Hydraulic excavator**: tracked undercarriage, cab glazed with transparent bricks
- **Front-end loader**: a compact wheeled loader for working the stockyard

### 3.2 Transport Vehicles

A brick mining scene needs several kinds of transport:

- **Mining truck**: a large dump truck on wide tires, with the bed built from slope bricks
- **Rail ore cart**: runs on track, with a 1x6 plate base and a body made from curved bricks
- **Conveyor belt**: chain links or Technic track parts running over rollers to make the belt system

### 3.3 Drilling Equipment

Adding drilling gear to the extraction face makes the scene more believable:
- Build the drill rig tower from Technic parts
- Use silver and gray bricks for a metallic finish
- Add rubble at the base of the rig

## 4. The Rail Transport System

### 4.1 Laying Track

Track links the working face to the stockyard. Key points for track laying in this mine MOC tutorial:

- Use LEGO train track or track you build yourself
- Give the line a clear gradient, descending gradually from the extraction area to the stockyard
- Add guard rails on curves so the ore carts cannot derail

### 4.2 The Unloading Station

Build an unloading station at the end of the line:
- An elevated track platform so carts can tip their loads
- A collection hopper below, built from slope bricks
- A conveyor or a waiting truck beneath the hopper

## 5. Support Facilities

### 5.1 Ore Stockyard

The stockyard is where you show off the mine's output:
- Pile up ore heaps from large quantities of 1x1 round plates
- Different colors mean different minerals: black (coal), dark gray (iron ore), gold (copper ore)
- Fence or wall off the stockyard area
- Put a loader and a truck to work in the yard

### 5.2 Miner Living Area

Add some human scale to the scene:
- Miners' rest room: a small hut from basic bricks, with tables and chairs inside
- Changing room: a space with safety helmets and work clothes hung up
- Safety notice board: warning signs made from 1x2 tiles

### 5.3 Office and Monitoring

- Mine office: a two-story building at the site entrance
- Watchtower: a lookout tower on high ground, used to oversee extraction work
- Weigh station: a weighbridge at the exit road

## 6. Advanced Techniques

### 6.1 Lighting

Fit LED lights inside the mine to create a moody underground atmosphere:
- Miner headlamps: tiny LEDs mounted on safety helmets
- Tunnel lighting: small yellow lights spaced along the tunnel roof
- Equipment lights: headlights added to excavators and trucks

### 6.2 Motion

Bring the brick mine scene to life:
- Use electric motors to drive the conveyor and the rail ore carts
- Make the excavator on the extraction face rotate
- Add smoke effects (cotton wool stands in for blasting dust)

### 6.3 Layered Display

For a large mine, consider designing it in layers:
- Underground level: mine tunnels and miners at work
- Surface level: the extraction faces and equipment
- Upper level: haul roads and the stockyard

## 7. Final Thoughts

Building an impressive brick mine takes patience and imagination, but once you have the right construction methods in hand, any brick builder can create a mining world of their own. This mine MOC tutorial is meant to give you a clear plan and practical techniques so you avoid the common dead ends.

From shaping terrain to building equipment, from laying track to adding detail, every stage is a chance to show your creativity. Grab your parts and start building that striking brick mine. And when it is finished, share it on BrickPlans and swap ideas with other builders.
