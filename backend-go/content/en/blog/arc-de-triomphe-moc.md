# Triumphal Arch MOC Tutorial: Arch Curves and Relief Walls

The short version first: the triumphal arch is one of the tidiest subjects in the European-landmark MOC category, and it builds in a genuinely relaxing way. Around 1,800 parts on a single 32x32 baseplate get you a 34 cm tall arch, the cream-and-off-white color scheme holds up over time, and the whole process runs about 5 hours — beginners can finish it without trouble.

## Step 1: Read the Structure Before You Build

The arch breaks down into a base, a central archway, four columns and a top platform, and it is perfectly symmetrical left to right. Study the instructions and confirm the layers first: the base is about 6 cm tall, the archway spans about 14 cm, and the finished model measures 34 cm tall, 28 cm wide and 12 cm deep.

That symmetry is the single biggest time-saver in the build. Once one side is done, the other side is a straight repeat — you effectively build half a model twice instead of a whole model once, and your pace roughly doubles.

## Step 2: Base and the Arch Curve

Lay out the base frame in cream 1x4 bricks, leaving the center open for the archway. The arch itself goes up from both sides toward the middle using curved bricks, with each layer stepped inward by half a stud. That half-stud offset per course is what produces the smooth, rounded crown rather than a stepped or blocky opening.

Line the inside of the archway with plates to close the floor, then seat a curved edge piece at the top to cap it off. Before you go any higher, place markers for the four columns at the corners of the base — you will raise those columns course by course later, and having the footprints marked keeps them aligned.

## Step 3: Columns and Cornice Details

Each of the four columns is stacked from 2x2 round bricks. Every few courses, add a ring of grooved pieces to imitate the fluting of a classical column shaft. The column tops tie into the cornice, which is built from slope bricks to create the overhanging eave line.

The relief on the walls is where the model earns its character. Use 1x1 bricks on the surface to build raised and recessed texture, or apply stickers to reproduce the sculpted relief panels. Concentrate that decoration on the wall sections flanking the archway, since those are the areas the eye lands on.

## Step 4: Top Platform and Finishing Touches

Cap the model with large plates to close the top platform, then run a low parapet wall around the perimeter. The center of the platform is a good spot for a small flag or a cherub figure.

The cream palette is the classic choice, but you can switch to light grey if you want a weathered, aged look. Finished at 34 cm tall, the arch sits well at the end of a street-scene layout or on a study bookshelf. Budget on roughly 1,800 parts and about 150 yuan for the full build.

## FAQ

**Q: How much do triumphal arch MOC instructions usually cost?**
A: Instructions for an arch like this run about 25 yuan on platforms such as brickplan.cn, with a permanent PDF download that includes the parts list and the building manual. Sourcing all 1,800 parts comes to roughly 120 yuan, so the complete package lands around 150 yuan.

**Q: Is a triumphal arch MOC hard to build?**
A: Not really. The structure is symmetrical left to right and the steps repeat, so 1,800 parts take about 5 hours. The arch curve is the only section that asks for a little patience, and beginners can complete the model independently.

**Q: How many parts does the triumphal arch MOC need?**
A: About 1,800, mostly cream bricks, including roughly 120 curved bricks and about 60 2x2 round bricks. The color count is low, which makes sourcing easy, and rare parts are only a small share of the total.

**Q: How is this different from a Parthenon MOC?**
A: The triumphal arch is a Roman-style structure with a single archway plus columns, and it is compact. The Parthenon is a Greek temple with multiple columns, a colonnade wrapping the building and flatter, longer proportions. The arch uses fewer parts and is easier to get into.

**Q: Can I add lighting to the triumphal arch MOC?**
A: Yes. An LED set can be hidden inside the archway and under the top platform, and warm yellow light spilling out through the arch looks great. A USB-powered light strip is the safer option for a modification like this.
