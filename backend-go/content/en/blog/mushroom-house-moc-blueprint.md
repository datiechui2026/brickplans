# Mushroom House MOC Blueprint: A Beginner Build Guide

The short version: this mushroom house MOC blueprint is one of the friendliest themes a beginner or a parent-and-child team can build. The finished model stands about 22 cm tall and nails four fairy-tale cottage details — a red cap with white dots, a chimney, a wooden door and windows. You need roughly 800 pieces, a budget of about 250 yuan, and around 3 hours. The curved cap edge is both the hardest step and the one that makes the build.

## Step 1: Build the round base and interior floor

Use wedge plates and curved bricks to form a circular base about 20 cm in diameter, leaving gaps where the wooden door and the windows will sit. The base uses roughly 120 pieces. Sort them into the zones shown on the blueprint before you start — it makes it much easier for a child to grab pieces by colour instead of hunting through the whole pile.

Keep the base flat on the table as you close each ring, and check that the door and window openings line up with the marks on the plan before you press anything down hard.

## Step 2: Raise the cottage body

Build the cylindrical wall in beige brick to a height of about 10 cm. Leave a door opening at the front and cut two windows into the side. Use a running-bond, or staggered, technique: offset each layer by half a brick so the seams never stack. That interlocking pattern is what keeps the round wall rigid, and it is the reason the model will not fall apart when someone picks it up by the cap.

## Step 3: Make the wooden door and windows

Build the door from dark brown bricks — an arched frame with horizontal plank texture across it. For the windows, use transparent pieces for the glass and white frames to create the cross mullions. This detail group is only about 40 pieces, but it carries most of the fairy-tale feeling. Set both windows and the door in place dry first, then adjust the surrounding wall bricks if the openings are tight.

## Step 4: Build the red cap with white dots

The cap is the soul of the mushroom house. Start at the centre and lay red curved slope bricks outward ring by ring. Each ring pulls inward by roughly one brick of width, which is what produces the curved taper of the cap. Then dot the surface with about 30 white round tiles. Where the taper meets, fill the gaps with 1x1 wedge slopes so the rim stays smooth and rounded rather than jagged.

Practically, this means building outward in a spiral rather than in one pass, checking the profile from the side every couple of rings. The cap is the step where the blueprint's close-up detail drawings matter most — they show exactly how each ring overlaps the last.

## Step 5: Add the chimney and finish

Angle a brown chimney into the right side of the cap, then cap it with two rings of grey round bricks to suggest the flue opening. Once everything is seated, the model stands about 22 cm tall — a complete fairy-tale forest cottage that fits on a desk or in a display cabinet.

## FAQ

**Q: Can a beginner build this mushroom house MOC without help?**

A: Yes. The full model is about 800 pieces on a roughly 250 yuan budget, the blueprint steps are clear, and the curved cap section includes enlarged diagrams. A beginner finishes in about 3 hours, and building it together with a child is a better experience than solo.

**Q: How do I stop the cap edge from curling up?**

A: Pull each ring inward by about one brick of width and fill the gaps with 1x1 wedge slopes. Press the rim flat with plates at the end, and leave a two-brick overlap where the taper closes — that is enough to keep the edge rounded and level.

**Q: What scenes does the mushroom house look good with?**

A: It pairs best with brickplan's forest baseplates, small trees and flower pieces. You can also set it beside a street-scene food stall as a fairy-tale accent; at about 22 cm tall the scale mixes easily.
