# Xiaomi Brick Review: Value and Build Experience

Bottom line first: Xiaomi bricks deliver more than 80% of the flagship building experience for roughly a third of the price. The construction machinery and supercar lines are the ones worth chasing — just be aware that some sets are already out of production.

Xiaomi bricks were once the clearest synonym for "value for money" among Chinese brick brands. From construction machinery to licensed supercars, every release tried to sell near-flagship build quality at half the price. This Xiaomi brick review pulls together hands-on time with several sets to answer whether they are actually worth buying.

## 1. The Three Lines and What Each Does Best

Xiaomi bricks come in three main lines. The **construction machinery series** is where the reputation began: sets such as the excavator and the tracked crane reproduce hydraulic rods and track-driven travel, and the build logic is easy to follow. The **Jupiter Dawn series** takes a sci-fi mecha route, with humanoid mechs and spaceships that lean heavily on futuristic styling — a favourite with younger builders. The **licensed supercar series** recreates classic Lamborghini and Ferrari models, with convincing bodywork curves and wheel-hub detailing, priced between 400 and 700 yuan, a big step below what flagship brands charge for the same subject matter.

## 2. Part Quality and Who Makes the Elements

Xiaomi brick elements are produced by specialist contract factories such as Gobricks, and quality sits in the top tier of domestic production: **tight clutch power and well-controlled tolerances**, smooth insertion, and high compatibility with LEGO parts. Printed details are mostly stickers, but the sticker printing is precise and, once aligned, looks close to a printed element. In testing there were no obvious missing pieces or flash on the parts, bag numbering is logical, and the instruction steps are clearly drawn. Some sets also support app-based building guidance, which is friendly to newcomers.

## 3. Build Experience in Practice

The overall build experience is best described as "smooth": the structural design is mature, there are no baffling interference points, and large sets generally take three to six hours to finish. The construction machinery line packs in plenty of linked mechanisms, so playability after the build is strong; the supercar line looks stunning on display, and the chassis suspension and opening doors are genuine bonuses. One warning: the joints on some sci-fi mechs are on the tight side and can be tiring on the fingers — work each joint through a few times before you start posing the model.

## 4. Value for Money and Who Should Buy

The verdict of this Xiaomi brick review: at roughly a third of the price of flagship brands, you get more than 80% of the build experience, which makes the value extremely strong. These sets suit students on a tight budget, beginners who want to try mechanical structures at low cost, and collectors who enjoy domestic brick brands. Do keep in mind that some Xiaomi brick sets have been discontinued, so popular models require watching channel stock levels and resale premiums.
