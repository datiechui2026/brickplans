# Adult Brick Beginner's Guide: How to Choose a First Set

The short answer first: when an adult gets into brick building, the first set should sit in the 1,000–2,000 piece range with a budget of roughly 300–600 CNY. Choose it on three rules — interest first, moderate piece count, reliable brand — and you will dodge most of the classic beginner mistakes. The smoothest way in is to build finished models first and only later start creating MOCs.

**Step 1: Pick a theme you actually like, not the one everyone else is buying.** The single biggest trap in this hobby is buying a set that gets called a masterpiece online and then discovering you do not care about the subject at all. Into cars? Go for a supercar. Into architecture? Go for a street scene. Into science fiction? Go for a spaceship. Only a theme that genuinely hits your interests will carry you to the last bag. A 2,000-piece street scene runs about 8–10 hours of building on average; someone with no interest in the subject will park it on the shelf after roughly 2 hours.

**Step 2: Keep the piece count and the budget under control.** A first set of 1,000–2,000 pieces is the sweet spot. Fewer than that and there is no sense of achievement; much more and the build tends to stall half-finished. On price, a first-tier domestic brand runs about 150–250 CNY for 1,000 pieces and 300–500 CNY for 2,000 pieces. International brands at the same piece count cost roughly 2–3 times as much. Do not open with a 5,000-piece monster set: the frustration of never finishing is the number one reason people leave the hobby, accounting for more than 40% of cases where someone quits.

**Step 3: Put together the basic tools.** At the start you need exactly three things: a parts sorting box (around 20 CNY), a brick separator (often included in the set, or about 10 CNY on its own) and a bright desk lamp. Sorting parts by color or by shape inside the box can cut your searching time in half. Hand cream and rubber finger cots count as advanced gear — beginners do not need to buy them yet.

**Step 4: Move through the hobby in the right order.** First, build 1–2 official sets from start to finish so you learn the common part types and how building logic works. Next, try the beginner-level MOC instructions on brickplan, rebuilding and adapting them with the parts you already own. Only after that should you start buying loose parts against an instruction file. This sequence lets a beginner build confidence on the smallest possible spend, and after 3–4 sets most people can complete a small MOC on their own.

**Step 5: Join a community and keep leveling up.** Once your first set is done, post it on a community like brickplan and study how other builders have modified the same model. Put in 5–10 hours a week and within about 3 months you can go from "following the instructions" to "changing the design myself" — which is where the real pleasure of adult brick building lives.

## FAQ

**Q: How many pieces should an adult's first brick set have?**
A: 1,000–2,000 pieces is the recommendation, with a budget of 300–600 CNY. That range gives you 8–10 hours of building: enough for a sense of achievement, not so much that the model is left unfinished. Sets above 5,000 pieces are better attempted once you have three or more builds behind you.

**Q: Is brick building childish for an adult?**
A: No. The adult brick market is well established, with plenty of mechanical, architectural and spaceship themes aimed squarely at grown-up builders. The building process relieves stress and trains patience, and many players treat it as a serious hobby in the same way as scale models or LEGO collecting.

**Q: Is there still a big gap between domestic and international brands?**
A: The gap has narrowed noticeably over the past five years. First-tier domestic brands now come close to international quality in part precision and clutch power, at roughly one-half to one-third of the price. For a beginner, a high-value domestic set is a completely reasonable choice.

**Q: How long does a first set take to build?**
A: About 4–6 hours for 1,000 pieces and 8–10 hours for 2,000 pieces. Splitting the work over 2–3 evenings feels comfortable — around 2 hours a night protects your neck and back and keeps the build fresh, so you are less likely to abandon it.

**Q: What is the cheapest sensible way to start?**
A: Choose a first-tier domestic brand set of 800–1,500 pieces; 150–300 CNY buys a complete experience. Add free MOC instructions and the secondhand parts market, and a total outlay under 500 CNY is enough to produce some genuinely good builds.
