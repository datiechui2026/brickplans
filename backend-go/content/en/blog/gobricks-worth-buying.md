# Is Gobricks Worth Buying? Precision, Feel and Value Tested

The short answer: Gobricks is worth buying for MOC builders who care about precision and structural stability, and largely unnecessary for beginners who only assemble official sets. Pricing lands at roughly one third of LEGO.

"Is Gobricks worth buying?" comes up in MOC circles almost every week. Unlike brands that shift volume through finished sets, Gobricks is the parts specialist of the Chinese brick scene — it produces ABS elements for brands such as SEMBO, Double Eagle and Panlos, and also sells loose parts directly to builders. The verdict up front: **Gobricks is worth it for MOC builders chasing precision and stability; for newcomers who only build sets and never touch loose parts, the need is minimal.** Here is the breakdown across four dimensions.

## What Gobricks Is: A Parts Specialist With a Factory Background

Gobricks started out molding ABS pellets for domestic and overseas brands, then opened loose-piece retail. It sells no finished sets today and focuses on a single category, which is exactly why its parts sit in the top tier domestically. The catalog lists more than 2,000 active part numbers, covering basic bricks, technic elements, printed parts, transparent elements and the rest — making it one of the main part sources for Chinese MOC builders. That positioning puts it on a different track from LEGO and SEMBO, so the real question is whether buying loose parts from Gobricks is worth it.

The testing focused on three things: tolerance, clutch power and consistency. Gobricks rates its mold precision at the 0.005mm level, on par with LEGO. In practice, standard bricks clutch firmly with no noticeable play, and a stack 30 courses high stays straight without lean or collapse. The feel runs tight overall — pressing takes slightly more force, but joints rarely work loose, and cantilevered or inverted structures hold better than with most domestic parts. The weak spots are transparent elements and special-color parts, which occasionally show fine scratches and look slightly inferior to LEGO. **For precision-focused builders, Gobricks is the closest domestic match to LEGO feel, at about a third of the price.**

## Colors and Special Parts: An Ammunition Depot for MOC Builders

Gobricks offers more than 100 colors in production, including rare shades LEGO has discontinued such as sand green, pearl gold and trans-light blue — especially useful when recreating old sets or matching retired color codes. On special parts, slopes, wedges, curved plates, hinges and common technic elements all have high coverage, so architecture, vehicle and mech builds generally need no brand switching. A few obscure parts still have to come from Double Eagle or LEGO. For builders who are picky about color matching, this section is hard to substitute and is a major point in the value column.

## Price Comparison and Value: How to Buy Loose Parts Economically

Compared on 2026 market pricing: Gobricks loose parts run about 0.03-0.15 yuan per piece depending on size and color; Double Eagle runs about 0.02-0.10 yuan, slightly cheaper but with fewer color codes; LEGO loose parts start at 0.5-1 yuan each, several times higher. For the same 1,000-piece parts list, Gobricks comes to roughly 80-150 yuan to fill, while LEGO exceeds 400 yuan. So back to the core question — Gobricks is close to the best balance of precision and cost available. Money-saving tips: use Gobricks for structural elements and mix in LEGO for rare special parts; buy through official channels or authorized stores, and watch out for lookalike sellers trading on the Gobricks name.

## Who Should Buy, and Buying Advice

**Gobricks is worth it for precision-focused builders with budget, veteran builders recreating old sets, and architecture MOC builders who need large volumes of same-color brick. Beginners who only want to build a design once or twice should start with a finished set instead.** For a first order, begin with basic bricks and common plates, skip obscure colors, and expand once you know the color codes.
