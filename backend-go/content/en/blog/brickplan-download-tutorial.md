# BrickPlan Download Tutorial: From Signup to Printed MOC

For MOC builders, finding a good set of instructions is only the beginning. Between that file and a finished model on your shelf sit a handful of steps: downloading, printing, and building page by page. This **BrickPlan download** tutorial takes you from registration to finished model, breaking each stage down so you can follow along without getting stuck.

## 1. Create an Account and Complete Your Profile

Go to the BrickPlans homepage (brickplan.cn) and click "Register" in the top right corner. You can sign up with an email address or a phone number, verify, and log straight in. **It pays to fill in your avatar and nickname right away** and to confirm your download permissions under personal settings. Most instructions are open to registered users, while some of the higher-quality designs may require a set number of credits or be unlocked by sharing. Get the basics filled in first and everything afterwards runs more smoothly.

## 2. Search and Filter for Instructions

Once you are logged in, use the search box at the top of the page. **Type your keywords directly** (such as "sports car", "castle", or "pirate") or enter an instruction number to jump to the model you want. The results page lets you sort by category, part count, build difficulty, and newest uploads. It is worth narrowing things down with filters first: beginners can tick "under 500 parts" and "difficulty: beginner", while experienced builders can browse by theme and rating. Click a card to open its detail page and study the **preview images, parts list, and comment section** carefully. Make sure it matches what you expect before you download, so you don't burn credits on the wrong file.

## 3. Download the PDF Instructions

When you are sure, hit the "Download" button on the detail page. **BrickPlan downloads** are usually supplied as PDF files, and some designs also come with a separate parts list document. Your browser saves the file straight to the download folder, and on a phone it lands in the file manager. If a download fails partway through, it is usually a network issue, so retry later or switch browsers. After downloading, check the file size: a complete instruction file is normally several MB, and if it is only a few tens of KB, the file is probably incomplete and needs downloading again.

## 4. Print Settings and Binding

Once you have the PDF, do two things before printing: **check the page size and the print scaling**. MOC instructions are mostly laid out for A4, so choose "Actual size (100%)" rather than "Fit to page", otherwise the step images lose their proportions and nothing lines up when you build. Print color instructions in "Color" mode so parts are easier to tell apart, and black-and-white files in mono mode to save ink. When printing is done, bind the pages in numerical order, or use a folder to keep the step pages and the parts list in separate sections so it is easier to flip through while building.

## 5. Practical Tips for Building from the Instructions

With your printout in hand, read through the whole sequence once before you open any bags. **Sort the parts roughly by color and size** and check the quantities against the parts list. Start at step 1, and after each step run a ruler or your fingertip along the seams to confirm they sit flush, so small errors don't pile up and leave you stuck later. If you cannot identify a part from the drawing, use a magnifier or take a phone photo and zoom in to read the number. When the main build is finished, don't pack up immediately: go back and check for any decorative details you missed, then move the model to the display shelf.

From registration to finished model, the **BrickPlan download** process is actually very straightforward: register, search, download, print, build. Five steps and you have your own MOC. If you get stuck at any point, ask in the **BrickPlans (brickplan.cn)** community, where members are always happy to help. The [BrickPlans blog](https://brickplan.cn/en/blog) also has more instruction downloads and building tips. Happy building!
