# Brick Motorcycle MOC Plans Reviewed: From Classic Cruisers to Modern Sport Bikes

Motorcycles are one of the most appealing subjects in brick vehicle MOCs. Compared with cars, a bike has cleaner lines, but the mechanical packaging is tighter and the design demands are higher. From Harley-Davidson-style American cruisers to Ducati-style Italian sport bikes, every motorcycle MOC is a balancing act between mechanical realism and building technique. This review walks through several **brick motorcycle MOC plans** so you can pick the project that fits your parts bin and your skill level.

## What Makes Motorcycle MOCs So Appealing

The charm of a motorcycle MOC is that it is small but dense. A 1:8 scale bike typically needs only 500-1000 pieces, yet it still has to show an engine, exhaust system, suspension, instrument cluster and chain drive — every one of them carefully designed. A good **brick motorcycle MOC plan** achieves a balance between exterior look and working features inside a very limited space.

If vehicle MOCs are new to you, start with our [brick truck MOC plans](/en/blog/truck-moc-blueprint) to learn the fundamentals of building vehicles. On [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) we collect motorcycle plans ranging from beginner builds to master-level designs.

### 3. Dirt Bike — Flying Through the Mud

**Core features**:

- Tread pattern on the off-road tires recreated with specialized parts
- High-mounted exhaust to keep water out of the engine
- Lightweight body with the excess fairing panels stripped away

## How the Mechanical Systems Work

### Steering

Motorcycle MOCs generally handle steering one of two ways:
1. **Direct steering**: turning the handlebars drives the front fork directly
2. **Geared steering**: a gear train transfers the handlebar rotation to the front wheel

### Suspension

- **Front suspension**: a telescopic fork is the most common solution, using spring elements for the rebound
- **Rear suspension**: a single central shock or twin shocks, with a spring connecting to the rear swingarm

### Drivetrain

- **Chain drive**: chain link pieces stand in for a real motorcycle chain
- **Shaft drive**: universal joints and a driveshaft, well suited to large motorcycle models
- **Belt drive**: rubber bands or belt elements mimic a belt final drive

## Parts to Stock for Motorcycle MOCs

These are the specialized parts worth having on hand before you start a motorcycle MOC:

1. **Motorcycle tires**: 24mm, 30mm and 43mm diameters — pick according to scale
2. **Suspension springs**: spring elements in a range of lengths
3. **Chain links**: for recreating the motorcycle chain
4. **Curved panels**: for wrapping the body and creating a streamlined silhouette
5. **Transparent elements**: for the headlight, taillight and instrument cluster
