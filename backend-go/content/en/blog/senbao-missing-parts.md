# SEMBO Missing Parts: How to Get Replacement Bricks

SEMBO is one of the newer names among Chinese brick brands, and it has built a solid following thanks to its wide range of themes and strong value for money. Like every brick brand, though, the occasional set arrives with a piece missing. So when you need a replacement part from SEMBO, what exactly do you do? This guide walks you through the whole process.

## 1. First, Confirm the Part Really Is Missing

Before you file a replacement request, make absolutely sure the piece is actually absent. Tip the entire set out, then **work through the parts list in the instruction manual piece by piece**. While you check, look in the places small parts like to hide: the tabletop, the bottom of the bags, the folds of the packaging. It is also worth retracing your build to see whether the part ended up somewhere else by mistake.

If you are confident the piece is missing, **photograph its position in the instructions** along with the part number printed in the manual. Having those images ready makes the conversation with customer service much smoother.

## 2. Find the Right Customer Service Channel

SEMBO offers several ways to get in touch, so pick whichever is easiest given how you bought the set. If you **purchased through an e-commerce platform such as Taobao or JD.com**, contact the store's customer service directly, since that is by far the fastest route. If you bought **through another channel or in a physical store**, try SEMBO's official customer service instead.

You can usually reach SEMBO's official support through the **brand's website or its WeChat official account**. When you make contact, state which set you bought and include the details about the missing piece, and the team can process your request quickly.

## 3. Prepare the Information Your Request Needs

When you submit a SEMBO replacement part request, have the following ready in advance. First, the **set number**, which you will find on the box or in the instruction manual. Second, the **part number of the missing piece**, listed in the manual's parts inventory. Third, the **quantity and colour** of the missing part. Fourth, your **shipping address and contact details**.

If you can, attach a **photo showing where the part sits in the build**. It helps customer service identify the exact piece you need, which speeds everything up. The more complete your information, the higher your chances of a smooth replacement.

## 4. Understand the Process and Timelines

Once your request is in, SEMBO customer service verifies the details, confirms everything checks out, and arranges to send the part. Processing usually takes **anywhere from a few days to a week or two**, depending on stock and shipping conditions. Replacements are generally **free of charge**, but it is worth confirming the exact policy with customer service when you apply.

If you hear nothing for a long stretch after submitting, **follow up on your own initiative** to make sure the request is actually being handled. Stay patient and keep the lines of communication friendly. These issues almost always get resolved.

## 5. What to Do While You Wait

While the replacement is on its way, you can **build the sections that do not depend on the missing piece**. Many SEMBO sets are designed as separate modules, so you can complete the main structure first and leave the gap until last. That way you make use of the waiting time without falling behind on the build overall.

If the missing part is a common one, you can also **borrow a temporary substitute from another set you own** and swap it out once the official part arrives. Just make sure the stand-in matches the original's specifications, or you may compromise the structural strength of the model.

## 6. Small Habits That Prevent Missing Parts

To save yourself the hassle of a replacement request, **count every part against the inventory before you start building**, and keep your work surface tidy so nothing goes astray mid-build. Only if a part genuinely cannot be found should you consider filing a request.

To sum up, a successful SEMBO replacement comes down to four things: confirming the part is missing, choosing the right customer service channel, supplying complete information, and following up patiently.
