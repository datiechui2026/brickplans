# Brick Display Case Guide: Dust, Moisture, and Sizing

The short answer: when you shop for a brick display case, judge two hard specs before anything else: dust sealing and shelf load capacity. With a budget of CNY 300-600, choose an acrylic display cover to protect a single model. With CNY 600-1,500, choose a full cabinet with glass doors, LED lighting, and adjustable shelves. For multi-level or connected displays, add desiccant packs. Do not buy on looks alone.

Once a model is built, two problems matter most: dust and brittleness. Dust means repeated cleaning, and cleaning can scratch bricks and studs. Moisture can make ABS plastic brittle and cause colors to yellow. A reliable display case keeps dust and damp out while lighting helps the model look its best.

## 1. Core Specs First: Sealing and Load Capacity

To judge whether a case actually keeps out dust and moisture, check three things: whether the glass or acrylic door has a sealing strip, whether the cabinet seams are tight, and whether there is space for a dehumidifier box. For a full cabinet, prefer a model with a silicone sealing strip; when the door closes firmly, dust should have almost no path inside. Shelf strength is just as important. A standard brick modular street building weighs about 2-3 kg, so each shelf should be rated for at least 15 kg. That margin helps prevent sagging later.

If the cabinet has multiple shelves or you plan to display large vehicles, metal support columns under the shelves are a safer choice. The goal is not to test the limit, but to leave headroom.

## 2. Match the Setup: Acrylic Covers vs Full Cabinets

If you have only one or two models, an acrylic display cover is the best value. A single cover costs CNY 300-600. The clear cover does not take up much space, is easy to move, and can sit on a desk or TV stand. It is a simple way to protect a finished build without committing to a large piece of furniture.

Once you have five or more models, move up to a full cabinet with glass doors. Expect CNY 600-1,500. Interior shelves are usually adjustable, LED strips can be added, and both display quality and dust protection are stronger. One cabinet can handle the whole collection and keep the display looking deliberate rather than scattered.

## 3. Measure Before You Buy

Before ordering, measure the height of the model and add 5-8 cm for base clearance and breathing room. A 1:8 car model is about 40 cm long and needs at least 50 cm of shelf height. A single modular street building is about 33 cm tall, so 45 cm of shelf height is enough. For a three-in-a-row block of street buildings, choose a horizontal cabinet at least 90 cm wide.

Favor extra height over a tight fit. If you leave too little room, you will need a new cabinet when you build a larger model. A little empty space also makes the display easier to light and clean.

## 4. Lighting and Moisture Control Extras

If the cabinet comes with LED strips, look for warm white light around 4000K. It renders colors naturally without glare and helps brick colors look richer. Avoid leaving the light aimed at light-colored bricks for long periods, since this can contribute to local yellowing.

In humid regions, place color-changing silica gel desiccant packs inside the cabinet. Check them monthly, and every two weeks during the damp season in the south. When the beads change color, dry them out and put them back. This helps hold cabinet humidity at 40%-50%, which slows brick aging. Keep the desiccant away from direct contact with the models.

## 5. Pros, Cons, and the Recommendation

The trade-offs are clear. Acrylic covers are inexpensive and flexible, but they are not suited to large models or multi-level displays. Glass-door full cabinets seal better, support LED lighting, and create stronger visual depth, but they take up floor or wall space and cost more. For most builders, the best setup is a full cabinet as the main display plus acrylic covers as supplements for special models.

If you want to save budget, find plans on https://brickplan.cn and build a model from loose parts first. Once the model is finished, measure it and buy the cabinet to fit. That order avoids buying a case that is too small or too large.

## FAQ

**Q: Should I buy an acrylic or glass brick display case?**

A: For a small number of small models, choose an acrylic cover at CNY 300-600 per piece; it is flexible and does not take up much space. For a larger collection with big models, choose a glass full cabinet with a sealing strip. Dust and moisture protection and display quality are clearly stronger.

**Q: How much shelf load capacity do I need?**

A: Aim for at least 15 kg per shelf. A standard brick modular street building weighs about 2-3 kg. For three or more shelves or large vehicles, shelves with metal support columns are more secure.

**Q: How do I keep moisture out of a brick display case?**

A: Put color-changing silica gel desiccant packs inside the cabinet. In the humid season in the south, check every two weeks; dry and reuse the packs when they change color. Keep humidity at 40%-50% to slow yellowing and brittleness.

**Q: How should I measure for a display cabinet?**

A: Measure the model height and add 5-8 cm. A 1:8 car model is about 40 cm and needs 50 cm of shelf height. A single modular street building is about 33 cm and needs 45 cm. For a connected row of street buildings, choose a horizontal cabinet at least 90 cm wide.

**Q: Is lighting important for brick displays?**

A: Yes. Warm white LED strips around 4000K make brick colors look fuller and more three-dimensional, and they make evening viewing easier. Avoid long exposure of light-colored bricks to direct light to reduce local yellowing.
