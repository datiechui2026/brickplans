# LEGO Harry Potter Sets: Castle, Diagon Alley and More

"Welcome to the wizarding world, Muggles." Ever since J.K. Rowling's Harry Potter series first appeared, that world of spells and adventure has stayed lodged in the minds of readers and film fans alike. With the sets below, you can build Hogwarts Castle, Diagon Alley and the Forbidden Forest yourself and let the magic come back to life in bricks.

## Why Are LEGO Harry Potter Sets So Popular?

Every set in this line is more than a pile of parts — it is a careful reconstruction of a place fans already know by heart. From the tall towers of Hogwarts to the cosy timber of Hagrid's Hut, from the scramble of a Quidditch pitch to the tension of the Chamber of Secrets, each model tells a Harry Potter story. For someone who is both a Potter fan and a brick builder, that double payoff is hard to beat.

If this is your first time with Harry Potter themed bricks, start with our [beginner MOC guide](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) and these [simple building techniques](/en/blog/simple-brick-plans-beginners) to get your footing. Over on [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) you will also find a large library of wizard-world MOCs from other fans — unicorns, dragons, Pensieves and Vanishing Cabinets among them.

## The Recommended LEGO Harry Potter Sets

### 1. Hogwarts Castle

This is the flagship of the whole theme. The sprawling castle includes the Great Hall, the Headmaster's Office, the Potions classroom, the Gryffindor common room and several other landmark locations. The minifigure line-up covers the core cast: Harry, Ron, Hermione, Dumbledore and Snape. The detail here is genuinely impressive — the Great Hall ceiling can change its lighting effect, the portraits rotate and the staircases move.

### 2. Diagon Alley

The most famous shopping street in the wizarding world, rebuilt in bricks: Ollivanders wand shop, Weasley's Wizard Wheezes, Gringotts Wizarding Bank and Flourish and Blotts. Each building can be displayed on its own or joined into one continuous Diagon Alley block. If you enjoyed the scene construction in our [Disney brick review](/en/blog/disney-brick-review), the detailing on this set will be right up your street.

### 3. Hogwarts Express

Departing from Platform Nine and Three-Quarters, this dark red steam train carries a lot of wizarding dreams. The set reproduces the locomotive, the tender and the passenger carriage, and includes the platform scene with the classic moment of Harry pushing his luggage trolley through the wall. The train works as a stand-alone display, but it also connects seamlessly with the Hogwarts Castle layout.

## Harry Potter MOC Building Techniques

### Recreating Magical Effects

Use [brick modification techniques](/en/blog/moc-brick-modification) to turn spells into bricks: transparent parts for magical light, gears and levers for moving staircases and rotating portraits, and ball joints for flying brooms and Dementors.

### Minifigure Customisation and Expansion

The Harry Potter world has hundreds of distinctive characters. Our [PDF plan format guide](/en/blog/brick-plans-pdf-format) explains how to get detailed minifigure assembly instructions — from Luna's radish earrings to Mad-Eye Moody's magical eye, every detail is worth rebuilding in brick.

### Large-Scale Scene MOCs

If you want a bigger challenge, try the full Hogwarts campus, a Forbidden Forest biome, or the Triwizard Tournament maze. The [MOC community guide](/en/blog/moc-player-community-guide) will help you find like-minded builders to swap experience and ideas with.

## Crossovers with Other Worlds

Mashing the wizarding world up with superhero universes such as Marvel and DC is a popular direction for MOC builders. Combine the Hogwarts buildings with the Avengers Tower from our [Avengers brick set recommendations](/en/blog/avengers-brick-sets) for a wizards-versus-Iron-Man crossover, or display them beside the mechs in our [Gundam LEGO guide](/en/blog/gundam-lego-guide) so magic and technology sit side by side in brick form.

On [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn), community builders have already shared plenty of cross-IP MOCs to keep the ideas coming.

## Final Thoughts

Building these sets is not just assembly — it is constructing a wizarding world of your own. Every part you click together is a nod to that fantasy. From the Great Hall of Hogwarts to the dark depths of the Forbidden Forest, from a Quidditch chase in the air to the hidden entrance of the Chamber of Secrets: pick up your bricks, say the spell, and let the magic happen at your fingertips.
