# How to Build a Brick Phone Stand: 30-Piece DIY Tutorial

The short answer: a brick phone stand is the ideal first MOC. It takes about 30 basic pieces and 10 minutes. Build an L-shaped stand with a 60–70 degree viewing angle and make it at least 12 studs wide so a 6.7-inch phone sits securely. Cost is basically zero — loose bricks you already have will do.

The idea is simple: a weighted base plus an angled backrest. The base keeps the stand from tipping, the backrest sets the viewing angle at 60–70 degrees, and a low lip at the front stops the phone sliding off. The structure is basic but genuinely useful for streaming, following a recipe, or video calls.

## Step 1: Gather the Parts

Core parts list: 2x6 plates ×2, 2x4 bricks ×4, 1x4 bricks ×6, 1x2 bricks ×8, 2x2 plates ×6 — roughly 30 pieces in total. All are common basic elements, and standard studs from any brand are compatible. For extra stability, add two more 2x8 plates to enlarge the footprint of the base.

## Step 2: Build the Base

Start by joining the two 2x6 plates side by side to make a 4x6 base. Then stack two layers of 2x4 bricks along the front edge to create a lip two bricks high. That lip height matters: too low and the phone slides out, too high and it covers the bottom of the screen. Leave the rear of the base empty for the backrest.

## Step 3: Build the Angled Backrest

At the rear of the base, alternate 1x4 and 1x2 bricks upward. From the third layer on, offset each layer one stud further back to create a slope of about 65 degrees. By 8–10 layers the backrest is roughly 8 cm tall, which is just enough to hold the bottom edge of the phone against the front lip. Press down on every seam when you finish so the connections are tight.

## Step 4: Reinforce and Test

Put a phone on it: at a 65-degree angle, phones from 6.1 to 6.7 inches all stand securely. If the center of gravity feels too far back, add two 2x2 plates at the front of the base as counterweight. If the phone is slippery, stick a small piece of non-slip pad on the inside of the lip. Once it passes the test, it is ready to use.

This stand also makes a nice small gift. Swap in transparent pieces for an "ice crystal" look, or build a minifigure sitting beside it and you have a one-of-a-kind desk piece. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I make a brick phone stand — do I need to buy special parts?**

A: No. Use loose basic bricks you already have, around 30 pieces. The key parts are 2x6 plates, 2x4 bricks and 1x4 bricks, standard studs from any brand are compatible, and it takes about 10 minutes.

**Q: What size phone can a brick phone stand hold?**

A: Build it at least 12 studs wide and it will hold large-screen phones up to 6.7 inches securely. The crucial details are a front lip 2 bricks high and a backrest angled at about 65 degrees, with the weight kept toward the front so it will not tip.

**Q: Do I need instructions to build a phone stand?**

A: No professional instructions are needed — just follow the three-stage approach: base, front lip, angled backrest. For your first build you can refer to photos online; after that you can change the size and colors freely, and even add a minifigure for decoration.

**Q: Will a brick phone stand fall apart?**

A: It holds up fine for normal streaming and video watching. As long as each layer is pressed down and the seams are tight, the structure is quite solid. If you are worried, add two plates to the base as counterweight and stability improves noticeably.
