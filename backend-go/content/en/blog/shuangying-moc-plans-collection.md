# Double Eagle MOC Plans Review: Excavators, Dozers & Cranes

## Product Overview

Double Eagle has built a solid reputation among Chinese brick brands, especially in technic parts and construction machinery. BrickPlans hosts a large library of Double Eagle MOC plans, and this review works through the **Double Eagle MOC plan collection** as a whole — excavators, bulldozers, cranes, trucks and other heavy equipment, plus a few technic drivetrain models. Every plan listed can be found on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) by searching the "Double Eagle" tag.

## Build Difficulty

**Difficulty rating**: ★★★☆☆

Medium. Double Eagle MOC plans span a wide range, from small entry-level machines to flagship fully remote-controlled construction vehicles. The collection reviewed here leans toward intermediate builds, with an estimated build time of 4–8 hours. Double Eagle's technic system is fully compatible with LEGO, so the build logic in these plans is clear and easy to follow for anyone with some technic experience. Beginners can filter by the "Double Eagle + beginner" tags on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) and start with a simpler machine.

## Part Review

Part counts across this series range from **500 to 2,000 pcs**, covering small through large MOCs. Recommended brand: **Double Eagle** — these plans are tailored to Double Eagle parts, so original Double Eagle elements give the best compatibility.

Key parts breakdown:

- **Technic beams and pins**: Double Eagle's beam tolerances are tight, pins seat with the right amount of friction, and the building feel is excellent
- **Gear system**: the alloy gears use a mature heat-treatment process, wear better than similarly priced competitors and transmit efficiently
- **Tracks and tires**: track link molds for the construction line are precise, and the links stay connected without falling apart
- **Pneumatic elements**: the pneumatic cylinders are well made and can simulate realistic hydraulic motion
- **Motors and battery boxes**: if you plan a powered conversion, original Double Eagle motors are punchy and the battery boxes are compact

## Build Experience

The build experience is smooth throughout, helped by a complete parts system and clearly drawn plans. Take the classic **excavator plan**: the build starts at the undercarriage, then moves up through the slew platform, cab, boom and working attachment. Double Eagle's gear meshing is genuinely impressive — the multi-stage reduction runs freely and the bucket lifts demonstration bricks with ease.

The **bulldozer plan** shows off Double Eagle's strength in track systems. The blade lift uses a lead-screw-plus-gear drive, so turning the knob raises and lowers the blade smoothly with a precise feel. The cab is detailed, with a steering wheel and seat inside.

In the comments on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn), plenty of builders share modification ideas. The most popular is **motorization** — adding motors and a remote-control system to a Double Eagle construction model for full RC operation. Detailed conversion tutorials are available in the community and are beginner-friendly.

The **crane plan** is the hardest of the group, involving a complex outrigger deployment structure and a telescoping boom. Double Eagle's specialized pulley parts keep boom raise and lower smooth, and the four outriggers adjust independently to mimic a real crane's working stance.

## Finished Models

Double Eagle construction MOCs typically measure 30–50 cm and have a rugged look with paint schemes close to real machinery. The classic yellow-and-black livery is instantly recognizable. There is plenty of movement: the excavator's boom, stick and bucket all articulate together, the bulldozer blade raises and lowers, the crane telescopes and slews 360°, and the truck bed tips. Paired with scenery, they make a complete construction-site diorama.

## Verdict

**Overall: ★★★★☆**

This Double Eagle MOC plan collection is the best of what domestic construction machinery bricks have to offer. Part quality is consistent, the plans are well designed, and the community around them is active. Search the "Double Eagle" tag on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) and start with whichever machine appeals to you. If you want value for money and the fun of a mechanical build, these plans are well worth trying.
