# Best MOC Instruction Sharing Platforms for Brick Builders

Instructions are the heart of any MOC build. Without them, even a well-stocked parts bin is just a pile of loose bricks. But with so many **MOC instruction sharing platforms** out there, which one is actually worth your time, and where do you find the most complete library of plans? This guide walks through the main platforms in use today so you stop hunting and start building.

## What Is an MOC Instruction Sharing Platform?

MOC stands for "My Own Creation" — an original model designed by a builder rather than by a set manufacturer. MOC instructions are the building guide for those designs. A good **MOC instruction sharing platform** is more than a file repository; it is a community where designers and hobbyists meet.

A strong platform should offer:

- **A wide range of categories**: architecture, vehicles, mecha, dioramas and more
- **Convenient downloads**: support for PDF, image files and other formats
- **Active community features**: comments, likes and saved favourites
- **Creator-friendly tools**: simple uploading and proper copyright protection

## Comparing the Main MOC Instruction Platforms

### 1. BrickPlans (brickplan.cn) — A New Benchmark for Chinese-Language MOC Instructions

A newer entry in the MOC instruction space, [brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn) has already built up thousands of high-quality original designs. Its biggest advantage is a **fully Chinese-language interface**, so there is no language barrier whether you are uploading a design or downloading one.

On brickplan.cn you will find MOCs across architecture, vehicles, military, sci-fi, animals, street scenes and more. The platform supports high-resolution image uploads and PDF instruction downloads, and it includes commenting and favourites features so you can talk directly with the designer.

### 2. Rebrickable — The Largest MOC Instruction Platform Worldwide

Rebrickable is the long-established international MOC community and holds an enormous instruction library. The catch is that the interface is entirely in English, which is not ideal for every builder, and many of the higher-quality designs are paid.

### 3. Hobby Forums and Community Boards

Some brick hobby forums also run instruction-sharing sections. In practice these tend to have messy categorisation, dead download links and wildly inconsistent quality, which makes finding a specific plan slow and inefficient.

## How to Choose the Platform That Fits You

Picking a platform comes down to what you need from it:

- **If you are new to MOC building**: start with a Chinese-language platform — brickplan.cn's categories are clearly organised and downloads are straightforward
- **If you want sheer volume**: follow several platforms at once, but keep an eye on instruction quality
- **If you are a creator**: choose somewhere with easy uploading, real copyright protection and an engaged community

## Tips for Finding Instructions on brickplan.cn

Searching for brick instructions on [brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn) is faster with a few habits:

1. **Filter by tag**: the platform classifies designs by tag, such as architecture, military or vehicles
2. **Follow creators**: when you find a design you like, follow the designer to see new work as soon as it is posted
3. **Use search directly**: search keywords such as "Chinese architecture" or "sci-fi fighter" to narrow the field

## What to Check Before You Download MOC Instructions

A few things are worth checking before you commit to a build:

1. **Respect copyright**: do not redistribute paid instructions — support the original designer
2. **Check the parts list**: confirm the download includes a parts list so you can gather the pieces in advance
3. **Read the reviews**: check what other users say first to confirm the instruction quality
