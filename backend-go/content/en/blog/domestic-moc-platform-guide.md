# Where to Find High-Quality Chinese MOC Instructions Online

As Chinese brick brands have grown stronger, more builders are moving from "follow the manual" to "design it myself." Instructions are the unavoidable first step toward your own MOC. So which domestic platforms are actually reliable for Chinese brick MOC instructions, and how do they differ in quality and how you get the files? This guide walks through each type.

## 1. General-Purpose Instruction Communities

These platforms are built around a "community plus instructions" model: you browse other people's builds and upload your own designs.

**BrickPlan** has drawn a lot of attention in recent years for its Chinese-language interface and locally tuned experience. It gathers a large number of MOC instructions that work with Chinese brick brands and lets you filter by theme, part count and difficulty. Files usually come with clear step-by-step building instructions and a parts list, and some of the better ones can be previewed online. As a one-stop instruction platform, it suits newcomers who want to find something suitable and start building quickly.

**GoBricks Forum** sits on top of one of the country's largest parts supply chains and has a strong community atmosphere. Instruction quality here is generally high — many experienced MOC designers publish original, play-tested designs and link to matching parts packs for purchase. Access is usually through replying to a thread or paying for the file. The upside is that instructions and parts come as one package; the downside is that some of the best content sits behind a barrier.

## 2. Platforms Where Original Designers Gather

**Rebrickable** is an international site, but plenty of Chinese designers publish there, making it an important source of original instructions for domestic builders. Its standout feature is reverse lookup: enter the parts you already own and see which instructions you can build. The interface is mostly English, which is slightly unfriendly to beginners, and many designers charge for their files.

Video platforms such as **Bilibili** and **Douyin** are a different kind of distribution channel. Many MOC designers post build videos and offer PDF instructions via the description or a direct message. Free instructions are easy to come by this way, but quality varies widely and you have to judge each one yourself.

## 3. Brand-Official and Instruction Marketplaces

**LEGO Ideas**, the **LEGO official site**, and the official communities of major Chinese brands such as **SEMBO**, **Qiji** and **Double Eagle** all release official collaboration instructions or fan submissions from time to time. Part compatibility and structural stability tend to be best with these, which suits builders chasing a flawless result.

Dedicated instruction marketplaces are also worth a look, **MOCBay** among them. These sites use a designer-onboarding, per-instruction pricing model: files are reviewed, quality is more dependable, and delivery is usually instant after online payment. They suit advanced builders who are willing to pay for good design.

## 4. How to Choose the Right Platform for You

With so many options for Chinese brick MOC instructions, use this checklist:

- **New to the hobby**: start with a Chinese-language platform like BrickPlan, where tutorials are complete and difficulty tiers are clearly labelled. The learning curve is gentler.
- **Chasing quality**: pick sources with a review process, such as the GoBricks Forum or MOCBay. Instructions are more stable and error rates are lower.
- **Free and plentiful**: browse video platforms like Bilibili and Douyin, and read the comments for extra details.
- **Budget to spare**: buy authorised instructions on Rebrickable or in official communities. You support the original designer and get better after-sales answers to your questions.

## 5. Final Thoughts

Whichever platform you pick, start with a small model before working up to a complex scene build. After downloading instructions, check the parts list against what you actually have on hand, and consider using BrickPlan's parts library feature to refine your plan. Every builder deserves to find their own kind of fun in the world of Chinese bricks.
