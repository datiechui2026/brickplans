# Brick University MOC: Main Hall, Library and Campus Layout

The short version first: a university is one of those subjects that lets architectural MOC builders go really big. Around 3,200 parts and two 32x32 baseplates joined together give you a complete campus corner with a main hall and a library. The main hall stands roughly 30 cm tall, the whole build runs about 10 hours, and it suits advanced builders who want a large-footprint project.

## Step 1: Planning the campus footprint

For a university scene, join two 32x32 baseplates into a single 64x32 base. Put the main hall on the left half and the library on the right, leaving the middle open for a plaza and a planted strip. Lay the baseplates down first, mark the roads with light grey bricks, and use green baseplates for the lawn areas. The main hall is about 20 cm deep and the library about 16 cm, with a 6 cm wide plaza between the two buildings so there is room for minifigures and trees.

## Step 2: The main hall facade

The main hall is the face of the campus, so build it in brick red with white trim and a three-part elevation: a dark base course at ground level, red brick with large glass windows through the middle, and a white cornice capping the top. Give the entrance an arch with columns and hang a university name plaque over the door. The hall is about 30 cm tall and 26 cm wide across 3 storeys, with 4 windows per storey and pilasters separating them, which is enough to lock in the classical collegiate look.

## Step 3: The library and its dome

Build the library in light beige brick with a round dome at its centre: stack curved bricks inward ring by ring to form a hemisphere, then add a finial on top. Under the dome sit two floors of reading rooms, with a large glass curtain wall on the front and bookshelves and small tables and chairs inside. The library is about 24 cm tall with a dome roughly 10 cm across, making it the visual high point of the scene; a small light under the dome gives a nicer glow through the top.

## Step 4: Plaza landscaping and details

Place a fountain at the centre of the plaza, stacking transparent pieces for the water column, plant 4-6 trees on either side, and set benches and minifigures out on the lawn. Lay the roads in light grey brick in a cross pattern and stand 2 street lamps along the roadside. Once everything is done you are looking at about 10 hours of building and a budget of roughly 280 CNY, with the finished campus measuring 64 cm long and 32 cm wide, which brings a real academic atmosphere to a shelf at home.

## FAQ

**Q: How many parts does a brick university MOC need?**
A: About 3,200. Brick red wall bricks make up around 35%, light beige bricks around 25%, and there are roughly 60 transparent glass pieces. Two baseplates are joined together, the mix of part types is moderate, and the budget comes to about 280 CNY.

**Q: Is a university MOC hard to build?**
A: It is medium-to-hard. The symmetry of the main hall facade and the curve of the library dome both demand patience. With 3,200 parts it takes around 10 hours, so it is best to tackle at least two street scene builds before taking this on.

**Q: How do you build the library dome on a university MOC?**
A: Step curved bricks inward ring by ring from the bottom up, reducing the diameter by one stud per ring, and about 8 layers will close into a hemisphere. Add a finial on top, and seal the underside with plates so it does not collapse.

**Q: How many minifigures suit a university MOC?**
A: 8-12 is a good range, including students, professors and a security guard. Student figures can carry backpacks and book accessories, and spreading a few across the plaza and the library makes the scene feel lived in.

**Q: What separates a university MOC from a secondary school MOC?**
A: The university is larger in scale, with a library dome and a planned plaza, and the architecture leans harder into the collegiate style. A secondary school is more compact and centres on a classroom block and a sports field. A university works well as the core building of a large scene.
