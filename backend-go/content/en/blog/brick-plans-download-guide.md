# How to Download MOC Instructions: 7 Top Platforms & Tips

For brick builders working on their own models, a good set of instructions is where every project starts. The problem is volume: the web is flooded with files, and beginners rarely know where to look or how to tell a solid plan from a sloppy one. This guide covers the whole process, from seven platforms worth your time to the practical habits that make downloading faster.

## Why download MOC instructions?

Before you start hunting, it helps to know what a good plan actually delivers. A well-made MOC instruction set is more than assembly steps — it is a designer's thinking laid out in order. Quality plans include:

- A complete parts list (BOM)
- Step-by-step building instructions with a clear image for every step
- Finished-model renders from several angles
- Building techniques and things to watch out for

If MOC building is still new to you, start with the [MOC plans beginner guide](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) for the basics. On [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) you will find a large library of both free and paid high-quality MOC instructions.

The biggest MOC instruction community anywhere, with more than 100,000 files and fine-grained filtering by part count, difficulty and theme.

**Pros**: The largest catalogue, a well-developed rating system, support for importing your own parts inventory
**Cons**: English-only interface, some instructions are paid, access from mainland China can be slow

### 3. Bricklink — the long-running brick community

Bricklink is the old home of brick fans, and its instruction side (Bricklink Studio) draws a large pool of high-level designers.

**Recommended approach**: Use the Bricklink Studio software to download a huge range of digital plans for free and view the 3D building steps right inside the program.

### 4. BrickSet — the official set instruction archive

If you need the official PDF booklet for a LEGO set, BrickSet is the best choice. It holds instruction manuals for LEGO sets from the 1960s through to today.

### 5. Xiaohongshu — China's newer MOC scene

**Search terms**: MOC instructions, brick instructions, brick MOC

Xiaohongshu has a large volume of free plans shared by Chinese MOC builders. Quality varies a lot, but pleasant surprises turn up often. Follow accounts that post instructions regularly and browse their back catalogue.

### 6. Baidu Tieba — brick hobbyist forums

**Recommended boards**: the main brick building, MOC and LEGO sub-forums

Tieba is the traditional gathering place for Chinese brick fans, and many experienced builders share free plans there. Pinned and featured threads hold a large stock of downloadable instruction resources.

### 7. Taobao and Pinduoduo — buying instruction bundles

If you would rather not spend hours searching file by file, search Taobao or Pinduoduo for brick instruction downloads or MOC instruction collections. A few yuan usually buys a bundle of several hundred plans.

**Note**: Confirm the file format and resolution before paying, so you do not end up with low-quality scans.

## Practical instruction download tips

### Tip 1: How to judge instruction quality

| Criteria | High quality | Low quality |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Parts list | Includes part numbers, colours and quantities | Missing or vague |
| Step instructions | Every step clearly labelled | Steps skip ahead, no intermediate stages |
| Image clarity | Sharp, zoomable detail | Blurry and pixelated |
| Colour scheme | Clear colour recommendations | Colour plan unclear |

### Tip 2: Choosing a file format

- **PDF**: the most recommended — printable, zoomable and supported on every platform
- **Image formats** (JPG/PNG): convenient on a phone, awkward to print
- **LDD/LXF**: digital model formats that require LEGO Digital Designer to open
- **IO format**: the Bricklink Studio format, the most capable of the four

## FAQ

**Q: What if a downloaded instruction file will not open?**
A: Open PDF instructions in Adobe Acrobat Reader. For LDD files, install LEGO Digital Designer first.

**Q: What if I cannot buy the parts listed in the instructions?**
A: Every instruction on BrickPlans includes a parts list feature that links straight to the matching parts pack on Taobao or Pinduoduo.

**Q: Is there a big quality gap between free and paid instructions?**
A: Paid instructions generally offer clearer steps, more complete parts lists and better-thought-out designs, but plenty of free plans are just as good.
