# Best Marvel LEGO Sets to Buy in 2026: A Complete Buying Guide

Marvel brick sets are consistently one of the hottest categories on the market. From Iron Man's armor lab to the Avengers' final battle, from Spider-Man's street patrols to the Guardians' cosmic adventures, every Marvel set tells a superhero story. This Marvel LEGO guide runs from entry-level builds up to collector-grade display pieces so you can pick the sets most worth buying in 2026.

## How to Choose a Marvel Brick Set?

Before getting into specific recommendations, it helps to match the set to the builder.

| Buying factor | Beginners | Advanced builders | Collectors |

| Budget | ¥100-300 | ¥300-800 | ¥800-3000+ |

## Entry-Level Marvel Sets (¥100-300)

### 1. Spider-Man: No Way Home — Battle at the Statue of Liberty
**Pieces**: 320 | **Price**: about ¥249 | **Minifigures**: 3

This is the best starting point in the Marvel range. The set recreates the battle atop the Statue of Liberty from Spider-Man: No Way Home and includes three minifigures: Spider-Man (red and blue suit), Spider-Man (black and gold suit) and Mysterio. The build is moderate in difficulty and takes roughly 45 minutes.

**Why it's recommended**: As an entry-level pick, the minifigure quality is excellent — the black and gold suit Spider-Man is an exclusive minifigure. The scene includes a rotating scaffold and a launcher, so playability is strong.

### 2. Iron Man: Armor Lab
**Pieces**: 260 | **Price**: about ¥199 | **Minifigures**: 2

This one includes two armors, Iron Man Mark 3 and Mark 5, plus lab equipment such as a workbench and mechanical arms. The workbench rotates and the arms can grab parts, giving it plenty of interactive play.

## Mid-Range Marvel Sets (¥300-800)

### 3. Avengers: Quinjet
**Pieces**: 850 | **Price**: about ¥699 | **Minifigures**: 4

The Quinjet is the Avengers' signature vehicle, and this set reproduces the classic look from The Avengers. The jet features folding wings, an opening and closing cockpit, a rear hatch and a hidden weapons system.

**Review highlights**:
- With the wings deployed the span reaches 45cm, which looks extremely impressive
- The cockpit seats 2 minifigures
- The rear hatch opens onto internal storage space for weapons and gear
- Includes four minifigures: Captain America, Iron Man, Black Widow and Hawkeye

### 4. Thanos: Sanctuary II
**Pieces**: 680 | **Price**: about ¥549 | **Minifigures**: 3

Thanos' Sanctuary II is a key location in both Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame. The set includes Thanos' throne room, an Infinity Gauntlet display stand and a Space Stone device. The standout here is the throne design — a large number of purple transparent pieces create the energy effect of the cosmic stones.

## Collector-Level Marvel Sets (¥800-3000+)

### 5. Avengers Tower
**Pieces**: 3200 | **Price**: about ¥2499 | **Minifigures**: 10

This is one of the largest Marvel brick sets currently available. Avengers Tower contains six functional levels, each recreating a classic scene from the films.

| Floor | Function | Highlight |
|------|------|------|
| Level 1 | Lobby / reception area | Avengers logo on the floor |
| Level 2 | Dr. Banner's lab | Gamma radiation research equipment |
| Level 3 | Tony Stark's workshop | Armor assembly mechanical arms |
| Level 4 | Tactical command center | Holographic projection table |
| Level 5 | Lounge | Sofa, kitchen, recreation room |
| Rooftop | Landing pad | Quinjet takeoff and landing platform |

### 6. Wakanda: City of Technology
**Pieces**: 2800 | **Price**: about ¥1899 | **Minifigures**: 6

This set recreates Wakanda as seen in Black Panther and Avengers: Infinity War. It includes several modules: the throne hall, Princess Shuri's laboratory, the vibranium mine and the shield emitter. The minifigure lineup includes Black Panther, Princess Shuri, Okoye, M'Baku and Erik Killmonger.

## Marvel Set Collecting Advice

1. **Watch for exclusive minifigures**: Many Marvel sets include minifigures you cannot get anywhere else, and prices climb sharply once a set retires.
2. **Prioritize large buildings**: Big scene-based sets usually retire faster and have more room to appreciate.
3. **Check part usefulness**: Choose sets with versatile parts so you can reuse them for MOC building later.
