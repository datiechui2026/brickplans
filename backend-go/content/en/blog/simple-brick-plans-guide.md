# Simple Brick Plans for Beginners: 5 Fast Building Projects

Most people who are new to bricks hit the same wall: the instructions look too complicated to follow. In practice, the barrier to building is much lower than it appears. With the right simple plan in front of you, almost anyone can finish a first model in 15 minutes. The five projects below are chosen for complete beginners — low part counts, clear steps, and a finished result you can put on a shelf.

## Why Should Beginners Start With Simple Plans?

Plenty of newcomers jump straight into a large set and then get discouraged by hundreds of parts and a hundred-page booklet. Starting with simple plans avoids that:

- **Fast wins**: a small project is done in 20 minutes, so you feel the payoff immediately
- **Core skills**: you learn how bricks clutch, how to read a plan, and how to sort parts
- **Patience and focus**: short builds establish the habit before you take on long ones
- **Cheap mistakes**: with few parts, a wrong step is easy to pull apart and redo

If MOC (My Own Creation) building interests you, read the [MOC plans beginner guide](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) first, then come back here for hands-on practice.

## Project 1: Mini House

The classic first build, and the most basic of all simple plans.

| Parameter | Value |
|---------|------|
| Parts | about 80 |
| Build time | 15-20 minutes |
| Difficulty | ★☆☆☆☆ |
| Parts needed | 2x4 bricks, 2x2 bricks, 1x2 bricks, roof plates, door and window frames |

**Steps**:
1. Build the foundation from 2x4 bricks, 4x6 studs in size
2. Stack the walls in layers, leaving gaps for the door and windows
3. Fit the door and window frames
4. Add a pitched roof using roof plates
5. Finish with a chimney and a small garden

Simple as it is, this house contains the core techniques of brick building: stacking from foundation to body, leaving openings, and roofing. The finished model works as a desk piece or as a reference for a larger architectural MOC.

## Project 2: Simple Car

For anyone who likes vehicles, this is the natural starting point.

**Parts list**:
- 4x6 baseplate × 1
- 2x4 bricks × 6
- 1x2 bricks × 4
- 1x4 bricks × 2
- Slope bricks × 2 (for the hood)
- Wheels × 4
- Transparent bricks × 2 (for the windows)

**Key techniques**: watch the orientation of the slope bricks at the front, and keep the wheel positions symmetrical. [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) has many more vehicle plans, from race cars to trucks.

## Project 3: Small Animals

Building animals from a handful of bricks is a good way to train your sense of shape.

| Animal | Parts | Build time | Difficulty |
|-----|-------|---------|------|
| Bird | 35 | 10 minutes | ★☆☆☆☆ |
| Dog | 55 | 15 minutes | ★☆☆☆☆ |
| Cat | 60 | 15 minutes | ★☆☆☆☆ |
| Rabbit | 45 | 12 minutes | ★☆☆☆☆ |

The trick with animal MOCs is capturing the defining features: the shape of the ears, the length of the tail, the curve of the body. Swap colors and part combinations and you can build a whole series of your own.

## Project 4: Simple Tree

Plants are essential in any scene, yet many beginners assume they are complicated. A simple tree takes just over 20 parts.

**How to build it**:
- Trunk: stack brown 2x2 bricks four layers high
- Canopy: combine green slope bricks and tiles into a ball shape
- Base: a green 4x4 baseplate as grass

## Project 5: First MOC — Mini Street Corner

Once the four projects above are under your belt, try your first real MOC: a mini street corner. It combines the house, the car and the tree into one small scene.

**Scale**: about 200 parts, roughly one hour of building

**Approach**:
1. Start from a 32x32 baseplate
2. Put the mini house in the corner
3. Park the car in front of the door
4. Plant the tree
5. Add details such as a street lamp and a bench

Finish this and you have officially stepped through the door into MOC building. From there, move on to the more advanced projects in the [brick plan downloads](/en/blog/brick-plans-download-guide) guide.

## Beginner FAQ

**Q: Which brand of bricks should I buy?**
A: Any brand works, but pick one with good compatibility. See the brand recommendations on BrickPlans.

**Q: Where do I buy individual parts?**
A: The parts library on [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) lists sources and price comparisons for common brick parts.

**Q: What if I can't follow a plan?**
A: Simple plans use color 3D renders, with the parts for each step highlighted in different colors. Work through the numbered steps in order and don't skip ahead.
