# Zelda MOC Plans: Build Link, Hyrule Castle and the Master Sword

Since 1986, The Legend of Zelda has built one of the most respected legacies in games: a vast world, distinctive art direction and stories that stick with you. With good **Zelda MOC plans**, brick builders can now pull Hyrule's landscapes and heroes off the screen and onto the shelf. Here are the Zelda-themed builds worth your parts collection.

## Why Zelda Works So Well in Bricks

Every era of Zelda has its own recognisable look, from the painterly finish of Breath of the Wild to the floating sky islands of Tears of the Kingdom and the storybook charm of Ocarina of Time. That variety is ideal raw material for brick building. A strong **Zelda MOC plan** has to do two things at once: nail the character silhouette and colour blocking, and capture the sense of adventure that makes Hyrule feel like Hyrule.

## Building Tips and Colour Schemes

Colour is the hard part of a Zelda build. Hyrule's palette leans warm and natural, with a lot of green, brown, gold and blue. A few practical rules:

1. **Lead with earth tones.** Use tan, olive green and dark brown for the main structure of buildings.
2. **Add gold sparingly.** Gold parts sell the royal Zelda look — use them as accents, not as a base colour.
3. **Use transparent parts for magic.** Blue transparent elements read as the glow of Sheikah technology.
4. **Fill scenes with plant parts.** Leaves and flower elements in quantity are what make a Hyrule scene feel alive rather than architectural.
