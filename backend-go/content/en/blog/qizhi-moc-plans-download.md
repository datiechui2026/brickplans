## Build difficulty

**Difficulty rating: ★★★★☆ (high)**

**QiZhi MOC instructions** tend to be complex, especially the mech designs, which usually carry a lot of joint structure and articulated mechanisms. Instructions in the 800-1,500 piece range are a solid challenge for builders with some experience; first-timers should start with a 400-600 piece small mech and work up. Some designs involve gear linkages and ball-joint assembly that reward patience and careful work — rushing the sub-assemblies is the usual way these builds go wrong, so sort parts before you start and build the joints as separate modules.

## Part review

We put QiZhi's core parts through a systematic test:

- **Mould precision: ★★★★☆** The specialised shaped parts are moulded to an excellent standard, though basic bricks occasionally show slight flash that doesn't affect the build.
- **Joint strength: ★★★★★** The ball joints and rotating joints are the standout feature — well-judged friction that holds a pose during handling instead of sagging.
- **Special part variety: ★★★★★** Mech-specific parts such as shoulder armour, chest plates and weapon accessories are plentiful and strongly designed; other brands struggle to match them.
- **Printed parts and stickers: ★★★☆☆** Print accuracy is acceptable, but sticker adhesion could be better — handle with care.

Overall the **QiZhi brick review** picture is clear: the strength is the completeness and design quality of the mech part range, not the commodity bricks. If mech MOC building is your thing, QiZhi is hard to beat, and the joint range alone justifies building a parts stock.
