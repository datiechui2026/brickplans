# Brick Chick MOC Review: 86 Parts, 40 Minutes, Very Cute

The short version: this brick chick MOC runs on just 86 parts, goes together in about 40 minutes, and stands roughly 9 cm tall. The rounded yellow body, the small beak and the little feet earn it a 9 out of 10 for cuteness against other models at the same price, which makes it a strong value pick for beginners and anyone who builds small animals.

## Design: Rounded Yellow Silhouette, Cute by Default

The chick is built almost entirely in bright yellow. Curved bricks and slope bricks are stacked to create a plump, rounded outline, and right-angle lines are nearly impossible to find on the finished model. The head-to-body ratio sits close to 1:1.2, which matches a real chick, and the round head plus large eyes give it that baby-animal look on their own.

That shaping is the whole point of the design. Nothing here is trying to be realistic or technical; the goal is a silhouette that reads as soft from any angle. Sit it on a desk or a bookshelf and it works as a small accent piece rather than a display-centerpiece build.

## Details: The Beak and Feet Get Real Attention

The orange beak is recreated with only 2 parts, yet it still produces a convincing pointed shape. The feet are attached with hinge pieces, so they can swing through a small range while the stance stays stable. The wings are made from 1x2 tiles laid on at an angle, which is a simple solution that still reads clearly as folded wings.

Packing 5 points of movement into 86 parts is where the design earns its credit. Small builds often skip articulation entirely, and this one does not. None of that movement is decorative-only either: the hinged feet are what let the model stand without wobbling.

## Build Experience: Smooth Sequence, About 40 Minutes

The instructions are split into 4 step bags, averaging a little over 20 parts each, so the hunt for the next piece stays light. In a timed build the model came together in about 40 minutes. The head and beak take up roughly half of that time and are the parts that ask the most patience, mostly because the small curved and sloped elements have to line up cleanly.

Clutch is firm throughout and nothing sits loose on the finished model. For a build in the CNY 50 bracket, the hand feel sits in the middle-to-upper range: no mushy connections, no parts that need forcing.

## Cute-First Positioning and Where It Fits

This chick is built to be looked at. At 9 cm tall it takes up almost no space, which makes it an easy fit for a work desk, a shelf or a child's room. The brief is simply "it makes you happy when you see it," and it delivers that without asking for a large parts budget or a long build session.

## FAQ

A: Work through the 4 step bags in order. The body comes first, then the head, and the feet are connected last. The full build takes about 40 minutes across 86 parts. No special tools are needed, everything is hand assembly, and a first-time builder can finish it in one sitting.

**Q: Can the finished chick stand up for display?**
