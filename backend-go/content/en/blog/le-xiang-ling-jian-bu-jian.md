# Lexiang Missing Parts Guide: How to Get Replacement Bricks

Few things kill a build faster than opening a fresh box and finding a critical part missing, or watching a tiny element roll off the desk mid-assembly. For dedicated **Lexiang** builders, **Lexiang part replacement** is a topic that comes up constantly. This guide walks you through the whole process so a missing element never stalls your build again.

## Why You Should Know the Lexiang Replacement Process

Lexiang has earned a following among Chinese brick fans thanks to its broad range of themes, solid part quality and reasonable pricing. But no manufacturing process is perfect, and the occasional missing or damaged element is unavoidable. Knowing how **Lexiang part replacement** works saves you time and keeps a small parts problem from ruining the fun of a build.

The usual situations that call for a replacement request:

- **Missing from a new box**: individual elements absent from a newly purchased set
- **Damaged parts**: cracked or warped elements caused in transit
- **Accidental loss**: small pieces that roll away and disappear during a build
- **Special requirements**: specific colours or sizes you need for an MOC

## The Official Lexiang Replacement Process

### 2.1 Official Support Channels

Lexiang offers several official routes for submitting a **Lexiang part replacement** request:

1. **Official WeChat account**: follow Lexiang's official account and go to "After-Sales Service" → "Part Replacement" in the menu, then fill in the required details
2. **Official Tmall / JD.com flagship stores**: contact the shop's customer service, describe the missing part, and the agent will guide you through the process
3. **Official website**: visit the Lexiang site and find the replacement request entry under "Service & Support"

### 2.2 What to Prepare for a Request

To get your request processed quickly, have the following ready:

- **Product name and model**: the product number printed on the box or the cover of the instruction booklet
- **Missing part number and quantity**: look up the number in the parts list in the instructions
- **Proof of purchase**: an order screenshot or photo of the receipt
- **Photo of the problem**: a clear image showing where the part is missing

### 2.3 Turnaround Time and Cost

Lexiang's official replacement service is normally **free**, especially for parts missing from a newly purchased set. Handling typically takes **3-7 working days**, depending on stock and shipping arrangements. Note that for parts lost through your own fault, some channels may charge a part fee plus postage.

## How to Look Up Lexiang Part Numbers

Before you file a **Lexiang part replacement** request, identifying the part number accurately is the key step. These methods cover almost every case:

### 3.1 The Parts List in the Instructions

The last page of every Lexiang instruction booklet contains a full parts list, showing the number, colour, quantity and illustration for every element. This is the most direct and most accurate way to check.

### 3.2 Online Part Databases

Part information for some Lexiang series can be looked up online. Useful tools:

- **BrickPlans part library**: search a Lexiang product number on BrickPlans to see the corresponding parts list
- **Rebrickable**: the well-known international brick database, which already covers some Lexiang sets
- **Lexiang's official mini program**: scan the QR code on the packaging to open the digital instructions and parts list

### 3.3 How Lexiang Part Numbers Work

Lexiang part numbers generally use a **4-6 digit** code system. Some numbering overlaps with LEGO's, but Lexiang also maintains its own independent numbering. Understanding the system helps you describe the part you need accurately when filing a **Lexiang part replacement** request.

## Alternatives When the Official Route Is Too Slow

If the official replacement process is slow or you urgently need one specific element, consider these options:

### 4.1 Buying from Marketplace Platforms

- **Taobao / Pinduoduo**: search for "Lexiang parts" or "Lexiang replacement" to find shops selling loose Lexiang elements. Check the part number and colour before ordering, and favour sellers with strong ratings and high sales
- **Xianyu**: some builders sell off spare Lexiang parts, usually cheaper than new ones and well suited to bulk replacement

### 4.2 Cross-Brand Compatible Parts

Lexiang elements are dimensionally compatible with standard bricks, so you can use equivalent parts from other brands as a temporary stand-in. For example:

- **Common compatible elements**: basic bricks, plates and slopes are highly interchangeable across brands
- **Special elements**: for parts unique to Lexiang, use the official channel first to avoid compatibility problems

### 4.3 Help from the Community

Ask in the BrickPlans community, brick forums or Lexiang fan groups. Another builder may have exactly the spare you need and be willing to trade or give it away. This **Lexiang part replacement** route saves money and connects you with like-minded builders.

## Tips for Cheaper, Smoother Replacements

### 5.1 Batch Your Requests

If several sets need parts, submit everything in one request. You save on postage and make it easier for support to handle it as a single case.

### 5.2 Keep a Spares Bin

After every build, sort the leftover spare elements into storage. Over time you build up a useful parts library, and you can check your own stock first the next time something goes missing.

### 5.3 Check the Parts List on Arrival

When the replacement arrives, go through the instruction parts list element by element to confirm quantities and colours. If anything is still short, contact support again promptly.

### 5.4 Protect Small Parts While Building

Build over a light-coloured tray or a dedicated building mat so small elements cannot roll away. This matters most with sets that use a lot of small parts; good working habits sharply reduce the odds of accidental loss.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does Lexiang charge postage for part replacement?**
A: Official replacement for parts missing from a new set is normally shipped free. For replacements caused by your own loss, you may need to cover postage; confirm the details with customer service.

**Q: How long before I hear back about a request?**
A: Official support usually replies within **24-48 hours**, and you can check progress through customer service.

**Q: Are Lexiang parts interchangeable with LEGO parts?**
A: Most basic elements are interchangeable, but Lexiang has its own special parts and colour system. For **Lexiang part replacement**, use original parts where you can.

**Q: Can I still get parts for a discontinued set?**
A: Official stock for out-of-production sets is limited. If the official channel cannot supply the part, try marketplace platforms or the second-hand market.

**Lexiang part replacement** is simpler than most people expect.
