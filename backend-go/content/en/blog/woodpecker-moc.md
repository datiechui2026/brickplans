# Woodpecker MOC Review: 486-Part Pecking Mechanism Build

Here is the verdict up front: this woodpecker MOC uses 486 parts, stands 18 cm tall, and takes roughly 2.5 hours to build. The red-and-black colour scheme lands at about 90% accuracy to the real bird, and the geared pecking mechanism in the head is genuinely fun to operate. Overall score: 8.5/10 — worth picking up if you build animal models or like mechanical action in your MOCs.

## Red and Black Colour Accuracy

The designer splits the plumage into three zones — deep red, bright red and pure black — to reproduce the woodpecker's signature red crown and black feathers, with light grey used as a transition across the belly. The stance and colour blocking sit close to a real great spotted woodpecker. Red covers roughly 15% of the head, which puts the visual focus exactly where it belongs, and the brick colour consistency is well controlled: no obvious shade mismatches whether you look at it up close or from across the room.

## The Pecking Pose Design

The model is frozen at the moment of impact. A triangular support piece at the tail braces against the tree trunk, the body leans forward at 45 degrees, and the head is linked to the trunk through a gear and connecting-rod assembly. Press the trigger at the tail and the bird's head hammers the trunk repeatedly with a sharp, crisp knock. The stroke is about 2 cm, and the rhythm comes close to a real woodpecker's drumming rate.

## Playability and Handling

Beyond the pecking mechanism, the trunk hides two "egg" parts that you can retrieve by opening the bark, and the beak can be swapped for a worm-carrying version. After 10 minutes of continuous play there was no noticeable looseness, and the joint damping feels right. It suits builders aged 8 and up. As a desk piece, the 18 cm height makes it easy to spot on a bookshelf.

## Value and Instructions

The parts work out to about 0.35 yuan each, for a total of roughly 170 yuan — around 30% cheaper than an official set of comparable size. The full building instructions and parts list are available on brickplan.cn, with step-by-step viewing and parts-number search, so even a first-time builder can replicate it without trouble.
