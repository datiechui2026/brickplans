# SEMBO MOC Plans Review: Military Tanks and Street Scenes

## Product Overview

SEMBO is one of the domestic Chinese brick brands that stands out most clearly in two areas: military hardware and city street scenes. The BrickPlans community holds a large library of SEMBO MOC plans, and this review focuses on the two flagship categories of **SEMBO MOC plans** — military equipment and urban street architecture. Everything from main battle tanks to European-style corner buildings, from military transport aircraft to garden cafés, can be found on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) by searching the "SEMBO" tag. Every plan comes with a detailed parts list and a building guide.

## Build Difficulty

**Difficulty rating:** ★★★☆☆

Medium difficulty. SEMBO MOC plans cover a moderate spread of complexity. The military series is mostly vehicles with clear, logical build sequences; the street scene series is mostly architecture with rich detail but a comfortable building pace. A single model takes roughly 5–10 hours to complete. SEMBO's part system is mature and consistent, and the step design in the plans is very sensible, making these suitable for builders with 3–6 months of MOC experience. Beginners should first search "SEMBO starter plans" on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) and begin with a small building or vehicle.

## Part Review

The plans in this series range from **800–2,500 pcs**, covering mid-size to large MOCs. Recommended brand: **SEMBO** — these plans are optimised for SEMBO parts, and both the colour scheme and part selection are based on the SEMBO product line.

Key parts analysis:

- **Military camouflage parts**: SEMBO's army green, sand and dark green elements have clean, true colours that suit military vehicle reproduction
- **Building exterior bricks**: SEMBO's beige, white and reddish-brown bricks have a fine surface texture that works well for street scene architecture
- **Window and door frames**: SEMBO offers windows and door frames in a range of styles; the European curved windows and arches have excellent quality
- **Plants and decorative elements**: the street scene series needs plenty of flowers, trees and street lamps, and SEMBO's accessory range is broad
- **Weapon accessories**: the guns, ammo crates and communications equipment in the military series are detailed and well executed

## Build Experience

**Military series** — take the 99A main battle tank MOC plan as an example. The build starts with the suspension system. SEMBO's road wheels and track links are moulded precisely, and the assembled model rolls smoothly without catching. The turret uses a multi-layer stacked structure that reproduces the 99A's wedge-shaped turret profile. The reactive armour blocks are built as separate small modules that can be removed to show the interior structure. In the plan community on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn), military enthusiasts have shared display-base builds that pair with this model to striking effect.

**Street scene series** — take the European café MOC plan as an example. The build starts at the foundation and works upward floor by floor. The ground floor is the shopfront, with a large picture window paired with an awning, plus a small flower bed and street lamp at the entrance. The second floor is the residential level, with plants decorating the balcony. The roof is built from slope bricks to recreate the pitched roofline of European architecture. The interior is just as impressive — bar counter, coffee machine, tables and chairs, and bookshelves are all included. The designer also provides an expansion plan on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) for interior lighting; with an LED strip installed, the night-time effect is gorgeous.

**Military airfield scene** plans are another highlight. They include a hangar, runway and control tower, and combined with military aircraft MOCs they let you build a complete military base scene.

## Finished Model Showcase

Military series finished models measure roughly 25–40 cm. The tanks look rugged and the camouflage finishes are highly faithful. Movable features include 360° turret rotation, gun elevation, running tracks and opening hatches. Paired with a display base, they present extremely well.

Street scene finished models measure about 25 cm × 20 cm × 30 cm for a single building, with refined shaping and rich detail. The removable floors make it easy to view the interior. Placed side by side, several buildings form a complete city street, and with minifigures and vehicles added the scene comes alive.

## Verdict

**Overall: ★★★★★**

SEMBO brick MOC plans perform excellently in both the military and street scene categories. Part quality is consistent, the colour range is broad, and the designers' attention to detail is impressive. Search the "SEMBO" tag on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) to explore more plans. Whether you are a military fan or a street scene builder, SEMBO MOC plans deliver a first-rate building experience.
