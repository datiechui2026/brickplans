# Brick Train MOC Blueprint Review: Steam to High-Speed Rail

## Product Overview

Trains hold a special place in brick MOC building — from the vintage locomotives of the steam era to the streamlined noses of modern high-speed rail, every model carries a piece of railway culture. This review covers the **brick train blueprint** series created by several train-focused designers in the BrickPlans community, spanning steam locomotives, diesel locomotives, electric locomotives and high-speed EMU sets. All blueprints can be found and downloaded by searching on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn), and every set ships with a detailed parts list plus track layout suggestions.

## Build Difficulty

**Difficulty rating**: ★★★★☆

On the higher end, and the main challenges are **reproducing the streamlined bodywork** and **integrating the drivetrain**. Steam locomotives involve a large number of curved plates and cylindrical structures, while high-speed trains need precise wedge-plate transitions at the nose. Expect 8–12 hours per locomotive, and considerably more once you add coach formations. Beginners should search "beginner train MOC" on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) first and start with a four-wheel cart to get comfortable with how train builds are structured.

## Parts Review

The blueprints in this series run from **1500 to 3000 pcs** (locomotive plus 2–3 coaches), which puts them in large-MOC territory. Recommended brands: **SEMBO** (a wide variety of curved plates, and dark-colour parts with consistent shading — ideal for train exteriors) or **Double Eagle** (a complete power-parts range, with reliable motors and gearboxes).

Key parts breakdown:

- **Wheels and connecting rods**: steam locomotives need plenty of red wheels and linked rods; Double Eagle's metal axle sets run smoothly
- **Curved roofs**: SEMBO offers curved bricks and slope bricks in multiple sizes, perfect for recreating the boiler shape of a steam locomotive
- **Track system**: pair the models with standard track; Double Eagle's curved track and switches are well compatible
- **Power modules**: if you want to motorise the build, Double Eagle's L motor is compact enough to hide inside the boiler
- **Transparent windows**: SEMBO's clear parts are very clean, so passenger coaches let light through nicely

## Build Experience

The steam locomotive blueprint starts from the **chassis frame**, and the first job is the wheel linkage — the most recognisable moving structure on any steam engine. The designer based the mechanism on the linkage principle used on real steam locomotives, using a crankshaft to pass piston motion to the drive wheels, so the wheels turn together as you push the model along. It feels genuinely mechanical.

The boiler is built as a cylindrical stack, clad on the outside with curved bricks and detailed with piping along the sides. Inside the cab there's a control desk, pressure gauges and a seat — a small space, but packed with detail. In the blueprint community on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn), one builder has shared a clever mod that adds a steam effect using cotton wool, and it photographs beautifully.

The high-speed rail blueprint puts the emphasis on the streamlined nose. The designer uses SNOT techniques and wedge plates, narrowing the profile layer by layer into a bullet shape. The headlights combine transparent parts with LED elements, and the effect is convincing once powered up. The coach interiors can be laid out with seating, and multiple coaches can be coupled into a longer formation.

## Finished Models

The finished steam locomotive measures roughly 50–60 cm including the tender, and looks vintage and ornate. The classic green-and-black scheme, set off with gold trim lines, gives it a real sense of refinement. Working features: linked wheel rods, piston movement, opening cab doors and an opening boiler hatch.

The high-speed train comes in at roughly 70–90 cm including four coaches, with a white streamlined body and blue striping that captures the prototype closely. The nose light illuminates, the coaches have LED lighting, and the whole thing feels thoroughly modern. On an oval track it can run at full speed, and the visual effect is striking.

## Verdict

**Overall: ★★★★★**

Train MOCs are one of the most rewarding categories in brick building. The mechanical beauty of a steam locomotive and the modern tech feel of high-speed rail each have their own appeal, and SEMBO's part quality lets the exterior detailing shine. Search the "train" category on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) — whether you want a static display piece or something that actually runs, there's a blueprint for you.
