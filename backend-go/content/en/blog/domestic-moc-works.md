# Domestic MOC Review: Chinese Builders and Brands to Watch

Chinese brick brands and the builders behind them have moved fast. What began as re-creating existing designs with aftermarket parts has turned into a mature ecosystem with original design and homegrown IP, and the best **domestic MOC builds** now stand comparison with anything released internationally. This review covers the creators, the brands and the parts that make them work.

## How Domestic MOC Got Here

There have been three distinct phases:

| Phase | Years | Characteristics | Notable work |
|------|-------|------|-------|
| Early | 2015-2018 | Mostly re-creations and copies, little original design | Gobricks replica street scenes |
| Growth | 2018-2021 | Original designs start appearing, part quality improves | SEMBO Chinese-style mecha |
| Mature | 2021-present | Independent IP, high-quality originals, international influence | Double Eagle technic supercars, Quanguan military line |

## Creators Worth Following

### 1. Designer "Brick Maker"

**Signature work**: Chinese street scene series, Forbidden City corner tower

"Brick Maker" is one of the best-known MOC designers in the country, known for Chinese-style architecture. The defining traits are dense detailing and carefully judged colour, blending traditional architectural aesthetics with brick technique.

**Build review**:

| Model | Parts | Difficulty | Build time | Rating |
|------|-------|------|---------|---------|
| Forbidden City corner tower | 3200 | ★★★★★ | 12-15h | ★★★★★ |
| Jiangnan water town | 1800 | ★★★★☆ | 6-8h | ★★★★★ |
| Huizhou-style dwelling | 1200 | ★★★☆☆ | 4-5h | ★★★★☆ |

**Design notes**:
- Heavy use of Chinese red, glazed gold and dark wood-tone parts
- Roofs built from multiple layers of flying eaves, reproducing the curves of Chinese architecture
- Fully furnished interiors with armchairs, folding screens and lanterns

### 2. Designer "Mechanics Master"

**Signature work**: V8 engine model, technic supercars

"Mechanics Master" works almost exclusively in Technic-style MOC, and the builds are known for precise mechanical structures. His **domestic MOC builds** carry a strong reputation among technic builders.

**Build review**:
- **V8 engine teaching model** (800 parts, ★★☆☆☆): a complete reproduction of V8 piston motion, crankshaft rotation and camshaft timing — a good mechanical starting point
- **Double Eagle supercar** (2800 parts, ★★★★★): fully independent suspension, 8-speed gearbox, opening scissor doors; mechanically as complex as a LEGO flagship

### 3. Designer "Model Maniac"

**Signature work**: Chinese military series, sci-fi mecha

"Model Maniac" focuses on military and science-fiction subjects, with builds known for rigorous research and accurate proportions.

## How Domestic Brands Compare for MOC

### Gobricks
**Part quality**: ★★★★★ | **Original design**: ★★★☆☆

Gobricks is known for part quality; its precision and clutch feel are essentially on par with LEGO, making it the most common parts brand among domestic MOC builders. Most part lists on [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) support a Gobricks parts option.

### SEMBO
**Part quality**: ★★★★☆ | **Original design**: ★★★★★

SEMBO has some of the strongest original design capability of any domestic brand. Its Chinese-style mecha and street scene lines are popular both at home and abroad.

### Double Eagle
**Part quality**: ★★★★☆ | **Original design**: ★★★★☆

Double Eagle stands out in technic. Its licensed supercar series (Lamborghini, Ferrari) has drawn a lot of positive feedback from MOC builders.

### Quanguan
**Part quality**: ★★★★☆ | **Original design**: ★★★★★

Quanguan concentrates on military-themed MOC. Its Type 99A tank and Dongfeng missile launcher builds are well received among military fans.

## Strengths of Domestic MOC Parts

| Comparison | Domestic parts | LEGO parts |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Price | Roughly 1/3 to 1/5 of LEGO | Higher |
| Precision | Top brands are close to LEGO | Very precise |
| Colour range | Wide, with unique colour codes | Standardised |
| Specialised parts | Some specialised parts are scarce | Full range |
| Where to buy | Taobao / Pinduoduo, convenient | Official stores / overseas shipping |
