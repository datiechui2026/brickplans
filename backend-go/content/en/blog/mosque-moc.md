# Brick Mosque MOC Review: Dome, Twin Minarets and Value

Short answer first: this brick mosque MOC holds up well. Roughly 2,800 parts produce a complete mosque with a large dome and twin minarets, standing about 26 cm tall at the main body and 34 cm at the towers, with the whole package landing near ¥250. If Islamic architecture is your corner of the hobby, it is worth a look.

## 1. The Dome and Overall Silhouette

The centerpiece is the large dome, built from curved bricks stepped inward layer by layer — 12 layers in total to close into a full hemisphere — topped with a crescent finial. The curvature reads at roughly 90% accuracy against the real thing.

The main body uses cream-white brick paired with light green mosaic tiles, and a green decorative band wraps around the exterior walls. The palette is clean and dignified rather than busy. Proportions hold together well: the main hall measures 32 cm wide by 24 cm deep, so it carries real presence on a display shelf.

## 2. Minarets and Detail Work

The two minarets flanking the hall reach 34 cm, each shaft stacked from 2x2 round bricks and finished with a small balcony and a spire. Grooved pieces are inserted at intervals along the shafts to suggest carved ornament.

The main entrance is built as an arch, with doors and windows framed by pointed-arch bricks, and stickers supply the geometric patterns on the walls. Inside, a few rows of small benches stand in for the prayer area. There is no instruction manual for the interior — the empty floor space is left deliberately open so you can furnish it yourself.

## 3. Build Experience and Value

The instruction manual runs 240 steps. Actual build time came in around 9 hours, with the dome and the minaret shafts eating most of it.

Cost breaks down as roughly ¥40 for the instructions and about ¥210 for the parts pack, so around ¥250 all in. That works out to about ¥0.09 per part, which is normal territory for an architecture MOC. Green decorative bricks account for around 15% of the parts list, so check stock on brickplan.cn before you commit. If you already have a deep loose-parts bin, buying only the instructions saves you roughly ¥150.

**Q: How is this different from a standard church MOC?**
