# 2026 Chinese Brick Brand Review: Gobricks, Cada, SEMBO Compared

![Finished model built from a Chinese brick set](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/brick-review-recommendation/brick-review-recommendation-1.jpg)

## Introduction

![Step-by-step assembly photo from the review build](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/brick-review-recommendation/brick-review-recommendation-2.jpg)

The Chinese brick market in 2026 looks nothing like it did a few years ago. A sector once written off as a copycat business now ships original designs, high-tolerance moulds and deep product lines, and it has become a force the global brick scene cannot ignore. Whether you are brand new to the hobby or a veteran MOC builder buying parts by the thousand, this review will help you pick the brand that fits how you build.

## 1. What We Scored

Every brand was judged on five core dimensions:

1. **Part precision**: mould accuracy, clutch power, ejection quality
2. **Colour performance**: colour accuracy, batch-to-batch consistency, transparent part quality
3. **Design**: originality, structural engineering, build experience
4. **Value**: price per part, how complete a set feels
5. **Part range**: breadth of specialised elements, technic coverage

## 2. In-Depth Reviews of the Leading Brands

### Gobricks

**Overall score: 9.2/10**

Gobricks is the benchmark for Chinese bricks in 2026. Its part precision now reaches more than 95% similarity to LEGO, and in a few areas it goes further.

**Pros:**
- Extremely accurate moulds; clutch is smooth and consistent
- More than 3,500 different elements, covering almost every common category
- Transparent parts with very high light transmission, close to optical grade
- Excellent colour consistency: whites do not yellow, blacks do not turn grey

**Cons:**
- Fewer specialised elements such as minifigures
- The lineup is mostly MOC part packs, so finished sets are limited

**Best for:** MOC builders, parts collectors, builders who put quality first

On brickplan.cn, Gobricks is the brand most often recommended in MOC instructions, because its precision guarantees the finished build matches the design.

### Double Eagle (Cada)

**Overall score: 8.8/10**

Double Eagle is the undisputed king of technic parts, with a mechanical ecosystem that is now fully developed.

**Pros:**
- An enormous technic range: beams, pins, gears and motors all covered
- Power components (motors, battery boxes, remote controls) are compatible with the LEGO PF system
- Technic sets are well designed and great fun to play with
- Outstanding value: the same part count costs about one third of the LEGO equivalent

**Cons:**
- Standard bricks occasionally show slight colour deviation
- Minifigure design is average

**Best for:** technic fans, mechanical MOC builders, radio-controlled model builders

### SEMBO

**Overall score: 8.5/10**

SEMBO has deep experience in military and diorama sets, and its design work has improved noticeably in recent years.

**Pros:**
- A large military catalogue, from tanks to warships
- High-quality printed parts: crisp graphics that resist wear
- Complete set design and a smooth build experience
- Attractive packaging that makes sets easy to gift

**Cons:**
- Some specialised parts clutch too tightly
- The basic brick range is narrower than Gobricks

**Best for:** military fans, diorama builders, set collectors

### Mould King

**Overall score: 8.3/10**

Mould King is known for motorised sets and has a distinct edge in powered bricks.

**Pros:**
- Motorised versions are excellent value; sets that include motors are reasonably priced
- Technic parts are compatible with Double Eagle and LEGO
- The app control modules work well
- Part precision is consistent and has improved clearly in recent years

**Cons:**
- Static versions offer few design highlights
- Coverage of specialised parts is limited

**Best for:** motorised MOC builders, RC model builders, technic beginners

## 3. Up-and-Coming Brands

### Qi Zhi
**Overall score: 7.5/10**
Focused on the budget end, with extremely cheap basic bricks that suit bulk purchases for filler. Precision and colour consistency still have room to improve.

### Gudi
**Overall score: 7.8/10**
Strong on girl-oriented themes and creative series, with bold colour choices and playful designs.

### Wange
**Overall score: 7.5/10**
Known for architectural micro-bricks and street scene series. Micro-brick precision is acceptable and suits anyone who loves miniature buildings.

## 5. Which Brand for Which Job

### If you want to build MOCs
**First choice: Gobricks** — the highest precision and the widest part range, ideal for detailed work
**Pair with:** Double Eagle technic parts plus Gobricks standard bricks

### If you want technic machines
**First choice: Double Eagle** — the most complete technic ecosystem and the widest choice of power components
**Pair with:** Double Eagle as the core plus Mould King motors

### If you want military dioramas
**First choice: SEMBO** — the richest military catalogue and the best printed parts
**Pair with:** SEMBO sets plus Gobricks for spare parts

### If you are new to the hobby
**Recommended: a Double Eagle or SEMBO set** — good value and an enjoyable build
**Next step:** find a MOC instruction on brickplan.cn that interests you and buy the parts from its parts list

## 6. Chinese Brick Trends in 2026

### Trend 1: Original design keeps getting stronger
Chinese brands are moving from followers to leaders, and more original IPs and designs are winning market acceptance.

### Trend 2: Part quality keeps climbing
Mould precision at the top brands now approaches or matches international standards, and the overall competitiveness of Chinese brick brands has risen sharply.

### Trend 3: An ecosystem takes shape
Brands are moving from rivalry to collaboration: Gobricks produces parts for several other brands, forming an open parts supply chain.

### Trend 4: MOC culture thrives
With platforms such as brickplan.cn gaining ground, MOC culture is booming and players are creating more than ever.
