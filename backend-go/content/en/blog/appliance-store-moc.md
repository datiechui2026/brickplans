# Brick Appliance Store MOC: Display Stands and Facade Tutorial

Here is the short version: roughly 2,800 parts on a single 32x32 baseplate will give you an appliance store MOC with six product display stands, at a loose-parts cost of about 180-300 CNY and a build time of 5-7 hours. The whole concept rests on three things: lit display stands, appliances with real detail, and a facade with a proper sign. Nail the TV, fridge and washing machine trio and the tech-retail feel carries the entire street.

## Step 1: Plan the Display Layout

Start by drawing zones on the floor. To the left of the entrance, place two TV wall displays, each six studs wide. On the right, put three large-appliance stands holding two fridges and one washing machine. Directly opposite the door, reserve an 8x6 stud area for the checkout counter.

Keep every display stand at a uniform height of three bricks (about 2.4 cm). Use dark gray bricks for the base and black tiles for the countertop, and leave a cable channel in the middle of each unit so you can hide lighting wires later.

## Step 2: The TV, Fridge and Washing Machine Trio

For the TV wall, use a 1x2 transparent black brick as the screen and frame it with 1x1 black bricks. Back it with a 4x3 tile to suggest the chassis, then add a 1x1 transparent light brick on top for the screen glow.

Build the fridge as a 4x6 stud double-door unit: white tiles for the body and silver 1x2 bricks for the handles. The washing machine is a 4x4 stud body with rounded corners, with a 1x1 transparent round brick on the front as the drum window.

Give every appliance two 1x1 black round plates underneath as feet or castors. That single detail makes the models read as real products immediately.

## Step 3: Checkout Counter and Store Flow

Build the counter as an 8x4 stud L-shape. On top, place a 2x3 stud register with a 1x2 transparent brick for its screen, plus a 2x2 stud mini fridge. On the wall behind the counter, hang a 6x4 stud price board, spelling out your sale message with 1x1 white bricks.

Keep the aisles four studs wide. Run 1x2 gray bricks between the display stands as floor guide lines. Position minifigures in front of the stands and the shopping scene assembles itself.

## Step 4: Facade Sign and Finishing Touches

For the storefront sign, use 2x6 dark blue bricks as the backing and spell the store name in 1x1 white bricks (or attach a printed sign piece downloaded from brickplan). Fit 1x2 transparent light bricks on both sides as light boxes.

The entrance uses two 1x4 transparent glass sliding doors, with a row of three 1x1 light bricks above the lintel. Finally, place a 2x3 stud vending machine and a potted plant outside the door, and the corner-shop feel appears at once.

## FAQ

**Q: Where do I find instructions for this appliance store MOC?**
A: Search for the appliance store or home appliance keyword on brickplan.cn and you will find complete instructions in the 2,500-3,200 part range. Every set of instructions includes a parts list, so you can gather the pieces directly from it after downloading.

**Q: Is it difficult? Can someone who has never built an MOC manage it?**
A: The difficulty is moderate. The real techniques are in the transparent parts and the lighting-brick wiring; the wall construction is all basic bricks. Build it once from the instructions, which takes about 5-7 hours, and you will be ready to redesign the layout yourself.

**Q: How much will 2,800 parts cost?**
A: Loose domestic-brand parts run about 180-300 CNY, averaging 0.06-0.1 CNY per piece. If you already own street-scene parts, topping up from the brickplan parts list usually costs only 80-120 CNY.

**Q: How big is the finished model, and will it sit alongside a furniture store MOC?**
A: It measures about 25.6 x 25.6 cm and stands roughly 19 cm tall on a standard 32x32 baseplate. It is fully compatible with eight-stud-wide street models such as a furniture store, supermarket or restaurant, and the overall effect doubles once you line them up into a shopping street.
