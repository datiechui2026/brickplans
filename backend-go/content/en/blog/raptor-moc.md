# Raptor MOC Review: 14 Joints, 1,200 Parts, 45 cm Long

Here's the short version: these raptor MOC instructions are a strong piece of design work. Roughly 1,200 parts produce a 45 cm adult raptor with 14 points of articulation across the jaw, limbs and tail, and the whole thing comes in at around 130 yuan. If you build dinosaurs, it's worth your attention.

## 1. Accuracy to the Reference

The instructions work from the classic film raptor silhouette, and the skull contour, teeth and claws each get dedicated separate pieces rather than brick-built approximations — accuracy lands at roughly 88%. The torso is assembled from curved bricks and slope bricks to get the streamlined body shape, and the dark/light two-tone color scheme gives the scale texture clear layering instead of a flat single-color shell. The lower jaw opens, with a red tongue piece built in, so posed shots of an aggressive stance photograph well.

## 2. Articulation and Build Experience

Ball joints are designed into the neck, all four limbs and the tail, so the finished model can hold running, lunging and biting poses. The manual runs 180 steps; in testing the build took about 4.5 hours. The internal skeleton goes together first, so it pays to follow the numbered order strictly and avoid having to redo sections. The joint at the very end of the tail runs tight — you need to line it up with the slot when rotating it — but the remaining steps are all fairly smooth.

## 3. Value and Display Suggestions

The instructions cost about 20 yuan and the parts pack about 110 yuan, so 130 yuan total, or roughly 0.108 yuan per part — normal for a dinosaur MOC at this scale. The finished model measures 45 cm long and 22 cm tall, which suits either a Jurassic diorama or a standalone display shelf. Around 10% of the parts list is rare color elements, so check stock on brickplan.cn before you order bricks to fill out the list.
