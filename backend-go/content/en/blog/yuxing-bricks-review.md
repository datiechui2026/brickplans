# Mould King Review 2026: Big Part Counts on a Budget

![Finished Mould King set model](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/yuxing-bricks-review/yuxing-bricks-review-1.jpg)

Mould King probably has the most split reputation of any Chinese brick brand. Fans point to **huge part counts at honest prices** and call it unmatched value; critics point to **part quality and instruction design** and say it still falls short. Both camps are basically right — the question of whether Mould King is worth it only makes sense if you break it down category by category.

## Mould King Brand Background

![Mould King building step](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/yuxing-bricks-review/yuxing-bricks-review-2.jpg)

Founded in 2018, Mould King is one of the younger names among Chinese brick brands. It gained traction fast with a "big part count, low price" strategy. The lineup is dominated by **large technic mechanical sets** — race cars, construction vehicles, heavy machinery and aircraft — typically between 2,000 and 5,000 pcs per set, with some flagships past 6,000 pcs.

In the [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) community, Mould King's large MOCs draw steady attention, and its technic flagships are routinely put head-to-head against equivalent sets from other brands in comparison reviews.

## Mould King Part Quality

### Clutch Power

Parts are where the controversy is loudest. Mould King's clutch sits at **the mid range for Chinese bricks**. Basic bricks (2x4, 2x2) grip at a reasonable force, but consistency across specialty and technic parts still has room to improve. In testing we noted:

- **Some technic parts run loose**: gears and cross axles occasionally develop play at the joint, which hurts drivetrain efficiency
- **Long plates can arrive warped**: some long strip plates come out of the box slightly bent, though they usually flatten out after sitting for a while
- **Color consistency**: same-color parts in a large set occasionally show subtle shade differences, but overall within an acceptable range

### Material and Molding

Mould King uses domestic ABS plastic of mid-tier quality. Surface finish is passable, but **light flash** shows up on some parts and needs cleaning up by hand. Against the precision molding of Gobricks, Mould King still has ground to cover.

### Printed Parts and Stickers

Print accuracy is average; artwork is not as crisp as SEMBO or Pantasy. Stickers are mid-grade — adhesion after application is fine, but **color fidelity** on some sheets is off, especially lighter designs applied over dark bricks, where the effect suffers.

## Design Originality

### Technic Mechanical Design

Mould King's real strength is **the design of its large technic sets**. Flagship models show genuine mechanical ambition, particularly in:

- **Gearboxes**: multi-speed transmissions with real complexity and play value
- **Suspension**: independent suspension and shock absorption are laid out sensibly, giving stable driving behavior
- **Drivetrain**: full-time four-wheel drive and differential locks are implemented at a level that shows real design skill

### Exterior Styling

On looks, Mould King runs an "original plus reference" play. Some products carry clear original design work, while others show styling similarities to models from other brands. In terms of **originality**, Mould King sits slightly above the middle of the Chinese brand pack.

## Value for Money

### Parts-to-Price Ratio

**Value is Mould King's headline selling point.** A 4,000 pcs technic flagship typically sells for RMB 500–700, while a LEGO set with a comparable part count usually runs RMB 3,000+, and Double Eagle's equivalents land in the RMB 1,000–1,500 band.

On value, Mould King is one of the **highest parts-per-yuan brands** in the Chinese market. If your goal is the most parts for the least money, few brands compete.

### Hidden Costs

Here's the catch: some large sets **ship without motors or remote-control components**, so going motorized costs extra. Factor that into the total when you compare prices.

## Building Experience

![Mould King part detail](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/yuxing-bricks-review/yuxing-bricks-review-3.jpg)

### Instruction Clarity

Instructions are full color, with clarity **slightly above average**. In testing we found:

- **Steps are chunked too coarsely**: some steps add a large number of parts at once; finer step breaks would help
- **Color discrimination**: some dark parts are hard to tell apart in the manual and need careful checking
- **Thin annotation on key steps**: gear meshing and cross-axle insertion would benefit from more callouts

### Bag Numbering

Large sets use numbered bags packed by step, so finding parts is a decent experience. The ratio of different parts within a bag could still be tuned — occasionally a single step forces you to open several bags at once.

### Build Time

A 4,000 pcs technic set usually takes 8–15 hours depending on experience. If you are disciplined about **sorting and storing parts**, part-finding speeds up noticeably and build time drops with it.

## Who Should Buy Mould King?

### Buy It If...

- **You want big sets on a budget**: 5,000+ pcs without the premium price — Mould King is the value king
- **You're new to technic**: it has the lowest entry cost for trying out technic building
- **You need a MOC parts source**: breaking down a large set yields plenty of technic and basic parts to stock your bin
- **You enjoy a hands-on challenge**: Mould King's large sets are hard builds with real difficulty

### Skip It If...

- **You demand top-tier part quality**: if precision and clutch feel matter most, go with Double Eagle or Pantasy
- **You're a complete beginner**: large Mould King sets are unfriendly to newcomers; start with small or mid-size sets
- **You collect licensed themes**: Mould King has little IP licensing — SEMBO is the better route for specific franchises

## Mould King FAQ

### Is Mould King compatible with LEGO?

**Yes.** Mould King uses standard LEGO-compatible dimensions, so parts mix with other brands.

### How is Mould King after-sales service?

Mid-tier among Chinese brands. Missing-part replacements usually take 3–7 working days, so count your parts as soon as the box arrives.

### What do I need for motorized sets?

Motorized versions normally include motors and a battery box, but the remote is sold separately or handled through a phone app. Check the configuration list on the product page before buying.
