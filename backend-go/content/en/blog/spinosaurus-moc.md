# Spinosaurus MOC Review: A 60 cm Brick Dinosaur Build

Straight to the verdict: this Spinosaurus MOC is aimed at advanced builders. Around 2,600 pieces produce a 60 cm-long Spinosaurus, with the signature sail and a posable lower jaw as the headline features. The full package runs about CNY 300, which makes it worth considering if you want a display-centerpiece dinosaur.

## Size and accuracy

Spinosaurus is one of the largest carnivorous dinosaurs known, and this design is built at 1:35 scale. The finished model measures 60 cm long and 28 cm tall, with accuracy to the reference sitting at roughly 85%.

The sail is the part everyone looks at first, and it is handled with multiple curved plates staggered into a wavy silhouette rather than a flat slab. The bipedal stance holds up well and does not feel like it is about to tip. Up front, the long snout, the teeth and the claws are all built as separate, finely detailed sub-assemblies, which is where a lot of the piece count goes. The overall presence is imposing — this is not a model that disappears on a shelf.

## Build difficulty and experience

The instruction booklet runs 320 steps. Measured build time came out at around 9 hours, which puts this firmly in the mid-to-large project bracket rather than a weekend build.

The sail is the structural challenge. It needs to be installed row by row, working in color zones so the staggered pattern stays consistent, and it is worth checking that every row's clips are properly seated before starting the next one. The limb joints are reinforced with extended technic pins, so the model stands without wobbling. The trade-off is that you should support the torso when lifting or moving it, otherwise the joints can pull apart.

## Cost and display

Instructions cost around CNY 35 and the parts pack around CNY 265, for a total near CNY 300 — roughly CNY 0.115 per piece. About 15% of the parts are rare colors such as dark brown and sand, so it is worth checking the parts list on brickplan.cn before you order anything.

For display, the model pairs naturally with a river or swamp scene. A standard display cabinet needs at least 60 cm of clear length, so measure before you commit shelf space.

## FAQ

**Q: Where can I buy the Spinosaurus MOC instructions?**
A: They are available on the brickplan.cn platform — just search for "Spinosaurus". The PDF costs about CNY 35 and comes with a complete parts list and the 320-step instructions. Purchase gives you a permanent download plus online preview.

**Q: Is the Spinosaurus MOC hard to build?**
A: On the harder side. The 320-step booklet takes about 9 hours, with the sail and the head as the tricky sections, so prior experience with large sets helps. Beginners can split the work into four sub-builds and do one a day to keep fatigue down.

**Q: Is the sail structure sturdy?**
A: Yes. The sail is built from interlocking curved plates, and each row clips together tightly, so it will not loosen under normal display conditions. Avoid flexing it repeatedly, and if the model will be handled often, gluing the sail seams is a sensible precaution.

**Q: What does the Spinosaurus MOC cost in total?**
A: About CNY 35 for the instructions plus roughly CNY 265 for parts, so around CNY 300 all in. If you already have a deep loose-parts bin, buying only the instructions and sourcing bricks yourself can save close to half — though the rare color parts will cost a little more when bought individually.

**Q: Spinosaurus MOC or Velociraptor MOC — which should I get?**
A: It depends on what you want. The Spinosaurus is big at 60 cm and has real presence, but it takes about 9 hours to build. The Velociraptor is around 1,200 pieces and can be finished in about 4.5 hours, so if your budget or patience is limited, the Velociraptor is the friendlier choice.
