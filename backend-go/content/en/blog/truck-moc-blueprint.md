# Brick Truck MOC Blueprints: From Haulers to Off-Road Beasts

Trucks are one of the most popular subjects in brick vehicle MOCs. From American long-nose conventionals to European cab-overs, from off-road monsters to heavy construction machines, the design language of the truck is broad enough to suit almost any builder. This guide reviews the **brick truck MOC blueprints** available today so you can pick the project that fits your parts bin and your skill level.

## Why Truck MOCs Are Worth Building

What makes a brick truck special is its mechanical density. Steering, suspension, driveshafts, engine detail — these are the elements that turn a truck MOC from a static display model into a working mechanical object. A good **brick truck MOC blueprint** strikes a balance between exterior accuracy and internals that actually function when you push it across the floor.

## Truck MOC Mechanisms Explained

### Steering

Truck MOCs usually use a trapezoid steering linkage, with rack-and-pinion gears or connecting rods turning the front wheels. More advanced blueprints add power-steering simulation, driving the steering mechanism with a small motor.

### Suspension

- **Leaf spring suspension:** suited to heavy trucks; simple structure with strong load-bearing capacity
- **Independent suspension:** suited to off-road trucks; each wheel moves separately for better ground contact
- **Air suspension:** suited to premium transport trucks; chassis height can be adjusted via air pressure

### Drivetrain

Drivetrain options run from simple rear-wheel drive to full-time four-wheel drive. Beginners should start with rear-wheel drive and pick up differentials, driveshafts and gearboxes step by step from there.

## Buying the Right Parts

A truck MOC needs a few specialist parts in stock:

1. **Wheels and tires:** truck MOCs generally call for large tires; keep both 43mm and 56mm diameters on hand
2. **Gears and driveshafts:** for building the steering and drivetrain systems
3. **Panels:** for bodywork and a smooth, streamlined exterior
4. **Transparent parts:** for windows and lights
