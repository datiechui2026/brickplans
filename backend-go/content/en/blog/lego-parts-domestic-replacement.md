# LEGO Parts Replacements: Choosing Compatible Chinese Bricks

Short answer first: replacing LEGO parts with Chinese-made equivalents works. Mainstream domestic parts share more than 95% compatibility with LEGO's standard clutch specification, a single ordinary brick costs roughly one-third of the LEGO price, and MOC builders who buy by part number from the brickplan parts market can shave a serious amount off their budget.

## Why Chinese parts can stand in for LEGO

LEGO and the vast majority of Chinese brick brands follow the same 8mm x 8mm stud standard. Spacing, height and clutch power have converged over years of iteration to the point where the systems are largely interchangeable. Among domestic makers, Gobricks, Double Eagle and SEMBO all run moulds with precision close to LEGO's, and ordinary bricks, plates and slopes swap in and out cleanly. The risk areas are narrower: printed parts, transparent parts and specialty pieces can show colour deviations.

## Match replacements precisely by part number

When you substitute, always buy by part number rather than by looks alone. The brickplan parts market supports number-based search, so common elements are easy to source: 3001 (2x4 brick), 3020 (2x4 plate), 4216 (slope) and similar workhorse parts are all readily available. For specialty pieces, check the part library first to confirm dimensions and colour, so a wrong purchase doesn't stall your build schedule.

## How to choose between brands

Gobricks has the widest catalogue, with more than 4,000 part types and moderate, well-balanced clutch power, which makes it the first choice for MOC work. Double Eagle's technic elements (liftarms, pins) are high precision and suit replacements in LEGO Technic builds. SEMBO's strength is a rich supply of set-derived parts at good value, with average per-piece prices around 0.1 to 0.3 yuan, which makes it economical for filler and internal structure.

## What to watch out for

For load-bearing structural parts, stick to large manufacturers such as Gobricks so nothing works loose. Keep original parts for transparent and printed elements wherever possible, since domestic versions tend to show colour shifts. Verify colours against LEGO's official colour codes: common tones like white and light grey match well in domestic parts, while rarer colours may differ slightly, so do a small test assembly before committing.

## FAQ

**Q: Are Chinese LEGO part replacements fully compatible?**
A: Common elements such as bricks, plates and slopes reach over 95% compatibility with a firm, tight clutch. Printed parts, transparent parts and specialty structural pieces may show colour differences or tolerance variation, so keeping LEGO originals for those is recommended.

**Q: Are Chinese replacements expensive?**
A: No. Ordinary domestic parts run about 0.05 to 0.3 yuan per piece, a third of the cost of LEGO loose parts or even lower. Buying by part number from the brickplan parts market makes a real difference on large MOC projects.

**Q: Which parts suit replacement, and which don't?**
A: Structural elements, standard bricks and common colours are all good candidates. Transparent parts, printed parts and high-precision technic pieces are better kept original to avoid poor light transmission, colour mismatch or tolerance issues that make assembly fight you.

**Q: Will replacement parts feel different in clutch power from LEGO?**
A: Parts from major makers like Gobricks and Double Eagle come close to LEGO, with a moderate, comfortable grip. Unbranded parts can run loose or overly tight, so check buyer reviews on brickplan before substituting and favour brands with a solid reputation.
