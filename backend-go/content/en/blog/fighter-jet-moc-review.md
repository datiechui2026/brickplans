# Brick Fighter Jet MOC Review: From P-51 Mustang to F-22 Raptor

Fighter jets are one of the most demanding and most visually rewarding subjects in brick building. From Second World War propeller fighters to modern supersonic stealth aircraft, every airframe carries its own aerodynamic shape and design logic. This review works through a range of **brick fighter jet MOC plans** from different eras so you can see what each one demands of a builder.

## What Makes a Fighter Jet MOC Worth Building

The appeal sits in the combination of a clean, streamlined silhouette and a dense mechanical interior. A good fighter MOC has to deliver all of the following in a very small volume:

- A convincing aerodynamic shape, with correct proportions and angles for wings, fuselage and tail
- Working control surfaces such as flaps, ailerons and rudders
- Cockpit interior detail, including instrument panel and ejection seat
- Reproduced weapon systems such as missiles, bombs and cannon

## Building Techniques for Fighter Jet MOCs

### Technique 1: Building the Fuselage

The fuselage is the core of the build. Three approaches work well:

1. **Skeleton method:** build the internal frame first, then skin it with outer panels
2. **Module method:** build the forward, centre and rear fuselage as separate sections, then join them
3. **Stacking method:** layer from the inside out, which suits small-scale fighters

### Technique 2: Building the Wings

Wings are the most challenging part of a fighter MOC. Keep in mind:

- Wing thickness should taper gradually from root to tip
- Leading and trailing edge curves can be achieved with slope bricks and curved bricks
- Wingtip shapes need special handling

## Parts to Stock Up On

Building fighter jet MOCs requires a supply of these specialist parts:

1. **Curved panels:** for streamlined skinning of fuselage and wings
2. **Slope bricks:** for the tapered transitions at nose and tail
3. **Transparent parts:** for canopies and lights
4. **Ball joints:** for vector nozzles and movable control surfaces
5. **Small-scale parts:** for missiles, sensors and other fine detail
