# LEGO Marvel Series Review: Minifigures, Vehicles and Mechs

The short version: the Marvel line lives or dies on its exclusive minifigure prints, and the vehicles and buildable mechs are where your money works hardest. A hundred-yuan set is a low-risk taste test; a thousand-yuan set is a collector's purchase.

Since the first 761xx sets arrived in 2012, LEGO Marvel has tracked the Marvel Cinematic Universe for over a decade, and the numbering has since shifted from 761xx to 762xx. In this review we focus on the three threads that define the theme: superhero minifigures, vehicles and buildable mechs, and ask how well the line actually holds up.

## 761xx vs 762xx: Two Eras of Set Design

The 761xx period (2018-2021) leaned hard on movie tie-ins, with sets built around Avengers: Infinity War and Endgame scenes and a general "vehicle plus minifigures" formula. With the move into 762xx, LEGO clearly raised design density, adding more play features and articulated figure forms. Neither numbering system is better than the other. The early 76191 Infinity Gauntlet (590 pieces) recreates Thanos' signature moment with gold bricks and posable fingers and remains a classic Marvel display piece; 76215 Hulkbuster answers with a large-scale articulated model that shows off what the newer design generation can do.

## Iron Man and Spider-Man Vehicles: Play Value Beyond the Minifigs

The vehicle line is the most dependable source of play in the theme. 76216 Iron Man Armory continues the storable-armor concept, and a row of armor bays is a display case in its own right; the Spider-Man side regularly ships Spider-bots and street-chase vehicles at the mid and lower price points, with plenty of mechanisms, quick builds and the durability to be played with over and over. What both strands share is exclusive minifigure printing, such as the battle-damaged MK85 or the Venom symbiote version, so minifigure collectors can find treasure inside vehicle sets too.

## Buildable Mechs and How the Price Tiers Break Down

Buildable mechs are the theme's most successful recent innovation. 76226 Spider-Man Mech and 76206 Iron Man Mech turn two or three hundred pieces into a large articulated figure with poseable joints and an opening chest plate, and a minifigure can sit in the cockpit. They display well and play well. Pricing across the Marvel line is unusually clear: the hundred-yuan vehicles and mechs are ideal for trying the theme, while the thousand-yuan large sets are for collecting. You can combine tiers to fit your budget rather than forcing yourself to chase the flagship.

## Verdict and Buying Advice

The conclusion of this LEGO Marvel review: minifigure printing and exclusive decorations are the headline selling point, while vehicles and buildable mechs are the best-value way in. The theme suits Marvel fans and kids who like to build and then role-play with what they built. ## FAQ

**Q: Is there a difference between LEGO Marvel's 761xx and 762xx sets?**
A: Neither is superior; they are two design languages. The 761xx era focused on movie tie-ins and a vehicle-plus-minifigures formula, while the 762xx era raised design density with more play mechanisms and articulated figure forms.

**Q: What is the most worthwhile thing to buy in the Marvel line?**
A: Minifigure printing and exclusive decorations are the biggest draw, and vehicles and buildable mechs are the best-value entry point. The hundred-yuan vehicles and mechs are good for trying the theme; the thousand-yuan large sets are for collecting.

**Q: Are the buildable mechs worth picking up?**
A: Yes. 76226 Spider-Man Mech and 76206 Iron Man Mech turn two or three hundred pieces into a large articulated figure with poseable joints, an opening chest plate and a cockpit a minifigure can sit in, so they display and play well.

**Q: Which vehicle line holds up best to repeated play?**
A: The vehicle line is the most reliable for play. 76216 Iron Man Armory stores armor, and a row of armor bays works as a display case on its own; the Spider-Man line regularly delivers Spider-bots and street-chase vehicles with plenty of mechanisms and fast builds.
