# Ice Cream Shop MOC Guide: Cones, Freezer Case and Summer Color

Mention summer and most builders picture the same thing: an **ice cream shop MOC** with bright cones, a fogged-up freezer case and a queue of minifigures. It is a small footprint, high-charm scene, and one of the best practice projects for beginners who want to work on rounded shapes and cool-toned color schemes. Start with a single cone, then build the shop around it.

## 1. Cone shapes: learn to roll an ice cream first

The cone is the shop's signature product, and it deserves its own practice session. Build the **wafer cone** from small tan and beige pieces stacked on a diagonal to form a taper, offsetting each layer slightly so the surface reads as a spiral. For the **scoops**, stack light-colored dome pieces or curved slope pieces: strawberry pink, matcha green and vanilla white piled together gives you the classic three-scoop stack. Add a small red cherry piece on top and the cone is done.

There is one key trick: **scoops first, cone last**. Stack the scoops until they are stable, then flip the whole thing over and attach the cone, which stops the model from going top-heavy and collapsing. Display several cones in different colorways in the window and on the counter. Build the tall one from long bar pieces as a "giant" size, and keep a short one as the kids' portion. Mixing heights is what makes the display interesting. Once the cones look right, the whole scene's icy feel is established.

## 2. The freezer case and sales area: where the chill comes from

The cool atmosphere in an **ice cream shop MOC** comes mostly from the freezer case. Build the sales counter on a square base of **white plates**, then use transparent window pieces across the front to make the glass case. Inside, line up rows of popsicles and boxed ice cream, using small blue, white and green pieces to suggest different flavors, arranged as neatly as a convenience store freezer. Put a retro cash register on top of the counter and hang a small blackboard sign reading "house special."

Install **cool white light pieces** inside the counter so white light spills out through the glass — that is the detail that sells the cold. Add an anti-slip floor mat in front of the counter, pose customer minifigures holding cones in a queue, and stand a staff minifigure behind the counter holding one out. The sales flow comes alive instantly. Do not forget a cone warmer in the corner of the counter; stacked cylindrical pieces are all it takes. The closer the details get to a real shop, the longer people look.

## 3. Signage and outdoor seating: write "summer" on the doorstep

The shop sign is the store's second face. Use a **pink, blue and yellow clash** for the sign, spell the shop name with large letter pieces and run a ring of neon light pieces around the edge. Stand a giant cone-shaped 3D sign at the entrance, taller than the second floor, so it reads from far away. Add an awning over the porch built from red-and-white striped pieces and you immediately get the seaside shop look.

The outdoor seating area is what makes the summer mood: a few small round tables with colorful tall stools and an orange parasol. Put a small fan and a couple of beach balls beside the tables, pose a child minifigure licking an ice cream while a parent sits nearby checking a phone, and you have a living summer street corner. Use **high-saturation colors** for chairs and umbrella so they contrast cleanly with the white and pink walls — the brighter, the more summery.

## 4. Finishing details and build order

Finish by cooling the whole shop down with details: lay the floor in alternating light blue and white bricks to suggest chilled tile, hang a string of colored lights under the eaves, and put a standing freezer plus a stack of spare paper cups in the corner. At night, light the colored string and the cool white freezer glow, then add one warm yellow street lamp. The two color temperatures crossing each other maxes out the summer evening atmosphere.

For build order, **do the cones first, then the freezer case, then the signage and seating**. Stock up on tan cone pieces, pink, green and white dome pieces, transparent window pieces and cool white light pieces.
