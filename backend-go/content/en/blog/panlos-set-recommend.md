# PANLOS Sets Worth Buying: Military, Machinery & Street Scenes

Bottom line first: PANLOS builds three hard-edged lines — military, machinery and architecture — and the sweet spot for value sits in the RMB 100–300 band. The parts are fully compatible with LEGO.

PANLOS is a Chinese brand that skips film and TV licences entirely and lets subject accuracy and moulding quality do the talking. If you are researching which PANLOS sets to buy, you will find the range clusters between RMB 100 and 600, with piece counts close to LEGO sets on the same subject while the price lands at roughly a third. This guide works through the three main lines — military, machinery and architecture — and picks out the sets worth your money by price and piece count.

## Brand positioning and reputation

The two words players keep coming back to with PANLOS are "hardcore subjects" and "high value". Clutch is in the top tier of domestic brands, at around 85 percent of LEGO's level. Dark-colored military parts come out with rich, saturated color and a properly matte surface finish. Printed elements are accurate, and the instructions are bagged in build order with clear steps — a mid-sized set is usually a one-evening build. The acknowledged weak points: transparent parts occasionally arrive with scratches, and the seam lines on a few specialised moulds are more visible than they should be. PANLOS recommendations usually lead with the military vehicles and the engineering machines; the street scene line is home base for builders who care most about atmosphere.

## Military vehicles: the standouts

Military is PANLOS's flagship, covering both modern hardware and retro heavy armour, and the movable structures are the main selling point:

- **M1A2 main battle tank**: around 1,200 pieces, RMB 200–260. The turret rotates a full 360°, the tracks roll when you push it, and there is an ammunition rack interior. The benchmark entry point into the military line.
- **J-16 fighter**: around 900 pieces, RMB 150–180. Variable-sweep wing structure with well-detailed weapons bay.
- **Type 055 destroyer**: a large set at over 2,000 pieces, RMB 450–550. Deck aircraft and vertical launch cells are all present, and the finished model has serious presence.

The military line leans heavily on dark-colored parts and runs tight on clutch, with plenty of long plates — the finished builds feel solid and survive handling without shedding pieces. This is the strongest value tier in any PANLOS recommendation.

## Engineering machines

Engineering vehicles are PANLOS's high-difficulty route, with hydraulic rods, gear transmissions and poseable arms throughout:

- **Hydraulic excavator**: around 1,500 pieces, RMB 300–350. The main arm is poseable in several sections, and the bucket raises and lowers through a gear linkage — very mechanical.
- **All-terrain crane**: around 2,000 pieces, RMB 400–500. Telescoping boom plus counterweight blocks, on a stable rotating base. A display-shelf favourite.
- **Wheel loader**: an entry-level set of around 800 pieces, available in the hundred-yuan bracket and a good first taste of mechanical building.

These sets are heavy on functional parts. When building, just watch the meshing direction of the gears — the instructions mark it — and note that builders often convert them into original MOC vehicles.

## Street scenes

PANLOS street models follow a modular approach, with a standard baseplate footprint so neighbouring sets connect seamlessly:

- **Café / bookstore / florist**: each around 1,500–2,500 pieces, RMB 150–300. Facade signage, shop windows and awnings are detailed, and the interiors are not skimped. One set displays fine on its own; several make a street.
- **Corner building**: around 2,000 pieces, RMB 250–320. Its right-angle layout makes it a natural anchor for the corner of a block.

The street line uses a warm palette — cream, brick red and mint green all photograph well — and suits builders who want to assemble a whole street one building at a time.

## Buying advice by price band

- **Under RMB 100**: wheel loader, small military vehicles — good for first-time buyers.
- **RMB 100–300**: main battle tank, fighter, single street building — the main value band and the core of any PANLOS recommendation.
- **Above RMB 300**: crane, destroyer and other large sets — best for advanced builders and display-focused collectors.

Whichever line you pick, PANLOS parts are fully LEGO-compatible, so there is plenty of room to break a set down and rebuild it as a MOC.
