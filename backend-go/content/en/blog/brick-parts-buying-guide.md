# How to Buy Brick Parts: Loose Bricks, Replacements & Bulk

Short answer: there are three routes for buying brick parts. Missing pieces from a set go through brand customer service or the BrickPlan replacement section; specific loose parts are ordered by part number from Gobricks and Taobao loose-part shops; and when you need volume, bulk mixed lots sold by weight on secondhand marketplaces win on price. Loose parts usually cost 2 to 3 times as much per piece as bulk lots, so building a parts list before you order can cut your budget roughly in half.

## 1. Step One: Build a Parts List Before You Order Anything

Find the matching instructions on BrickPlan first, export the parts list, then organize it into a table by part number, color and quantity. An 800-piece MOC typically involves 60 to 120 different part types. List common parts and rare parts separately, and source the rare ones from whichever channel actually has stock — one shop being out of stock can stall the whole build.

## 2. Step Two: Replacements Go Through Official Channels, Free and Simple

If a set is missing pieces, contact the brand's customer service first. Chinese manufacturers such as SEMBO and Double Eagle generally replace missing parts in genuine sets for free; submit the part numbers and your address and the parts arrive in 3 to 7 days. If a loose part is missing, use the BrickPlan replacement section, which supports searching by part number across domestic parts including Gobricks. Individual pieces average 0.05 to 0.3 yuan, and orders over 20 yuan ship free, making it the entry point MOC builders use most.

## 3. Step Three: Buy Specific Loose Parts by Number

When you need a particular part number, the official Gobricks store carries more than 4,000 categories with a full color range and consistent tolerances. Taobao loose-part shops price by weight instead: 500 grams runs about 30 to 60 yuan and holds 800 to 1,200 small pieces, which suits topping up basic bricks. Before ordering, double-check the number of digits in the part number (usually 4 to 6) and the color code — return shipping for the wrong color can cost more than the parts themselves.

## 4. Step Four: Bulk Lots and Secondhand Sources Reward Volume

For large MOCs or filling out a street scene, bulk mixed lots sold by weight and broken-down lots on secondhand marketplaces are the best value. Cost per piece can drop to 0.02 to 0.05 yuan, saving more than 60 percent versus buying loose parts by number. Breaking down used sets is another good route: a used 1,000-piece domestic set costs 80 to 150 yuan, which works out under 0.15 yuan per piece on average, with rare parts thrown in.

## 5. Step Five: Three Ways to Save Money

First, stock up on common basic colors (black, white, gray) in bulk lots during sales. Second, push loose-part orders over the discount and free-shipping thresholds — don't pay 10 yuan shipping for a handful of parts. Third, compare unit prices across channels on BrickPlan before ordering; the same part number can differ by up to 0.1 yuan per piece between shops, which is 10 yuan across 100 pieces.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the cheapest way to buy brick parts?**

A: Bulk secondhand lots are cheapest, with per-piece costs down to a few fen. Buying loose parts by number is the most precise but costs 2 to 3 times more per piece. Stock common parts in bulk, buy rare parts loose, and build your list on BrickPlan before comparing prices.

**Q: Do I have to pay for missing-piece replacements?**

A: Missing parts in a genuine brand set are usually replaced free through official customer service, arriving in 3 to 7 days. Loose-part channels charge per piece, roughly 0.05 to 0.3 yuan. The BrickPlan replacement section supports searching by part number for domestic parts including Gobricks, with free shipping over 20 yuan.

**Q: Are loose parts sold by weight a good deal?**

A: Weight-based lots suit large quantities of basic parts: 500 grams for 30 to 60 yuan holds 800 to 1,200 small pieces, saving more than 30 percent versus buying per piece. Colors are random, though, so they work as MOC stockpile material rather than for hitting a specific color scheme.

**Q: What if I don't know the part number?**

A: The parts list in the instructions shows 4 to 6-digit numbers. If the instructions are lost, look up the set's plans on BrickPlan for the list, or reverse-search the number by shape on BrickLink. Confirm the number before ordering so you don't get the wrong size or color.
