# Marvel LEGO MOC Plans: Iron Man, Spider-Man & Avengers

The Marvel Cinematic Universe is the most successful film franchise in the world, and recreating its heroes in brick form is a long-standing goal for a huge number of fans. ## Iron Man MOC Plans

Iron Man is one of the most popular MOC subjects in the whole Marvel universe. Tony Stark's Mark suits run from Mark 1 to Mark 85, and every generation has its own design language.

### Popular Iron Man MOC Plans

| Plan | Parts | Difficulty | Height | Articulation |
|---------|-------|------|------|-------|
| Mark 3 classic red and gold | 280 | ★★☆☆☆ | 14 cm | 11 points of articulation |
| Mark 42 assembly armor | 450 | ★★★☆☆ | 18 cm | Wearable design |
| Hulkbuster | 1200 | ★★★★☆ | 25 cm | Articulated arms, hinged helmet |
| Mark 85 nano armor | 320 | ★★☆☆☆ | 15 cm | 14 points of articulation |

**Recommended starting point:** the Mark 3 classic red and gold suit is the best entry into Iron Man MOCs. The part count is moderate, the red-and-gold color scheme is the iconic one, and 11 points of articulation across the body let you pose it in a wide range of combat stances. This **Marvel LEGO plan** has detailed step-by-step instructions on the BrickPlans platform, which makes it very beginner-friendly.

### Iron Man MOC Building Tips

- **Color scheme:** for the classic Iron Man red and gold, use LEGO color code #021 (Bright Red) for the red and #297 (Warm Gold) for the gold
- **Joints:** ball joint pieces give the shoulders and hips multi-angle articulation
- **Arc reactor:** a 1x1 trans-light blue round brick recreates the look of the chest reactor
- **Hand weapons:** combine a 1x1 cylinder brick with a 1x1 tile to build the repulsor effect in the palm

## Large-Scale Avengers Scene MOCs

If you prefer big projects, Avengers scene MOCs are the obvious choice.

### Avengers HQ Tower

This is the most iconic large building in Marvel MOC building. A complete Avengers tower plan includes:

- 6 removable floors
- A separate interior scene on every floor
- A rooftop landing pad with a takeoff and landing platform for the Quinjet
- An underground garage area
- Transparent pieces for the exterior glass curtain wall

**Building advice:** the full tower runs about 3500 parts, so build it module by module and check your work each time you finish one. If large architectural MOCs are your thing, the castle techniques in our [Zelda replica plans](/en/blog/zelda-replica-plans) are also worth a look.

### Wakanda Scene

Black Panther's homeland Wakanda is the most visually striking Marvel location. MOC plans for it usually include:

- The throne room (the core structure)
- The tech lab (Shuri's workspace)
- The vibranium mine
- The shield generator
