# SEMBO MOC Instructions Review: Licensed Kits Tested

![Finished SEMBO MOC model](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/senbao-moc-review/senbao-moc-review-1.jpg)

SEMBO sits in the top tier of Chinese brick brands for **licensing depth and category spread** — military, space, street scenes and film/TV are all covered. To find out whether the instructions are actually worth your time, we cracked open several sets and built them through. Below is the breakdown by product line, difficulty, parts, build experience and finished model.

## What these instructions actually are

![SEMBO MOC building steps](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/senbao-moc-review/senbao-moc-review-2.jpg)

SEMBO's MOC instruction line is very broad, and part counts vary enormously — anywhere from **300 to 4,000 pcs**. The standout feature is the **licensed IP roster**, which includes official rights to China's space programme and several well-known film and TV properties. That gives SEMBO a natural advantage when it comes to how faithfully a subject is reproduced: liveries, markings and structural shapes come straight from the source material rather than being approximated. In the [brickplan](https://brickplan.cn) community, SEMBO-style MOC submissions turn up constantly, with military and space subjects especially popular among builders.

## Build difficulty

**Difficulty rating: ★★★★☆ (medium to hard)**

Difficulty depends heavily on the subject rather than the brand. Street-scene builds are moderate, with clear steps, and suit intermediate builders well. Technic-style sets and large military vehicles ask considerably more of your patience and spatial imagination. Turret assemblies and track systems on some of the military MOCs are genuinely fiddly to install and reward careful sequencing. Beginners are better off starting with a SEMBO street scene, building up some familiarity with how the brand's parts behave, and then moving on to the large-scale subjects.

## Parts review

SEMBO's part quality lands at the **upper-middle level** among Chinese brick brands:

- **Brick feel**: Clutch is moderate — neither loose nor overly tight — and the insertion feel is close to roughly 80% of LEGO's level. Basic bricks and plates are especially consistent from bag to bag.
- **Printed parts**: This is a SEMBO strength. Thanks to the licences, print precision is high: sharp graphics, vivid colour and good wear resistance, with very little off-register printing or fading over time.
- **Transparent parts**: Light transmission is good, but scratch control is not on the level of Gobricks. Inspect them as soon as you open the bags, and contact customer service to replace any transparent parts that arrive scratched.
- **Special parts**: Minifigures are finely made, with detailed face printing and body decoration — a real plus for collectors.

## Building experience

![SEMBO MOC part detail](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/senbao-moc-review/senbao-moc-review-3.jpg)

SEMBO manuals are **printed in full colour** and the step diagrams are reasonably sharp, though some of the long builds (3,000+ pcs) could break their steps down more finely than they do. One welcome improvement: recent manuals include a **true-scale part reference image**, so you can confirm a part's size at a glance while hunting through the bags — a genuinely thoughtful detail. Build time runs from about 2 hours up to 10 hours depending on the scale of the model.

Bag numbering is average. Large sets are split reasonably finely, but some smaller sets (under 500 pcs) are not split at all, so finding parts takes a bit of patience.

## Finished model

The finished result is where SEMBO tends to shine, across three areas:

- **Visual accuracy**: Licensed subjects are reproduced in detail, whether that is a spacecraft livery or the architectural style of a street scene.
- **Structural stability**: The overall structure holds up well, though some very large builds can sag slightly after long-term display — reinforce the key load-bearing points yourself if the model will stay on the shelf.
- **Playability**: Street scenes are packed with interior detail and photograph well as dioramas; military subjects have sensibly designed moving features, with working turret rotation and opening hatches.

**Summary**: SEMBO's MOC instructions are a first choice for licence fans and for military and space builders. Part quality has not yet reached the top tier, but the range of subjects and competitive pricing make SEMBO one of the strongest names among Chinese brick brands.
