# Gudi Brick Sets: 2025 Guide to the Best Series

Gudi has become a notable name among Chinese brick brands, thanks to strong value, a wide range of IP licenses and steadily improving brick quality. If you are new to the brand or looking for your next build, this guide covers the series worth considering and who each one suits.

## Why Choose Gudi Brick Sets?

Gudi stands out in the domestic Chinese brick market for clear strengths:

- **Solid brick quality**: ABS plastic parts with high precision, moderate clutch power and smooth building.
- **Approachable pricing**: for the same piece count, sets cost only one third of international brands or even less.
- **Original designs and licensed IP**: plenty of original designs, plus several popular IP licenses.

If you are looking for high-value brick sets, this Gudi list is a useful place to start.

## Gudi Recommendations: The Must-Have Series

### Military Series: A Strong Pick for Hardcore Builders

The Gudi military series is one of its most popular lines, with tanks, fighter jets and warships that capture detail well.

**Recommended model: Gudi Military Tank Series** — the turret rotates, the tracks roll and the hatches open. The build takes about 2-3 hours, offers moderate difficulty and has excellent display presence.

**Recommended model: Gudi Fighter Jet Series** — from the F-22 to the J-20, these modern aircraft have smooth lines and detailed cockpits, and each comes with a display stand.

### Street Series: A Favorite for City Builders

The Gudi street series is known for modular design. Each building can be displayed on its own or combined freely into a complete block.

**Recommended model: Gudi Street Coffee Shop** — a three-story building with a coffee shop, a bakery workshop and a rooftop terrace. The clear window glass and printed parts have an excellent feel, and the scene has a strong everyday atmosphere. **Why Gudi recommends it**: it is a top entry point for street scenes and works well as a build to complete with children.

**Recommended model: Gudi Flower Shop and Bookstore** — the two buildings can be placed side by side. The flower shop has a flower stand and awning, while the bookstore has bookshelves and a reading area. The details are worth lingering over.

### Mecha Series: Fun for Sci-Fi Fans

**Recommended model: Gudi Mecha Warrior** — multi-segment articulated joints in the arms, legs and waist allow many combat poses, and interchangeable weapon accessories add plenty of play value. The finished model is about 25 cm tall, making a bold desktop display.

### Space Series: For Dreams of Exploring the Universe

**Recommended model: Gudi Launch Vehicle** — it closely recreates a real rocket and includes a launch tower and transport vehicle. The rocket can be taken apart in sections to show its internal structure, combining education and play.

## Gudi Recommendations: Buying Advice by Builder Type

### For New Builders

Start with a medium set of 500-800 pieces. A small military tank or a single street building are good choices: the difficulty is moderate, and you can fully enjoy the build.

### For Families Building Together

The best Gudi picks for parent-child building are the street architecture and animal series. The scenes are warm and the colors are rich, so parents and children can work together and finish with a real sense of achievement.

### For Experienced Builders

MOC builders looking for a challenge can choose the large mecha series and thousand-piece-class street sets. The wide variety of parts helps you build a stock of special pieces for future MOC work.

## Where to Buy Gudi Brick Sets

1. **E-commerce platforms**: official flagship stores are available on Taobao, JD and Pinduoduo. Watch major sales events to buy at a lower price.
2. **Secondhand platforms**: player resales on Xianyu are useful for finding out-of-print sets.
3. **Physical stores**: some toy chains and brick specialty stores stock the brand.

Gudi has earned its place in the domestic brick market with strong product performance, friendly prices and a broad product line. Whether you are a new builder or an experienced one, this Gudi guide should help you find the set that fits you best. May your next build bring you focus and joy.
