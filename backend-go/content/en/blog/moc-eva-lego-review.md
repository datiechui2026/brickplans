# EVA LEGO MOC Review: Unit-01 and Unit-02 Build Plans

![Completed EVA mecha brick model](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-eva-lego-review/moc-eva-lego-review-1.jpg)

Neon Genesis Evangelion needs no introduction, and its mecha designs still look futuristic today — which is exactly why they remain one of the most popular subjects in mecha MOC building. The catch is that EVA units are organic, elongated and top-heavy, the opposite of what brick geometry naturally wants to do. This EVA LEGO recommendation round-up puts the most popular plans currently in circulation side by side and reviews what each one actually demands from the builder.

## 1. Popular EVA MOC Plans

![EVA mecha brick build step photo](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-eva-lego-review/moc-eva-lego-review-2.jpg)

### 1. EVA Unit-01 (EVA-01)
- **Plan name**: EVA Unit-01 1:35 scale posable model
- **Part count**: about 2,200
- **Difficulty**: High
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

Unit-01's signature purple and green colour scheme comes through cleanly in brick. The highlight of this plan is the shoulder pylons: they are built as a detachable structure, which lets you switch between the standard configuration and Berserk mode without tearing the torso apart.

### 2. EVA Unit-02 (EVA-02)
- **Plan name**: EVA Unit-02 1:35 scale
- **Part count**: about 2,000
- **Difficulty**: High
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

Unit-02's red paintwork is extremely striking in brick. The high-speed mobility units in the legs are driven by a gear mechanism, so pushing and pulling them reproduces a running motion.

### 3. Unit-01 Berserk Mode
- **Plan name**: EVA-01 Berserk Mode
- **Part count**: about 1,800
- **Difficulty**: Medium-high
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆

This version of Unit-01 adds red eye effect parts and damaged armour, and the visual impact is very strong.

## 2. Key Building Points

### 1. Body Proportions

The slender proportions of an EVA unit are the real difficulty. Every section has to be scaled precisely against the others so the finished model reads as one coherent silhouette rather than a stack of limbs.

### 2. Pylon Design

The shoulder and chest pylons are the defining feature of an EVA, and they require SNOT techniques to build — the armour has to sit at an angle the stud grid will not give you for free.

### 3. Colour Scheme

Follow the EVA palette strictly and use bricks in the matching colours. A single off-shade plate in the torso is very visible on a model this tall.

## 3. EVA vs Other Mecha MOC

![EVA mecha brick part detail photo](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-eva-lego-review/moc-eva-lego-review-3.jpg)

Compared with Gundam, EVA units are harder to build because their biomechanical look demands far more curved building techniques. The payoff is a correspondingly bigger sense of achievement when it is finished.
