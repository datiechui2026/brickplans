# Brick Gift Guide for Kids: What to Buy at Every Age

The short answer first: when you give a child bricks, age matters far more than price. Ages 1-3 call for large-particle starter bricks, ages 4-6 are ready for themed scene sets, and from 7 up you can move into technic and mechanical builds. A budget of 100-300 yuan already buys a genuinely good gift, while 300 yuan and above makes sense for older kids who already have a clear favourite theme.

## Step 1: Ages 1-3, Go Large and Safe

At this stage children mostly want to grab, stack and knock things down. Choose large bricks with a diameter of 3 cm or more so there is no risk of swallowing. A basic bucket set is the classic pick: roughly 60-80 pieces for 100-200 yuan, with rounded corners for extra safety.

Material matters as much as size. Look for food-grade ABS and a no-odour certification on the box. The goal here is not impressive models; it is hand-eye coordination and the simple satisfaction of connecting two pieces. Skip anything with tiny accessories, stickers or fragile decorated parts.

## Step 2: Ages 4-6, Go Themed and Playable

Somewhere around four, role play kicks in and the build becomes a stage for stories. Fire stations, zoos and castles are the reliable themes, usually around 200-400 pieces at 200-350 yuan.

Sets with minifigures and small mechanisms win with this age group: car doors that open and close, a crane arm that rotates, a hatch that lifts. Keep the building difficulty within a 30-60 minute window so the child can finish in one or two sittings. A set that drags on for hours tends to lose them halfway, and the bricks end up back in the box.

## Step 3: Ages 7+, Go Technic and Mechanical

From about seven, kids can handle gears, pulleys and motors. Technic-style sets in this bracket run roughly 500-1000 pieces at 300-600 yuan. The payoff is that the model actually moves, which makes the logic of gear ratios and linkages tangible instead of abstract.

If your child already has building experience, compatible domestic brands are worth a look: value for money is better, and accuracy has come close to the mainstream names in recent years. You can also find extension build instructions on brickplan.cn once the official model has been built and rebuilt a few times.

## FAQ

**Q: Which brand should I choose when buying bricks for a child?**
A: If the budget allows, LEGO is the safe choice for quality and after-sales support. If you are chasing value, compatible domestic brands are a solid option, and their brick tolerance has got quite close to the big names in recent years.

**Q: At what age can a child start playing with bricks?**
A: Around 18 months they can handle large-particle starter bricks, from about three they can move on to small-particle themed sets, and from seven the technic and mechanical sets become realistic. Follow the age in steps rather than jumping ahead.

**Q: How much is reasonable to spend on a brick gift?**
A: A sensible range is 100-200 yuan for ages 1-3, 200-350 yuan for ages 4-6, and 300-600 yuan for ages 7 and up. Holiday gift-box editions usually feel more like a proper present.

**Q: How do I judge whether a set suits my child?**
A: Check the age marking and the particle size first, then match the theme to the child's interests. A vehicle fan wants a transport theme, an animal fan wants a zoo set. When the interest fits, the set does not end up gathering dust on a shelf.

One last note: the value of a brick gift is not in the price tag, it is in the time spent together. Sitting down to build with your child, then taking the model apart and rebuilding it into something of their own, is worth more than any single set.
