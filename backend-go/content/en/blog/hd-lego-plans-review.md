# HD LEGO Plans: Where to Find Sharp PDF Instructions

For brick MOC builders, plan sharpness directly shapes the building experience. A blurry plan does not just make it hard to see where a part goes — it leads to wrong steps, disassembly, and starting over. Finding genuinely **high-definition LEGO plans** is therefore a required skill for any MOC builder. This review walks through the main sources and helps you find the sharpest, most reliable building instructions.

## Why Does HD Matter So Much?

Before the source-by-source review, here is why resolution is the deciding factor.

| Sharpness level | Resolution | Zoom behaviour | Building experience |
|-----------|-------|---------|---------|
| Standard | 72dpi | Blurs when zoomed; part edges unclear | Error-prone, poor experience |
| HD | 150dpi | Usable at 2-3x zoom, details readable | Smooth building |
| Ultra HD | 300dpi | Usable past 5x zoom, print-grade | Best experience |

## How to Judge Whether a Plan Is Sharp

### Method 1: Check the file properties
In your file manager, right-click the plan file > Properties > Details, then check the horizontal and vertical resolution values.

### Method 2: Zoom in
Open the PDF, zoom to 200%, and look at:
- Whether part edges are crisp
- Whether part numbers and colour labels are readable
- Whether arrows and callout lines are clearly visible

### Method 3: Print test
Print a single page and check:
- Line quality — any jagged edges?
- Colour labels — accurate?
- Small parts — still identifiable?
