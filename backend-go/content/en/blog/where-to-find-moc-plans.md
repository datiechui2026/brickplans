## 4. How to Choose High-Quality MOC Instructions

Once you know where to find MOC instructions, the next skill is judging them. These are the traits shared by the strongest instructions on brickplan.cn.

Good instructions typically have:

- The parts used listed for every step

- Multiple viewing angles so nothing is ambiguous

Every instruction on brickplan.cn carries a clear difficulty rating from 1 to 5 stars. Beginners should start at 1–2 stars and work up to harder builds.
