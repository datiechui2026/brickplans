# Gundam LEGO MOC Guide: Build RX-78-2, Unicorn and Zaku II

"Stand up, boy!" With that line, Amuro Ray climbed into the RX-78-2 and opened the Universal Century. Since *Mobile Suit Gundam* first aired in 1979, the franchise has become the standard-bearer for Japanese robot animation and has shaped mecha culture around the world. The Gundam LEGO MOC plans below let you build your own mobile suit by hand, turning the mechanical aesthetic and the heat of battle into brick form.

## What Makes Gundam MOCs So Appealing

Gundam MOCs earn their following because they capture the heart of the series' mechanical design: a precise inner frame, layered outer armor, and a weapons loadout that runs from beam sabers to shields. Every detail carries the industrial look of a mobile suit. For MOC builders, Gundam is the ultimate stress test of mecha technique — you need SNOT (studs-not-on-top) work, frame construction and joint design to make a model that both looks right and moves right.

If you are new to Gundam MOCs, start with the [MOC beginner guide](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) and [simple brick building techniques](/en/blog/simple-brick-plans-beginners) to lay a foundation. On [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) you will find a large library of plans created by Gundam fans worldwide, covering everything from Universal Century classics to CE-era mobile suits.

## Recommended Gundam MOC Plans

### 1. RX-78-2 Gundam

As the starting point for every Gundam, the RX-78-2 is a required subject for any Gundam MOC builder. Strong versions usually include a poseable inner frame, modular outer armor, an opening cockpit, and standard equipment such as the beam rifle and shield. This classic of the Gundam lineup sits at a moderate difficulty, which makes it a great first attempt at mecha building from scratch.

### 2. Unicorn Gundam (Destroy Mode)

Unicorn Gundam is known for its elaborate mechanical design and the distinctive Unicorn-to-Destroy transformation mechanic. A MOC version has to reproduce the deployment structure of the psycho-frame, the armor transformation at the shoulders and legs, and the signature beam gatling and shield. This is an advanced build that demands solid technique, but the finished model is genuinely striking.

### 3. Zaku II

As the Principality of Zeon's mass-produced mainstay MS, the Zaku II is loved for its rugged industrial styling. MOC versions typically include the mono-eye camera (which can rotate), the heat hawk, the Zaku machine gun and the shoulder shield. If you enjoy the character shaping in [Dragon Ball brick sets](/en/blog/dragon-ball-brick-sets), the Zaku's heavy mecha look will be an easy favorite.

## Gundam MOC Building Techniques

### Frame and Articulation

The core of any Gundam MOC is the frame. The [BrickPlans PDF plan format](/en/blog/brick-plans-pdf-format) gives you detailed frame layouts, and you can study how ball joints, gear racks and friction hinges are used to achieve high articulation. A frame that holds its pose without sagging is what separates a display model from a fragile one.

### Armor and Detail

Armor design is the key to Gundam's visual appeal. Using the SNOT techniques, plate stacking and sticker detailing covered in [brick modification techniques](/en/blog/moc-brick-modification), you can give your Gundam MOC sharper panel lines, layered plating and cleaner surface detail.

### Scale and Size

Common Gundam MOC scales include 1:100 (around 20 cm tall) and 1:60 (around 30 cm tall). Pick a working scale that suits your shelf and part budget, then use the [free BrickPlans plans](/en/blog/moc-free-plans-guide) to plan the dimensions of your build before you start ordering parts.

## Gundam Crossovers with the Mecha World

Pitting the Universal Century against other mecha properties is a popular direction in MOC building. Combine a mobile suit from these Gundam plans with the Iron Man armor from [Avengers brick sets](/en/blog/avengers-brick-sets) and you can stage a "Gundam vs Iron Man" sci-fi showdown. Or display your mobile suit next to the Batmobile from [Batman brick sets](/en/blog/batman-brick-sets), so mecha and vehicle together form a complete sci-fi scene.

On [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn), community builders have already shared a large number of mecha-themed MOCs, including crossovers with classic properties such as EVA and Transformers, giving you a steady stream of inspiration.

## Final Thoughts

These Gundam MOC plans let you do more than assemble an impressive mobile suit — they teach the engineering logic behind mecha design. From the inner frame to the outer armor, from moving joints to weapons, every part is rebuilding the epic of the Universal Century. Pick up your bricks, take the controls, and write your own mobile suit story in brick.
