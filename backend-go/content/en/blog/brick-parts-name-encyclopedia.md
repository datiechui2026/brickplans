### 1.2 Special Bricks

Within any brick parts classification, special bricks serve specific situations:

- **1x1 brick with side stud** (part 87087) — the core part for SNOT techniques
- **1x1 brick with handle** (part 3957) — for hanging and connecting
- **1x2 brick with groove** (part 32124) — for inverted mounting
- **2x2 corner brick** (part 2357) — curved corners
- **2x2 brick with ball socket** (part 3937) — articulated joints

## 6. How to Look Up a Brick Part Number

When you are following a MOC plan on brickplan.cn, knowing how to search a brick part number is genuinely useful:

### 6.1 Online Lookup Tools

- **BrickLink**: the world's largest brick part database, searchable by number, colour and category
- **Rebrickable**: a MOC instruction platform that also offers part lookup
- **BrickSet**: set information lookup, with parts inventories attached

### 6.2 Reading Part Numbers

Brick part numbers are usually made up of 4 to 6 digits:
- 3000-3999: basic bricks
- 4000-4999: special bricks
- 5000-5999: plates
- 6000-6999: tiles
- 7000-7999: slopes
- 8000-8999: Technic parts
- 9000-9999: minifigures and accessories
