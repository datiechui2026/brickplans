# Avengers Brick Sets: Marvel Superhero Builds Reviewed

The Marvel Cinematic Universe has been running since Iron Man landed in 2008 — close to two decades of films by now. For a lot of fans, an **Avengers brick set** is more than a display piece: it's a way to rebuild the battles you grew up with, brick by brick. Whether you're new to the hobby or a seasoned MOC builder, a well-designed Avengers set gives you both a model to assemble and a parts pack to build with afterwards.

## Why Choose Avengers Brick Sets?

There are plenty of brick brands on the market, but the strongest **Avengers brick sets** tend to stand out on two things: accuracy of detail and play value. From the Avengers HQ tower to Thanos's Sanctuary II, from the Battle of New York to the time-heist scenes of Endgame, each set tells a Marvel story. They also hand you a generous parts library that feeds straight into your own MOC (My Own Creation) work later on.

If you're new to MOC building, start with our [MOC beginner's guide](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) and [simple brick building techniques](/en/blog/simple-brick-plans-beginners) — both will get you building quickly. Avengers-focused builders will also find plenty of fan-made plans on [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn), from Iron Man's Mark armors to the Infinity Gauntlet.

## Popular Avengers Brick Sets

### 1. Avengers HQ Tower

This set is modelled on the team's signature headquarters building, with multiple removable floors. Each floor recreates a scene from the films — Dr. Banner's lab, Thor's Bifrost-style transport device, and the rooftop helipad on top. The minifigure lineup is generous too, covering the six original Avengers. The real strength of this **Avengers brick set** is its modular design: you can combine it with other buildings to assemble your own Marvel city layout.

### 2. Endgame Time Heist Scene

A recreation of the time-travel experiment from *Avengers: Endgame*. The set includes the quantum tunnel device, Ant-Man's test suit, and several scene fragments drawn from different timelines. If you like acting out scenes rather than just displaying a model, this set has a lot of play value.

### 3. Thanos's Sanctuary II

One of the most powerful villains in the MCU gets a flagship to match. This MOC-scale set is built from thousands of pieces and includes opening hangar doors, an interior command room, and firing weapon systems. If large-scale MOCs are your thing, this is one worth collecting.

## How to Build Your Own Avengers MOC

If you already own a few base sets and want a harder challenge, try one of these directions:

- **Armor series**: From Iron Man's Mark III to the Mark 85, every generation of armor has its own silhouette and features. Our [brick modification techniques](/en/blog/moc-brick-modification) guide shows how to get a sharper likeness using the parts you already have.
- **Scene recreations**: The Battle of New York, the Battle of Sokovia, the plains of Wakanda — any of these can be your MOC's subject. Check the [BrickPlans PDF format guide](/en/blog/brick-plans-pdf-format) for detailed building instructions.
- **Custom minifigures**: Swap parts, repaint, and assemble your own version of each hero.
