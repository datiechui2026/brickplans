# Bus Stop MOC Tutorial: Shelter, Sign and Bench in 4 Steps

Here is the short version: a bus stop is one of the best-value scenes you can add to a brick street. A single 32x32 baseplate holds the shelter, the stop sign and a bench, it takes roughly 600 parts and about 1.5 hours to finish, and the parts cost comes in around 120 RMB. The instructions are a free download at brickplan.cn, and once you drop in a couple of minifigures the whole corner starts telling a story.

## Step 1: Lay the Baseplate and Ground

Start with a 32x32 baseplate as your foundation. Build the sidewalk from 2x2 light grey plates laid three rows deep, and use dark grey plates for the roadway. Run a line of 1x4 white bricks down the middle as a tactile paving strip. The zones read instantly, and you have not used a single specialized part yet.

## Step 2: Build the Shelter

Each of the four posts is a stack of 2x2 bricks, ten layers high, which puts them at about 8 cm tall. The roof is a 6x16 plate with two 1x2 slope bricks added along the edge as eaves. On the inner face of the posts, fit two transparent 1x4 glass elements to stand in as dividers. The light coming through them is what sells the shelter.

## Step 3: Make the Stop Sign and Info Board

Mount a 1x2 tile with a printed element on a pole built 12 bricks tall, then cap it with a 1x1 round plate as a decorative ball. Beside it, stand a 4x4 billboard and apply the route map sticker. Spell out the station name in 1x1 letter bricks: B, U, S.

## Step 4: Place the Bench and Details

The bench is two 1x6 brown plates for the seat over 2x2 bricks for the legs, and it takes about 10 minutes. Scatter the finishing pieces: a trash can, a streetlight, and two minifigures waiting for the bus. That is the moment the street scene comes alive. If you plan to link this into a full street, leave two studs of connection on one side of the baseplate.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this bus stop MOC suitable for beginners?**

A: Very much so. There are no gears and no complex mechanisms anywhere in the build, just bricks stacked on bricks. At around 600 parts and roughly 1.5 hours of building time, anyone aged 8 and up working from the brickplan.cn instructions should get through it without getting stuck. It is one of the best first projects for someone learning street building.

**Q: How is a bus stop MOC different from a street scene set?**

A: A set gives you a fixed design in fixed colors. A MOC lets you swap colors and change dimensions freely, for example raising the shelter or giving it a transparent roof. The parts are also more universal, so when you take it apart they can go straight into other street builds, which makes it better value and more fun to keep reworking.

**Q: How do I reinforce the shelter roof so it does not drop pieces?**

A: Add four 1x2 bricks with holes underneath the roof plate as anchor points, then run two 6-stud axles through them to lock everything together. That is far more stable than simply stacking. When you move the model, take the roof off first and move the base separately, which prevents about 90 percent of the collapses.

**Q: How much does a bus stop MOC cost to build?**

A: Loose parts run about 100 to 150 RMB, with the transparent glass elements and printed pieces being the main expense. If you already have leftovers from street scene sets, you can usually fill the gaps for under 50 RMB. The brickplan.cn instructions include a list of alternative transparent parts you can substitute.
