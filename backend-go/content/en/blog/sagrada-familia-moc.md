# Sagrada Familia MOC Review: 4,200-Part Gaudi Church Plan

Bottom line first: this Sagrada Familia MOC plan builds into a model roughly 36 cm tall on a base about 28 cm long, uses around 4,200 parts, and the instructions cost about 65 yuan. A confident builder needs roughly 16 hours, and the parts work out to about 1.4 fen per piece. The tower cluster and stained-glass windows land at around 80% fidelity, which puts it among the better church MOCs out there — a display-first build for advanced builders rather than a weekend starter.

## Form and Fidelity

The hardest things to reproduce on Sagrada Familia are its forest of towers and its layered arch construction. This plan forms the facade silhouette from four main towers plus two shorter ones. The tower shafts are built mostly from light beige bricks, and the tops finish with cone pieces and cross ornaments, with proportions kept under control throughout.

The arch section stacks curved pieces to build double-layer arched doorways, and the columns carry a textured surface of recesses and ridges. The mix of Gothic structure and Art Nouveau ornament reads correctly at model scale, which is not a given for this building.

The standout is the stained glass. Trans-clear and colored bricks are arranged into geometric patterns, and the light transmission under a lamp is genuinely striking. Overall fidelity sits near 80%: it has real presence from across a room and still holds up when you look closely.

## Building Experience

The roughly 4,200 parts are divided into four booklets: base, facade, towers, and dome and spires. Each tower is a repeated structure, so once the second one is done your pace picks up noticeably — figure on about 260 parts per hour. The arches and stained-glass panels demand careful alignment, so press each layer firmly down as the step diagram shows; skipping that is how you end up with a leaning facade later.

Total build time is about 16 hours. At two hours a day that is roughly a week. There are few dark-colored parts in the mix, so sorting by color goes fast and never becomes a chore.

## Value and Recommendation

The instructions run about 65 yuan, and the full set of loose parts costs about 580 yuan, which comes to roughly 1.4 fen per piece — close to half the price of a LEGO set at the same size. This MOC is positioned as a large display piece, and at 36 cm tall it stands out on top of a cabinet or in an entryway.

If you like Gaudi's architecture and want a church model with instant recognition, it is worth buying. If mood lighting matters more to you than the daytime look, look for the modified version of the plan that includes a light kit instead. The plan can be found on brickplan.cn by searching for the cathedral; check that the parts list actually includes the trans-colored pieces before you order.
