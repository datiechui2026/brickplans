# Where to Find Sluban Instructions in 2026: 6 Channels Tested

Short answer first: after two weeks of testing, the fastest route to a Sluban instruction PDF in 2026 is the QR code printed in the manual, then the brand's WeChat official account, and only then the third-party instruction hubs. I worked through six channels one by one — the manual QR code succeeded on nearly 100% of sets, while third-party hubs took an average of three page loads before a working link turned up. The channels below are listed in the order I would actually use them, with a note on what each one is good at and where it fails.

## Channel test: which ones actually deliver the file

### Step 1: the manual QR code (try this first)

Sets produced from 2024 onward print a QR code on the manual's front cover and last page; scanning it in WeChat jumps straight to the official download page. I tested five boxes from the 2025-2026 lineup and all five worked, averaging under a minute each. The limitation is age: pre-2020 sets don't carry the code, so you have to drop down to the next method.

### Step 2: the official WeChat account

Follow the "Sluban Blocks" official account and look under Player Services > Instruction Downloads, then enter the set number (for example M38-B0799). Of eight pre-2020 sets I tried, six returned results — roughly a 75% hit rate. The search is exact-match only: one wrong digit and you get nothing back, so confirm the number on the side of the box before typing.

### Step 3: the official website and customer service

The website's download centre only carries models from the last three years, so older instructions are effectively absent. The live chat is the hidden bonus — in March 2026 I asked once and had a cloud-drive link to an out-of-print instruction within ten minutes. Very few builders know this route exists.

### Step 4: third-party instruction hubs and resale platforms

- **BrickPlan instruction community**: an aggregator for Chinese brick brands. Searching "Sluban" filters directly by set number, and there is image search for when the manual is gone entirely. Expect to click through a couple of result pages before the actual PDF appears.
- **Xianyu / Taobao**: 2-5 yuan buys you a bundled pack, but quality varies wildly. One of the three I bought was a blurry scan, and I gave up halfway through the build because the steps were unreadable.
- **Tieba / Xiaohongshu**: luck-of-the-draw searching "set number + instructions". Popular mecha series are easy to find; obscure series are basically not there.

## My recommendation

If you still have the manual, scan the code — nothing else comes close on speed. If it is lost, try the WeChat account by set number first, then an aggregator like BrickPlan, and treat Xianyu as a last resort. For older sets, save the PDF to cloud storage the moment you find it: Sluban occasionally pulls old files, and roughly 20% of the pre-2020 links I tested were already dead. When buying a used lot, ask the seller for a photo of the manual's last page, where the QR code is visible.

## FAQ

**Q: Where can I download Sluban instructions officially?**
A: There are three official routes: the QR code in the manual, the Instruction Downloads menu in the Sluban Blocks WeChat account, and the website download centre. The QR code is fastest, WeChat covers older sets, and the website only holds the last three years — they complement each other.

**Q: What if I can't find instructions for an old Sluban set?**
A: Search by set number in the WeChat account first; if that fails, ask the website's live chat, which usually supplies a cloud-drive link. After that, try a third-party hub like BrickPlan or Xianyu, though sharpness is not guaranteed.

**Q: Are Sluban instructions free?**
A: The official channels are all free — QR code, WeChat account and asking customer service. Third-party hubs are partly free; Xianyu sellers charge 2-5 yuan for a bundle, with the risk of blurry scans, so use the free official routes first.

**Q: How do I find the set number if the manual is lost?**
A: Check the white label on the side or bottom of the box; numbers look like M38-B0799. If the box is gone too, photograph the parts and run BrickPlan's image search to reverse-look-up the set — about a 60% success rate.

**Q: Are Sluban instructions interchangeable with LEGO ones?**
A: No. Sluban parts are broadly compatible with LEGO, but the instructions are designed independently and the build order is completely different. You must use the official file for that specific set; mixing them causes structural errors.
