# Lotus Brick MOC Review: Layered Petals and Pond Scene

Bottom line first: the brickplan lotus MOC is the best-looking set in the botanical category. The layered pink petals are genuinely striking, a single flower runs about 200 parts, the full set is around 550 parts, and the budget sits at roughly 200 yuan. Accuracy score: 9/10. The downside is that the lily pad parts are a little soft, so you need patience when adjusting angles during display.

## Shape and Accuracy

The layered petals are the main attraction. brickplan stacks petal pieces of different sizes one inside the other, grading from pale pink up to deep pink. With the three layers offset at different angles, the flower reads as properly three-dimensional rather than flat. The lily pads use disc pieces with slightly upturned edges, giving a natural curve. The pond surface is laid flat with transparent light-blue pieces, and the lotus sits half-submerged in the water. The overall mood is captured convincingly: placed on a 16x16 stud water surface it looks elegant, and it is a textbook example of an Eastern-aesthetic subject.

## Parts Quality

The gradient pink pieces transition evenly with no colour mismatch, and the petal edges are rounded rather than sharp on the fingers. The full set of roughly 550 parts arrives split across two bags, and a single flower accounts for about 200 of them, taking around 40 minutes to build. The lily pad discs are on the soft side, so when you push a stem through you need a light touch to line up the hole, not brute force. Parts quality lands at 8.5/10 overall, and the finished model is light but stable, suitable for long-term display.

## Scene Pairing and Value

At a 200 yuan budget for roughly 550 parts, the cost works out to about 0.36 yuan per piece, which is mid-to-upper value. The lotus subject is high on looks and works well as a piece for a tea table or study desk, and it stands out even more against an aquarium background. You can display a single flower for a minimalist look, or build the whole set into a lotus pond scene. Among botanical MOCs it is a collector's first pick, and it is presentable enough to give as a gift.

## FAQ

**Q: How many parts are in a lotus brick MOC, and what does it cost?**
A: A single flower is about 200 parts and roughly 40 minutes of building. The full set is around 550 parts at a 200 yuan budget. You can buy one flower on its own or build the whole pond, and the looks sit in the top tier of botanical MOCs.

**Q: Do the lotus MOC lily pads bend easily?**
A: The lily pad discs are fairly soft, so slide the stem in slowly along the hole instead of forcing it. Under normal display it will not deform, just avoid leaving heavy objects resting on it for long periods.
