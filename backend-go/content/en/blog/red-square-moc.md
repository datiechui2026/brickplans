# Red Square MOC: St. Basil's and Kremlin Wall in 3 Steps

The short version: this Red Square MOC uses 7,800 pieces, measures 110 cm long and 45 cm wide, and covers St. Basil's Cathedral, the Kremlin red wall, GUM department store and the central plaza. Expect roughly 45 hours of building, and the instructions are priced at 268 yuan on brickplan.cn. It sits in the mid-to-large scene category, and the three steps below take you from an empty baseplate to a full Red Square skyline.

## Step 1: Lay the Central Plaza Foundation

Step 1 uses about 1,800 pieces and takes around 8 hours. Start by positioning a baseplate measuring 62×110 studs. Cover the central plaza in light gray tiles, then scatter 2×2 tiles at intervals to create a cobblestone texture. Leave foundation slots for the buildings around the plaza edge, and mark the positions of the cathedral, the red wall and the department store with 1×1 round plates in different colors.

Tip: press 2×4 weighted bricks onto all four corners first so the 110 cm baseplate does not warp when you move it. Leave 2 studs of tolerance at the edge of each building foundation so you can fine-tune the fit later.

## Step 2: Build St. Basil's Cathedral and the Red Wall

Step 2 is the main event: about 4,200 pieces and around 24 hours. Begin with the Kremlin red wall, stacking 1×2 dark red bricks in a staggered running bond. Every 10 layers, add a course of 1×1 black bricks to suggest the mortar lines, and finish the top with crenellations. The wall stands about 16 cm tall and runs about 70 cm long.

Then comes St. Basil's Cathedral. The central tower rises about 42 cm, surrounded by 8 smaller towers. Four of the onion domes use 1×4 curved pieces stacked in a spiral, rotated 15° per layer to produce the signature twisted pattern. The onion domes account for roughly 900 pieces and are the hardest part of the whole build. Build them from the tip downward, and use transparent round pieces for the crosses.

## Step 3: Add GUM and Scene Details

Step 3 uses about 1,800 pieces and takes around 13 hours. Build the GUM facade from beige bricks as a two-story arcade with 4 arched windows in the middle. In the center of the plaza, add the podium in front of Lenin's Mausoleum and about 40 1×1 minifigure positions; once figures are placed, the scene comes alive immediately. Finish with the small elements — tree pits, lampposts and flower beds — and Red Square is complete.

When it is finished, the scene is about 110 cm long, paired with a 30 cm tall main tower, and the visual impact is strong. It suits a display cabinet along an entire wall or the center of a large table. Overall accuracy is around 8.8 out of 10, making it another grand scene beyond Chinese-themed subjects.

## FAQ

**Q: With this many parts, what is the most economical way to buy the Red Square MOC?**

A: brickplan.cn provides a parts list. Buying individual parts costs roughly 3,000-3,800 yuan; a complete pack is about 4,500 yuan. Individual parts require about 10 hours of sorting yourself, so if your budget is tight, go with loose parts.

**Q: The spiral curve of the onion domes is hard to build. Any tips?**

A: Stack 1×4 curved pieces with a 15° rotation per layer, ring by ring. This is about 50% faster than bending curved surfaces by hand. The instructions highlight the tower tips, so following the order and building from the tip down is the easiest approach.

**Q: How long does the whole Red Square take, and can one person do it?**

A: An experienced builder needs about 45 hours. Split it into 10-12 sessions of 4-5 hours each. One person can absolutely complete it, but for Step 2 it helps to have two people working together, which saves about 8 hours.

**Q: Will a 110 cm scene fit in my home?**
