# Brick Subway MOC Instructions Review: Design, Difficulty, Parts

## Introduction

Subways are the arteries of the modern city, and they have long been one of the themes brick builders most love to recreate. From detailed platform scenes to linked track systems, and from classic rolling stock to futuristic train designs, subway MOCs (My Own Creation) keep gaining momentum in brick communities around the world. This article picks out a range of high-quality subway MOC instructions and evaluates them across four dimensions: design quality, build difficulty, part requirements and the overall building experience, so you can find the plan that fits you best on a dedicated MOC instruction platform.

## 1. Design Quality: Accuracy and Creativity in Equal Measure

Design quality in a subway MOC starts with how faithfully it reproduces a real train. A good set of instructions will stretch a limited parts palette to capture the defining features of the vehicle: the streamlined profile of the cab, the window line running down the carriages, the way the doors are divided, and even the platform screen doors and wayfinding signage.

Take one popular domestic subway train MOC as an example. The designer uses slope bricks and tiles to trace the angled nose, while the headlights combine transparent elements with light bricks for a particularly striking effect at night. The platform section is modular: platform level, track level and concourse level can be removed and reassembled independently, which makes the interior details easy to display and the whole thing easy to move and store.

Another build takes a bolder approach with a cutaway scene, presenting the train, the platform and a cross-section of the tunnel together on a single diorama base. The visual impact is considerable. This approach has been well received on brickplan.cn and is especially well suited to builders working on city-themed dioramas.

## 2. Build Difficulty: A Full Range From Beginner to Expert

Subway MOC instructions vary enormously in difficulty, so there is something for every stage of builder.

**Beginner (★★☆☆☆)**: aimed at new builders. These plans usually focus on a single carriage or a small platform, with 200-500 parts and a build time of roughly 1-2 hours. The steps are clearly laid out, there is almost no call for unusual parts, and a common brick set is often enough to complete the model. A good first taste of subway MOC building.

**Intermediate (★★★★☆)**: for builders with some experience. These plans include a full train consist (2-4 carriages) plus a platform scene, with 1000-2000 parts and a build time of roughly 4-8 hours. Designers introduce more advanced techniques here, such as SNOT (Studs Not On Top) to create smooth carriage sides and hinged elements to build working bogies.

**Expert (★★★★★)**: a serious challenge. These are full-scale subway diorama instructions covering a complete loop of track, a multi-platform interchange station, a depot and a tunnel system. Part counts can exceed 5000 and the build runs for weeks. Plans at this level assume some understanding of structural engineering to keep a large diorama rigid, and they also need to account for electrical work such as lighting runs.

## 3. Part Requirements: Compatibility and Availability

Parts are the raw material of any MOC. When evaluating subway instructions, we focus on the following points.

**Share of common parts**: well-designed subway instructions use generic elements wherever possible and avoid leaning too heavily on rare parts or elements exclusive to a particular set. Most subway MOC plans today run at 80% common parts or better, and builders can use the parts list feature on brickplan.cn to filter out what they already own, which keeps the cost of topping up low.

**Colour choices**: subway trains tend to be silver-grey, white, blue or red, and all of these are well supplied in the brick market. One thing to watch: certain greys used in some plans, such as dark blue-grey, may not match standard grey exactly across different brands. Do a small colour comparison test before committing to the full build.

**Special parts**: the elements used for tracks, platform screen doors and ticket machines can be bought separately from brick accessory sellers. Some plans also offer a "no special parts" alternative that recreates the subway scene with ordinary bricks, which is ideal for builders on a tighter budget.

## 4. The Building Experience: From Instructions to Finished Model

The building experience is the heart of any MOC, and good subway instructions tend to show real care in how the process is structured.

**Step-by-step sequencing**: high-quality plans walk you through in the order of chassis, then carriage body, then interior, then exterior detailing, then the platform scene. Each step comes with a clear parts list and assembly diagram, so you never lose your place.

**Structural logic**: large subway MOCs put real demands on structural strength, especially builds that get moved often or take part in live displays. Good instructions flag the key load-bearing points and suggest reinforcement, for example using Technic elements at carriage connections to resist twisting and layering plates into the platform base to add weight.

**Room to expand**: many subway plans support later upgrades. You can add a motor kit to run the train automatically, fit LED strips to simulate platform lighting, or even use a Bluetooth module to drive the train from a phone. These extensions give an MOC a long working life.

## 5. Overall Recommendations

Weighing design quality, difficulty fit, part availability and building experience together, three categories of subway MOC instructions stand out:

1. **Single-vehicle display builds** (rating: ★★★★☆) — ideal for a desk or display cabinet. Few parts, quick to build, refined when finished, and an excellent entry point into subway MOCs.
2. **City diorama builds** (rating: ★★★★★) — these include platform, track and surrounding street scenes, slot neatly into a city-themed diorama and make an ideal addition for modular street builders.
3. **Full-function scene builds** (rating: ★★★★☆) — train, platform, tunnel and control centre in one package, aimed at hardcore builders chasing maximum accuracy, with room for motorisation and a high degree of interactivity.

The appeal of subway MOC instructions is that they turn a scene you know from the daily commute into something you can hold in your hands and build yourself.
