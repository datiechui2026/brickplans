# Batman Brick Review: LEGO Sets vs Budget MOC Alternatives

Batman is the most popular hero in the DC catalogue, and that popularity shows up on the shelf: licensed sets, compatible third-party kits and fan-designed builds all compete for the same display space. This review breaks the options down by piece count, price, play features and display value so you can pick the Gotham build that actually fits your budget.

## The Three Main Types of Batman Brick Sets

Before comparing individual kits, it helps to know which of three product lines you are shopping in:

| Type | Example brands | Price range | What you get |
|------|---------|---------|------|
| Officially licensed | LEGO | ¥200-3,000 | Refined design, richly detailed minifigure cowls |
| Domestic compatible | SEMBO / Quanguan / Qiji | ¥50-800 | Strong value, some genuinely good original designs |
| MOC recreations | Gobricks / Double Eagle | ¥100-500 | Highly customisable, works alongside your own parts bin |

On [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) you will find a large library of Batman-themed MOC instructions and build plans, covering everything from classic vehicles to Gotham City architecture. ## Batmobile Sets Reviewed

### 1. LEGO 76181 Batmobile: The 2022 Movie Car

Pieces: 392 | Price: about ¥350 | Age: 8+

This one recreates the muscle-car Batmobile from the 2022 film. Play features include turning wheels, a raiseable rear fin and a hidden launcher. The minifigures are Batman and the Riddler, and both exclusives carry very high-quality printing.

**Pros:** accurate shaping, smooth-working front-wheel steering
**Cons:** on the small side, so it does not command much shelf presence

### 2. Gobricks MOC Batmobile

Pieces: 580 | Price: about ¥180 | Part compatibility: ★★★★★

For value-focused builders this is the kit worth watching. It is bigger and more detailed than the official car, with an opening cockpit canopy, an adjustable rear spoiler and a complete engine bay. Thanks to the quality of Gobricks parts, the build feel is close to LEGO while the price lands at roughly half.

### 3. The 1989 Classic Batmobile

A remake of the Tim Burton-era car: a stretched, streamlined body wrapped in glossy black parts that photographs extremely well. It is a display-first build rather than a play set. If you are new to MOC building, our [beginner guide to simple brick plans](/en/blog/simple-brick-plans-guide) will get you through the basic techniques quickly.

## The Batcave and Arkham Asylum

### Batcave Playsets

The Batcave is Batman's base and the most substantial location build in the range. LEGO's Batcave (76183) packs in a cave laboratory, the Batcomputer and a Batmobile turntable, giving it very high play value — but ¥1,299 puts plenty of builders off.

Domestic MOC Batcave instructions, by contrast, are free or inexpensive on [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn). Using our [brick plans download guide](/en/blog/brick-plans-download-guide) you can source the parts yourself and keep the total cost to around ¥400.

### Arkham Asylum

Arkham Asylum is one of Gotham's best-known landmarks. The LEGO version (70912) has been out of production for years and now trades at ¥3,000 and up. The good news is that the MOC community offers plenty of excellent alternatives, including modular cell blocks, a guard room and a dedicated Joker area — in some cases more detailed than the official set ever was.

Ratings weigh price against piece count, play features and how the finished model looks on a shelf. The gap widens fastest on retired sets, where you are paying a collector premium rather than for bricks.

## Bottom Line

Officially licensed LEGO sets genuinely win on design polish and branding, and the minifigure detail is hard to match. On value, though, the domestic MOC route is clearly more attractive: more pieces, bigger models and roughly half the outlay.

If your budget is limited, start from the instructions rather than the box. Pick a MOC plan, pair it with quality Gobricks parts, and you get the build experience plus a large display model without the licensed markup. Builders who want the Batcave or Arkham Asylum in particular will find the savings hard to ignore.
