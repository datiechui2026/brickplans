# Military Brick MOC Blueprint Review: Tanks, Jets, Warships

## Product Overview

Military subjects have long been one of the most popular categories in the brick MOC (My Own Creation) world. From armour columns rolling across the ground to fighter jets climbing into the sky and warships cutting through the waves, military brick MOC blueprints let builders recreate classic war machines out of bricks. This review covers five of the most sought-after military MOC blueprints currently available, spanning land, air and sea, to give military fans and brick builders a complete buying reference.

## Parts Review

Military MOC blueprints place extremely high demands on **colour accuracy** and **detail expression**. The blueprints reviewed here generally use military colour schemes such as **army green, desert tan and dark grey**, and part compatibility is good across the board.

The **99A Main Battle Tank** blueprint stands out in its track design, using an innovative chain-link connection method that effectively solves the problem of traditional tracks slipping off. The **F-22 Raptor** uses a dark trans-clear canopy piece that captures the stealth fighter's signature cockpit perfectly.

Worth noting: detailed parts lists for all of these blueprints can be downloaded on [brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn), so builders can gather their inventory in advance.

## Building Experience

Military MOC blueprints generally emphasise **structural stability**. The 99A Main Battle Tank's turret rotation mechanism is cleverly designed around a gear linkage system, and the rotation feel is smooth. The F-22 Raptor's variable-sweep wing mechanism is the highlight of the build, using a complex linkage system to adjust the wing angle dynamically.

The Shandong Aircraft Carrier takes the longest, estimated at 15-20 hours, but the sense of achievement from its huge hull and dense air wing is unmatched. The Z-10 is comparatively friendly and a good choice for beginners trying a military-themed build for the first time.

## Finished Model Showcase

Completed military MOC models have very high **display value**. The finished 99A Main Battle Tank measures roughly 35cm long, with a gun barrel that elevates and depresses and a turret that rotates a full 360 degrees. The F-22 Raptor spans 40cm across the wings, has retractable landing gear and cuts a striking silhouette. The Shandong Aircraft Carrier comes in at over 80cm long and is a genuine centrepiece for the shelf.
