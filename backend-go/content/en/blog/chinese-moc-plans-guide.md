# Chinese Brick MOC Instructions: Free Sources and Brand Picks

As Chinese brick brands have grown, more and more builders are hunting for Chinese brick MOC instructions. Compared with imported brands, these parts offer friendly pricing and steadily improving quality, which has won them a large following. But newcomers keep asking the same two questions: where do you actually find MOC instructions for Chinese bricks, and which brands are worth buying? This guide maps out the whole landscape so you can start building MOCs from zero.

## Why Build MOCs with Chinese Bricks?

### A clear price advantage
Chinese bricks typically cost a third of imported brands or even less. That means the same budget buys you far more parts, and bigger MOC projects become realistic. For anyone testing the MOC waters for the first time, free Chinese brick MOC instructions are plentiful and the cost of a failed experiment is very low.

### Compatibility is mature
Mainstream Chinese brands such as Gobricks, Double Eagle, SEMBO and Qiji all use brick systems that are fully compatible with LEGO, so MOC instructions work as-is. Instructions you download from a community, whether they were designed for LEGO or for Chinese bricks, will generally build without modification.

### Chinese brick brand ranking (2026)
Based on builder community reputation and product quality, these are the brands worth watching right now:

1. **Gobricks** — part quality closest to LEGO, extremely high precision; the go-to parts supplier for MOC builders
2. **Double Eagle** — excellent technic parts, with stable motors and drivetrain systems
3. **SEMBO** — strong original design work and a rich line-up of licensed IP; set parts are well suited to MOC rebuilding
4. **Qiji** — a veteran manufacturer with good compatibility and consistently regular parts
5. **Wange** — a clear strength in scene and diorama sets, well suited to street-scene MOCs

## Where to Find Chinese Brick MOC Instructions

### 1. Dedicated MOC instruction platforms
A dedicated brick MOC instruction sharing platform is the best channel for finding plans. On **brickplan.cn** you will find a large library of MOC instructions optimised for Chinese bricks. ### 2. Official brand communities
Many Chinese brick brands run their own builder communities where fans share the MOC instructions they have designed. Gobricks' official forum, for example, has a dedicated MOC section where builders download instructions for free and swap building tips.

### 3. Social media and video platforms
Search for Chinese brick MOC instructions on platforms such as Bilibili and Xiaohongshu and you will find a huge amount of shared plans and build tutorials from creators. Many of them post free download links alongside video walkthroughs, so even beginners can follow along comfortably.

### 4. Adapting plans from overseas instruction platforms
Instructions hosted on international platforms such as Rebrickable can usually be built with Chinese bricks directly. Just download the plan, import the parts list into a parts marketplace, and pick Chinese brick suppliers at checkout.

## How to Judge the Quality of a MOC Instruction File

### Clarity of the steps
A good MOC instruction file breaks the build down into clear steps, with the part changes in each step obvious at a glance. Every plan on brickplan.cn goes through a quality review to make sure the steps are clear and the labelling is accurate.

### Parts availability
Check whether the parts a plan calls for are easy to obtain. Instructions on brickplan.cn note where the parts come from and recommend Chinese brick alternatives, so you never end up with a plan you cannot actually build.

### Stated build difficulty
Good instructions clearly state a difficulty level and an estimated build time, so you can pick a project that matches your experience.

## Advanced Tips for Chinese Brick MOCs

### Sort and manage your parts
Before starting a large MOC, sort your parts by colour and type. Sorting boxes or trays will noticeably speed up your build.

### From following instructions to original design
Once you have enough building experience behind you, try modifying existing plans and gradually work towards a fully original MOC design. brickplan.cn has plenty of base plans that are well suited to modification, making it an excellent starting point for learning MOC design.

### Join the builder community
Join Chinese brick MOC groups to exchange experience and share builds with other hobbyists. Many of the better brick MOC instruction sharing platforms include community features, so you can find like-minded builders there.
