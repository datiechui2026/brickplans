# Captain America MOC Plans: Shield and Super-Soldier Builds

Captain America is one of the most recognizable heroes in the Marvel universe, and his star-spangled uniform and vibranium shield have long since crossed over into pop culture. This review looks at several Captain America MOC plans so you can rebuild the super-soldier in brick form, from a single minifigure to a full movie diorama.

## Popular Captain America MOC Plans

### 1. Classic Captain America Minifigure
- **Plan name**: Captain America classic-look minifigure
- **Piece count**: around 400
- **Difficulty**: Low
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

This Captain America plan recreates the World War II era look. The blue uniform is paired with red stripes, and the white star on the chest is done with a printed piece. The "A" emblem on the helmet is reproduced with a sticker.

### 2. Vibranium Shield
- **Plan name**: Vibranium Shield, 1:1 scale
- **Piece count**: around 350
- **Difficulty**: Low
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

The shield is Captain America's signature piece of equipment. It is built from round tiles and curved bricks, with the red, white and blue concentric rings clearly separated. A stand can be added so it works as a wall display.

### 3. Avengers: Age of Ultron Captain America
- **Plan name**: Avengers Age of Ultron look
- **Piece count**: around 850
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆

The redesign from the second Avengers film uses a darker blue and much finer detail work. Poseable joints let you set him up in a shield-throwing combat stance.

### 4. Captain America vs Winter Soldier Diorama
- **Plan name**: Captain America vs Winter Soldier duel scene
- **Piece count**: around 1500
- **Difficulty**: High
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

The build includes the highway showdown, two vehicles and minifigures of both Captain America and the Winter Soldier. Once it is finished you can recreate the film's most famous sequence.

## Key Building Techniques

### 1. Shield Structure

Build the circular shape from wedge bricks arranged around a central point, and check that the concentric rings line up as you go. Alternating the red, white and blue sections evenly is the core skill here — a half-plate offset in one ring is immediately visible once the shield is finished.

### 2. Uniform Texture

Use tiles and smooth plates to capture the clean finish of the uniform, then switch to slope bricks at the ribs and waist to create the transition between sections. Keeping the tile work flat against the body is what separates a sharp figure from a lumpy one.

### 3. Helmet Shape

Captain America's helmet carries the "A" emblem plus the small wings on each side. These are built from a combination of 1x1 clip bricks and wedge bricks, which lets the wings sit slightly proud of the helmet shell without a visible gap.
