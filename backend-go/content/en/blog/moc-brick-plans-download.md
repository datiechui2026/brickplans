## Build Difficulty

MOC plans vary enormously in difficulty, and platforms usually grade them like this:

- **Beginner (★★☆☆☆)**: micro buildings or small vehicles, 50-200 parts, suited to builders new to MOC
- **Intermediate (★★★☆☆)**: mid-sized scenes or vehicles, 200-500 parts, requiring basic building skills
- **Advanced (★★★★☆)**: large buildings or complex mechanisms, 500-1500 parts, demanding patience and spatial imagination
- **Expert (★★★★★)**: very large scenes or poseable models, 1500+ parts, aimed at experienced builders looking for a challenge

When choosing an MOC plan, match it to your own experience and work upward from beginner to intermediate rather than jumping straight in.

## Parts Review

Plans on different platforms have their own characteristics when it comes to parts demand:

- **Completeness of the parts list**: a quality plan provides a detailed parts list with part numbers, colors and quantities. Every plan on BrickPlans comes with a complete parts list, so you can gather everything you need directly from it.
- **Parts compatibility**: most MOC plans are compatible with standard LEGO bricks and also work with Chinese brands such as Gobricks and SEMBO. On **brand recommendations**, Gobricks offers the highest precision and a building feel close to LEGO; SEMBO has a wide variety of special parts at a friendly price.
- **Color consistency**: Gobricks and LEGO both perform well on color matching, with parts from different production batches holding the same color so the finished model looks clean.
