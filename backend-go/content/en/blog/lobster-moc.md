## 1. Appearance and Accuracy

The body breaks into three sections: the carapace, the abdomen and the tail fan. The carapace uses large curved slope bricks to build a rounded silhouette, while the abdomen steps inward layer by layer through a hinged structure that captures the segmented feel. Overall accuracy lands around 80%. Dark red dominates the shell color scheme, with orange-red accents woven through, and the light-dark interleaving mimics the color transition of a cooked lobster. The eyes are black round bricks capped with transparent hemispheres, and the antennae are bent into natural curves using hose pieces, giving the model a convincing biological look from a distance.

## 2. Joints and Poseability

The model has 14 moving joints in total: 2 on each antenna, 1 on each of the six walking legs, 4 in the abdomen, and 2 in the tail fan. The walking legs connect through ball joints and swing forward and back to simulate a crawling stance. The abdomen hinges bend as a unit, so you can set up a defensive arched posture. In testing, joint damping felt right in the middle of the range, and poses hold without sagging. One caveat: the antenna hose pieces will keep a crease if they stay bent for a long time, so straighten them out when the model goes into storage.
