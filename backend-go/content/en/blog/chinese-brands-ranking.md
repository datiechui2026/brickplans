# 2024 Chinese Brick Brand Ranking: 10 Brands Worth Building

Chinese brick brands have moved a long way from the days of simply chasing LEGO. Part precision, clutch power and colour consistency have improved to the point where several domestic labels now match or exceed international standards, and original MOC design has become a real strength rather than an afterthought. That is why the Chinese brick brand ranking below has become a regular reference point for builders looking for quality at a lower price.

## 1. Why Chinese Brick Brands Are Worth Your Attention

The rise of these brands is not an accident. As the domestic supply chain has matured, part accuracy, fit and colour reproduction have improved steadily, while prices have stayed well below the international benchmark. For many builders this is what makes large-scale building affordable at all.

For MOC builders, compatibility is the bigger draw. The overwhelming majority of Chinese brands use a brick system compatible with LEGO, which means the MOC instruction downloads available on brickplan.cn can be built with domestic parts without modification.

## 2. 2024 Chinese Brick Brand Ranking: Top 10

### 1. Qiji — the MOC builder's first choice

Qiji has an excellent reputation in the Chinese brick community, particularly for its large MOC instruction library and consistent part quality. Clutch power is high, colours are vivid and hold up over time, and the parts suit complex MOC reproductions. Qiji-themed MOC instruction downloads consistently rank among the most popular on brickplan.cn.

- **Part precision**: ★★★★★
- **Original design**: ★★★★☆
- **Value for money**: ★★★★★

### 2. Sembo — the military specialist

Sembo is in a league of its own when it comes to military subjects. Tanks, armoured vehicles and warships form a deep product line with plenty of detail. If military models are your interest, Sembo is the brand to watch, and a search for "Sembo" on brickplan.cn turns up plenty of player-shared MOC instructions.

### 3. Double Eagle — the technic benchmark

Double Eagle is known for its technic line, and its mechanical parts sit at the top of the domestic field. Gears, linkages and motor components are made to a high tolerance, which makes them well suited to complex mechanical MOC builds.

### 4. Gobricks — shorthand for part quality

Gobricks built its reputation on high-precision parts, and many Chinese MOC designers recommend its elements when reproducing a set of instructions. In brickplan.cn's domestic brand comparison tests, Gobricks parts are routinely measured directly against LEGO.

### 5. Panlos — the architecture and street-scene expert

Panlos performs strongly on buildings and street scenes, and its modular design approach fits closely with MOC thinking. If you like assembling city layouts, both the parts and the instruction resources are worth a close look.

### 6. Yuxing (Mould King) — technic meets programming

Yuxing has spent years in technic and programmable bricks, and its motor modules and sensor components lead the domestic field. For anyone building technic MOCs, it is a brand you cannot really skip.

### 7. Wange — the value pick

Wange delivers a surprisingly good building experience at a very competitive price, which makes it a strong fit for beginners on a budget and for builders working through large numbers of MOC instructions.

### 8. Sluban — the original-IP explorer

Sluban has invested heavily in original IP, releasing a range of sets built around Chinese cultural themes. It comes up often in discussions about domestic alternatives to LEGO.

### 9. Qman — the children's market leader

Qman is one of the established names in the Chinese brick industry and has worked the children's market for many years. Quality is consistent and production control is solid.

### 10. CaDa — the technic newcomer

CaDa has expanded quickly in technic bricks in recent years, releasing a series of technic sets with motor components that technic MOC fans have taken to.

## 3. How Compatible Are Chinese Bricks With LEGO?

One question comes up constantly: how compatible are Chinese bricks with LEGO? In our own testing, the basic bricks from every brand in the ranking above reach 90% compatibility or better with LEGO. That means the MOC instructions you download from brickplan.cn can be built with domestic parts.

The exception is a small number of specialised elements — certain angled connectors, for example, or bricks in rare colours — which can be hard to source from Chinese brands. Part lists on brickplan.cn usually flag compatible domestic substitutes for these cases.

## 4. How to Choose the Right Brand for You

The right brand depends on what you build. Our suggestions:

- **New to MOC building**: start with Qiji or Wange — good value and reliable compatibility
- **Military subjects**: Sembo is the go-to, and brickplan.cn has plenty of Sembo MOC instructions
- **Technic and mechanisms**: Double Eagle or Yuxing technic parts are your best options
- **Architecture and street scenes**: Panlos high-precision building parts are worth the investment
