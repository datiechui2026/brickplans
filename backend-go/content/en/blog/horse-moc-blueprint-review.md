# Horse MOC Blueprint Review: 6 BrickPlan Designs Compared

The short version: horse MOC blueprints on BrickPlan sit in the upper-middle range overall, and one stands out. Western Steed offers 98% parts compatibility and clean 3D step images, builds in about 4 hours, and costs roughly CNY 120 in parts. The two realistic-style horse blueprints, by contrast, need more than 20% rare parts and are a poor first project for beginners. Here is the breakdown by category.

## 1. Blueprint Clarity

We reviewed six horse MOC blueprints on BrickPlan, and the clarity gap is wide. Western Steed and Cartoon Pony both use high-resolution 3D rendered step images, with no more than 8 new parts introduced per step, so part orientation stays obvious even when you zoom in. The two realistic-style blueprints rely on hand-drawn line art, where corners and angled connections are easy to misread — roughly a 30% rework rate from misreads. Clarity feeds directly into build time: line-art blueprints take 40-60 minutes longer on average.

## 2. Parts List Accuracy

The parts list is the make-or-break element of a horse MOC blueprint. Western Steed totals 860 pieces across 45 part types, its list matches actual usage 100%, and 98% of those parts are standard bricks — most builders can fill the gaps from their own spares. Galloping Racehorse is missing 3 small part types from its list, so you will have to buy those separately mid-build. Our advice: after downloading, run the list through BrickPlan's parts library feature before you order anything. It saves a lot of waiting on replacement parts.

## 3. Build Experience

Western Steed has the most mature design of the group. The legs connect with ball joints and can hold a running pose, and the body is reinforced internally with skeleton bricks so it does not fall apart when touched. The full build runs about 4 hours, of which 1.5 hours goes to assembling the legs symmetrically — that section needs care. Cartoon Pony is only 420 pieces and takes 1.5-2 hours, which makes it a good fit for parents building with kids or for newcomers. In the two realistic-style blueprints, the head curves are stacked from a large number of slope pieces, which raises difficulty and leaves the structure fairly fragile — parts drop off when you pose it for photos.

## 4. Value for Money

At an average of CNY 0.14 per standard brick, Western Steed comes to about CNY 120 in parts and Cartoon Pony about CNY 60. Both are less than half the price of a comparable finished brick horse, which runs CNY 300-500. If the blueprint is free and you pull the parts from your own stock, the cost is close to zero. Taken together, Western Steed — a free download plus CNY 120 in parts for an articulated finished model — ranks first for value among blueprints on the site and is an easy strong recommendation.

## 5. Verdict

If you want a posable, playable brick horse, Western Steed is the first choice. If the model is for a young child, Cartoon Pony is the safer pick. Advanced builders chasing maximum realism can take on the realistic-style blueprints, but check your rare-parts inventory first. Before downloading anything, read the comment section for user photos and parts feedback — it will help you avoid blueprints with list errors.
