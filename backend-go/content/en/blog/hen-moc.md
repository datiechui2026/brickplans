# Brick Hen MOC Review: 380 Parts, Feather Layers, Stable Stance

The short version: this hen is the most forgiving animal MOC you can start with. Around 380 parts, roughly 9 cm tall when finished, 18 yuan for the instructions and about 70 yuan for the loose parts. Build time is around 1.5 hours. The plumage uses an overlapping, shingle-style construction that lands at roughly 85% likeness to a real hen, the two legs hold the body steady, and the round body shape is genuinely charming. If you are new to MOCs, this is a safe first pick.

## 1. Feather Rendering: Layers and Color

The plumage is the main event here. The back is covered in overlapping curved slope pieces laid like roof shingles, which is what gives the bird its volume instead of a flat, blocky shell. Red round studs are used for the comb on top of the head. The white wings are built from slope pieces angled slightly upward, so they read as folded wings rather than flat panels. Overall the feather layering is clearly defined and comes in at about 85% likeness.

The tail fans out from several brown curved pieces arranged in a spread. The palette — red comb, yellow beak, white body, brown tail — is bright and readable, and from a few steps back it looks like a plump, alert hen rather than a stack of bricks.

## 2. Build Experience: Structure and Stability

The legs are the part worth paying attention to. Each leg is built from stacked plates and connects to the body through a ball joint, which lets you set the angle before the bird goes on its feet. Once it is standing, the center of gravity sits slightly behind the middle of the body, so it stays put — a light nudge will not send it wobbling over.

The instructions are split into 5 steps, and each step keeps the part count under 15 pieces. That is a deliberate choice, and it works: you are never hunting through a pile of 60 pieces wondering where a particular plate belongs. The diagrams are clear enough that most builders will not need to zoom in. Total build time came in around 1.5 hours, spread across a couple of evenings if you build slowly.

The one warning worth passing on: the red comb pieces are small. When you open the bags, keep them separate from the white wing pieces — the two are easy to mix up, and you do not want to discover the mix-up at step 4.

## 3. Value: Instructions and Part Cost

The instructions cost 18 yuan. Ordering the loose parts yourself against the BrickPlan parts list runs about 70 yuan, so a full set lands around 90 yuan — roughly 0.24 yuan per piece. That is a reasonable rate for an animal MOC of this size.

The white and red pieces are common in most collections, so if you already have decent stock in those colors, the real cost drops to around 50 yuan. As a beginner animal MOC, both the price and the build experience are friendly: nothing here is rare, nothing is fragile, and there is no complicated sub-assembly to fight with.
