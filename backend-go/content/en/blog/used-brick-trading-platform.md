# Used Brick Trading Platforms: Safe Buying and Selling Guide

Brick building is hard on the wallet, and that is exactly why the second-hand market has become so useful. A used brick trading platform lets you find rare parts at lower prices and turn idle sets into cash. The catch is that the second-hand market is uneven: mixed listings and outright scams are common. This guide covers the main channels, how to judge parts, how to trade safely, and how to pack an order so both sides are protected.

## Mainstream Used Brick Trading Platforms

### Xianyu: The Main Domestic Marketplace for Used Bricks

Xianyu is the largest second-hand trading platform in China by volume, and its brick category is very active. You can find complete used sets, bulk loose-part lots sold by weight, and the occasional rare part. Listings move quickly, but quality varies widely.

- Pros: large user base, low prices, real chances to find bargains.
- Watch out: sellers are mixed, and genuine and imitation parts sometimes appear in the same category, so check every listing carefully.

### BrickLink: The International Standard for Second-Hand Parts

If you need rare parts or uncommon sets from outside China, BrickLink is the most widely recognized global choice. It has a mature feedback system: sellers are graded by star rating and positive feedback count, and you can filter by part condition (new, near-new, used). That structure makes it easier to compare offers and judge risk.

- Pros: organized, credible, and clear about condition grades.
- Watch out: international shipping is not cheap, so it makes the most sense for high-value or hard-to-find parts.

### Tieba and Second-Hand Groups: Quieter Channels for Hobbyists

Brick-focused Tieba forums, brand-specific boards, and many WeChat or QQ resale groups are important channels for experienced builders. Deals inside these circles often run on reputation, prices can beat public platforms, and you can talk directly with fellow hobbyists about builds and parts.

- Pros: good atmosphere and realistic prices.
- Watch out: there is no platform guarantee, so complete the deal through a platform with protection and keep records of the conversation and payment.

## How to Check Part Authenticity and Condition Like an Experienced Builder

On any used brick trading platform, the biggest fear is paying genuine prices for imitation parts. Check the seller, the photos, and the parts themselves from several angles:

| Item | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Part markings | Genuine parts usually carry a brand mark or part number; compare them carefully with known references. |
| Clutch feel | Genuine parts should have moderate grip; imitations are often too loose or too tight. |
| Color and finish | Genuine colors should be clean and consistent; imitations often look off-color or show flow lines. |
| Condition | Yellowing, scratches, and visible wear should lower the price as used parts. |
| Printed parts | Crisp printing with no ghosting is the baseline for a correct match. |

Condition is the heart of a second-hand part. For the same part number, a new piece and a 90% new piece can differ sharply in price. Before ordering, ask the seller for clear photos and a close-up video, especially for expensive parts, printed elements, and large lots. If a listing only shows a distant group photo, ask for individual shots of the studs, underside, and any worn areas.

## Transaction Safety Guide for Buyers and Sellers

Second-hand deals go wrong when one side skips basic protections. The rules are simple:

### For Buyers

1. Choose sellers with strong reputations, real reviews, and actual photos of the parts.
2. Use platform escrow for larger orders and refuse private transfers.
3. Unpack and inspect the order as soon as it arrives; open a case promptly if something is wrong.
4. Treat *too cheap to be true* listings with suspicion; they are often scams or lots with missing key parts.

### For Sellers

1. Describe condition honestly to avoid after-delivery price haggling.
2. Photograph the order before shipping and record the part count and condition.
3. **Pack to a standard**: bag loose parts separately and fill gaps so nothing shifts in transit.
4. Insure valuable shipments and keep the tracking number and shipping receipt.

## Packing Standards: The Details That Decide the Deal

Many new sellers lose on packing. Loose parts should be sorted into sealable bags by type before they go into the box, and gaps should be filled with bubble wrap or newspaper. Complete sets need extra reinforcement around the original box. A strong carton and careful separation prevent shipping damage and make a much better impression when the buyer opens the parcel. For high-value parts, add an inner bag and extra cushioning so the contents cannot move even if the outer box is bumped.
