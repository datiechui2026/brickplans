# DCMOC Blueprint Review: Batman Brick Models Build Guide

## 1. Product Overview

This review covers a DCMOC blueprint collection shared in the BrickPlans community: a Batman-themed series from an experienced MOC designer. It spans the character's most recognizable looks and locations, from the classic comic-book Batman to the Dark Knight film version. Batman has always been one of the most popular subjects in the MOC scene, and these plans translate the gritty Gotham guardian into brick form convincingly.

The collection contains six separate models: the Batmobile, the Batwing, a Batcave diorama, a classic comic Batman minifigure, a Dark Knight Batman, and a Joker minifigure. Every model comes with a detailed part list and step-by-step building instructions in clean PDF format that is easy to read on a tablet or desktop.

As a whole, this DCMOC set is genuinely friendly to newcomers. Starting with the small minifigures delivers a quick sense of achievement, while the Batcave offers plenty of headroom for advanced builders. The difficulty gradient is well judged, and the recommended order is minifigures → Batmobile → Batwing → Batcave, working up step by step.

## 3. Parts Review

The parts palette leans heavily on black and dark grey, which suits Batman's dark aesthetic perfectly. Most elements are common, and both Chinese third-party brick brands and genuine LEGO parts are compatible. A few points are worth flagging before you order:

1. **Rare parts:** the curved trans-clear panels used in the Batcave, along with several dark blue slopes, are hard to come by. Source them in advance on BrickLink or Taobao.
2. **Stickers vs. printed parts:** the Batman logo and the minifigure faces use printed parts, so no sticker sheet is required. That deserves credit.
3. **Substitutions:** the designer helpfully lists alternative part numbers for some elements, which greatly reduces the difficulty of sourcing.

## 4. Building Experience

In practice, the instruction quality here is satisfying. Each step highlights the parts added in a contrasting colour, so you never hit that "where is this piece supposed to go?" moment. The Batmobile's chassis structure is particularly clever: technic beams and pins build a solid suspension system, and the finished car rolls smoothly with plenty of play value.

The Batwing uses a modular approach, with the left and right wings built symmetrically. That cuts down on repetitive work while keeping the structure rigid. The real standout is the Batcave: multi-layer stacking and sideways building techniques create a convincing sense of depth and stratification inside a limited footprint, and the light bricks are the finishing touch.

On the minifigure side, the armour texture of the Dark Knight Batman is achieved through SNOT (studs-not-on-top) building, with very high fidelity to the source. The Joker's purple suit and exaggerated grin are captured just as neatly.

## 5. The Finished Models

The completed models look excellent in a display cabinet. The Batmobile measures roughly 35cm long and 12cm wide, with proportions comparable to official set 76139 but a more aggressive silhouette. The Batwing reaches a 48cm wingspan and is genuinely striking when hung on display. The Batcave measures 25cm × 20cm × 18cm and packs in interactive details including a batcomputer, a weapon rack and criminal case files, making it more playable than expected.

## 6. Verdict

**Pros:**
- Highly original design, faithful shapes with creative interpretation
- Clear instructions that are friendly to beginners
- A sensible difficulty gradient covering builders of different levels
- Good parts commonality with well-documented substitutions

**Cons:**
- The Batcave part list omits the part number for the light bricks, so you have to work it out yourself
- A few steps use camera angles that are skewed enough that the 3D rotate preview is worth using alongside them

For Batman fans and brick MOC builders alike, this DCMOC set is a strong pick. Whether you want to expand a DC-themed collection or push into more advanced building techniques, these blueprints deliver a substantial and enjoyable build. If DCMOC plans interest you, the Batman series is a solid place to start your MOC journey.
