# 5 Best LEGO MOC Plan Websites to Bookmark in 2026

![Finished brick model built from online MOC plans](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-plans-website-recommend/moc-plans-website-recommend-1.jpg)

A pile of plan sites has appeared over the past couple of years, and more choice has actually made picking harder. Your bookmarks bar fills up, but you keep going back to the same handful. This 2026 roundup of MOC plan websites keeps only five, all of them sites I use myself and have kept saved, so you can skip the filtering work.

## 1. brickplan (brickplan.cn) — the go-to for Chinese-language MOC plans

![Step-by-step build sequence from a MOC plan](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-plans-website-recommend/moc-plans-website-recommend-2.jpg)

**Why it makes the list: Chinese interface, a huge free plan library, an active community**

[brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn) is a newer brick MOC plan sharing platform that launched in 2025. After more than a year of growth it has built up thousands of high-quality original plans. As a latecomer in the MOC plan space, brickplan.cn has some clear advantages:

- **A fully Chinese-language interface**, so there is no language barrier
- **Clear plan categories** covering more than ten types, including architecture, military, vehicles, street scenes and mechs
- **High-resolution images and PDF plan downloads**
- **An active community**, with comments, likes and favourites all in place
- **Creator-friendly**: uploading a plan is simple and copyright protection is solid

If you only want to bookmark one Chinese-language brick plan site, brickplan.cn deserves the top slot.

## 2. Rebrickable — the largest MOC plan database in the world

**Why it makes the list: the biggest plan count, automatic parts lists**

Rebrickable is widely regarded as the number one plan platform in the international MOC scene, with more than 100,000 plans. Its standout feature is **automatic parts list generation** — you pick a plan and the system lists every required part, then tells you which of them you can pull from sets you already own.

For Chinese-speaking builders, though, the all-English interface and the partly paid plans are the main hurdles.

## 3. MocBay — a platform built around creator monetisation

**Why it makes the list: high-quality original plans, income protection for creators**

MocBay focuses on MOC plan transactions, and the quality of the plans on it is generally high. Creators can sell their own plans there, and the platform provides thorough copyright protection and a payment system.

If you are after high-quality MOC plans and are willing to pay for good content, MocBay is a solid choice.

## 4. BrickLink Studio — design and sharing in one place

**Why it makes the list: built-in design software, a rich plan library**

BrickLink Studio is not just a plan sharing platform — it also offers Studio 2.0, a free brick design program. You can design a MOC in the software and upload it to the platform to share in one step.

For builders who like designing their own MOCs, BrickLink Studio is the best option. The same language barrier applies here, though.

## 5. Baidu Tieba / brick hobbyist forums — the veteran plan-sharing communities

![Close-up of part detail in a MOC plan](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-plans-website-recommend/moc-plans-website-recommend-3.jpg)

**Why it makes the list: free, down to earth, full of experienced builders**

Baidu Tieba and the various brick forums may look a little dated, but they are still good places to find free plans. Many experienced builders share their own designs there and answer newcomers' questions enthusiastically.

The downside is that plans are not organised systematically, download links often go dead, and you have to spend time sorting through it yourself.

## Where MOC plan sites are heading in 2026

### More AI-assisted design

As AI technology develops, more and more plan sites are integrating AI-assisted design features to help builders generate plans quickly.

### Community-driven operation becomes the norm

Plain plan downloads no longer satisfy builders, and platforms with community features are more popular. brickplan.cn performs particularly well here: users can swap build tips and like each other's work on the platform.

### Domestic platforms on the rise

In the past, Chinese builders could only rely on overseas sites to find plans. Now domestic platforms like [brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn) are growing fast and offering better service to local builders.
