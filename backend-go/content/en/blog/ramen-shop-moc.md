# Ramen Shop MOC Review: Japanese Street Corner in 950 Pieces

The short version: this ramen shop MOC squeezes a Japanese cloth noren, a cooking stove and a shop sign onto a single 16x32 stud street baseplate. The detail density is high, the build difficulty is friendly, and it works well as an entry point into a street-scene collection.

## 1. Exterior Design

The shopfront is a classic small Japanese street business. The blue noren curtain is made from real fabric and can be flipped up out of the way. A red lantern and a wooden signboard hang at the entrance, both rendered with printed bricks rather than stickers. The kitchen is the star of the build: the stove top holds a soup pot and ramen bowls, and the bowls even contain noodles built from yellow pieces. The half-curtains under the eaves, the little cat on the windowsill and the lantern by the door all add up to a warm, lived-in feel.

What sells the scene is restraint. Nothing on the facade is oversized or busy for the sake of it. The wood tones, the red accents and the fabric noren do the work, and the result reads as a working shop rather than a display prop.

## 2. Structural Stability

The shop uses a modular layout, so the second-floor attic and the ground-floor shopfront can be separated and handled on their own. Walls are built from standard bricks locked in alternating directions, and the noren rod is held by a thin bar so the curtain does not fall off. The stove and counter are fixed to the floor by stud connections, which means they stay put during play. The whole thing is compact enough to pick up with one hand, so it sits comfortably on a desk.

## 3. Playability and Value

The roof lifts off as a single piece to reveal a small bedroom on the second floor. The pot lid on the stove opens, and inside is a hidden bowl of "special ramen." The full model is about 950 pieces. Sourcing the parts against the brickplan.cn parts list costs roughly 350 RMB, and the build takes around 3 hours. As a supplement to an existing street scene, that price is a good deal.
