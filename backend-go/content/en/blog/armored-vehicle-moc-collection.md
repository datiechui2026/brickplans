# MOC Armored Vehicle Plans Review: Scout Car vs 1:35 MBT

Short answer first: brickplan carries more than 20 brick-built armored vehicle plans, and two of them stand out — the wheeled armored scout car and the 1:35 main battle tank. The scout car runs about 1,500 parts and a six-hour build; the tank runs about 3,200 parts and roughly 14 hours. Both end up far more poseable and drivable than retail sets at the same price.

## Exterior Accuracy

Military MOCs live or die on accuracy, and these two hold up. The scout car uses eight-wheel independent suspension, with the sloped hull armor built up from stacked wedge pieces rather than flat plates. Its turret rotates a full 360 degrees. The main battle tank runs working tracks, with correctly proportioned idler wheels and road wheels, plus headlights, antennae and tool boxes. A armor enthusiast can identify the vehicle type at a glance.

## Structural Rigidity and Play Features

Both chassis use Technic cross axles as the load-bearing frame, so they hold together during rough off-road handling. On the wheeled vehicle the steering linkage moves smoothly and the gun barrel elevates and depresses. On the tank the track drive runs cleanly — push it along and the turret rotates in linked motion. The gear-ratio notes included in the brickplan instructions are what make that drivetrain work; the dynamic result is genuinely surprising for a brick model.

## Cost Comparison

Download the plans from brickplan, buy from the parts list, and the scout car comes to roughly CNY 250, the tank to about CNY 500. Finished sets of comparable size generally sell for CNY 800 and up. The plans also include step-by-step instructions and part numbers, so sourcing is straightforward, and if you misplace a piece mid-build you can trace it back against the numbered steps.

## Who Each Plan Suits

New builders should start with the 1,500-part scout car. Experienced builders can go straight at the 3,200-part main battle tank. Both plans offer two color schemes — desert yellow and jungle green — and once the model is finished you can repaint or rework the camouflage to your own taste.
