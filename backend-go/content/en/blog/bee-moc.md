# Brick Bee MOC Review: 380 Pieces, 9/10 Accuracy

The short answer up front: this brick bee MOC performs well above its price point. For under 100 yuan you get roughly 380 pieces and a worker bee that genuinely looks alive, plus about 1.5 hours of building. I'd score the accuracy 9/10. It makes an excellent entry-level animal build and I'd happily recommend it.

## 1. Accuracy to the Real Thing

The standout feature here is how closely the designer followed actual bee anatomy. Curved bricks in alternating black and yellow outline the striped abdomen, the transparent pieces standing in for wings are genuinely crystal clear, and the small hexagonal honeycomb base ties the whole composition together so it reads as a finished scene rather than a loose model. The compound eyes are built from two dark round bricks stacked on each other, and the antennae curve at a natural angle. Walk around it and it keeps working from every side — the posture suggests a bee that has just landed and is about to start foraging.

The honeycomb base is doing quiet heavy lifting. It's a small build, but it gives the bee scale and context, which is why the model photographs so well next to flower or garden builds.

## 2. The Build Experience

The construction moves in clear layers across four stages: legs, torso, wings, and the honeycomb base. The instructions split the steps sensibly, averaging just two to three pieces per step, so there are almost no awkward moments where you realize you have to backtrack and pull something apart. That fine-grained pacing is what makes the model friendly to newer builders.

You can finish the whole thing in about 1.5 hours. An adult can sit down and complete it in a single session, and because the steps are so granular it also works well as a parent-and-child project — there's enough variety between the legs, the body, and the wings to keep a younger builder engaged without overwhelming them.

## 3. Part Quality

Clutch power is tight and consistent. The friction when pushing pieces together sits right in the comfortable middle range, and the finished model holds together when you pick it up and turn it around — nothing slumps or drifts. Surfaces are flat with no flash or burring, the colors are highly saturated, and the boundary between the printed black and yellow sections is clean and even.

The one small complaint is the wing connector, which feels a bit thin. If you plan to fold or reposition the wings regularly, handle them gently. For normal display purposes, though, it's a non-issue.

## 4. Value for Money

At roughly 88 yuan for 380 pieces, you're paying about 0.23 yuan per part — comfortably in the upper-middle range for animal brick builds. Given the quality of the design and how faithfully it reproduces the subject, that isn't expensive. If you already own a beehive or garden themed scene, this bee MOC works as a real finishing touch, the small detail that makes the larger display feel inhabited.
