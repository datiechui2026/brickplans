# Owl MOC Review: 640-Piece Big-Eyed Owl Build and Display

Short version first: this owl MOC is the personality piece of the animal-MOC shelf. Around 640 parts, a finished model about 15 cm tall, 24 yuan for the instructions and roughly 118 yuan for the parts if you buy them loose — about 3 hours from sorting tray to display. The signature oversized eyes land at around 90% of the reference look, the feathers use a layered build that reads as genuinely three-dimensional, and the stance is stable. On a shelf it has real presence.

## 1. Capturing the Look: Big Eyes and Face Structure

Everything about an owl lives in the eyes, and this build gets them right. A transparent hemisphere sits over a black round plate, with a ring of yellow parts around it standing in for the iris — that wide-eyed, unblinking stare comes through at roughly 90% fidelity. The facial disc is formed from curved pieces arranged in a circle, and the ear tufts are two triangular parts standing upright. The light brown and dark brown plumage shifts gradually rather than jumping between shades, so the whole face reads as a real owl rather than a parts assembly.

## 2. The Build: Feather Layering and Stability

The feathers are the main event. On the back, curved pieces are stacked in layers, working from light to dark to create the wing's depth, and the wings themselves attach on hinge parts so they can be spread slightly. The feet connect to the body through ball joints, and together with the wide tail as a third point of support, the model stands firmly without a base. The instructions are split into 9 steps, each kept to 12 parts or fewer, which is why the whole thing comes together in about 3 hours.

The one real difficulty is alignment: each feather layer has to seat properly on its studs. Don't rush that section — line up a layer, press it down fully, then move on. Skipping ahead here is what turns a clean build into a wobbly one.

## 3. Value: Instructions and Parts Cost

The instructions cost 24 yuan. Sourcing the loose parts from the BrickPlan parts list runs about 118 yuan, so a full build comes to roughly 142 yuan — around 0.22 yuan per part. Brown is one of the most common colours in any collection, so if your stock bins are well supplied you can push the cost down to about 90 yuan. Given the character of the finished model and the time you spend building it, that is fair value.
