# SEMBO vs Double Eagle vs Gobricks: Which Should You Buy?

## Product Overview

In recent years, domestic brick brands have grown quickly, and SEMBO, Double Eagle, and Gobricks have become three of the mainstream names. Each brand has a distinct personality, their prices remain friendly, and their parts are compatible with LEGO standards, which makes them popular with MOC builders. On the [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) community, instruction downloads and discussion activity for these three brands stay in the top three all year. The three are often discussed together because they overlap in price and compatibility, but they do not serve the same builder in the same way. This comparison reviews part quality, build difficulty, instruction clarity, finished models, and recommended use cases so you can match a brand to the kind of build you actually want to make.

## Build Difficulty

- **SEMBO**: ★★☆☆☆
- **Double Eagle**: ★★★☆☆
- **Gobricks**: ★★★★☆

SEMBO sets are mostly low to medium difficulty and give beginners a comfortable entry point. Double Eagle's technic series requires some patience and is a better fit for builders with experience. Gobricks MOC sets are more complex, and some large models are aimed at advanced builders who want a serious challenge. The star ratings are relative: a two-star SEMBO set can still take time, while a four-star Gobricks build may demand careful sorting and step-by-step checking. Beginners should weight instruction clarity and part count heavily; experienced builders can accept more complex subassemblies and longer sessions. Choose based on the project in front of you, not just the brand name.

## Part Review

**SEMBO** parts feel solid overall. Injection gates are cleanly finished and clutch is moderate, though some specialty pieces show slight color variation. That makes SEMBO a strong match for street scenes and architecture MOCs, where large visible surfaces and consistent color matter, but where a few specialty parts can be placed out of direct view if needed.

**Double Eagle** stands out in technic parts. Gears, axles, pins, and other transmission pieces are precise and run smoothly, making it a first choice for mechanical MOCs. If your model needs motion, gear linkage, or repeated play, this is the brand to check first. It is especially relevant for vehicle and construction machinery builds.

**Gobricks** is known for high precision. Part surfaces are glossy and clutch is tight, with a feel close to, or even beyond, original LEGO. For builders who care about tight seams, clean edges, and a refined touch, Gobricks is the natural pick. It suits display models and complex MOCs where the finish is part of the appeal. When judging parts, check injection marks, color consistency, and clutch; for moving models, pay extra attention to gears and pins under load.

## Building Experience

SEMBO instructions use color printing with clear steps, but some complex structures are split into diagrams that are not detailed enough. Double Eagle instructions are mostly electronic PDFs, and [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) has many compatible Double Eagle MOC instructions covering tanks, race cars, construction machinery, and other categories. Gobricks instructions are mostly player-made MOC instructions with a professional edge, so read all steps carefully before you start. Overall, SEMBO is the most relaxed build, Double Eagle delivers the most sense of achievement, and Gobricks tests your patience the most. If you like printed pages beside you, SEMBO has an edge; if you prefer working from a screen and searching a large instruction library, Double Eagle and BrickPlans are a practical pairing; if you want a serious MOC project, Gobricks gives you the challenge. A PDF can be zoomed and searched, while printed steps are easier to keep open on a desk.

## Finished Model Showcase

SEMBO finished models are known for refined looks and work well as display pieces. Double Eagle models have strong posability; technic sets in particular offer rich play through gear linkage. Gobricks models have stable structures and rich detail that hold up to close or distant viewing. In other words, SEMBO leans toward shelf presence, Double Eagle leans toward movement and play, and Gobricks leans toward structural quality and detail. If you plan to rebuild or modify a model, stable structure matters; if you plan to display it, surface finish matters. Whichever brand you choose, you can find matching instructions and building tutorials on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn).

## Recommendation

- **Beginner entry**: SEMBO ★★★★★ | Double Eagle ★★★☆☆ | Gobricks ★★☆☆☆
- **Technic and mechanical**: SEMBO ★★☆☆☆ | Double Eagle ★★★★★ | Gobricks ★★★★☆
- **Display pieces**: SEMBO ★★★★☆ | Double Eagle ★★★☆☆ | Gobricks ★★★★★

**Bottom line**: There is no absolute best brand, only the brand that fits you best. Beginners should start with SEMBO, technic fans should look first at Double Eagle, and builders who want maximum precision should choose Gobricks. Use the part count ranges as a planning guide: SEMBO covers smaller and mid-sized projects, Double Eagle reaches into larger technic builds, and Gobricks offers the widest span from small MOC parts packs to very large models.
