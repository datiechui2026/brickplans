# Build a Subway Car MOC: Folding Doors and Handrail Interior

Bottom line first: roughly 900 parts and about 4 hours gets you a modern subway car MOC that measures 38cm long. The bi-fold doors open and close by hand, and the interior comes fully fitted with handrail rings, seats and a stop-announcement screen. It pairs perfectly with a city street scene.

Subways are the workhorse of modern city commuting, and recreating a car in brick is rewarding whether you park it beside a street layout or display it on its own shelf. This BrickPlan plan is built around interior detail, with the bi-fold doors and handrail rings as the two headline features.

## Step 1: Gather the parts

The car body is mainly white and silver-grey with a blue waistline stripe for contrast. Core parts: one 6x32 car floor plate, four door panels (1x6 plates), eight hinge pieces, ten transparent window elements, twelve handrail ring elements and eight seat elements. Total cost comes to around 200 yuan.

The hinge pieces and transparent elements are the priciest items in the list, so a city-themed set already in your collection can save you a fair amount on basic bricks.

## Step 2: Build the car skeleton

Wall the floor plate in with bricks around the perimeter, leaving openings on both sides for the doors and windows. Cover the roof with two large 6x16 plates, leaving a gap in the middle for the air-conditioning vent.

Building the skeleton first is the smart move: once the frame is standing, the interior and the outer walls can be worked on separately, which is far more efficient than trying to do both at once.

## Step 3: Make the bi-fold doors

This is the cleverest structure on the whole car. Each door leaf uses two 1x6 plates, anchored to either side of the door frame with hinge pieces. When closed, the two leaves meet in the middle; when open, they fold back flat against the walls on both sides.

In testing the action runs smoothly, and the gap stays within one stud, so the look is very close to the real thing.

## Step 4: Install the handrail and interior fittings

Mount a handrail ring element on the inside of the roof every 2 studs, connected with 1x1 transparent bars, with the height set so the rings sit just above a minifigure's head. Place four seats along each side of the car, and add a horizontal grab bar above each row.

Finish the interior with the printed stop-announcement screen element and a route-map sticker above the doors, and the whole thing suddenly comes to life.

## Step 5: Driver's cab and finishing touches

Build the driver's cab at one end of the car, separated with a transparent screen panel, and fit a steering wheel element and console inside. The finished model is 38cm long and 6 studs wide. Combined with the BrickPlan subway platform plan, it makes a complete miniature subway scene.

## FAQ

**How much does a subway car MOC cost?**

Loose parts come to roughly 200 yuan, with the hinge pieces and transparent elements accounting for most of it. If you already own city sets, you can reuse more than half of the basic bricks, which brings the real cost down.

**How do you keep the bi-fold doors opening smoothly?**

The key is to mount the hinge pieces symmetrically and keep the door leaves a uniform 1 stud thick. Once assembled, open and close them by hand a few times to bed them in. If the action feels stiff, put a single drop of lubricant on the hinge pin.

**Can the doors be motorised?**

Yes, but that requires adding a small motor and a linkage, and interior space is limited, so it eats into the detailing. Beginners should build the manual version first and consider a powered conversion once they are comfortable with the design.

**Is this subway car MOC suitable for beginners?**

Yes. The structure is mostly symmetrical assembly with no complex mechanisms, and it takes around 4 hours. It is simpler than a train MOC and makes a great first interior-focused build.

**How is it different from a regular train set?**

Regular train sets are all about running on track. This subway car MOC is about static interior accuracy: the bi-fold doors, handrail rings and announcement screen are details standard sets rarely attempt, so the shelf presence is completely different.
