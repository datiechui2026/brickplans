# How to Repair LEGO Parts: Cracks, Yellowing and Loose Clutch

The short answer first: how you repair a LEGO part depends on what is wrong with it. Cracks get glued, yellowing gets reversed with hydrogen peroxide, and loose clutch power gets thickened. About 90% of old parts can be fixed at home, at under 2 yuan per piece.

To actually answer the question, separate the three common types of damage and treat each one on its own terms.

**Step 1: Repairing cracked parts.** For a light crack, use model glue or an ABS-specific adhesive and run a thin layer along the crack, then clamp the part and hold it for 24 hours — the strength comes back. For a part that has snapped fully in two, align the broken faces first, then apply the glue; once it has cured, sand the joint flush. Load-bearing parts are better replaced outright, because a glued joint only reaches about 70% of the original strength.

**Step 2: Dealing with yellowed, discolored parts.** Put the yellowed parts in a container, pour in 3% hydrogen peroxide, and leave them in direct sunlight for 6 to 8 hours. Take them out, rinse and dry, and they will be noticeably whiter. White and light gray parts respond best, with roughly 80% of the original color restored. Dark-colored parts are not recommended for this treatment. A bottle of hydrogen peroxide costs about 10 yuan and can be reused many times.

**Step 3: Solving loose connections.** Parts loosen up because the studs wear down. Paint a thin coat of clear nail polish or a dedicated thickening agent onto the studs, let it dry for 30 minutes, and the clutch feels tight again. Treating one part costs about 0.5 yuan, which is far cheaper than spending 50 yuan on new parts — a 2,000-piece old set can save you over a hundred yuan this way.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is the strongest way to repair a cracked LEGO part?**
A: Use an ABS-specific adhesive, apply a thin layer along the crack, and clamp it for 24 hours. The bond reaches about 70% of the original part's strength. For key structural or load-bearing parts, just buy a replacement — decorative parts repaired this way show no visible difference in appearance.

**Q: Can yellowed LEGO parts get their original color back?**
A: Yes. Soak them in 3% hydrogen peroxide and leave them in sunlight for 6 to 8 hours. White and light gray parts recover visibly, at roughly 80% of the original color. Dark parts are not recommended: results are poor and the surface gloss can be damaged.

**Q: What is the cheapest fix for loose parts?**
A: Paint a thin coat of clear nail polish or thickening agent on the studs and let it dry for 30 minutes. It costs about 0.5 yuan per part. Compared with buying new parts, restoring a 2,000-piece old set this way saves over a hundred yuan — the best value option available.

**Q: Can repaired parts still be built with normally?**
A: Yes. Glued parts are strong enough for everyday handling once cured, and thickened parts have clutch power close to new. As long as you don't use repaired pieces in structures that get repeatedly disassembled under load, they work perfectly well.

**Q: Is DIY LEGO part repair worth the effort?**
A: Yes. Tools and materials cost under 50 yuan in total and can fix well over a hundred parts, averaging below 2 yuan per piece. New parts from a bulk parts order run about 0.5 to 2 yuan each, so restoring an old set end to end saves more than half the cost.
