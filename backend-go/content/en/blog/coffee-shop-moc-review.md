Here is the short version: this brick coffee shop MOC runs about 2,100 pieces, measures 32 studs wide and roughly 26 cm tall, costs around 299 yuan, and takes about six hours to build. It is worth picking up, and it earns an overall 8.5/10. ## Exterior: The Storefront Is the Highlight

The dark green awning paired with a gold signboard is the first thing you notice. The signboard is a printed element, so there is no sticker to align and it looks right straight out of the bag. The display window uses clear panels to create a large pane of glass, and a cake display stand sits behind it. A small chalkboard hangs on the side of the storefront, lettered with the daily special.

The color scheme leans retro European: cream white walls with dark brown door frames. That palette slots in cleanly next to most street scenes rather than fighting them.

Exterior score: 9/10.

## Interior: More Detail Than You Can See at a Glance

The ground floor is the counter and work area. You get an espresso machine, a grinder and a cash register, with two bar stools on the customer side of the counter. The second floor is the seating area, furnished with two table-and-chair sets, a small bookshelf and a wall-mounted picture element.

The nicest surprise is the back kitchen, which goes as far as including a coffee bean roaster.

Interior score: 8.5/10. The one real weakness is that the upper floor has no separate staircase, so you have to lift the roof off to place minifigures.

## Value: Above Average

At 299 yuan for roughly 2,100 pieces, the cost works out to about 0.14 yuan per piece, which is below the average for domestic street-scene sets. There are six printed elements and clear pieces make up around 15% of the parts, so there is no sticker anxiety. Compared with other street scenes at the same price point, this one packs in a higher density of interior detail.

The downside is that no lighting elements are included with the base, so any night scene effect is a DIY job.

Value score: 8/10.

All told, this coffee shop MOC suits street-scene builders and coffee lovers alike; it works both as a corner store and as a standalone display piece. If you want to see more instructions from the same series, search for the coffee shop street scene on brickplan.cn.
