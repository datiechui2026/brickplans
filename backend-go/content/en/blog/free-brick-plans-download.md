## Product Overview

Downloading free brick building instructions has become one of the main ways builders find their next project. Free instructions cover everything from simple mini models to complex architectural scenes, so there is something for every skill level.

## Build Difficulty

Free brick instructions come in a wide spread of difficulty levels, which is part of why they suit so many different builders:

- **Beginner friendly (★★☆☆☆)**: mini buildings, small animals, simple mechanisms. 30–150 parts, 1–2 hours of building time.
- **Intermediate challenge (★★★☆☆)**: medium buildings and vehicle models. 150–400 parts, and you need basic brick building skills.
- **Advanced (★★★★☆)**: large buildings and complex mechanical structures. 400–800 parts, and you need patience and spatial awareness.
- **Expert level (★★★★★)**: very large scenes and models with moving parts. 800–1500 parts, and these are the standout pieces among free instructions.

Beginners are best off starting with entry-level plans, building up experience gradually before taking on harder builds.

## Parts Review

Free brick instructions tend to share a few characteristics when it comes to the parts they call for:

- **Common parts dominate**: designers of free instructions try to stick to common part types so that builders can finish the model with bricks they already own.
- **Recommended brands**: Gobricks offers the best value for money, with part precision and feel close to LEGO at a much lower price. SEMBO has a wide range of special parts, which suits instructions that need distinctive shaped pieces. LEGO parts cost more but their quality is hard to fault.
- **Part count range**: free instructions usually call for between 30 and 800 parts, and some large plans go past 1000.
- **Colour choices**: colour schemes in free instructions are usually fairly conservative and built around basic colours, which makes it easier to gather all the parts you need.
