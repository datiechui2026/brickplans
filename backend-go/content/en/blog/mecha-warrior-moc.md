# How to Build a Mecha Warrior MOC: Skeleton to Weapons

The short answer: you do not need expensive technic parts to build a mecha warrior MOC. With a two-layer "skeleton plus armor" structure, about 320 ordinary bricks and roughly 2 hours of building, you can finish a mecha that stands 25 cm tall and has 12 movable joints. The plans are available for download at brickplan.cn.

**Step 1: Build the leg skeleton.** Use 2x2 bricks and hinge pieces to form the thighs and lower legs. Add an angle bracket at the knee so the bend can reach 90 degrees or more. Widen the feet by stacking three 1x4 plates, which keeps the mecha standing steady instead of tipping forward or backward.

**Step 2: Build the torso core.** Connect the upper and lower body at the waist with a turntable piece so the mecha can rotate left and right. Lay 1x6 bricks horizontally across the chest to form the armor band, and leave two stud connections on the back so you can later mount thrusters or a backpack.

**Step 3: Build the arms and shoulder armor.** Attach the shoulders with ball joint pieces. Split each arm into three sections: upper arm, elbow and forearm. Use a toothed clutch piece at the elbow so poses hold firm instead of sagging. Angle the shoulder armor with slope bricks for a stronger, more powerful silhouette.

**Step 4: Build the head and weapon.** Make the head from 1x1 bricks with transparent red pieces for the monitor eyes. For the weapon, a long spear works well: place three 1x8 rods side by side and cap them with a muzzle piece. At about 18 cm long, it scales correctly against the 25 cm mecha body.

Finally, assemble all the sections and check how tight each joint feels. Any joint that is too loose can be shimmed with a 0.5 mm thin plate to add friction. If you want a higher-fidelity result, search "mecha" on brickplan.cn for more color schemes and parts lists, such as a gray main body with orange accents on the chest energy core.

## FAQ

**Q: How do you build a mecha warrior MOC?**

A: Build in the order of leg skeleton, torso, arms, then head and weapon, doing the skeleton first and the armor afterward. Beginners should start with the legs, because the leg structure is simple and builds confidence quickly. Once you are comfortable, the whole mecha takes about 2 hours.

**Q: Can you build a mecha with ordinary bricks?**

A: Yes. Mecha MOC joints rely mainly on hinge pieces, turntable pieces and ball joints, all of which are very common parts. You only need about 320 pieces, including 6 ball joints and 2 turntables, so an ordinary brick set can be broken down and rebuilt into one.

**Q: Is it worth paying for mecha MOC plans?**

A: Yes. Good plans list the part number and quantity for every piece, so you do not buy the wrong parts. Mecha plans on brickplan.cn also include step-by-step build order diagrams and multi-angle photos of the finished model, which saves about half the time compared with squinting at blurry finished-build photos online and guessing.

**Q: What is the difference between a mecha MOC and a Gundam model?**

A: A mecha MOC is built from bricks, so it can be disassembled and modified repeatedly and the parts can be reused. A Gundam model is a snap-fit kit whose parts cannot be taken apart again. Mecha MOC puts more emphasis on creativity and playability, and the cost is considerably lower, since one set of parts can build several different forms.
