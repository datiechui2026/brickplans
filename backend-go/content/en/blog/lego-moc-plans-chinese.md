# LEGO MOC Instructions in Chinese: Top Platforms, Free Downloads

![Finished model built from Chinese-language LEGO MOC instructions](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/lego-moc-plans-chinese/lego-moc-plans-chinese-1.jpg)

Finding MOC instructions in Chinese has long been the sticking point for Chinese-speaking builders: the major international platforms run entirely in English, right down to the captions, and plenty of people close the tab as soon as they open it. In reality the Chinese-language scene has grown a lot over the past two years — it is just scattered. Here is a walkthrough of Chinese-friendly platforms, free download channels, and how to actually use them, in order.

## The instruction problem Chinese-speaking builders run into

![Build steps from a Chinese-language LEGO MOC instruction set](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/lego-moc-plans-chinese/lego-moc-plans-chinese-2.jpg)

### Language is the biggest barrier

Mainstream international instruction platforms such as Rebrickable and Bricklink are English-only: the notes, part names and building steps are all in English. For builders who are not comfortable reading English, that adds real learning overhead. Demand for Chinese-language MOC instructions keeps growing as a result.

### Parts sourcing is unclear

The parts recommended by many international plans are hard to buy domestically, or simply cost too much. Pairing a plan with a compatible-brick alternative lets you swap expensive LEGO parts for domestic equivalents and cut the cost substantially.

### Localized community support is thin

Discussions, tutorials and Q&A in English-language communities are awkward for Chinese-speaking users to take part in. Domestic builders want an MOC community they can talk to in Chinese.

## The best Chinese-friendly instruction platforms

### 1. brickplan.cn — the go-to Chinese MOC library

**brickplan.cn** is the most active brick instruction-sharing community in China, and it is built specifically for Chinese-speaking users:

- **Fully Chinese interface**: browsing, search, downloads and comments are all in Chinese
- **A large Chinese-language library**: many plans come with Chinese building notes
- **LEGO instructions supported**: each plan lists compatibility information, so you can see at a glance whether it works with LEGO bricks
- **A dedicated free download section** for LEGO MOC instructions
- **Localized parts lists**: parts lists carry LEGO part numbers plus recommended compatible-brand alternatives

### 2. Rebrickable (with a translation tool)

Rebrickable is an English platform, but it is usable alongside a browser translation extension. Its strengths:

- The largest instruction library in the world (over 100,000 plans)
- You can filter for plans you can build from the parts you already own
- Designer standards are generally high

One caveat: most plans on Rebrickable are designed around LEGO, so if you want to build with alternative bricks you have to work out the substitutions yourself.

### 3. The Bilibili MOC creator community

Bilibili hosts a large number of quality MOC tutorials and instruction shares, and many creators offer free downloads. Searching for Chinese-language LEGO MOC instructions turns up plenty of material. The downside is that plans are scattered across individual creators with no central platform managing them.

## Free LEGO MOC instruction download channels

### Completely free sources

1. **The brickplan.cn free section**: a large volume of high-quality free LEGO MOC instruction downloads across many themes
2. **Official LEGO free instructions**: LEGO's own site publishes free PDF building instructions for some official models
3. **Designers' personal sites**: many MOC designers share instructions for free on their own websites or social media

### The part-free, part-paid model

Most platforms run a freemium model: basic plans are free, premium plans are paid. On brickplan.cn you can download free plans first to test the water, then decide whether a paid plan is worth it once you have checked the quality.

## Compatible bricks that lower MOC costs

![Close-up of bricks from a Chinese-language LEGO MOC plan](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/lego-moc-plans-chinese/lego-moc-plans-chinese-3.jpg)

### Why use compatible bricks?

LEGO parts are expensive — the parts for a single medium-to-large MOC can run past a thousand yuan. A compatible-brick approach saves 50%–70% of that cost.

### Recommended compatible brands

1. **Gobricks** — the closest in quality to LEGO, with very high precision and moderate clutch
2. **Double Eagle** — excellent technic parts, and motors with outstanding value for money
3. **SEMBO** — a rich supply of minifigures and scene-building parts
4. **Qiji** — stable quality on basic bricks at a friendly price

### Compatibility notes

Most domestic brick brands are fully compatible with LEGO, but they can differ in a few areas:

- **Clutch**: some brands run tight or loose, so pay attention while building
- **Color**: the same color can vary slightly between brands
- **Special parts**: a few complex parts may only exist from LEGO

On brickplan.cn, plans usually list the recommended parts brands, which helps you choose the right compatible bricks.

## How to build an MOC from Chinese instructions

### Step 1: choose the right plan

On brickplan.cn you can filter plans by theme, difficulty and part count. Beginners should pick a medium-difficulty plan in the 200–500 part range.

### Step 2: gather the parts

Working from the parts list in the plan, you can:

- Use parts already in your own stock
- Buy a parts pack on brickplan.cn
- Substitute domestic compatible bricks

### Step 3: build step by step

The advantage of Chinese-language instructions is that every step comes with clear written notes; combined with 3D renders, you can build easily even without a video tutorial.

### Step 4: share and discuss

When the model is finished, share your build in the brickplan.cn community and swap notes with other builders. Many designers refine their plans based on builder feedback, which feeds a healthy community cycle.

## Hands-on experience with alternative-brick MOCs

Many builders worry that alternative bricks will hurt the final result. In practice, as long as you choose the right brands, an alternative-brick MOC meets the bar completely. A few practical tips:

- **Display models**: use basic bricks from Gobricks or Qiji — the result is almost indistinguishable from LEGO
- **Technic and mechanical models**: Double Eagle's technic parts perform well and cost about a third of LEGO
- **Large scene MOCs**: mix brands — domestic bricks for the basic structure, LEGO for the core parts, which gives the best value
