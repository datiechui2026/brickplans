# Brick MOC Budget Planning: From Parts List to Real Savings

Start with the answer: a domestic brick MOC build is fully controllable on cost. A mid-size MOC of 2,000-3,000 pieces can be brought in at 300-500 RMB, and the whole thing comes down to how you plan the parts list and which purchase channels you combine.

Plenty of builders see an instruction file and order on impulse, then end up with duplicated parts, off-color bricks and shipping fees that cost more than the plastic. Budget planning for a domestic brick MOC is really a parts-list optimization exercise. Follow the three steps below and you will rarely blow the budget.

**Step 1: Export and clean up the parts list**

Once you have the instructions, export the parts list with your software and check three things: whether the part types exceed 150, whether special parts (technic gears, rare panels and so on) make up more than 10 percent, and whether the color palette is too scattered. The rule of thumb: the fewer part types and the tighter the color range, the lower the cost per piece. Replacing rare parts in the list with common equivalents usually saves 20-30 percent of the budget.

**Step 2: Split your purchasing channels by part type**

Parts for a domestic brick MOC come from three main channels: Gobricks (high precision, consistent color, roughly 0.08-0.15 RMB per piece), loose clone parts (roughly 0.03-0.06 RMB per piece, but with obvious color variation), and parted-out full sets (good for large volumes of basic bricks). The recommendation is Gobricks for visible exterior parts and load-bearing structure, clone parts for interior filler and hidden structure. Mixed sourcing brings the average down to about 0.05 RMB per piece, nearly half the cost of an all-Gobricks plan.

**Step 3: Work backward from a budget cap to a part count**

Set the total budget first, then reverse-engineer. For a 400 RMB budget: 2,500 Gobricks parts runs about 250-300 RMB, 3,000 clone parts about 150 RMB, and keep 50 RMB as a buffer for shipping and replacement parts. Before ordering, verify part quantities against the brickplan instruction library so you do not buy the same thing twice.

A few more practical savings details: bulk-buying common colors such as black, white and gray gets you 10-20 percent off; shipping is a major line item, so combine orders up to the free-shipping threshold; only order replacements for structurally critical parts, and fix small cosmetic flaws with stickers or paint. It is worth reading other builders' build logs and cost breakdowns in the brickplan community — many instruction files list a reference parts cost underneath, which helps you avoid the usual traps in advance. For more budget planning ideas, see https://brickplan.cn/en/blog.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does a domestic brick MOC usually cost?**
A: A mid-size MOC (2,000-3,000 pieces) typically runs 300-500 RMB, while a large MOC can exceed 1,000 RMB. Mixing Gobricks with clone parts and limiting the number of part types are the most effective ways to control the total.

**Q: How do I choose between Gobricks and clone parts, and is the difference big?**
A: Gobricks offers high precision and consistent color at roughly 0.08-0.15 RMB per piece; clone parts are cheaper but show obvious color variation. Use Gobricks for visible parts and load-bearing structure, and clone parts for interior filler — that is the best value combination.

**Q: What if the parts list has too many rare parts and the budget is blown?**
A: Replacing rare parts with common ones or adjusting the design usually saves 20-30 percent. Keep the visually critical parts, be bold about swapping hidden structure, and try to keep special parts under 10 percent of the list.

**Q: Is it worth placing a separate order just for replacement parts?**
A: Not frequently — shipping costs make it poor value. Check the list in one pass, combine the order up to the free-shipping threshold, and only replace structurally critical parts. Small cosmetic flaws can be handled with stickers.

**Q: How can I quickly estimate the parts cost of an instruction file?**
A: Multiply the total part count by an average of 0.05-0.08 RMB per piece, then adjust upward based on the share of special parts. You can also check other builders' cost breakdowns on brickplan for reference.
