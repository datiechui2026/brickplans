# Brick Cherry Blossom MOC: Build a Spring Tree That Never Fades

Here is the short version: a cherry blossom tree MOC of roughly 800 pieces takes about 3 hours to complete and stands around 28 cm tall. The trunk is a skeleton of brown Technic beams, and the canopy is built up layer by layer from pink flower elements — so the tree stays in bloom at home through every season.

## Step 1: Trunk and Branch Skeleton

Begin with brown or dark brown Technic beams and connectors to form the main trunk, then split it into 3 to 5 main branches spreading outward. This stage uses about 250 pieces. Taper the trunk as it rises: dropping one connection point per level gives a noticeably more natural silhouette than a uniform column. If you want extra stability, add a baseplate of roughly 10x10 under the roots and cover it with green plates to read as grass. Getting this frame right matters more than it looks — every later layer of blossom sits on it, so check that each branch is firmly pinned before moving on.

## Step 2: Canopy and Flower Layering

The canopy is the soul of a sakura build and accounts for about 450 pieces. Mix light pink, pink and white flower elements at a 7:2:1 ratio, then layer them outward from the branch tips, building clusters at varying heights rather than a single flat mass. Add 2 to 3 single-piece flowers at the end of each branch to mimic the lightness of petals caught in the wind; the depth of the whole build improves immediately. Keep the color mix alternating as you go — a run of the same pink in one spot flattens the shape, while staggered tones make the canopy look fuller than the piece count suggests.

## Step 3: Details and Scene Dressing

Under the tree, place a white bench, a small pink umbrella and two minifigures to make the classic blossom-viewing scene. This adds about 100 pieces. Scatter a few loose petal elements on the ground and the picture reads much more clearly. For a night-blossom version, hide a light brick inside the canopy — warm light glowing through the flower elements is genuinely striking and needs no other changes to the build.

## Color and Parts Advice

- Main colors: about 300 pink, 100 light pink and 50 white pieces. The light-to-dark contrast is what creates the sense of depth in the canopy.
- Budget: 800 pieces runs roughly 100 to 180 CNY. Flower elements can be bought by color from parts stores first, which is usually cheaper than chasing whole sets.
- Seasonal play: swap the flower element colors and the same tree becomes a red maple in autumn, giving you a second display from one frame.

## FAQ

**Q: How many pieces does a brick cherry blossom tree MOC need?**
A: The full version is about 800 pieces — roughly 250 for the trunk, 450 for the canopy and 100 for the scene accessories — with a budget of 100 to 180 CNY. If you want to keep costs down, build only the trunk and canopy; about 600 pieces is enough to complete the tree.

**Q: Will a brick cherry blossom ever wilt?**
A: No, and that is exactly the appeal. It needs no watering and never fades, so it stays in full bloom year-round wherever you display it. There is also no pollen to worry about, which makes it far less fuss than a real cherry tree.

**Q: Is a cherry blossom tree MOC hard to build?**
A: Not hard — the difficulty sits at the lower end of medium, and it takes about 3 hours. The only part that asks for patience is the canopy layering: alternate the flower colors and stagger their distribution, and you get a full, natural-looking result. Beginners can handle it comfortably.

**Q: How much does a brick cherry blossom MOC cost?**
A: At 800 pieces, expect around 100 to 180 CNY in total, with pink flower elements costing a little more per piece. You can also mix in loose bricks you already own, which can bring the cost under 80 CNY. It scales to whatever budget you have.
