# Carousel MOC Review: Gear Drive, 8 Animals, Retro Colors

The short version: this is a very complete carousel MOC instruction set. The rotation mechanism runs smoothly, the animal figures are charming, and the retro cream-and-burgundy color scheme holds up to long-term display. If your parts budget sits around CNY 800, it is a mid-size display model worth building.

## Rotation Mechanism: How the Main Selling Point Performs

The heart of any carousel is the turn. This design uses a central gear train driven by a hand crank on the side. In testing, one full turn of the crank carries the canopy through roughly 1.5 revolutions. Drive is transferred through three meshing pairs of 8-tooth gears, and the resistance sits in a comfortable middle range — a child of six or older can crank it independently without straining.

The base also includes a reserved motor retrofit position. Swap out a single drivetrain part and an M motor can be connected externally, which turns the model into a self-rotating piece for shelf display. That is a smart choice for a display model: the manual version is the default build, and the powered version is an option rather than a requirement.

## Animals and Minifigures: A Cast of 8 Animals Plus 4 Figures

The instructions cover eight animals in total: two horses, two deer, two swans and two rabbits, paired with four minifigures to form a complete amusement park scene.

Each animal attaches to a rising-and-falling column with two pins, so swapping or removing them is quick and does not require dismantling the canopy. The minifigures wear classic circus red-and-white striped torsos, which tie back neatly into the retro palette rather than fighting it. Small detail work like this is where the design earns extra credit.

## Retro Color Scheme: Cream, Burgundy and Gold Accents

The color plan is the strongest part of the whole build. The canopy is built primarily in cream white, the eaves are outlined in burgundy, and the columns are accented with gold parts. The result reads as a 1920s amusement park rather than a modern plastic fairground.

The full model comes to 1,864 parts, of which roughly 40 are rare. Gold and burgundy parts together account for about 15 percent of the total, so a few individual colors may need to be sourced separately if your collection is thin in those shades.

## Build Difficulty and Instruction Experience

The instructions run 312 steps split across six booklets, averaging around six new parts per step. That pacing is sensible: it keeps each step readable without stretching the build into a slog. Difficulty lands at medium-low.

The one small flaw is in the canopy's curved section, where five spots use symmetric parts that are easy to install facing the wrong way. Pay attention to the orientation callouts in the step images and you will avoid rework.
