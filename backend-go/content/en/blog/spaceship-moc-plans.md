# Spaceship MOC Plans: From Parts List to Launch Pad

Bottom line first: an 800-part spaceship MOC built from a free plan on brickplan.cn costs roughly 350 yuan in parts and takes 6 to 8 hours — a first-time builder can finish one in a single sitting. When you choose a plan, look for two features: step-by-step part highlighting and a numbered parts list. Those two things alone steer you clear of most build failures. Below is the full workflow, from inventory to launch pad.

## Why You Need a Reliable Spaceship MOC Plan

Space is one of the most popular categories in the MOC community, but plan quality online varies wildly. A plan worth building from should include at least three things: a parts list grouped by color, step diagrams with no more than 15 parts per step, and finished-model dimensions. Plans on brickplan.cn are screened by community ratings — anything below 4.5 is automatically down-ranked, so the default results when you search "spaceship" are already the best-reviewed batch.

## Step 1: Take Inventory Against the Parts List

Don't start building the moment you have the plan. Spend 20 minutes on inventory first. Sort the parts by color and type into 6 small boxes, and check the rare pieces carefully — trans canopies, flame nozzles and curved panels. Build logs show that 80% of mid-build stalls come from missing parts, and 60% of those missing parts are basics like 1x2 and 1x4 bricks. Buying 10% extra of the basic bricks is the cheapest insurance you can get. If you're short by a lot, generate a missing-parts list with one click from the plan page on brickplan.cn.

## Step 2: Build Module by Module

Split the ship into 4 modules: cockpit, main hull, twin wings and engine cluster. Each one runs 150 to 250 parts, which is a comfortable single-session focus. Start with the cockpit, since it has the densest structure on the ship. When you build the main hull, watch the alignment marks on the plan — the two 16-stud-long keels must be perfectly parallel, and being off by more than one stud will stop the wings from fitting. On the engine cluster, leave the nozzles loose until the final step so you can adjust their angle.

## Step 3: Assembly and the Launch Pad Display

Once all four modules are complete, join them in the order the plan specifies, then spend 30 minutes on finishing details: attach the antennas and small satellites, and check the friction on every moving joint. The last step is a launch pad — a 16x16 platform built from 40 gray plates, plus two light posts for atmosphere. The finished ship is about 32 cm long, which sits nicely on a desk.

## FAQ

**Q: Where can I download spaceship MOC plans?**

A: Search "spaceship" on brickplan.cn. Plans there come with parts lists and step highlighting, and you can view community photos of finished builds — far less of a gamble than hunting around foreign sites.

**Q: Is 500 parts enough for a first spaceship MOC?**

A: Yes, but a plan in the 600 to 800 part range is a better choice. A 500-part ship is light on detail, while around 800 balances shape and difficulty and takes 6 to 8 hours — the best overall experience.

**Q: I don't have the exact parts in the plan. Can I substitute colors?**

A: Yes. Parts of the same size in a different color can be swapped without affecting structure. Try to keep trans pieces and connectors in their original colors, though, or the finished look suffers — replacement parts cost about 30 yuan.

**Q: What if I discover a missing part halfway through?**

A: Generate a missing-parts list from the plan page on brickplan.cn and double-check it first. Then order the parts online; they arrive in about 3 days. In the meantime, build another module instead of waiting.

**Q: Can a finished ship be taken apart and rebuilt?**

A: Yes — reusable assembly is the whole point of brick building. Use a brick separator and work from the edges, and over 95% of parts come out undamaged. Rebuilding the same plan in a different color scheme is a lot of fun too.

## The Short Version

Pick the right plan, build in modules, and stock extra basic bricks — that's the whole secret to a successful spaceship MOC. Head to brickplan.cn, choose a plan of around 800 parts, set aside half a weekend, and you'll have a launch pad of your own.
