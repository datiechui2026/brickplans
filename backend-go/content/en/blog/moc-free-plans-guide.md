# Free MOC Instructions: Where to Find Them and How to Use Them

![Finished model built from a free MOC instruction set](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-free-plans-guide/moc-free-plans-guide-1.jpg)

Free MOC instructions are the closest thing to buried treasure for brick builders. Below is a rundown of the places where you can reliably get free plans, both in China and internationally, so you always have something new to build without spending anything.

![Building steps from a free MOC instruction set](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-free-plans-guide/moc-free-plans-guide-2.jpg)

## 2. Free Instructions Shared by the Community

### 1. The MOC player community

In the community section of brickplan.cn, builders post free MOC designs of their own. These plans tend to be wildly inventive, and they really are free.

### 2. Social media

On platforms such as Xiaohongshu and Bilibili, many MOC builders share instructions they have designed themselves. Following a few active creators is the fastest way to get new material as soon as it appears.

## 3. How Good Are Free Instructions?

A lot of people assume free plans are lower quality than paid ones, but that is not really true. Many free MOC instructions are excellent, because the designer made them purely to share and to trade ideas with other builders. That said, it is worth checking a few things before you download:

- Whether the images are sharp enough to read
- Whether a parts list is included
- What other builders say about the plan

## 4. Tips for Using Free Instructions

![Close-up of the parts used in a free MOC instruction set](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/moc-free-plans-guide/moc-free-plans-guide-3.jpg)

1. **Check your parts first.** After downloading, read the parts list and confirm you actually have enough bricks to finish the build.
2. **Follow the steps in order.** Build strictly according to the instructions rather than skipping ahead, especially on the sections where plates and tiles stack in a specific sequence.
3. **Organise your files.** Sort your free plans into folders by theme so you can find them again later.

Once you have built up experience using free MOC instructions, you may want to buy a few high-quality paid plans and support the designers who create them.
