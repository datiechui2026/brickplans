# DC Brick Building Guide: From Batman to Superman

DC's comic universe has been running for more than 80 years, and since Superman's first appearance its heroes have inspired fans around the world. DC brick building takes that huge superhero world and shrinks it down to brick scale, so any builder can assemble a Justice League of their own. Whether you follow Batman or Superman, there is an enormous amount of creative room here.

## Why Build DC Models?

The appeal of DC brick building comes from its deep character roster and its very distinct settings. The DC universe has a strong dark aesthetic and a real sense of epic scale: the gothic gloom of Gotham City, the futuristic skyline of Metropolis, the ancient mystery of Themyscira. Every location looks different from the others.

For MOC builders, the DC theme is excellent raw material. The seven core members of the Justice League plus their many villains give you plenty to work with at minifigure scale. Landmark buildings such as Wayne Manor, the Daily Planet and Arkham Asylum are classic MOC subjects, and vehicles like the Batmobile, the invisible jet and the Watchtower are just as rewarding to design. If you are new to custom building, start with our [MOC beginner guide](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) to pick up the basics first.

## Five DC Brick Building Themes

### 1. Batman

Batman is the most popular DC brick building theme by far. Build Gotham's skyscrapers in dark grey and black bricks, then add neon-coloured accents to create the feeling of nightfall. The evolution of the Batmobile is essential study for any DC builder: the retro 1989 version, the Nolan Tumbler tank, and the 2022 muscle-car design are each worth recreating. Build Arkham Asylum and put the Joker, the Penguin and the Riddler up against Batman for a scene with real drama.

### 2. Superman

Superman builds lean toward the futuristic side of Metropolis. The rotating globe on top of the Daily Planet building is the detail that makes the model, and transparent blue bricks are the way to render it. Elements like Kryptonian crystals and the Fortress of Solitude work best in transparent and metallic colours, which give a sense of how advanced Kryptonian civilisation is.

### 3. Wonder Woman

Themyscira's architecture is dominated by white marble and gold trim, which makes it a natural fit for white and gold bricks. Use a soft brick chain for the Lasso of Truth, and silver curved bricks for the Bracelets of Submission. Build an Olympus temple scene and pit Wonder Woman against Ares, god of war; Greek-style columns and altars can all be modelled in brick with plenty of detail.

### 4. Justice League

This is the toughest challenge in DC brick building. The Hall of Justice needs a large quantity of marble-toned and white bricks, with interior areas such as a meeting room and a training room. Place chairs for the seven founding members around the circular conference table. The ring structure, docking ports and solar panels of the Watchtower space station can be built with technic pieces, and the model looks spectacular displayed against a black starry backdrop.

### 5. Flash and Green Lantern

For the Flash, transparent bricks and streamlined red pieces capture the sense of motion at super speed. Green Lantern builds centre on willpower made physical, so use transparent green bricks to construct energy blades, shields and mechs; an Oa scene built from lots of transparent green bricks creates exactly the right mysterious atmosphere.

## Building Tips and Tricks

**Colour matching:** DC's colour system is clear and consistent, with Batman's black and grey, Superman's red, blue and yellow, Green Lantern's green and the Flash's red. Keep each character's theme colours unified, and settle on the palette before you start building.

**Scale control:** Minifigures of different characters need to stay in proportion with each other. A Batman minifigure pairs with a standard-scale Batmobile, while a Superman figure placed in a building scene needs to sit correctly against the skyline. Keeping minifigures and settings at a consistent scale is the key to a convincing display.

**Fine detail:** Use slope bricks, curved bricks and printed elements to render details such as the texture of Batman's armour, the movement of Superman's cape and the pattern on Wonder Woman's bracers.
