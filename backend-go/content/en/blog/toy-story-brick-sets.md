# Toy Story Brick Sets: Woody, Buzz and Andy's Room Picks

"To infinity and beyond!" For a whole generation, that line is pure childhood. Since Pixar's first Toy Story film premiered in 1995, the series has become one of the most influential animated franchises in the world. With Toy Story brick sets, you can rebuild Woody, Buzz Lightyear and the rest of the gang from Andy's room and bring that animated magic into your own brick world.

## What Makes Toy Story Brick Sets So Appealing

The appeal of Toy Story brick sets comes down to how faithfully they capture the characters and locations that feel so alive on screen. From Woody's cowboy hat to Buzz's space suit, from the bed in Andy's room to the Pizza Planet arcade, every detail brings back memories of the films. For builders, these sets are more than a construction project — they are an emotional connection.

If you are still new to building, start with the [beginner's guide to MOC planning](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) to pick up the basics. Over on [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) you will also find plenty of fan-designed Toy Story MOC instructions, from the three-eyed aliens to Bo Peep, so you can expand your brick world further.

## Classic Toy Story Brick Sets Worth Building

### 1. Andy's Room

This is one of the most accurate Toy Story scene sets out there. The build recreates Andy's bedroom, including the bed printed with the name "Andy," star-patterned wallpaper, and the storage bin the toys love to hide in. The minifigure lineup includes Woody, Buzz Lightyear, Slinky Dog and Mr. Potato Head. This Toy Story brick set is packed with detail, and the "secret base" area under the bed is a detail that will make you smile.

### 2. Pizza Planet Adventure

Pizza Planet is the hangout the toys love most in Toy Story. This set recreates the restaurant's iconic exterior, complete with a revolving door, an arcade area and a pizza counter. The highlight is a full lineup of the main Toy Story characters plus a buildable rocket ship model. If you enjoy [Disney brick reviews](/en/blog/disney-brick-review), this one belongs at the top of your collection list.

### 3. The Cowboy and Spaceman Adventure

This is a MOC-level build that depicts the classic scene where Woody and Buzz escape from the gas station. It includes a drivable pickup truck, a trailer and a desert highway setting. What makes this Toy Story brick set fun is its range of mechanisms — the truck's trunk opens, and the space capsule on the trailer can separate — which makes it a great parent-and-child build.

## MOC Inspiration for Toy Story Builds

### Expanding the Cast

Beyond Woody and Buzz, the films are full of supporting characters just waiting for a brick version. Think Jessie, Bullseye the horse, Stinky Pete the prospector and Rex. Using [brick modification techniques](/en/blog/moc-brick-modification), you can turn the parts you already own into distinctive character builds.

### Building Scenes

The move to Andy's new house, Sunnyside Daycare's new-toy intake system, the incinerator crisis at the dump — each one is excellent MOC material. Browse the [free instructions on BrickPlans](/en/blog/moc-free-plans-guide) for inspiration and to learn how to tell a film's story with bricks.

### Interactive Mechanisms

Part of Toy Story's charm is the idea that the toys come alive. Adding moving joints, spring-loaded mechanisms and rotating platforms to your build can make your model genuinely come to life.
