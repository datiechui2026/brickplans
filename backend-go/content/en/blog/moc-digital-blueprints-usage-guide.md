# How to Use Digital MOC Instructions: PDF, 3D and Apps

Short version first: digital MOC instructions fall into three groups — static PDF plans, interactive 3D models, and instruction apps. PDFs are best for printing and building at the table, 3D files are best for inspecting structure, and apps are best for checking steps on the move. Choose the right format and device, and your building speed goes up by at least 30%.

## The Three Types of Digital Instructions

**PDF** — A static layout prepared by the designer, containing the parts list (BOM) and step images, usually 1–3 building steps per page. Files typically run 5–50 MB and are meant to be printed and followed on paper.

**3D files** — Interactive models generated from LDraw or Studio. You can rotate 360°, zoom in and out, and hide parts, which makes them ideal for studying internal structure and complicated connection points.

**Instruction apps** — Such as the official brickplan app and Rebrickable. They include a plan library, progress tracking and parts-list checking, and open directly on a phone or tablet without a computer.

## PDF Instructions in Three Steps

**Step 1: Pick a reader.** On desktop, Adobe Acrobat Reader (free) does the job. On a tablet, GoodNotes or Notability work well — both support pinch-to-zoom and annotation, so you can mark up step images.

**Step 2: Print what you need.** If you like building from paper, print in color on A4 and set the scale to "Actual size" so nothing is resized or distorted. Printing only the parts-list pages makes inventory checks faster and saves roughly 70% of your ink.

**Step 3: Work through the steps.** Cross off each step with a highlighter once it is done so you never hunt for the same parts twice. When a detail is hard to read, use the reader's magnifier to inspect that area at 200%–400%.

## How to View 3D Instructions

**Step 1: Install a viewer.** The usual choices are Bricklink Studio (free) and LeoCAD, which ships with LDraw. Both open .io, .ldr and .mpd files.

**Step 2: Learn three core controls.** Hold the left mouse button to rotate the view, scroll to zoom, right-click to pan. Paired with the hide-parts function, this shows you exactly where gears and axles sit inside the model.

**Step 3: Generate your own step images.** In Studio you can break the model down by color or by building order and export custom instructions — often a better fit for your own building rhythm than the designer's original layout.

## Choosing a Device

A tablet is the best all-round device for reading digital MOC instructions: an iPad or a mainstream Android tablet (10 inches or larger) with a stylus makes zooming and annotating easy. Phone screens under 6.5 inches get cramped on complex steps, while a computer is the right place to preview files before printing and to work with 3D instructions. On a tight budget, an entry-level tablet at around 800 CNY is enough.

## FAQ

**Q: How do digital MOC instructions differ from paper ones, and is the digital version worth buying?**
A: Digital plans usually cost one-third to one-half of the paper price, can be reprinted unlimited times, let you zoom in on details and rotate 3D views, and stay on your device permanently. For most builders that is the better deal.

**Q: How do I print digital MOC instructions at the correct scale?**
A: In the print dialog, check the preview before choosing "Actual size (100%)" or "Fit to page" so the plan is not rescaled. Print in color with high resolution selected so part colors stay distinct.

**Q: Can I open 3D instructions on a phone?**
A: Yes. Import the .io or .ldr file into an instruction app that supports 3D preview, or open it in the browser version of Studio. Rotating works on a phone, but the small screen makes it less comfortable than a tablet.

**Q: What if a digital MOC instruction file will not open?**
A: Confirm the format first — open PDFs in Acrobat Reader and 3D files in Studio. If the file reports as damaged, ask the seller to resend it; established platforms such as brickplan generally offer free replacement within 7 days.

**Q: How much do digital MOC instructions cost?**
A: Typical prices run from 5 to 30 CNY, with large complex MOCs reaching 50 CNY or more. Many platforms also keep free sections, and the brickplan community adds a fresh batch of free digital plans every week.
