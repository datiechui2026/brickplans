Free Chinese MOC instruction files cover an extremely wide difficulty range. Designs commonly sold alongside parts on Taobao run from 200-piece mini street scenes up to 5,000+ piece mega-buildings. Beginners should start with a 500-800 piece street scene: these are dominated by basic bricks, the build logic is easy to follow, and the sequence is forgiving if you misplace a part. ## Parts Review

Taobao offers plenty of purchase channels for Chinese parts. Here is how the main brands perform:

- **Gobricks**: the highest precision of the group and the best LEGO compatibility, at a mid-range unit price. The pick for builders who care about quality.
- **Double Eagle**: strong value on Technic-style parts; gears and motors are LEGO-compatible, making it the choice for motorised, moving MOCs.
- **Wange**: very cheap basic bricks in bulk quantities. Ideal for large-scale purchasing on architectural MOCs.
- **SEMBO**: good quality printed elements and a rich supply of minifigure accessories. Suits scene-based and diorama MOCs.

The design quality of Chinese MOC instructions has improved year on year, and more original designers are publishing high-quality files. Matching parts packs for those designs can also be found on Taobao, so one-stop purchasing is genuinely convenient now.

## Building Experience

We tested an approximately 800-piece Chinese MOC street scene file. After downloading the instructions from [brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn), we used the parts list to order from three separate Taobao stores. Total cost came to about 120 yuan. The build took roughly 4 hours. Mixing brands in a single model worked well — no loose connections and no parts that refused to seat. The finished model's sturdiness was satisfying.

## Finished Model

The result is a three-storey European-style street building with plenty of detail: balconies, signage and floor-to-ceiling windows. Because the parts came from mixed brands, colour consistency was slightly uneven, though the overall look still came out well. The lesson: when buying on Taobao, try to source the same colour range from a single brand so the shades stay uniform across the model.
