# Brick Crab MOC Review: Is This 420-Piece Ocean Build Worth It?

The short answer first: this brick crab MOC performs well overall. Exterior accuracy is high, the build runs smoothly, and it earns an overall score of 8 out of 10. It is worth picking up, and it works especially well as a first ocean-themed project for newer builders.

## 1. Exterior Accuracy

The crab uses a red-orange main color scheme, paired with a white underside plate and black eye stalks, reaching roughly 90% accuracy to the real animal. The model totals about 420 pieces, and the finished build measures 22 cm long and 18 cm wide, so it stands out clearly on a desk.

The two large claws and eight legs give 12 movable joints in total. That lets you pose it walking sideways, raising its pincers or foraging, so the model reads as lively rather than static.

## 2. Build Experience

The instruction manual divides the steps clearly into 6 stages, each covering roughly 70 pieces. A beginner can finish the whole thing in about 2 hours. Clutch strength sits in a comfortable middle range: parts come apart without effort and the design tolerates small mistakes, so there is almost no need to press hard or reach for tools. The build stays relaxed and fluid from the first bag to the last.

## 3. Part Quality

Brick color is consistent, with no obvious shade differences or flash on the edges, and gate marks are cleanly finished. Corners are rounded and comfortable in the hand. The gear drivetrain turns smoothly, and the claw mechanism was opened and closed 20 times in repeated testing without working loose. The material feels solid throughout and matches mainstream brand standards, with good durability for repeated posing.

## 4. Value for Money

The set is priced at about 168 yuan, which works out to roughly 0.4 yuan per piece — a mid-to-low price point among comparable MOC designs. Against official mass-market sets of the same size, the price advantage is obvious, and a display base is included as well. That makes it a reasonable buy whether you are giving it as a gift or keeping it for yourself.
