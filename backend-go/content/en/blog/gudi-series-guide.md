# Gudi Brick Series Guide: Which of the 6 Lines Suits You?

Gudi is one of the names you inevitably run into when you start exploring Chinese brick brands. Solid quality control, inventive design and friendly pricing have earned it a strong reputation among builders. If you are stuck on the question "which Gudi series should I buy?", this breakdown of the six most popular lines should help you find the one that matches how you like to build.

## 1. Space Series — Gudi's Flagship Line

Gudi holds an official partnership with a space culture and creative brand, and has released a range of sets themed around China's space program, including Long March rockets, space stations and lunar rovers.

**Why it stands out:** Officially licensed, with a high level of detail — everything from the escape tower to the solar panels aims at accuracy. It has real educational value, which makes it good for parent-child building and science learning. Finished models display impressively; the large-scale rockets stand fifty to sixty centimeters tall.

**Who it suits:** Space enthusiasts, families building together, and anyone who wants a big centerpiece on the shelf.

**Recommended sets:** Long March 2F carrier rocket, Tiangong space station core module, Chang'e 5 lunar probe.

## 2. Military Series — Best Value for Hardcore Fans

Gudi's military line covers tanks, fighter jets, armored vehicles and warships, with particularly strong part quality and building experience. Compared with international brands, Gudi costs roughly a third of the price while giving up little in how complete the finished model feels.

**Why it stands out:** Wide subject range, from WWII classics to modern equipment. Sturdy construction that survives play without falling apart. Minifigures are sharply printed, the gear is complete, and the sets build into convincing scenes.

**Who it suits:** Military buffs, model collectors, and builders who enjoy dioramas.

**Recommended sets:** Type 99A main battle tank, J-20 stealth fighter, Liaoning aircraft carrier.

## 3. Street Scene Series — A Feast for City Builders

The street scene line is built around modular buildings that combine freely into your own city block. The designs use classic street scene elements while adding distinctly local Chinese streetscape character.

**Why it stands out:** The modular approach makes expansion easy — display a single building or connect several into a row. Detail is rich, with a fully furnished scene inside every room. Value is excellent, so the entry barrier is low.

**Who it suits:** City architecture fans and newcomers who want to start building a brick town.

**Recommended sets:** Gudi street corner café, Chinese-style tea house, corner bookshop.

## 4. Technic Series — A Treasure Trove for Gearheads

Gudi's technic line focuses on construction vehicles, race cars and mechanical contraptions, making heavy use of gears, linkages, differentials and other technical parts. Play value is high.

**Why it stands out:** Mechanical functions are genuinely engaging, with working steering, lifting and suspension. Parts are compatible with mainstream brands, so you can mix them into your own MOCs freely. At roughly 40% of international brand pricing, it is an excellent entry point into technic building.

**Who it suits:** Builders who love mechanical structures, and MOC makers.

**Recommended sets:** All-terrain off-roader, tracked excavator, F1 race car.

## 5. My Little Pony Series — A Favorite for Girls and IP Fans

Gudi holds an official My Little Pony license and has released a run of brightly colored, cute sets that recreate the show's classic characters.

**Why it stands out:** Officially licensed, with faithful versions of characters such as Twilight Sparkle, Rainbow Dash and Pinkie Pie. The palette is soft and bright, matching the dreamy style. Build difficulty is moderate, which suits younger children and brick beginners.

**Who it suits:** My Little Pony fans, girls, and younger kids getting started with bricks.

**Recommended sets:** My Little Pony friendship castle, Cloudsdale, Twilight Sparkle's bookshop.

## 6. Mecha Series — Where Sci-Fi Meets Creativity

Gudi's mecha line centers on humanoid robots and mecha vehicles, styled toward Japanese sci-fi and Chinese-style mecha design. The looks are striking and the articulation is generous, which has made it popular with younger builders.

**Why it stands out:** Strong silhouette design with balanced proportions and clean lines. Plenty of articulated joints let you pose them in a range of combat stances. The price barrier is low — a single set usually costs only a few dozen yuan.

**Who it suits:** Mecha fans, sci-fi lovers, and anyone who likes handling poseable humanoid models.

**Recommended sets:** Thunder Mecha, Shadow Warrior, Dragon Rider fighter craft.

## Conclusion: Which Gudi Series Is Right for You?

Back to the original question — **which Gudi series is best?** There is no absolute answer; it depends on what you want:

- Want a **striking display piece** and a **space culture experience** → choose the **Space series**
- Into **military subjects** and **hardcore models** → choose the **Military series**
- Love **city architecture** and **scene building** → choose the **Street Scene series**
- After **mechanical functions** and a **technical challenge** → choose the **Technic series**
- Buying a gift for a **girl** or an **IP fan** → choose the **My Little Pony series**
- Drawn to **sci-fi mecha** and **bold looks** → choose the **Mecha series**

In recent years Gudi has kept improving part precision, clutch power and color consistency, and the quality is now very stable. Whichever series you pick, you get a building experience that is worth more than the price tag. Hopefully this guide makes the decision easier and sets you off on a good run of Gudi builds.
