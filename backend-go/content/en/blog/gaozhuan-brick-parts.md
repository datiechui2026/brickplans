# Gobricks Parts Explained: Why Chinese Brick Fans Love Them

![Finished model built from Gobricks parts](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/gaozhuan-brick-parts/gaozhuan-brick-parts-1.jpg)

In the Chinese brick scene, the name Gobricks — usually shortened to Gaozhuan, literally "high-grade brick" — is treated as a quality guarantee. MOC builders check for it first when restocking parts, and brands check for it first when picking a manufacturing partner. But what actually makes these parts good, and how far ahead of ordinary domestic parts are they? Here is the full breakdown: molds, material, how to identify genuine parts, and a real-world comparison with LEGO.

## Who Is Gobricks? From Parts OEM to Quality Benchmark

![Assembly step photo of Gobricks parts](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/gaozhuan-brick-parts/gaozhuan-brick-parts-2.jpg)

Gobricks is not a brick brand. It is a dedicated brick part manufacturer, founded in 2014 and headquartered in Guangdong, operating as an OEM/ODM factory focused on part development, mold making and production. In practice, parts inside sets from several domestic brands — Pinqi and Double Eagle among them — are actually produced by Gobricks.

Two things built the reputation:

- **High mold precision**: tolerances controlled at the international top level, giving consistent clutch and hand feel
- **Good material and process**: imported ABS with mature injection molding, delivering strong color, strength and wear resistance

In the BrickPlan community, MOCs tagged as using Gobricks parts generally draw more attention — builders assume a smoother build and a more solid finished model.

## Core Advantages of Gobricks Parts

### 1. Mold Precision: The Ceiling for Domestic Bricks

**Mold precision is the single biggest advantage.** Brick tolerance is measured in "si" (0.01 mm), and Gobricks holds its mold tolerance within **±0.02 mm**. That is close to LEGO's ±0.01 mm standard and well ahead of the ±0.05–0.1 mm typical of most domestic brands.

Once precision is there, you notice three things:

- **Consistent clutch**: the 1st brick and the 1000th brick feel exactly the same
- **Solid structures**: high-precision parts build frames that hold and resist loosening
- **Excellent compatibility**: mixes with LEGO and other brands without any trouble

### 2. Material: The Feel of Imported ABS

Gobricks parts use **imported ABS from BASF in Germany**. The material's characteristics:

- **Vivid color**: high saturation that resists fading during long-term display
- **High strength**: excellent impact and bending resistance, unlikely to crack
- **Smooth surface**: no burrs or flash, fine hand feel

Ordinary domestic parts more often use domestic ABS or recycled plastic, and the gap in color, strength and surface finish is obvious. That is why many builders opening a bag of Gobricks parts react with "this feels genuinely good".

### 3. A Wide Part Range

Gobricks currently runs **more than 3,000 part molds**, covering nearly every category from basic bricks to specialty elements. That means:

- **One-stop sourcing for MOC builders**: most parts on a parts list can be found in the Gobricks catalog
- **Complete supply for brands**: no need to split orders across multiple suppliers for different part types

## How to Identify Genuine Gobricks Parts

![Close-up of Gobricks part texture](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/gaozhuan-brick-parts/gaozhuan-brick-parts-3.jpg)

Some sellers use "Gobricks quality" as a selling point while shipping something else entirely. So how do you tell real **Gobricks parts** from fakes?

### Method 1: Check the Packaging

Genuine Gobricks bags normally carry the **Gobricks logo and an anti-counterfeit mark**. The logo is a stylized "GZ" combination, clearly printed in vivid color.

### Method 2: Check the Injection Point

Gobricks parts have their injection point (gate) finished very cleanly, with almost no visible trace. Counterfeit parts usually have a rough gate with an obvious bump or sink mark.

### Method 3: Feel the Clutch

This is the most direct test. Gobricks clutch feels **even, smooth and slightly springy** — never sloppy, and never so tight that you have to force a part down. If clutch varies across a batch of parts, it is probably counterfeit.

### Method 4: Buy Through Official Channels

The safest route is buying from an **officially authorized Gobricks dealer or platform**. Gobricks has several authorized dealers in China, and buying through them guarantees genuine parts.

## Buying Advice for Gobricks Parts

If you plan to buy **Gobricks parts**, a few practical tips:

1. **Buy in bulk for better pricing**: loose-part prices drop as quantity rises, so buying 50–100 of the same spec at once lowers the unit cost
2. **Stock common sizes first**: 2x4 bricks, 2x2 bricks, 1x4 plates and 1x2 plates are used most often and are worth stocking up on
3. **Watch batch consistency**: color consistency is good, but slight shade differences can still appear between batches, so buy everything for a large MOC in a single order
4. **Buy against a MOC parts list**: once you have picked a MOC plan on [BrickPlan](https://brickplan.cn/en/plans), export the parts list and purchase exactly what it calls for to avoid waste
