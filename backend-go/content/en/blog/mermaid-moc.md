# Mermaid MOC Review: 950-Piece Tail Scale Build

The short version: this mermaid MOC instruction set looks the part. Roughly 950 parts build a mermaid display piece on a wave-shaped base, and the seven-layer tail scale gradient in two shades of blue is the standout feature. The full package runs about 110 yuan, it works equally well as a shelf piece or a gift, and beginners can handle the build.

## 1. Design and Accuracy

The mermaid is designed at an enlarged minifigure scale, standing about 20 cm tall with roughly 90% accuracy to the reference art. The tail alternates pearl blue and light azure bricks to build the scale gradient, which gives the model a strong sense of depth and layering.

The long hair, shell bodice and pearl accessories are all individual pieces rather than printed or stickered approximations, and the printed face piece has a nicely detailed expression — it is the finishing touch that pulls the whole build together.

## 2. Build Experience

The instructions run 150 steps, and in testing the build took about 3 hours, which puts it in the entry-friendly bracket. The tail scales use SNOT (studs not on top) technique, and that section is the most worthwhile part of the whole instruction set to study — working through it gives you a much better grasp of off-axis building methods that carry over to other MOCs.

The base is a round reef formation, supplied with 3 corals and 2 starfish, so the scene reads as complete rather than a figure standing on a bare plate.

## 3. Value and Use Cases

The instructions cost about 18 yuan and the parts pack about 92 yuan, for a total of roughly 110 yuan. The parts are mostly light colours, with around 20% pearl special elements — when you check the parts list on brickplan.cn, confirm stock on those pearl pieces specifically.

The finished model pairs well with ocean-themed street scenes and mermaid castle builds, and it makes a solid gift for children; the difficulty is friendly for builders aged 8 and up.
