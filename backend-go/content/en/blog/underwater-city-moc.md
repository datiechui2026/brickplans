# Underwater City MOC: How to Build a Submerged Brick Scene

Short version first: a 1,200-piece underwater city MOC costs roughly 500 yuan in parts and takes 10 to 12 hours. The three rules that make it work are clear parts at about 30% of the total, a domed roof built in layers, and a base raised on a pedestal. Hit that ratio and your sunken city will have the depth and translucency of a display-quality build. Here is how to plan it, step by step, and how to pick the right instructions.

## Three visual keys to an underwater city

The biggest difference between a seabed scene and a land building is the sense of medium: water is transparent, so clear parts are the soul of the build. On brickplan.cn, the highest-rated underwater city models generally run 25% to 35% clear elements. The second key is the dome — a half-sphere canopy instantly turns an ordinary structure into a seabed base. The third is the base: lift the city 6 to 8 studs and leave open space underneath for coral and fish, and the composition becomes three-dimensional immediately.

## Step 1: Check the clear-parts list before you download instructions

When you search for underwater city models on brickplan.cn, open the parts list first and look at three things: transparent dome pieces, trans-light-blue 1x2 bricks, and curved transparent panels. If those three together come to fewer than 300 pieces, skip the instructions — you cannot achieve a convincing submerged look. Models rated 4.8 and above in the community keep their clear-part ratio under control, so they are safe to build from.

## Step 2: Build the base and main structures in layers

Split the city into two tiers: seabed terrain and architecture. Start by laying dark blue and sand-colored plates and bricks across a 32x32 baseplate, mixing several blues freely to create depth variation and mimic light falling through water. For the buildings, aim for 3 to 5 structures at staggered heights: a central tower about 25 cm tall, two domed huts around 12 cm beside it, and an airlock chamber at 8 cm. Leave a 2-stud street between each building so you can slot in fish schools and a small submarine later.

## Step 3: Use water elements to create the seawater atmosphere

This step is what makes the build photograph well. Fill the gaps between buildings with trans-light-blue bricks to suggest water volume, then place coral, seaweed and jellyfish creatures in a staggered rhythm — one cluster every 10 studs. Even distribution looks fake. Finally, fix a few 1x1 light-up elements to the inside of the dome to suggest sunlight cutting down through the water; it lifts the whole scene up a level. When it is finished, shoot from above with the light hitting at a 45-degree angle for the best result.

## FAQ

**Q: How many parts does an underwater city MOC need?**

A: 800 pieces is enough for a basic version. For the full effect with dome and building cluster, aim for around 1,200 pieces — about 500 yuan in parts — which gives you a finished model on a 32x32 baseplate.

**Q: Clear parts are expensive. Can I substitute ordinary blue bricks?**

A: Yes, but you lose a lot of translucency. Keep clear parts at the critical points — the dome and building exteriors — and use light blue bricks elsewhere; that saves roughly 30% of the total cost.

**Q: Are the underwater city instructions on brickplan.cn worth downloading?**

A: Yes. The listings note how many clear parts are required, include photos of the finished build, and show the designer's build notes, which saves you a lot of work compared with assembling a parts list yourself.

**Q: Where do I buy the fish and coral parts for a seabed scene?**

A: The instruction pages on brickplan.cn list the creature parts needed. Search marketplace sites for brick fish or brick coral using that list; a full creature set runs about 40 yuan.

**Q: How should I display a finished underwater city without it gathering dust?**

A: Use a 40x40 clear display case, or put it straight into a glass-fronted bookcase. Dust it with a soft brush, and keep it out of direct sunlight to stop the clear parts yellowing.

## Wrap-up

Clear parts at 30%, a dome built in layers, and a raised base with room underneath — remember those three and your underwater city MOC is halfway there. For the rest, pick a high-rated set of instructions on brickplan.cn and hand your weekend over to the seabed.
