# MOC Instruction Generators: Design Brick Models With AI

Ever wished you could describe an idea and have a complete set of brick instructions appear? That is now possible. As AI tools improve, a new wave of MOC instruction generators has arrived, letting builders with zero design experience create their own models. Here is how these AI brick design tools actually work and how to get good results from them.

## What Is a MOC Instruction Generator?

A MOC instruction generator is a tool that uses artificial intelligence to create, or help create, brick building instructions. Working from a text prompt, a reference image or a set of design intentions, it produces a parts list, the building steps and a 3D preview of the finished model.

In plain terms: tell the AI "I want to build a two-story building in a Chinese architectural style" and it can hand you a complete MOC instruction set for it.

## How AI Brick Design Tools Work

Most current tools rely on one of three approaches.

### 1. Text generation powered by large language models

These tools have been trained on huge collections of existing brick instructions, so they have learned the patterns behind part combinations and structural design. You type a description and the AI returns a matching parts list and building plan.

### 2. Instruction generation from image recognition

Upload a reference photo, such as a picture of a building, and the AI analyses the shapes and structure in the image before producing matching brick instructions. This suits builders who see something and immediately want to recreate it in bricks.

### 3. Automatic generation through parametric design

Set parameters such as size, theme and complexity, and the AI generates instructions that fit the specification. This works well when you need a model to an exact size or style brief.

## How to Generate High-Quality MOC Instructions With AI

### Step 1: Define the brief clearly

Before the AI generates anything, decide exactly what you want. The more specific your description, the better the output. Compare these two prompts:

- **Too vague:** "Design me a house."
- **Specific and clear:** "Design a two-story modern villa with a roof garden, using white and grey as the main colours, roughly 30x30 studs in footprint."

### Step 2: Pick the right generation mode

Match the mode to your situation:

- **Text to instructions:** best when you already have a clear idea
- **Image reference:** best when you have seen a shape you want to reproduce
- **Parametric design:** best when the model must meet a specific size or spec

### Step 3: Refine the result by hand

AI output often needs small manual adjustments. On [brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn) you can download the generated instructions and edit them in brick design software, or simply build from the AI plan and adjust as you go.

### Step 4: Test and share

Once the model is built, check that the structure is stable and the shape matches what you wanted. If it works well, photograph it and upload it to brickplan.cn to share with other builders and collect feedback.

## Things to Watch Out For With AI-Generated Instructions

### Part availability

AI-generated instructions may call for uncommon parts. When downloading instructions on brickplan.cn, review the parts list and confirm that everything you need is easy to buy.

### Structural soundness

AI still has an imperfect grasp of structural mechanics, so a generated plan may have weak points. Check the key connection points before building and reinforce them where necessary.
