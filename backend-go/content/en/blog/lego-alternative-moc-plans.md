# LEGO Alternative Bricks for MOC: A Brand and Compatibility Guide

As MOC culture has grown, more builders are looking at alternative brick brands instead of paying full LEGO prices. A small MOC can easily run into the hundreds of yuan in genuine parts, and a large one goes past a thousand. Chinese brick makers have stepped into that gap with options that cost a fraction of the price. This guide covers whether substituting non-LEGO bricks for MOC work actually holds up, where the trade-offs are, and how to build with them without wasting money.

## Why Build MOCs With Alternative Bricks?

### Price: 50%-80% Savings

This is the most direct and most compelling reason. For the same parts, Chinese brands typically cost one-fifth to one-third of LEGO. Take 1000 basic bricks: LEGO might run 300-500 yuan, while Gobricks costs only 80-120 yuan. For MOC builders who need parts in bulk, that difference funds a much larger project.

### Quality Is Catching Up Fast

A few years ago the quality gap was real. That has changed. Brands led by Gobricks have closed most of the distance on mould precision, plastic formulation and clutch power. Compatibility with LEGO is also solid — the vast majority of parts can be mixed freely.

### A Wider Parts Palette

There are many Chinese brands, and each has its own specialty parts. That means access to a broader selection than LEGO alone offers, especially for unusual colours and rare elements.

## Brand Reviews

### Tier 1: Gobricks

**Quality: ★★★★★**
**Price: ★★★★☆**

Gobricks is widely regarded as the Chinese brand closest to LEGO in quality. Mould precision is high and clutch power sits in the sweet spot — neither too tight nor too loose. The catalogue is broad too, covering more than 90% of LEGO's part types. If maximum quality is the priority for your alternative-brick MOC, Gobricks is the first pick.

### Tier 2: Double Eagle

**Quality: ★★★★☆**
**Price: ★★★★★**

Double Eagle is particularly strong on Technic-style parts. Gears, axles and liftarms are accurate and durable, at roughly one-third of LEGO's price. Its motors and battery boxes are compatible with the LEGO system as well, making it the value choice for mechanical MOCs.

### Tier 3: Qiji and SEMBO

**Quality: ★★★★☆**
**Price: ★★★★★**

Both brands are consistent on basic bricks and priced accessibly. Qiji has good mould consistency; SEMBO stands out for original design. For scene-based MOCs that don't demand extreme precision, either is more than adequate.

## Compatibility With LEGO in Detail

### Basic Bricks

Basic bricks (2x2, 2x4, 1x2 and so on) are where compatibility is best — they mix almost seamlessly. Colour is the caveat: the same nominal colour will show slight variation between brands.

### Specialised Parts

Hinges, springs and unusual connectors vary by brand. Gobricks has the best compatibility and can generally replace the LEGO equivalent directly; other brands may need hands-on testing.

### Minifigures

Chinese minifigures are broadly compatible with LEGO, though the fit at the head and hand connections can differ. Styling varies too, but they can be swapped in and out in practice.

### Technic Parts

Double Eagle's Technic parts have the best LEGO compatibility, with matching hole spacing and axle diameters. Other brands may have issues here, so check user reviews on specific instructions at brickplan.cn to see how a given part performs before you buy.

## Which Brand for Which Build

### Scene One: Street and Architecture MOCs

**Recommended: Gobricks basic bricks + Qiji specialty parts**

Street builds eat basic bricks and decorative elements. Gobricks gives the best basic brick quality, while Qiji's windows, doors and railings offer strong value.

### Scene Two: Technic and Mechanical MOCs

**Recommended: Full Double Eagle Technic set**

Double Eagle's Technic range is complete, from gears to motors, and the price advantage is significant.

### Scene Three: Minifigure and Diorama MOCs

**Recommended: SEMBO minifigures + Gobricks scene bricks**

SEMBO has a wide, well-sculpted minifigure line; Gobricks scene bricks are high quality and build convincing three-dimensional settings.

## Practical Tips for Building With Alternative Bricks

### Mix Your Sources

You don't have to commit to one side. Mixing LEGO and Chinese parts works well: use LEGO or Gobricks for core structure and critical connections, then use cheaper parts for decoration and filler. You keep the build rigid and still control cost.

Even within one brand, colour can drift between production batches. Where possible, buy all parts for a project from the same batch so colour variation doesn't spoil the finished model.

## FAQ

### Q: Will Chinese bricks damage LEGO parts?

A: No. The plastics are similar and hardness stays within a reasonable range, so normal use won't damage either side.

### Q: How much does clutch power vary?

A: Quite a bit between brands. Gobricks is closest to LEGO — not loose, not tight. Some brands run tight enough to hurt your fingers during a long build; others run loose enough that models fall apart. Buy a small test lot first.

### Q: Is colour consistent?

A: Generally less consistent than LEGO. You can see variation between batches, sometimes within a single box. Bear that in mind for builds where colour matching matters.
