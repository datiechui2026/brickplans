# JAKI Brick Beginner's Guide: 5 Best Series to Start

If you're new to brick building and want a hobby that stays fun without wrecking your budget, **JAKI** deserves a serious look. The brand has risen fast among Chinese brick makers on the strength of original designs, solid part quality and aggressive pricing — which makes it especially well suited to builders with zero experience.

---

## 1. What Makes JAKI a Good First Brand?

**Original designs**: JAKI sticks to its own design language rather than copying whatever is trending. Its range runs from spaceflight to everyday scenes, and each set has a distinct look.

**Beginner-friendly building**: The instructions are logically laid out, steps are broken down sensibly and parts come clearly sorted. Follow the manual step by step and you'll rarely get stuck.

**Excellent value**: Prices come in at a third — or less — of what an imported brand charges for the same part count. Part quality ranks among the best of the Chinese manufacturers.

**Wide theme range**: Whether you like mechanical structures, space technology or cute display pieces, JAKI has a series for it.

---

## 2. Five Beginner-Friendly Recommended Series

### 1. Break of Dawn Series — A Starting Point for Mechanical Fans

This line focuses on **mechanical transmission**, with parts for engines, gears and pistons, so you can see how mechanical principles work as you build. The V8 engine model has 300–600 parts and takes roughly 2–4 hours — a moderate difficulty with excellent display value.

**Best for**: beginners who are into mechanics or cars.

### 2. Space Series — A Big Sense of Accomplishment

JAKI's flagship line, covering rockets, satellites and lunar rovers. The colour scheme is mostly white and metallic, so the sci-fi feel comes through clearly. The Long March rocket model (around 500 parts) is a great entry point, and the finished build looks genuinely imposing on a desk.

**Best for**: space fans and beginners who want a centrepiece for their desk.

### 3. Brick Flower Shop Series — Impressive Even With Zero Experience

A bestseller for JAKI and the lowest barrier to entry in the whole range. Part counts run 200–400 and builds take only 1–2 hours, so they fit easily into spare moments. The sunflower pot and the rose bouquet are both highly rated starter picks, and the finished model works just as well as decor for yourself as it does as a gift.

**Best for**: complete beginners and anyone who likes DIY decorations.

### 4. Street Scene Series — A Miniature City for Less

Each building has 500–800 parts, uses a modular design, packs in detailed interiors and includes lighting elements. Beginners can finish a single building without trouble, then add more over time to build up their own miniature block. The retro bookshop and the corner coffee shop are both solid entry-level buys.

**Best for**: beginners who like miniature scenes and plan to collect a series long term.

### 5. Mechanical Dinosaur Series — Poseable and Cool

A dinosaur theme with exposed mechanical detailing and articulated joints, so the finished model can be posed several ways and invites plenty of hands-on play. Parts are clearly colour-coded, and both kids aged 8+ and adult beginners can finish one without difficulty. The Triceratops and the Velociraptor are the recommended entry models at around 300–500 parts, each taking about 2 hours.

**Best for**: dinosaur fans and families building together.

## 3. Buying Advice for Beginners

| Budget | Recommended choice |
|------|----------|
| **Under 50 yuan** | A small potted plant or small display piece from the Brick Flower Shop series |
| **50–100 yuan** | A mid-size bouquet from Brick Flower Shop, or an entry-level mechanical model from Break of Dawn |
| **100–200 yuan** | A single Street Scene building — plenty of substance for the money |
| **Over 200 yuan** | A large rocket from the Space series, or a flagship mechanical model from Break of Dawn |

---

## 4. The Bottom Line

Original designs, friendly prices and a build experience that doesn't fight you make JAKI the obvious first stop among Chinese brick brands. Whether you're starting from zero, working to a tight budget, or picking out a child's first set, the range offers plenty of good options. The fun of brick building doesn't require a big spend or years of practice — pick a JAKI set and start building.
