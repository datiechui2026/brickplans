# Best Brick Brand to Buy First: 2026 Brand Comparison

Every time someone posts a photo of a finished Chinese brick model, the same question comes up: **which brick brand should you buy first?** Domestic brands have multiplied over the past few years — SEMBO, Double Eagle, Panlos and Gobricks each do something well — while prices stretch from a few dozen yuan to well over a thousand. That spread is exactly what makes newcomers freeze up at checkout. Below is a head-to-head comparison across the three things that actually matter: part quality, design and value for money.

## Part Quality: Feel and Tolerances Come First

Whether a build feels good comes down to the parts before anything else. **Gobricks** is widely regarded as the ceiling for domestic parts: high mold precision, tight, secure connections, and a build feel that comes close to — sometimes past — the international names. The trade-off is price, and the fact that many of its sets lean toward "lots of bricks, lots of small pieces." **Double Eagle** (S/SY) has improved noticeably in recent years, with smooth molding and even color, and its mechanical parts — gears, axles, chains — are especially strong, which makes it a natural fit for technic fans. **SEMBO** parts are consistent overall and large baseplate sets use solid material, though a few small parts are occasionally a touch loose on tolerance. **Panlos** sits in the upper-middle: perfectly fine for everyday building, but if you want the very best hand feel you will pay more to move up to Gobricks.

## Design and Creativity: Who Tells the Better Story

On design, **SEMBO** has earned its reputation as the flag-bearer for domestic brands. Its brick cars, military subjects and ancient-architecture series are rich in detail and highly original, which makes it the entry point for a lot of builders. **Double Eagle** is strongest in technic: remote-controlled, movable mechanical structures that look genuinely impressive and leave you with a real sense of achievement when the last pin goes in. **Panlos** plays with mecha, sci-fi and city street scenes, uses bold color schemes and suits anyone who likes a more individual look. **Gobricks** focuses on MOC compatibility and high-fidelity replicas — you buy it for the bricks themselves rather than for the box art.

## Value for Money: Your Budget Decides

- **Limited budget (under 100 yuan)**: go with **Panlos** first. Plenty of styles, friendly prices, and perfectly suited to practice builds or handing to a child.
- **Mid budget (100–400 yuan)**: **SEMBO** offers the best overall experience, balancing design, quality and price. It is the value king of the category.
- **Chasing the ultimate hand feel**: **Double Eagle** technic sets and **Gobricks** replica sets are worth every extra yuan.
- **Collectors and technic fans**: look straight at **Double Eagle**'s high-end line, or at **Gobricks** for pure brick-built models.

## Verdict

Back to the opening question — **which brick brand should you buy first?** The honest answer is that there is no single best brand, only the best fit. Pick Panlos to test the water, SEMBO for a balanced all-rounder, Double Eagle for hand feel and mechanical builds, and Gobricks if you are into MOC and replicas. Hopefully this comparison saves you some guesswork in a very crowded brick market, and lets you get straight to the part that matters: enjoying the build.

## FAQ

**Q: Which Chinese brick brand should I buy first?**
A: It depends on what you want. Panlos for an inexpensive entry point (strong value under 100 yuan), SEMBO for a balanced all-rounder (detailed car, military and ancient-architecture series), Double Eagle for hand feel and mechanical experience (remote-controlled technic sets with movable structures).

**Q: Which brand has the best part quality among Chinese bricks?**
A: Gobricks is generally recognized as the ceiling for domestic parts, with high mold precision and tight, secure connections; the build feel comes close to or even exceeds international brands. The downside is a higher price, and many of its sets are aimed more at MOC builders and replica fans.

**Q: What is a good Chinese brick set under 100 yuan?**
A: Panlos is the first choice: lots of styles, friendly prices, and suitable for practice builds or for kids. At this price point don't chase precision drivetrain mechanisms — focus on whether there are enough parts and whether the build goes together smoothly.

**Q: How should I choose with a budget of around 300 yuan?**
A: SEMBO is the recommendation at this price, with the most balanced overall experience, a high degree of originality and a wide range of subjects. If mechanical structures are more your thing, look at Double Eagle's technic sets — their remote-controlled, movable designs have a strong reputation among domestic brands.
