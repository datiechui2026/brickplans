# Where to Download MOC Instructions: Free and Paid Sources

If you have just fallen into MOC building, you have probably asked the same question as everyone else: **where do you actually download MOC instructions?** Resources look endless until you need one — some are paid, some are free but too blurry to follow, and some turn out to be missing steps halfway through. After years of building, I have sorted the main channels into one guide, from international platforms to Chinese-language communities, free and paid, with the strengths, drawbacks and best-fit audience of each laid out plainly.

## International instruction platforms: big libraries, slightly higher barrier

### Rebrickable: the largest MOC instruction library in the world

Rebrickable is the globally recognized hub for MOC instructions, hosting tens of thousands of designer uploads. Its biggest advantage is **structured data**: every model comes with a detailed parts list, the system can match missing parts against the bricks you already own, and it even suggests what to buy.

- Cost: most instructions are priced by the designer, with both free and paid models available
- Quality: popular models have clear step-by-step sequences and complete renders, friendly to beginners
- Watch out: the interface is entirely in English, so search with English keywords

### Bricklink: the official parts and instruction marketplace

Bricklink is home to a LEGO-owned platform — the world's largest marketplace for individual parts, which also carries a large amount of instruction content. Find a model here and you can drop its parts list into your cart in one click, **solving instructions and parts in one place**.

- Cost: instructions are usually priced by the designer and bought separately
- Quality: similar to Rebrickable, very consistent
- Watch out: better suited to advanced builders who also need to buy loose parts

## Chinese-language communities and sites: friendlier, easier to start

### BrickPlans: a Chinese-language brick instruction community

For Chinese-speaking builders, BrickPlans (brickplan.cn) is the least hassle. Instructions on the platform are filtered and organized, categories are clear, steps are complete, and Chinese build notes and part callouts come with them — beginners can follow along without getting stuck.

- Cost: mostly free sharing, with notes on selected works
- Quality: editor-reviewed, generally sharp and readable
- Advantage: Chinese interface, topic-based categories, easy contact with designers

BrickPlans covers a lot of ground: plenty of starter instructions suited to beginners, plus hardcore military, technic and architecture builds.

### Official instruction sites and free communities

Brick brand official sites release build instructions and manuals for their own themes from time to time — useful if you want to recreate a specific set's scene. Beyond that, free communities such as Tieba, Bilibili and WeChat public accounts gather plenty of keen builders who regularly compile and share high-resolution instruction collections.

- Official sites: authoritative but limited in number, mostly promotional
- Free communities: plenty and free, but quality varies and needs checking

## Which channel should you pick? One table

| Channel | Language | Cost | Instruction quality | Best for |

| Bricklink | English | Mostly paid | High | Builders who also need to buy loose parts |
| BrickPlans | Chinese | Mostly free | High | Beginners and Chinese-speaking builders |
| Official sites | Chinese / English | Free | High but limited in number | Recreating official scenes |

## How to find high-resolution MOC instructions fast

Knowing where to look is only half of it — you also need to know how to judge what you find. A few practical tips:

1. **Check render sharpness**: look at the preview before downloading; if the preview is blurry, the original usually is too
2. **Check step completeness**: good instructions state the total number of steps and include close-ups of the key ones
3. **Verify the parts list**: whether a parts list is included directly determines the building experience
4. **Read builder feedback**: comments and build reviews are the best mirror of instruction quality

Get these habits down and **where to download MOC instructions** stops being a problem — just match the right channel to your needs and budget. If you are new, start with free Chinese-language instructions on BrickPlans to learn the workflow, then move up to the international platforms.

Happy building — may you get through your backlog smoothly and finish your own signature model soon.
