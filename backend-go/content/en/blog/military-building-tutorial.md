# Brick Military MOC Tutorial: From Tanks to Warships

## Opening

![Brick military build step photo](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/military-building-tutorial/military-building-tutorial-2.jpg)

Military subjects are hard currency in the MOC world. Park a tank, a fighter or a warship on the shelf and it commands attention. These builds are also the easiest to get wrong: skew the proportions slightly and the whole thing loses its presence. This brick military building tutorial runs from scale selection through paint schemes and weathering, arranged in the order you will actually need it.

## 1. Military MOC Types and Their Characteristics

### Ground Forces: Tanks and Armored Vehicles

Tanks are the most classic subject in military MOC building. A tank MOC lives or dies on three things:

- **Track system**: use technic link treads or chain links, and make sure everything rolls smoothly
- **Turret structure**: a rotating turret is the soul of a tank
- **Sloped armor**: use SNOT techniques and slope bricks to reproduce real armor angles

On brickplan.cn you can find instructions for many military MOCs, including the 99A main battle tank. Studying builds like these is the fastest way to raise your own level.

### Air Power: Fighter Jets and Helicopters

Jet MOCs are difficult because of the streamlined fuselage. The usual techniques:

- Wedge bricks and slope bricks to build the fuselage curve
- Hinge pieces for movable control surfaces
- Transparent pieces for the canopy

### Sea Power: Warships and Naval Vessels

Warships demand more patience and precision, because the hull structure is long and complicated. When building a brick warship, watch hull strength, the layout of the superstructure, and the level of detail.

## 2. Core Building Techniques for Military MOCs

### Scale Choice and Planning

Military MOCs usually work at miniature scales:

- **1:35**: minifigure scale, rich in detail, ideal for display
- **1:48**: balances detail against size, good for small and medium vehicles
- **1:72**: saves parts, good for dioramas

Once you have picked a scale, search brickplan.cn for reference builds at the same scale and plan your dimensions before you start placing bricks.

### Camouflage Schemes and Color Matching

Camouflage is where much of the appeal of a military model comes from. There are three ways to achieve it in brick:

1. **Color block method**: build the camo pattern out of differently colored bricks
2. **Sticker method**: use custom stickers for complex patterns
3. **Layered method**: stack 1x1 plates of different colors on the surface

Classic military palettes include olive green + sand + dark brown (jungle camo), sand + dark brown + light gray (desert camo), and dark gray + light gray + black (urban camo).

### Detailing Weapon Systems

- Gun barrel: cylindrical pieces or technic axles
- Missile launcher: cylinder pieces combined with cone pieces
- Machine gun: small technic pins plus short axles
- Radar: transparent pieces plus round plates

### Dioramas and Display Bases

A good military MOC doubles in impact with a scene base under it. When building the base, keep in mind:

- Terrain relief: stack bricks for the base form, then cover with plates
- Vegetation: clip together green parts or use plant pieces
- Road texture: use 1x1 plates in different colors to suggest a gravel road

## 3. Classic Military MOC Projects in Practice

![Close-up of brick and plate detail](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/military-building-tutorial/military-building-tutorial-3.jpg)

### Tank MOC Building Points

Taking a 1:35 main battle tank as the example:

1. **Chassis**: build the load-bearing structure from technic frames and install the suspension
2. **Hull**: build the hull shape from bricks, watching the angles of the sloped armor
3. **Turret**: make a rotating turret ring and mount the main gun
4. **Details**: add tool boxes, antennas, add-on armor and similar touches

**Suggested parts list**: technic beams x20, track links x2 runs, 2x4 bricks x50, slope bricks x30, cylindrical pieces x5

### Fighter Jet MOC Building Points

The key to a jet MOC is lightness and clean lines:

1. **Fuselage frame**: build a lightweight skeleton from technic pieces
2. **Wings**: use wedge plates for the wings and check the symmetry
3. **Engines**: cylinder pieces plus round plates to suggest jet intakes and exhausts
4. **Cockpit**: transparent pieces plus curved slope bricks

### Warship MOC Building Points

Warships are the most challenging subject in the military category:

1. **Hull**: use prefabricated hull bottom pieces or build a V-shaped hull yourself
2. **Deck**: cover with flat plates and leave space for the superstructure
3. **Superstructure**: build the bridge, radar and funnels in layers
4. **Weapons**: place main guns, secondary guns and missile systems one by one

## 5. Advanced Tips: Making Military MOCs More Realistic

### Weathering

Weathering with bricks is limited, but you can suggest it in these ways:

- Mix gray and dark parts to show wear
- Use asymmetric building to suggest battle damage
- Add "field repair" patch structures

### Lighting Systems

Adding LEDs to a military MOC dramatically improves the display. Headlights, instrument panels and searchlights can all be done with mini LED beads.

### Functional Structures

Advanced military MOCs chase movement and function:

- Motorized tracks: Power Functions motors
- Rotating turret: a gear train
- Raising and lowering structures: linear actuators
