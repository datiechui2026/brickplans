# Island MOC Review: Beach, Palm Trees and Waves Tested

The short version: this island MOC is the most complete summer-scene build in its class. You get roughly 900 pieces for a 280 yuan budget, and the sand slopes, palm trees and wave pieces all sit at or above the expected standard. It is an easy recommendation for anyone who wants a summer display piece. The only thing to plan around is the build time, which runs longer than most sets this size.

## 1. Model Fidelity: How Convincing Is the Beach?

The sand slope is built from beige and light yellow pieces layered to create a gradient rather than one flat colour, and curved pieces are used to suggest the arc a receding wave leaves behind. The result reads as a real beach edge with noticeable depth instead of a flat plate.

The palm trees combine dedicated palm leaf pieces with curved trunk sections, and the trunk angle can be adjusted slightly, so you can lean the trees the way you want them. Fidelity here is high, and the crowns look full rather than sparse.

The wave pieces are the standout. They are transparent light blue and come as layered wave shapes stacked one over the other, which produces a convincing sense of moving water at the shoreline.

The vacation hut is finished with door and window details, and the roof uses reddish-brown slope pieces. Everything ties together cleanly. Overall fidelity score: 9/10.

## 2. Parts Quality and Build Experience

The roughly 900 pieces arrive in three numbered bags and take about 3 hours to assemble, making this the largest build in this review batch. Clutch power is firm throughout, and the palm leaf pieces have smooth edges with no flash or burrs. The colour palette stays tight around sand yellow, sea blue and coconut brown, which keeps the finished scene looking clean and summery.

The instruction manual is a half-size booklet of colour pages with clear steps. No missing pieces and no tolerance problems came up during the build.

## 3. Display Options and Value

At 280 yuan for about 900 pieces, the cost works out to roughly 0.31 yuan per piece, and that price includes a large 32x32 baseplate. Against comparable island sets, that is strong value.

Beach, palm trees, waves and hut are all included, so the model stands on its own as a complete scene. Add minifigures and a beach umbrella and it photographs even better. For a summer-themed display it is a first choice, and it holds attention whether it sits in a living room or a shop window.
