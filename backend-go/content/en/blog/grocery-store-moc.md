# Brick Grocery Store MOC: Shelves, Checkout and Storefront

The short version first: this grocery store MOC uses roughly 3,000 parts on a standard 32x32 baseplate to produce a two-storey street shop about 24 cm tall. Budget around 260 CNY and set aside about 8 hours. It suits builders who already have some street-scene experience and want a shop that feels lived in.

A grocery store is the most everyday-life shop type in a street scene: shelves packed full, a busy checkout, a colourful signboard, and the moment you place a minifigure inside, the story writes itself. The four steps below cover shelf zoning, the checkout counter, the storefront sign and the street atmosphere. Instructions are available on brickplan.cn, where you can cross-check the parts list before ordering bricks.

## Step 1: Baseplate and Wall Frame

Start by tiling a 32x32 baseplate with dark grey tiles to suggest a concrete floor, leaving a main aisle about 8 cm wide through the middle. Build the walls in sand green brick on three sides, leaving a 6 cm wide shop-window opening and a 2 cm wide doorway at the front. Reinforce the corners with 1x2 technic bricks (the 1x2 brick with hole) so the second-floor slab does not sag. This step eats about 900 parts, a third of the whole building, so square up all four corners before you build upward.

## Step 2: Shelf Zoning and Product Display

Line the ground floor with three shelf runs along the wall: snacks on the left, household goods in the middle, drinks and a cold case on the right. Each run is about 10 cm tall and 6 cm wide, with a 1.5 cm gap between runs so a minifigure pushing a shopping basket can pass through. Stock the shelves with 1x1 round plates and 1x1 bricks sorted by colour zone: mixed red, yellow and green in the snack section, plain white and blue for household goods, so the result reads as full but not chaotic. Add a 1x2 plate along the bottom of each shelf as a baseboard for extra realism.

## Step 3: Checkout Counter and Till Equipment

Place the checkout to the right of the doorway, built from white plates in an L-shaped counter about 4 cm high. On top sits a cash register (a 1x2 brick plus a transparent round tile as the screen), a barcode scanner and shopping baskets. Close the underside with dark grey brick, and stand a small "SALE" sign beside it, made from letter bricks or a printed piece. Leave 2 cm of clear floor in front of the counter for queuing minifigures. This step takes about 150 parts and is the interaction core of the whole shop.

## Step 4: Storefront Sign and Street Atmosphere

Above the entrance, build an awning from dark red 1x2 slopes, and hang a white signboard beneath it spelling "GROCERY" in letter bricks. Put a potted plant and a fruit crate on either side of the door, and lay a doormat on the pavement. Finish by adding two or three warm yellow transparent pieces inside the shop window to fake interior lighting, and a flower pot on the second-floor windowsill; about 50 parts close out the build. Once everything is done, slot the shop into your street layout and compare before and after to see how much the atmosphere changes.
