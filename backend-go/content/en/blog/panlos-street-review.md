# Panlos Street Series Review: Value Picks for Brick City Builders

## Before We Start

Recent years have seen domestic Chinese brick brands bloom, and Panlos has built a solid reputation fast on the strength of its **street series**. Among the Chinese street-scene brands most discussed by BrickPlans community members, Panlos keeps coming up — but is it actually worth buying? This review digs into part quality, design character and value for money to answer that.

> If you are hunting for other street-scene instructions or MOC inspiration, head over to the [BrickPlans street-scene section](https://brickplan.cn/en/blog) and browse the builds community members have shared.

## Part Quality: Clutch That Beats Expectations

Panlos street sets are produced with **high-precision moulds**, and clutch sits between LEGO and the tighter end of the Chinese part spectrum. The build feels smooth under the fingers, it does not hurt your hands, and connections rarely work loose. On large street sets of more than 2,000 pieces, tolerance control held up consistently — no obvious colour mismatch and no flash or burrs on the parts.

**Standout parts:** Panlos trans-clear elements (windows, display cabinets) have very high light transmission, and printed parts are both crisp and wear-resistant. On this front the brand sits in the top tier of Chinese brick makers.

## Design Style: Detail-Rich and Highly Compatible

Panlos street sets lean toward a **European vintage** look, leaning heavily on dark green, brick red and off-white to create a dense old-town atmosphere. Every set includes its own interior detailing — a bar counter and coffee machine in the café, shelving and a reading area in the bookshop — and that detail gives the finished model real display value.

What matters more for city builders: Panlos baseplate dimensions and connection specs are fully compatible with LEGO street sets, so the buildings slot straight into an existing layout without modification. For MOC builders who like to design their own facades, the BrickPlans community has plenty of [Panlos-compatible street instructions](https://brickplan.cn/en/blog) to reference and remix.

## Value for Money

Take a street set in the 2,000-3,000 piece range: Panlos typically lands at **CNY 200-350**, while a LEGO street set of the same specification runs above CNY 1,000. Panlos cannot match LEGO on licensed IP or brand premium, but judged purely on part count and build experience, the value is genuinely strong.

| Comparison | Panlos street sets | LEGO street sets |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Price (2,000+ pieces) | CNY 200-350 | CNY 1,000+ |
| Part precision | Good | Excellent |
| Design originality | Fairly high | High |
| Compatibility | Fully LEGO-compatible | Reference standard |

## Shortcomings

1. **Instruction detail:** part callouts in some steps are not clear enough, and newer builders may need to double-check repeatedly.
2. **Minifigure quality:** printing and moulding precision on the figures still have room to improve, and there is a visible gap versus LEGO minifigures.
3. **Packaging and environmental footprint:** over-packaging is an issue, and the way parts bags are numbered still lags behind LEGO and could be optimised.

## The Verdict

Panlos street sets are a **recommended** entry-level option among Chinese brick street scenes. The brand strikes a decent balance between part quality, design ambition and price. If you are assembling your own brick town and the budget is limited, Panlos deserves serious consideration.
