# TMNT MOC Plans Review: Brick-Built Sewer Heroes

For a whole generation of 80s and 90s kids, the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles are a shared memory: four mutated turtles who love pizza and keep the peace in the sewers under New York. These replica plans put Leonardo, Michelangelo, Donatello and Raphael back together in brick form. Here is a detailed look at several of the most popular plans out there.

## 1. Popular TMNT MOC Plan Picks

### 1. Leonardo
- **Plan name**: Leonardo, dual-katana warrior build
- **Piece count**: about 500
- **Difficulty**: Low
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

As the leader of the team, Leonardo fights with twin katana. This plan puts real work into the head — the blue eye mask is built from curved bricks, and the shell is assembled from dome bricks. The blades are silver blade pieces, and they can either be held in the hands or mounted on the back.

### 2. Michelangelo
- **Plan name**: Michelangelo, nunchaku build
- **Piece count**: about 480
- **Difficulty**: Low
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

Mikey is the joker of the group, and his orange eye mask pairs with a set of nunchaku. The nunchaku are made from hose pieces joined to short bars, so they swing freely. The pizza accessory is the highlight here, built from a round plate and small red pieces.

### 3. Turtle sewer lair
- **Plan name**: Turtle Lair sewer scene set
- **Piece count**: about 2,000
- **Difficulty**: High
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

The sewer lair is the most demanding build among these TMNT replica plans. It includes Master Splinter's training room, the pizza kitchen and the TV lounge area. The sewer pipes are built from a large number of curved bricks and cylindrical bricks, while green and brown bricks create the feel of an underground world.

### 4. Turtle battle vehicle
- **Plan name**: Party Wagon, 1:24 scale
- **Piece count**: about 900
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆

This is the classic shell-shaped battle vehicle, and it can carry four turtle minifigures. The shell form on the roof is built from curved bricks, and the wheels use large tire pieces.

## 2. Key Building Points

### 1. Shell structure

The shell is the most distinctive feature of the Ninja Turtles. Combine dome bricks and curved bricks to build the raised shell shape. The shell should be removable so the body is easier to assemble.

### 2. Weapon accuracy

The four weapons each have their own character: dual katana, nunchaku, a long staff and a trident-style sai. Build them from Technic parts and specialised blade pieces.

### 3. Sewer scene

Use green, brown and grey bricks to set the sewer mood. Add water-stain effects and pipe details to make the scene feel more convincing.
