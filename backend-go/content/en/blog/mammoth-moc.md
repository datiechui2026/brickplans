# Mammoth MOC Review: A 640-Piece Ice Age Beast

**Bottom line first:** the brickplan official instructions for this mammoth MOC earn an overall score of **8.6/10**. The silhouette is faithful, the shaggy coat is handled with real care, and the build takes about 2 hours — a good fit for builders with some experience. The tusks are slightly fragile and need gentle handling. Value for money is strong overall, and it is a rare display piece for the Ice Age theme.

## 1. Shape and Design

The model uses realistic mammoth proportions: a steady standing pose with the signature raised hump along the back, a hanging trunk that answers the upward sweep of the tusks. All roughly 640 parts sit in a restrained palette of cream white, dark brown and grey — limited in color but clearly layered in tone. The finished model stands about 12cm tall and 20cm long, so it holds real presence on a shelf, and the side profile is unmistakable at a glance.

## 2. Detail Work

The coat is the star of this MOC. Chest, belly, legs and back use stacked hinge pieces to set the direction of the fur flow, with light and dark brown offset in layers to create a fluffy Ice Age pelt rather than a flat, smooth-skinned surface. The two tusks are built section by section from curved pieces, then bent into an upward curve; the tips are sharp and the surface carries a color gradient, which gives a high level of accuracy. The ears and the area around the eyes are picked out with small pieces, so the details hold up to close inspection.

## 3. Build Experience

The instructions come from brickplan.cn and break the model down clearly into 4 main stages: leg frame, torso fur layer, head and neck details, and tusk attachment. The whole thing takes about 2 hours. There are no rare parts and no glue required. The only tricky moment is the tusk-to-head connection, which is fairly tight and benefits from a brick separator; the fur layer is repetitive by nature, but the pacing is decent.

## 4. Value for Money

At roughly 640 parts and a part cost of about 240 CNY, the price per piece is low, and once assembled the model needs virtually no extra parts. Compared with finished sets on the same theme, this MOC is more realistic in shape and more enjoyable to handle. Scored out of 100, it lands at **86**, and it is recommended for anyone who likes prehistoric animals or wants a high-fidelity display model.
