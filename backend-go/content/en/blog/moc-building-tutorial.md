# MOC Building Tutorial: From First Idea to Finished Model

Building a MOC (My Own Creation) is a structured creative process, not a lucky accident. Plenty of brick fans want to start but have no idea where to begin. This tutorial walks through every stage, from the first spark of an idea to the finished model on your shelf, so you always know what to do next.

## The Full MOC Workflow at a Glance

A complete MOC project breaks down into these stages:

1. **Concept**: decide on a subject and where the idea comes from
2. **Design**: from paper sketches to a digital model
3. **Preparation**: parts list and purchasing
4. **Building**: from inner frame to final details
5. **Refinement**: adjusting and improving
6. **Showcase**: photographing and sharing

Each section below covers the key points of one stage.

## Stage 1: Concept — Finding Your Direction

### Where ideas come from

**Observe the real world**
- Architecture: notice building styles and structures around you
- Nature: plants and animals are an endless source of shapes
- Machines: the mechanical structure of vehicles and robots

**Borrow from other media**
- Scenes and characters from films and animation
- Worldbuilding from games
- Form language from painting and sculpture

**Draw on the community**
- Browse other creators' builds on BrickPlan.cn
- Enter MOC challenges, where a fixed theme sparks ideas
- Talk with other builders and trade inspiration

### Choosing a theme

A good subject should be:

- **Something you love**: interest is the strongest driver
- **Recognisable**: viewers identify it at a glance
- **Suited to bricks**: bricks have their own expressive limits
- **Challenging but not overwhelming**: stretch yourself within your skill level

### Style and scale

**Style**
- Realistic: aiming for faithful reproduction
- Cartoon: exaggerated, more playful
- Abstract: capture the core features and simplify

**Scale**
- Minifigure scale (1:48): rich detail, well suited to buildings
- Micro scale (1:100+): suited to large scenes
- Free scale: no rules, defined by the builder

## Stage 2: Design — From Idea to Plans

### Sketching

Sketching is the most important step of the design stage. It does not need to be beautiful; what matters is capturing the idea.

**What a sketch should record**
- Front and side views
- Key dimensions
- Colour distribution
- Critical structural joints

### Digital design

Brick design software turns a sketch into a precise 3D model.

**Recommended software**
- **Studio 2.0**: the most complete feature set and parts library
- **Bricklink Studio**: integrated with the BrickLink parts database
- **LDD (Lego Digital Designer)**: beginner friendly

**Why design digitally**
- Precise control over every part's position
- Automatic parts list generation
- Rendered preview images
- Easy to edit and iterate

### Design principles

**Structure first**
- Build the internal frame before covering it with decoration
- Make connections solid so the model does not fall apart on contact
- Reinforce key load-bearing points

**Colour harmony**
- No more than three main colours
- Use neutral greys, white and black as a base
- Save accent colours to highlight key features

**Detail layering**
- Read well from a distance, reveal detail up close
- Detail serves the whole and should not steal the show
- Vary detail density between different areas

## Stage 3: Preparation — Parts List and Purchasing

### Generating the parts list

Design software produces the list automatically. Your job is to:

1. Export the list as CSV or XML
2. Filter out parts you already own
3. Mark the parts you need to buy

### Where to buy parts

**The BrickPlan.cn parts section**
- Ships domestically, fast logistics
- Transparent pricing
- Buy by the piece

**Other sources**
- BrickLink: the largest marketplace, but shipping costs are higher
- Taobao and Xianyu: good for retired parts
- Parting out official sets: an economical way to top up

### Colour substitution

When the colour you want is unavailable:

- Swap in a close match, such as dark blue for black
- Shift the whole model to a different colour scheme
- Accept the difference and treat it as a design feature

## Stage 4: Building — From Frame to Finished Model

### Build order

1. **Core frame**: build the internal support structure first
2. **Main body**: complete the major masses
3. **Outer skin**: cover with decorative parts
4. **Detailing**: work through each area in turn
5. **Final pass**: review and optimise the whole model

### Common problems

**Unstable structure**
- Cause: not enough internal support
- Fix: add cross bracing and use larger bricks

**Running out of parts**
- Cause: gaps in the parts list
- Fix: substitute temporarily or pause until the order arrives

**Proportions off**
- Cause: misjudged scale at the design stage
- Fix: rebuild the section, or adjust surrounding elements to compensate

## Stage 5: Refinement — Making the Model Better

### Structural refinement

- Check weak points and strengthen connections
- Simplify parts usage and cut unnecessary complexity
- Make sure moving parts such as doors and wheels still work

### Aesthetic refinement

- Adjust the colour balance and add transition shades
- Add detail to enrich the visual layers
- Apply weathering or special effects

## Stage 6: Showcase — Sharing Your Work

### Photography

Good photos make a build look far better:

- **Light**: natural light or a soft light box
- **Background**: a plain sweep or a scene matched to the subject
- **Angles**: shoot front, side and top views
- **Close-ups**: capture the design details that matter

### Sharing in the community

Upload your model to BrickPlan.cn and join the conversation:

- Write a build description explaining your thinking
- Note the parts and techniques you used
- Reply to comments and engage with the community
- Offer instructions for download to help other builders

## Final Thoughts

A MOC is a creation that goes from nothing to something, and every project teaches you a little more. This tutorial has mapped the whole route from concept to finished model, and the rest is mileage. There is no single correct answer in MOC building; enjoying the process matters most. Thousands of like-minded builders are waiting to talk bricks on BrickPlan.cn. Pick up your bricks and start building.
