# Streetview MOC Review: 1,580-Part Corner Café Blueprint

## Product Overview

Streetview remains one of the most enduring MOC themes. From the early LEGO streetview series to the many domestic brick brands now producing street scenes, builders keep coming back to detailed architecture. This review looks at the **streetview blueprint** for the Corner Café MOC set, designed by a BrickPlans community builder. It includes a three-story corner building, street-side greenery, a streetlight and pavement detailing, making it a strong choice for a mini city corner. The full blueprint is free to download on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) and comes with a detailed parts list.

## Build Difficulty

**Difficulty rating**: ★★★☆☆

The build is moderate overall. The main structure is regular and straightforward; the real time goes into interior decoration and arranging the street elements. Expect about 5 to 7 hours to finish. The blueprint uses a modular design, so each floor can be built independently and later modified or expanded without starting over.

## Part Review

The blueprint requires about **1,580 parts**, within a range of 1,200 to 1,800 pcs. Recommended brands: **SEMBO** for a wide color range that suits streetview buildings, or **Gobricks** for high part precision and sharper architectural detail.

Parts list highlights:

- A large number of window frames and glass: about 30 sets, used to recreate the facade windows of a European-style building
- Street brick paving: about 80 dark gray tiles for sidewalks and road surfaces
- Vegetation parts: about 50, including trees, flower pots and potted plants
- Interior furniture: tables, chairs, counter, bookshelf, lighting and similar small parts, about 60 pieces
- Signage parts: customizable shop-sign text, with tiles plus stickers or printed parts recommended

## Building Experience

The blueprint is a PDF with 198 steps, each with a clear 3D render. Building starts at the foundation, laying the road and sidewalk first, then raising the main building floor by floor. The ground-floor coffee counter and bar are richly detailed, including an espresso machine, cash register and display case. The second floor is a reading area with bookshelves and a comfortable sofa. The third floor is a roof garden filled with greenery.

The modular design is the blueprint's biggest strength. Each floor slab uses standard connections, so floors can be combined and swapped freely. If a café is not what you want, the interior can be converted into a bookstore, flower shop or small restaurant. On [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn), builders have also shared expansion packs, such as a second-floor sunroom, a top-floor garden and an outdoor seating area.

The exterior wall is the most impressive part of the build. The designer uses SNOT techniques cleverly to create a textured, uneven brick surface, and combines different brick colors to give the building a naturally weathered, retro European look. The curved window at the corner is the finishing touch, giving the whole building a more elegant character.

## Finished Model

The finished model measures about 25 cm x 25 cm x 32 cm, a standard streetview module size that connects directly to mainstream streetview brick sets. The facade is beige with dark brown trim in a retro European style. The ground floor has a coffee counter and outdoor seating, the second floor is a reading area, and the third floor is a roof garden. Streetlights, street trees and a mailbox add everyday life, and a row of these buildings creates a convincing city block.

## Recommendation

**Overall rating**: ★★★★★

This streetview MOC blueprint is an excellent choice for getting into streetview building. The modular design makes both building and modification easy, and the finished model looks refined enough to serve as the starting point for a city corner. Download the original blueprint on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn), then use the community expansion ideas to gradually build your own complete mini city.
