# Chinese Brick MOC Sharing: Top Genres, Build Steps, Platforms

After years of building, my biggest takeaway is simple: the domestic brick scene has genuinely arrived. The **Chinese brick MOC sharing** community in particular has moved well past the early days of copying overseas instructions, and now runs on its own visual language and taste. This guide skips the platitudes and gets straight to the useful part: which genres are worth your time, how to structure a build, and where to find plans and fellow builders.

## 1. Three Popular Genres Worth Following

**Mecha and robots** are the home turf of Chinese builders. Thanks to high-density parts and maturing in-house molds, domestic mecha MOCs hold up well on joint friction, gear linkages and posability. Both humanoid Gundam-style builds and realistic mecha styles find an audience, and plenty of individual builds on Bilibili pass a million views without effort.

**Chinese-style ancient architecture and dioramas** are the most distinctive original lane. The Forbidden City corner tower, Jiangnan gardens, Huizhou-style horse-head gable walls — recreating dougong brackets and upturned eaves in brick form. This kind of work, telling an Eastern story with Western-style studs, actually travels furthest in overseas communities, and it is the most memorable category in Chinese brick MOC sharing.

**Miniature street scenes and slice-of-life builds** are the most photogenic type. A late-night snack shop with the lights on, a bus stop on a rainy day — a few hundred parts capturing everyday atmosphere. Detail-obsessed builders fall for these instantly, and they make an ideal first MOC for newcomers to practice on.

## 2. Creative Approach: Four Steps From Parts to Finished Model

If you want to produce your own **Chinese brick MOC**, work through this sequence:

1. **Set theme and scale.** Decide what you are building and how big it will be. For example, a bookshop at 1:32 minifig scale — once you have that baseline, the build will not drift later.
2. **Frame first, shell second.** Use bricks to lay down the internal support structure so load-bearing and stability are settled, then apply the shaping parts on the outside. Most beginners watch their model collapse halfway through, and it almost always traces back to a weak step here.
3. **Use off-colour and used parts well.** Morandi palettes and clashing colour schemes stand out more; unconventional uses — a wheel as a pendant lamp, leaf elements built into feathers — are often the detail that makes a build.
4. **Photograph, disassemble, review.** Once finished, shoot the build from multiple angles, then take it apart and study the load paths. Do this a few times and your structural instincts will rise on their own.

## 3. Where to Find Instructions and Builders: Platform Picks

If you want people to talk to, plans to collect, and somewhere to show your work, these are worth keeping in rotation:

- **Bilibili / Xiaohongshu (RED):** search the Chinese phrase for Chinese brick MOC sharing and you get a mass of tutorials and unboxings, with a friendly, interactive tone. Good for inspiration and picking up technique.
- **Discord / Tieba boards:** where hardcore builders gather. Plan swapping and parts discussion run at high density — better suited to advanced builders.
- **BrickPlans (brickplan.cn):** a community built specifically around brick instruction sharing. The site collects a large number of Chinese MOC builds and building plans, clearly categorised, with direct download supported.

## 4. Getting Involved

From mecha to Chinese-style architecture to miniature street scenes, the standard of Chinese brick MOC work is visibly climbing — and that depends entirely on builders sharing and discussing without holding back. If you want to join in, post your builds and upload your plans at **https://brickplan.cn**. You will find a large library of Chinese brick MOC plans and building tutorials there, plus a group of people who love original work as much as you do. Upload your model, save the plans that catch your eye, and help keep the domestic building scene growing.
