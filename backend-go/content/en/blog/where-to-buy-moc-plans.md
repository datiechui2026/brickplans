# Where to Buy MOC Instructions in 2026: 4 Platforms Compared

## Product Overview

"I found the design — now where do I buy it?" That is the first roadblock most new MOC builders hit. As MOC culture has spread worldwide, the number of places selling and sharing instructions has grown fast, and the gap between them is wide: catalogue size, quality, pricing and the checkout experience vary enormously from the well-known Rebrickable to newer community platforms.

This guide rates today's main MOC instruction channels on four criteria: **how many instructions they carry, price range, quality assurance, and how easy they are to buy from**, so you can work out which source fits you.

## Build Difficulty (Choosing a Channel)

**Selection difficulty: ★★☆☆☆**

Picking a channel isn't complicated. It comes down to three questions: what is your budget, what subject matter do you build, and do you need Chinese-language or English-language support? Answer those three and the shortlist writes itself.

## Parts Review (Channel Comparison)

### Channel 1: Rebrickable (largest international MOC instruction platform)
- **Price:** Free to $15 (most instructions $5-10)
- **Catalogue size:** 100,000+ (the largest worldwide)
- **Chinese-language support:** ★★☆☆☆ (mostly English)
- **Recommended:** ★★★★☆ — the widest variety anywhere, but you need to read English

### Channel 2: BrickPlan.cn (curated Chinese MOC instruction platform)
- **Price:** Free to ¥50 (most instructions ¥15-35)
- **Catalogue size:** 3,000+ (growing steadily)
- **Chinese-language support:** ★★★★★ (fully Chinese instructions plus video tutorials)
- **Recommended:** ★★★★★ — excellent value, Chinese-friendly, solid after-sales service

### Channel 3: BrickLink Studio (community sharing)
- **Price:** Mostly free
- **Catalogue size:** 5,000+
- **Chinese-language support:** ★★★☆☆
- **Recommended:** ★★★☆☆ — plenty of free instructions, but quality is uneven

### Channel 4: Designer personal sites / crowdfunding platforms
- **Price:** $5-50 (depends on how well known the designer is)
- **Catalogue size:** Varies
- **Chinese-language support:** Depends on the designer
- **Recommended:** ★★★★☆ — the choice for builders chasing top-tier design quality

## Build Experience

The buying experience differs sharply between platforms.

**Rebrickable** suits builders who can pay internationally and read English comfortably. It generates parts lists automatically and exports them to BrickLink in one click — the technical integration is the best of any platform. The catch: some listings have no preview page, so buyers occasionally discover only after paying that an instruction set isn't what they wanted.

**BrickPlan.cn**, the newer domestic platform, has invested heavily in localising the user experience. It accepts WeChat Pay and Alipay, and every instruction set has a detailed preview page plus a parts-list preview, so you can judge difficulty and required parts before buying. The platform also runs community discussion boards where build questions get answered directly.

**BrickLink Studio**'s free instructions suit builders on a tight budget, but you need some ability to filter. Prioritise listings with high ratings and plenty of comments.

## Finished Model Showcase

Whatever channel you buy from, the finished model reflects the designer's skill, not the platform. Before you pay, check these indicators:

1. **The designer's back catalogue** — look at the quality of their earlier work
2. **Builder photos** — real photos of the finished model are far more useful than renders
3. **Parts value** — does the design lean on rare or expensive parts?

## Recommendation

**★★★★★**

For Chinese builders, [BrickPlan.cn](https://brickplan.cn) is the first stop for MOC instructions: Chinese interface, local payment and after-sales support all count in its favour. For anyone chasing work by the world's top designers, Rebrickable remains the default. The sensible play is to use the two together — day-to-day instruction purchases on BrickPlan.cn, and a trip to Rebrickable when you want a showpiece design.

**Recommended platforms:** BrickPlan.cn, Rebrickable
**Part count range:** Depends on the specific instruction set (anywhere from 200 to 5,000+ pieces)
**Price range:** ¥0-300 (average ¥25 per set on the domestic platform)
**Time-saving tip:** Follow the weekly featured instruction picks on [BrickPlans](https://brickplan.cn) — the platform has already filtered for quality, which saves you a lot of browsing.
