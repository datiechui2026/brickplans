# Gobricks Review: Are These Parts Worth It for MOC Builders?

The short answer first: Gobricks is a parts company, not a set company. With more than 1,000 individual elements on sale, including rare colours and specialised pieces, loose-part prices typically run 20%–30% below first-tier brands. Among MOC builders it is widely treated as the go-to source for filling gaps in a parts bin. The weak spot is the in-house set line, where original IP design is fairly ordinary, and a few production batches have shown colour mismatch.

"How good are Gobricks, really?" is one of the most frequently asked questions in Chinese brick-building communities. As one of the most polarising domestic brands, Gobricks has a loyal following and a fair number of critics. This review tries to stay neutral, looking at brand reputation, part quality and buying channels.

## Brand Reputation: How Builders See Gobricks

You cannot answer the Gobricks question without looking at its reputation in the community. Gobricks started out as a parts supplier, producing components for major domestic brick brands before launching sets under its own name — which is why builders jokingly call it "the parts factory that went legit."

### Where the praise comes from

Most of the positive feedback centres on the parts system. Gobricks is compatible with mainstream brick standards and offers an unusually deep catalogue, especially for rare colours and special elements — close to a first choice for restocking a parts bin. Many builders answering the Gobricks question add the same line: if you are buying parts, Gobricks is the safe pick.

### The complaints and common criticisms

Criticism focuses mainly on set design. Compared with the parts reputation, Gobricks' own sets show limited original IP, and some builders find the designs conservative. There are also colour-matching issues in certain batches. Overall quality control sits in the upper-middle range for domestic brands, but it is not perfect.

## Part Quality: Gobricks' Core Strength

Objectively, how good Gobricks is depends heavily on how you use the parts.

### Feel and clutch power

Clutch power on Gobricks elements sits in the top tier of domestic production — moderate, neither loose nor tight enough to hurt your fingers. On large MOCs this consistency matters a great deal, and it noticeably cuts down the frustration of a build coming apart halfway through.

### Colour and finish

The moulding process is stable, surface gloss is even, and sprue marks are cleanly finished. Transparent pieces are a particular highlight: light transmission is good, which suits lighting features, aquariums and any scene that needs a clear effect. That is why many MOC designers specify Gobricks transparent elements.

## Buying Channels and Advice

Gobricks parts reach buyers through three main routes: official flagship stores, third-party parts shops and secondhand marketplaces. Official channels back up their sales with after-sales service, but set prices run higher. Third-party parts shops sell by the piece, which suits MOC builders sourcing from a parts list. Secondhand platforms are where discontinued elements turn up, though you have to judge condition yourself.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Gobricks worth buying?**
A: For parts, yes. For sets, it depends. Gobricks is compatible with mainstream brick standards and strong on rare colours and special elements, making it a first choice for MOC restocking. The in-house sets are conservative in design, so compare carefully if original IP is your goal.

**Q: Are Gobricks parts compatible with first-tier brand parts?**
A: Largely yes. Gobricks produces to mainstream brick standards, and pins and bricks interlock interchangeably. Tolerances and colour can vary slightly between batches, so test-fit a small section before mixing in bulk.

**Q: Are Gobricks parts suitable for beginners building MOCs?**
A: Very much so. The range is wide, elements can be searched by number, and per-piece prices usually run 20%–30% below first-tier brands. Sourcing a complete 2,000-piece MOC in loose parts stays affordable, and beginners can start with a small batch to get a feel for the clutch.

**Q: Are Gobricks sets worth picking up?**
A: It depends on your goal. If you want original IP models, Gobricks sets are average on accuracy and design surprise. If you want to build mechanical or scene-based MOCs, buying loose parts packs directly is better value.
