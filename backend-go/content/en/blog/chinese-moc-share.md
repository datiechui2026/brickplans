# Chinese Brick MOC Sharing: Brands, Platforms, Community

Chinese brick brands have moved fast in recent years. Part quality, design ambition and brand reach have all climbed to a new level, and more and more builders are turning their attention to MOC (My Own Creation) work built from these parts. That ecosystem is lively and still growing. Below is a walkthrough of the whole loop: which brands to buy, where to post your builds, and how to behave once you are inside the community.

## 1. Brands Worth Buying for Chinese Brick MOCs

Parts come first. The brand you draw from shapes both your building experience and the quality of the finished model. These are the names that come up most often among MOC builders right now.

### Gaozhuan

Gaozhuan is one of the first choices for parts supply. Its biggest advantage is **an extremely wide catalog** — thousands of shapes and colors, covering almost everything a typical MOC scene needs. Precision is high and clutch power sits in the middle: not loose, not tight, and very close to what you feel from top international brands. For builders chasing fine detail and accuracy, Gaozhuan is hard to replace.

### Double Eagle

Double Eagle has deep roots in Technic-style parts and shines in mechanical transmission builds. If you like engineering vehicles, robotic arms or robots that need complex joints and gear trains, its parts deliver. Gears, cross axles and connectors are consistently made and priced very aggressively, which is why Technic-focused builders keep coming back.

### SEMBO

SEMBO is one of the biggest names in the Chinese brick industry, with a large portfolio of original IP and licensed lines. Colors are vivid and surface finish is clean, which suits architecture, street scene and diorama MOCs especially well. Its unusual parts and special colors often become the focal point of a build. Plenty of builders buy SEMBO sets purely to part them out for future projects.

### Mould King

Mould King is known for motorized bricks, with a mature system of motors, battery boxes and remote-control modules. If you want your MOC to move or drive by remote, its power components are a solid pick. Part accuracy is good and the range of motorized options is broad, letting a build go from static display piece to something interactive.

### Panlos

Panlos has risen quickly and performs strongly in military subjects and vehicle MOCs. Part quality is consistent and the design language is rugged, which appeals to military and vehicle fans. The company puts real investment into special parts, giving creators more options than before.

### Qizhi

Qizhi takes the value route: friendly prices and a catalog that keeps expanding. For newcomers on a tight budget who still want to try MOC building, it is a reasonable entry point. Compatibility is good, so mixing Qizhi with other brands is not a problem.

## 2. Where to Share Chinese Brick MOCs

Once you have something worth showing, the next question is where to post it. These are the main channels in China today.

### A Dedicated Instruction Platform — brickplan.cn

Among the various channels, [brickplan.cn](https://brickplan.cn) is one of the few platforms in China built specifically for sharing and discussing brick MOC instructions. It was designed for MOC builders and supports instruction uploads, category browsing, likes and comments, favorites and downloads. The catalog spans architecture, vehicles, mecha, military, animals, sci-fi and more, so whatever your subject, there is content aimed at you. As one of the central hubs of Chinese brick MOC sharing, brickplan.cn is not just a warehouse of instructions — it is a bridge between creators and fans.

### Xiaohongshu

Xiaohongshu has become an important place for brick MOC content to develop. Search for Chinese brick MOC there and you will find a large volume of polished finished-model shots and build diaries. The note format suits MOC photography and build reflections well, and a detailed image-and-text post is a good way to record your process. Many builders use it to post previews of upcoming work and attract followers.

### Bilibili

Bilibili is the go-to platform for video MOC content. You will find build-process videos, unboxings, part reviews and technique breakdowns. Video shows detail and motion directly, and for motorized builds it is the best way to demonstrate what the model can do. Plenty of creators have built loyal audiences by publishing high-quality content there.

### Baidu Tieba

Traffic on Tieba is not what it once was, but the brick-focused boards are still where veteran builders gather. The strength of the format is **concentrated topics and deep discussion** — a single thread can produce a genuinely high-quality exchange. For technical MOC questions, you can often get a detailed answer from an experienced builder.

### WeChat and QQ Groups

The most active sharing tends to happen in chat groups. There are many MOC-themed WeChat and QQ groups where members post new work, swap building techniques, organize group buys for parts and even run online MOC competitions. That instant, high-frequency interaction makes sharing far more lively.

## 3. Community Interaction Tips

Sharing a MOC is not only about showing the model — it is about growing and enjoying yourself through the exchange. A few practical suggestions.

### Share Boldly, Rough Edges and All

Many newcomers hold back because they feel their work is not good enough. That is unnecessary: the MOC community is generally friendly, and people care more about creativity and effort than technical perfection. Take the first step, post the model, and you will get encouragement and advice back.

### Document the Process

Beyond finished photos, share your design thinking, the problems you hit while building, how you solved them and why you chose particular parts. This **process material** is often more valuable than the model itself and much more likely to spark discussion and recognition from other builders.

### Engage First, Then Connect

Leave genuine comments under other people's work, join in community discussions, and take part in online or in-person MOC meetups. Those interactions are the fastest way into the scene and into friendships with like-minded builders. MOC building is individual expression, but MOC sharing is never a solo trip.

### Respect Original Work and Follow the Rules

When sharing or referencing someone else's model, always credit the source and respect the original creator's rights. Many creators give their instructions away for free, but that does not mean they can be resold or passed off as someone else's work. Good community norms depend on every member upholding them.

## 4. What Comes Next for Chinese Brick MOC

As Chinese brick brands keep improving and the MOC community keeps growing, the future of MOC sharing looks wide open. More brands are paying attention to MOC builders and releasing targeted part packs and support services. Dedicated platforms such as brickplan.cn continue to refine their features, offering creators a better environment for both showcasing and trading.

It is not hard to imagine that before long, Chinese brick MOC will stop being a niche hobby for a small group and become a mainstream way for more brick fans to build. Whether you are brand new or long experienced, the door is open: pick up your bricks, start creating, and share what you make.
