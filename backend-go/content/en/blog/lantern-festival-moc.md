# Lantern Festival Brick MOC: 600-Piece Lantern & Tangyuan Scene

Here's the short version first: a Lantern Festival MOC in the 600-piece range costs about 200 yuan in parts and takes four to five hours to build. What you end up with is a complete holiday scene — one large main lantern, six small lanterns and a table of tangyuan (glutinous rice balls) — that photographs beautifully on a hallway console or a TV stand. Two things make or break it: keep red to gold at a 7:3 ratio, and hide the light string inside transparent parts. The step-by-step build follows.

## Step 1: Build the 300-piece main lantern

The main lantern is the visual center of the whole scene. Frame the hexagonal body in red 1x2 bricks, eight bricks tall per face, then close the top off into a pagoda-style roof. Dress the outside with gold 1x1 pieces for the decorative pattern, keeping red to gold at 7:3. The key move: leave a hollow 4x4 core inside the body so the light string has somewhere to live. The frame alone comes to roughly 300 pieces, and finished it stands 18 cm tall — the brightest single object in the display.

## Step 2: Batch-build the small lanterns and light string

Small lanterns follow a simple formula: a red sphere plus a gold ring. Four curved red bricks make the ball, capped top and bottom with a gold 1x1 round ring — ten parts per lantern, six lanterns in half an hour. Fix them to either side of the main lantern on 2-stud transparent rods, staggered in height along an arc. Finally, run a 20 cm LED string in through the top of the main lantern, out the bottom, and loop it between the small lanterns. That string is the entire secret of the atmosphere.

## Step 3: Make a 100-piece table of tangyuan

The tangyuan table is the finishing touch. Use white 1x1 round bricks as the rice balls, three to a spoon, filling an 8x8 dark round table; the bowl is an inverted 2x2 curved slope ringed with white edging. The whole table is about 100 parts and costs under 15 yuan. Add two minifigures — one holding a lantern, one holding a bowl — and the scene instantly has a story. Once everything is in place: 70% red, 30% gold, with white as the accent.

## FAQ

**Q: How many pieces does a Lantern Festival MOC need, and what's the budget?**

A: The full scene is about 600 pieces and around 200 yuan. If you only build the main lantern, 300 pieces and under 100 yuan will do it — ideal for a first attempt.

**Q: What kind of light string goes inside the lantern?**

A: A USB warm-white string of about 20 cm works best; a 3000K color temperature reads most like candlelight. Check that the bulb head diameter is smaller than the 4x4 cavity, or it won't push in.

**Q: I don't have enough red parts — can I substitute other colors?**

A: Yes, but stay within the dark family. Mixing dark red and dark orange is fine; switch to blue or green and the holiday feel is gone. Yellow 1x1 pieces can stand in for gold.
