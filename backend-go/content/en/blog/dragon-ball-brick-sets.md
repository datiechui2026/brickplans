# Dragon Ball Brick Sets: 3 Builds From Budokai to Buu

"Ka—me—ha—me—ha!" That shout cut across generations and left a mark on an entire childhood. Akira Toriyama's Dragon Ball has been running since 1984 and has grown into one of the most influential manga and anime properties in the world. With **Dragon Ball brick sets**, you can rebuild Goku's journey by hand, from the Earth tournament grounds all the way to the Tournament of Power, and let the Super Saiyan energy burn in brick form.

## What Makes Dragon Ball Brick Sets Stand Out

**Dragon Ball brick sets** are popular with builders worldwide because they capture the series' signature characters, locations and fight moments with real accuracy. Goku's Turtle School gi, Vegeta's Saiyan battle armor, the World Martial Arts Tournament stage, the battlefields of Namek — every set tells one of those high-energy stories. For MOC builders, the theme is a rich playground: distinctive character silhouettes, dynamic action poses and a whole vocabulary of effect parts.

If you are new to anime-themed MOCs, start with the [MOC beginner guide](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) to pick up the core building techniques. On [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn) you will find a large library of designs made by Dragon Ball fans around the world, from detailed Super Saiyan forms to the mechanical internals of the Dragon Radar.

## Recommended Dragon Ball Brick Sets

### 1. World Martial Arts Tournament

This is one of the most recognizable locations in the whole series. The set rebuilds the tournament stage architecture, including the spectator stands, the announcer's booth, the fighter entrance and the iconic tournament signboard. The minifigure line-up covers Goku, Krillin, Tien Shinhan and Chiaotzu. The real highlight of this **Dragon Ball brick set** is that the stage can be disassembled and rebuilt, so you can display different match-ups between fighters.

### 2. Showdown on Namek

Based on the classic Goku vs. Frieza duel in Dragon Ball Z, this set recreates the green surface of Planet Namek, Frieza's spacecraft and the Dragon Radar device. The minifigures include Goku in Super Saiyan form, Frieza in his final form and Piccolo. If you enjoy the large mech builds covered in our [Gundam LEGO guide](/en/blog/gundam-lego-guide), the Frieza spacecraft in the Namek scene packs plenty of mechanical structure detail of its own.

### 3. The Buu Battle

Inspired by the Majin Buu arc of Dragon Ball Z, this set includes the temple in the Supreme Kai's realm, Buu's egg-shaped ship and energy effect parts for the Spirit Bomb. The minifigure roster here is generous: Goku in Super Saiyan 3 form, Vegeta, Gohan and Majin Buu in both his good and evil forms. The Spirit Bomb uses transparent light-up elements, which gives it a genuinely striking look on the shelf.

## A Guide to Building Dragon Ball MOCs

### Making the Characters

The Dragon Ball world has dozens of classic characters, and most of them have multiple form changes. The [free BrickPlans instructions](/en/blog/moc-free-plans-guide) include detailed minifigure build solutions, from Super Saiyan gold hair to Goku's Flying Nimbus cloud — every detail is worth refining.

### Depicting Combat Effects

Dragon Ball fights are defined by high-speed movement and energy waves. The [brick modification techniques](/en/blog/moc-brick-modification) covered on the site let you render ki blasts, Instant Transmission and energy shields in brick form. Transparent and glow-in-the-dark parts will give your model far more visual punch.

### Building the Scenes

From Master Roshi's house on Earth to the green plains of Namek, from the cartoon styling of King Kai's planet to the alien landscape of the God of Destruction's world, the Dragon Ball universe is visually varied. The [MOC community guide](/en/blog/moc-player-community-guide) is a good place to swap scene-building notes with fellow builders.
