# Brick Christmas Tree MOC Review: 40 cm, 7 Tiers, Rotating

The short version: this brick-built Christmas tree MOC stands about 40 cm tall, splits its canopy into seven tapered tiers from bottom to top, and uses a red-gold-green palette that reads festive at a glance. ## Shape Fidelity: A Classic Tower-Shaped Canopy

The tree uses a classic conical tower structure with seven tiers from bottom to top. Each tier flares slightly outward and then tapers, so the outline curve looks natural rather than stiff like a straight cylinder. The gold star at the top is built from special pieces into a five-point shape, and its scale is balanced against the canopy. The trunk is thickened, which helps the whole tree stand securely and makes it easier to wrap a 'gift pile' around the base as a scene extension. Overall, shape fidelity is upper-middle for comparable builds: it reads as a Christmas tree at first glance, not a 'green tower.'

## Color and Decoration: The Red-Gold-Green Holiday Language

The color scheme follows the classic red-gold-green route. A dark green canopy provides the base, with red balls, gold bells, and white snowflakes dotted across it. The decorations are spaced with variation in density, so the result does not look cluttered. The designer also hides several transparent light-emitting pieces inside the canopy; when paired with a lighting module, the tree gives off a warm yellow glow and the holiday mood jumps immediately. If you prefer a cleaner look, the instructions also include a 'bare tree' version without the decorative pieces. The extra negative space actually makes it look more refined.

## Movement and Structural Design

Movement is a bonus point for this Christmas tree MOC. A gear drive is built into the base of the canopy. Turn the knob on the side of the base, and the entire canopy rotates slowly; with the lights on, it resembles a display Christmas tree in a mall. The gear set is secured with technic axles running through it, and it runs smoothly without jamming. After several minutes of continuous rotation, there is no obvious wear. More thoughtfully, most decorations attach through single-stud connections, so they are easy to remove for off-season storage or a seasonal color swap.

## Parts List and Building Experience

The parts list is dominated by green plates and bricks, plus a small number of red and gold decorative pieces and transparent pieces. There are almost no rare special pieces, so most of the demand can be covered from a home bulk collection. The build order starts with the trunk base, then stacks the canopy tier by tier upward. The tiers are similar in structure, so progress speeds up as you get familiar with the pattern. One thing to watch: the gear drive has high alignment requirements. If one tooth is off, the rotation will jam, so check the instructions carefully. The whole build can be completed in about three hours.
