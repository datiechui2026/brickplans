# Naruto Brick Building Guide: Naruto, Sasuke and Key Scenes

Naruto is one of the most popular anime IPs in the world, and its characters and locations have long been a favourite subject for brick MOC builders. Whether you want to recreate a classic Team 7 scene or rebuild the final showdown between Naruto and Sasuke at the Valley of the End, Naruto brick building lets you relive those high-energy moments in brick form.

## 1. Prep Work Before You Start Building

Before you begin your Naruto brick building project, get the following in place:

### Parts to Prepare

Building Naruto characters requires plenty of articulated joint parts and specially shaped bricks. We recommend putting together the following basic parts packs first:

- Ball joints (for posable limbs)
- Slope bricks (for recreating character hair)
- Printed pieces (for character facial expressions)

### Choosing Plans

On brickplan.cn you will find a wide range of Naruto-themed MOC plans, from simple chibi characters to complex scene recreations. If you are new to the hobby, start with entry-level Naruto brick building plans.

## 2. Building Guide for Popular Characters

### Naruto Uzumaki

When building Naruto, the priority is capturing his signature spiky blond hair and orange jacket. Stacking slope bricks and wedge bricks is an effective way to reproduce the shape of his hair.

### Sasuke Uchiha

Sasuke is harder to build because of his Sharingan and his black cloak. Use printed pieces to reproduce the Sharingan detail, and sloped skirt-style pieces to suggest the flowing movement of the cloak.

## 3. Recommended Scene Builds

Beyond individual characters, scene building is the next level of Naruto brick building. These classic scenes are worth trying:

- **Valley of the End battlefield**: the iconic Naruto vs. Sasuke duel
- **Full Konoha village panorama**: including Hokage Rock, Ichiraku Ramen and other landmark structures
- **Chunin Exam grounds**: recreating the Forest of Death and the exam arena

## 4. MOC Modification Techniques

Want to make your Naruto characters more lifelike? Try these advanced Naruto brick building techniques:

1. **Posable joint modifications**: use ball joints to increase a character's range of movement
2. **Effect piece creation**: use transparent bricks and light bricks to build ninjutsu effects
3. **Scene linking**: combine several characters into a single battle scene
