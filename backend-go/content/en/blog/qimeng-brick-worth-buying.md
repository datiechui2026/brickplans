# Is Qiji Brick Worth Buying? Age Bands and Series Value

Short answer: Qiji builds its catalogue around age bands, the space series is the clear standout, MOC builders should part out space and city sets, and the girls' line is a weak parts source.

## Is Qiji worth buying? Start with the age bands

Whether Qiji is worth buying depends on the age of the child. The product line is layered cleanly by brick size: ages 1.5-3 get large bricks, rounded and free of tiny pieces, easy for small hands to grip; ages 4-6 move up to medium bricks and scene sets; ages 6 and over enter the small-brick stage, which is compatible with LEGO. Seen that way, as a preschool introduction it costs less than half of LEGO Duplo, so the value is obvious. For a school-age child, though, you have to judge series by series.

## Series by series: which ones are worth buying

**Space series** is Qiji's signature. Long March rockets, space stations and similar subjects are reproduced faithfully, with plenty of printed elements and few stickers, and the building experience sits close to mid-range domestic standard. Most sets run CNY 60-120 with solid part counts. On space subjects alone, is Qiji worth buying? Very much so — there is almost no direct competitor at the price.

**City series** covers fire, police and construction scenes and competes head-on with SEMBO's city line. Qiji's clutch is a little tight, which makes disassembly slightly stiff, but brick tolerance is controlled consistently and finished models are sturdy enough to play with. If you like play-scene sets, the city line is worth picking up; the downside is a lot of repetitive sets, so two or three is enough.

**Girls' series** (Flower Language, dessert sets and so on) uses soft colours and plenty of special elements, but some sets break down into fiddly small pieces, so younger children need a parent alongside. On looks and price combined, the line sits mid-to-upper level at its price point.

## Compared with SEMBO and Sluban

Among the three strongest domestic brands at the same price, SEMBO is strong on licensed IP and shaping detail, Sluban is strong on original mecha and part precision, and Qiji wins on **young-age suitability and theme coverage**. If the child is 3-5, is Qiji worth buying? It has fewer sharp-cornered pieces than SEMBO and is easier to handle than Sluban, so the answer is clearly yes. If you want a more complex building experience at the same price, SEMBO vehicles and Sluban mecha are the better recommendation.

## Part-out advice for MOC builders

For MOC builders, is Qiji worth buying? The space and city series have a high proportion of generic bricks in accurate colours, making them very good value as a parts source. The girls' series has many special elements and few generic bricks, so its part-out value is ordinary. Note also that some Qiji sets fit tightly, so parting them out is less smooth than with SEMBO.

## Summary

Is Qiji worth buying? The conclusion: **buy preschool sets without hesitation, pick theme sets to suit your needs, and for MOC part-outs prioritise space and city**.
