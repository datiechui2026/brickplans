# Universal Brick Parts: Compatibility, Buying and Care Guide

For anyone who builds with bricks, **universal parts** are a topic you cannot avoid. Whether you are filling gaps in a set, building freely for an MOC, or trying to keep modification costs down, understanding universal parts makes the whole process easier and cheaper.

## What Are Universal Brick Parts?

Universal parts are standard components that work across different brands and different sets. Most bricks on the market follow the same international stud standard — the stud system most builders already know — so basic bricks, plates, slopes and similar pieces can be combined freely between brands. Knowing which parts fall into this category lets your parts collection work at its full value instead of sitting sorted by brand.

## Common Types of Universal Parts

- **Basic bricks and plates**: standard bricks in sizes such as 2x4, 2x2 and 1x6, plus thin plates in 1x2, 2x4 and other sizes. These are the most fundamental universal parts.
- **Connectors**: pins, axles and cross axles, widely used in technic builds and equally important as universal parts.
- **Slopes and decorative pieces**: roof slopes, curved pieces, window frames and door panels — these greatly expand what you can shape.
- **Functional parts**: gears, wheels and hinges. These are semi-universal: they work across brands, but you need to check that the specification matches before you buy.

## Tips for Buying Universal Parts

When buying **universal brick parts**, start with the following points:

- **Confirm the standard size**: check that the part you want follows the international stud standard, so you do not end up with an odd-size piece that will not connect.
- **Buy in bulk for parts you use often**: common parts can be purchased as loose pieces in larger quantities, which lowers the cost per piece and suits long-term building.
- **Check precision and tolerance**: good-quality parts clutch firmly and have smooth surfaces; low-quality parts loosen easily or scratch other pieces.
- **Test the mix**: when a batch arrives, test-fit it against the parts you already own and confirm compatibility before buying in large volume.

## Use and Care Advice

Sort and store universal parts by type and colour in separate compartments so you can find them quickly while building. Avoid forcing pieces together, which wears down the studs, and use a dedicated brick separator when taking models apart to protect the parts. Proper care keeps your **universal parts** in like-new condition and ready for many more builds.

## Final Thoughts

Knowing how to choose and use universal bricks saves money and, more importantly, keeps your ideas from being limited by whatever parts happen to be in the box. Use this guide as a practical reference on your build table, and start turning that loose-parts bin into your own distinct world.
