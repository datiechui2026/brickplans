# Brick Killer Whale MOC Review: 92% Accurate Black-and-White Build

The short version: this brick-built killer whale MOC lands at roughly 92% accuracy. The black-and-white color blocking is clean, and the streamlined silhouette is among the best in its class. It comes to about 1,860 pieces, costs around CNY 268, and takes roughly 4.5 hours to build — a solid pickup for orca fans.

## 1. Black and White: The Orca's Signature Colors

The orca's iconic black-and-white pattern is the first real test of accuracy. This MOC uses large black plates across the body paired with white curved parts on the belly, with curved slope bricks bridging the transition so there are no harsh, blocky color breaks. The white patches on the pectoral fins and tail fluke sit in the right places, and the white belly covers roughly 35% of the body area — close to how the real animal's markings are distributed. The one small letdown is the lack of grey transition pieces around the eyes, which looks slightly abrupt at close range.

## 2. Streamlined Shaping: One Continuous Curve From Head to Fluke

The builder stacked wedge bricks at the head to form a rounded snout, and built the dorsal fin from three curved pieces layered together, closely matching the natural arc of a real orca's fin. The mid-body uses sideways SNOT building, so the flank lines run unbroken from front to back, and the tail fluke is angled down 15° for extra sense of motion. Total length is about 48 cm, which gives it real presence on a desk. One caution: the tail joint is held by only two studs, so handle it carefully.

## 3. Accuracy and Details: That 92% Is Earned

Beyond color and outline, the small details got attention too: the pectoral fins swing up and down through about 30°, the mouth uses small dark pieces to suggest a slightly open jaw, and the blowhole is picked out with a round tile. Compared with the official orca set, this MOC is closer to real body proportions (about 1:22 scale), though its belly detailing is a little weaker. Full instructions are available on brickplan.cn, and the included step-by-step renders do a lot to cut down on misbuilds. Overall, this is one of the more accurate brick killer whale MOCs currently available.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this killer whale MOC worth buying?**

A: If you are an orca fan or a builder of marine-life subjects, it is well worth it. The 92% accuracy plus the streamlined shaping makes for an excellent display piece. If you just want something casual to put together, CNY 268 is on the high side — wait for a sale.

**Q: How much does the killer whale MOC cost?**

A: The reference price is CNY 268 for about 1,860 pieces, which works out to roughly CNY 0.14 per piece — mid-range. Some shops discount it to around CNY 230 during promotions, so compare across platforms before ordering.

**Q: Is the killer whale MOC hard to build?**

A: Difficulty is medium-to-hard, with a build time of about 4.5 hours. The mid-body SNOT section and the tail closure will challenge newer builders. Build bag by bag following the instructions, and sort the parts before you start — it goes much more smoothly.

**Q: How does this MOC differ from the official set?**

A: Mainly in scale and detail. This MOC follows real orca proportions at about 1:22 scale and is more elongated than the official set. The official set wins on structural stability and part-color variety, while this MOC's black-to-white transitions are closer to a real orca.
