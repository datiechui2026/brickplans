# Chinese Brick MOC Brands by Budget: A 2026 Buying Guide

Here is the short version: in 2026 you pick Chinese bricks by price tier — SEMBO or QiJi under 100 yuan, Double Eagle's technic line at 100-300 yuan, and either Gobricks parts or Mould King mechanical sets above 300 yuan.

By 2026 the Chinese brick MOC ecosystem has matured considerably. Part quality, instruction quality and the player community have all levelled up together. Recommending Chinese brick brands for MOC building is no longer a matter of listing names; it means matching a brand to what you actually want to spend. This guide splits the market into three price tiers so your money goes where it counts.

## Under 100 yuan: entry tier

The whole point at this price is cheap trial and error. SEMBO is the safest call: subject matter spans city, military and street-scene builds, the instruction steps are clearly labelled, part precision sits in the upper-middle of the entry tier, and the minifigures are a genuine bonus. It is an excellent first stop for anyone new to Chinese brick MOC building. The other name worth watching is QiJi, which focuses on small technic items and creative vehicles — you can buy a small mechanical set with a simple transmission inside for under 100 yuan, which is outstanding value. At this tier, favour brands whose parts are highly cross-compatible so you can mix them with other brands later.

## 100-300 yuan: advanced tier

Once your budget reaches this range you can chase better part precision and more complete structures. Double Eagle's technic series is the benchmark here: gears, motors and transmission parts are solidly made, the instruction logic is clear, and mechanical MOC modifications succeed far more often. SEMBO's higher-end line is also worth considering, with plenty of large-scale vehicles and architectural scene parts, and step-by-step instruction breakdowns that rank in the top flight among Chinese brands. For anyone serious about Chinese brick MOC building, this is the tier with the best return on investment — enough of a challenge to build skill, not so much that a mistake hurts.

## Above 300 yuan: quality tier

At this level you are paying for the part system and the instruction quality. Gobricks started life as a parts manufacturer: colours are saturated, cluch power is consistent, and the range of special elements and rare colours is the most complete available. It is the go-to parts library for hardcore MOC builders, and you can source the corresponding parts for almost any large build within the Gobricks system. Mould King, meanwhile, concentrates on high-difficulty mechanical sets — full-vehicle dynamic functions and part counts that routinely run into the tens of thousands — which suits veteran builders chasing a challenge. The logic at this tier is simple: you are paying either for part precision or for mechanical complexity.

## Buying advice

Taken together, the 2026 recommendation comes down to this: beginners start with SEMBO, intermediate builders choose Double Eagle's technic line, and hardcore builders go with Gobricks parts and Mould King mechanical sets. Whatever tier you pick, look at the instructions before buying parts, then purchase in batches against the parts list rather than stockpiling blindly.
