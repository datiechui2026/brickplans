# Christmas Tree MOC Review: 2,680 Pieces of Holiday Cheer

The short version: this Christmas tree MOC listed on BrickPlan uses 2,680 pieces to build a festive street scene roughly 45 cm tall. The build flows well, the details keep surprising you, and the holiday atmosphere is top-tier among comparable designs. If you are shopping for a Christmas-season project, this is an easy recommendation.

## 1. Looks and Detail Accuracy

The tree itself is built in two-tone layers of dark green and bright green, finished with gold ornament pieces and a white snow-capped top, which gives it excellent depth. The accessories underneath — the pile of gifts, the snowman, the street lamp — account for about 300 specialized pieces, putting the detail density at the upper end of its price bracket. The star on top is made from a transparent glow-in-the-dark piece that gives off a soft light once the room goes dark, a clever touch you rarely see in official sets. Around the tree, white plates stand in for packed snow and echo the snow on the canopy, so the overall palette stays harmonious. It looks equally at home in a living room or an entryway.

## 2. Build Experience and Time

The full build runs about 5 hours and is split into four stages: base, trunk, canopy and scene accessories. A cross-axle structure inside the trunk makes the whole tree very stable, so you do not have to worry about it falling apart when you move it. The instructions divide the steps sensibly, averaging 10-15 pieces per step, which means beginners can keep up without trouble. The only small annoyance is that the decorative balls at the top of the canopy need a firm press to seat properly — a brick separator helps here.

## 3. Value and Who It Suits

At 259 yuan for 2,680 pieces, that works out to roughly 0.1 yuan per piece. For Chinese-designed MOC instructions this sits in the middle-to-upper range, but with specialized pieces making up about 11% of the count, the value is reasonable. It suits family builds over the Christmas season, parent-and-child sessions, and display duty as a holiday ornament — once it is set up at home, the festive mood goes straight to maximum. The official instructions also include a second color scheme, so builders can switch freely between a red-and-gold or a blue-and-silver theme. One set of instructions, two distinct looks, which is not something you see often in MOCs of this type.

## FAQ

**Q: How big is the finished Christmas tree MOC?**
A: The finished model stands about 45 cm tall with a base diameter of about 25 cm, which suits a living room TV cabinet or an entryway display. The size is moderate and does not take up much room, and after the season you can break it into two sections and store them in a 65L storage box for easy rebuilding next year.

**Q: Is the Christmas tree MOC hard to build?**
A: No. Overall difficulty is below average, and it takes about 5 hours to finish. The instructions are split into four stages with few pieces per step, so anyone aged 12 and up — or an adult working with a child — can complete it without trouble. It makes a good first MOC project.

**Q: Does the Christmas tree MOC include a lighting kit?**
A: The original instructions do not include an electronic light kit, but the star on top uses a transparent glow-in-the-dark piece that lights up in the dark. You can buy an LED string separately and wrap it through the branches; about 30 yuan is enough to upgrade it to a proper light display.
