# Brick Otter MOC Review: Cute Looks, 11 Moving Parts

The short version first: this brick-built otter maxes out the charm scale, hides a surprising amount of articulation under that round body, and costs far less than you would guess. It comes in at roughly 620 pieces, a reference price of 89 CNY, about two hours of building time and an 11 cm tall finished model — a solid entry point for anyone new to MOCs.

## Cute Looks: One Glance and You're Sold

Almost all of an otter's appeal lives in the round face and the stubby paws, and this design leans into both. Large curved smooth pieces shape the head into a soft, rounded dome. The ears are built from a 1x1 round plate combined with a slope piece, angled slightly forward, which gives the whole face an unusually well-behaved look.

The classic floating-on-its-back pose is reproduced properly: white belly facing up, four short paws curled naturally, convincing enough to sit beside an aquarium and pass at a glance. Small dark round dots serve as the eyes, paired with a mouth piece tilted up at the corners — the effect is immediate.

## Articulation: Not Just a Display Piece

The head turns roughly 45 degrees left and right. The front paws connect to the shoulders through ball joints, so it can be posed rubbing its hands together or holding an object. The rear legs swing forward and back, and the tail is built from three hinge pieces that bend into a natural curve.

The best surprise is the belly, which opens up to reveal space inside sized for a small fish piece — a small touch that adds a lot of play value. In total there are 11 points of articulation across the body, which is genuinely uncommon for a MOC in this price bracket.

## Value: What 89 CNY Actually Buys

620 pieces, an 11 cm model and 11 moving joints for a reference price of 89 CNY works out to about 0.14 CNY per piece, and that is a fair deal. Official sets at the same price typically carry only 300 to 400 pieces and offer almost no articulation at all.

There are two caveats. The white belly pieces pick up dirt easily, so wash your hands before handling. And the instruction steps are densely packed — around step 40 there is a reversed slope piece that is easy to mount backwards, so check the angle carefully before pressing it down. The complete instructions are available at brickplan.cn, and the accompanying step-by-step renders do a good job of preventing mistakes.
