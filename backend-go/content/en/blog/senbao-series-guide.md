# SEMBO Brick Series Compared: Space, Military, Street & Technic

The short answer first: choose by what you want out of a build. Space has the strongest subject accuracy, military is the most playable, street scene is the easiest way in, and technic is for anyone who wants to see a mechanism work. On a limited budget, start with a small street scene set.

Which SEMBO series is best is the question nearly every builder wrestles with before a first order. SEMBO's product line runs across space, military, street scene and technic, and the experience differs considerably from one line to the next. This comparison puts the four popular series side by side across four dimensions: subject accuracy, part quality, value for money and who each one actually suits.

## Space Series: The Pick for Subject Purists

Space is SEMBO's flagship line and the one with the strongest licensing pedigree. Space stations, launch vehicles and lunar probes are reproduced with impressive accuracy, with plenty of printed elements and excellent display presence straight off the shelf. It is a natural fit for space enthusiasts, for families building together with kids, and for newcomers touching SEMBO for the first time.

The trade-off is scale. Some of the larger sets carry high part counts and take a long time to assemble, so a little patience goes a long way. If you want a quick weekend build, start smaller; if you want a centrepiece, this is where the line delivers.

## Military Series: The Pick for Playability

Military focuses on modern equipment, with tanks, fighter jets and warships that are current in subject matter and generous in moving structure. Turrets rotate, tracks roll across a table, and the high proportion of dark-coloured parts pairs with a tighter clutch to give finished models a solid, sturdy feel that holds up to handling.

If you like a model you can still play with after the last part clicks into place, military deserves priority in your shortlist. Two practical notes: dark parts show fingerprints under direct light, and you will want to plan for dust protection when you store or display them.

## Street Scene and Technic: Value vs. Mechanical Depth

Street scene is SEMBO's best-value entry line. A single building sits between entry-level and mid-range pricing, and each model works either as a standalone display piece or combined with multiple sets into a full street. The instructions are friendly and almost no special building techniques are required, which makes it the safest recommendation for beginners and for parents buying a first proper set.

Technic takes the opposite approach, centring on gear drives and mechanical structure. It suits advanced builders who enjoy working out how a mechanism functions, and it offers plenty of modification headroom — the parts are often used as chassis material for MOCs. If you are hunting for modification inspiration, browse the related builds on the [brickplan blog](https://brickplan.cn/en/blog).

## Verdict: Which SEMBO Series Is Right for You?

Back to the opening question. Match the series to your need: space fans take the space line, players take military, beginners and families take street scene, and mechanically minded builders take technic. If the budget is tight, begin with a small street scene set or an entry-level space kit, confirm that you like how SEMBO parts feel in hand, and then move up to the larger sets. Following that order, you are unlikely to end up with a purchase you regret.
