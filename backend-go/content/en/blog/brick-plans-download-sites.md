# Brick Instruction Download Sites: 2026 Picks for MOC Builders

Find the right instructions and your MOC is already half built. The problem is that brick instruction download sites are everywhere, and beginners stall at the choosing stage: which ones are reliable, which are free, and is there an app worth installing? Here is the full 2026 rundown.

## Why use a dedicated brick instruction download site?

Plenty of people assume you can just search for a plan and pull something usable off the web. In practice, a high-quality MOC plan needs a proper platform behind it. A reliable site does more than hand over a file: it pairs the download with a parts list, building notes, a difficulty rating and a community to ask questions in. That is exactly why dedicated instruction platforms have become the default for serious builders.

## The instruction sites worth bookmarking in 2026

### 1. brickplan.cn — the all-in-one platform

**brickplan.cn** is one of the most active brick plan sharing communities around. Its strengths:

- **A huge library**: modular street scenes, technic, military, mecha, minifigures and more, covering most player interests
- **A friendly interface**: everything runs in Chinese, and beginners can pick it up quickly
- **One-click brickplan downloads** with no complicated steps
- **Parts lists included**: every plan ships with a detailed parts breakdown so you can source what you need
- **Community features**: comment, like, save and talk directly to the designer

For anyone just starting out, brickplan.cn is a site you can bookmark on day one.

### 2. Rebrickable — the largest international library

Rebrickable is the biggest MOC instruction community in the world, with more than 100,000 plans. Key features:

- You can upload your parts inventory and it automatically matches plans you can actually build
- Designer quality is generally high
- Some plans are paid

The catch: Rebrickable is entirely in English, which is a barrier for some users. Most plans are also designed around LEGO parts, so if you are substituting other brands you will need to work out replacements yourself.

### 3. BrickLink Studio — design and download in one place

BrickLink Studio is not just an instruction download platform but a powerful MOC design tool. You can:

- Design your own MOC in the Studio software
- Generate professional building instruction PDFs
- Upload straight to the BrickLink marketplace to buy parts

Studio has a steeper learning curve, so it suits players with some experience behind them.

### 4. Video platforms as an instruction source

On Bilibili, Douyin, Xiaohongshu and similar platforms, plenty of brick creators share the plans they have designed. Searching for free brick instruction downloads turns up a lot of material. The downside is that it is scattered and quality varies wildly, so you will spend time filtering.

## Where to get brick instructions for free

### Completely free sources

1. **The brickplan.cn free section**: the platform hosts a large amount of free material, so beginners can start practising on free plans first
2. **Brand community hubs**: official communities from brands such as Gobricks and Qiji offer free instruction downloads
3. **YouTube and Bilibili**: many creators share plan links for free, and following along with the video tutorial works better than the file alone

### When paid instructions are worth it

Paid plans are usually higher quality, with more detailed steps, and the designer will often provide support. A good plan typically costs 10-50 yuan, which is small next to what you already spend on bricks.

## How to choose the right platform for you

### By experience level
- **Beginner**: start with brickplan.cn — interface, free plans and parts lists in one place
- **Intermediate**: use brickplan.cn and Rebrickable side by side to widen your sources
- **Advanced**: learn BrickLink Studio so you can design and publish your own plans

### By theme
- **Modular buildings and architecture**: brickplan.cn has a deep catalogue of street scene plans

## Things to watch out for when downloading instructions

### Copyright

Check the copyright notice on any plan you download. Personal use is normally fine, but do not redistribute paid plans. Plans on brickplan.cn carry clear copyright labels, and respecting a designer's work is basic etiquette for every builder.

### File formats

Common formats are PDF, HTML and Studio files. PDF is the most universal and is easy to print for building at the table; HTML supports interactive viewing; Studio files are meant for use on a computer. brickplan downloads cover several formats so you can pick what suits your setup.

### Part checks

After downloading, read the parts list first and confirm you own enough pieces before you start building. Plans on brickplan.cn note part quantities and where they come from, so you can prepare in advance.
