# Missing WEIJI Instructions? How to Find or Rebuild Them

WEIJI is one of the newer creative brick brands on the scene, and it has won plenty of fans with fresh IP collaborations and sharp design work. So it stings when you are halfway through a build — or you have just opened the box — and the instruction booklet is nowhere to be found. Don't panic: a missing WEIJI manual does not mean the model can never be finished. Here is a full set of solutions.

## 1. Stay Calm and Confirm the Situation

When you first notice the booklet is gone, slow down and check the obvious. Think about where the instructions might be — still inside the box, slipped between the pages of a book, or left in another room. Sometimes it is only temporarily misplaced, and a careful search turns it up.

If it really is gone, there is still no reason to worry. Like other brands, WEIJI offers digital versions of most of its instructions, and these can fully replace the paper booklet for the whole build.

## 2. Look for the Official Digital Instructions

The most direct fix for a missing WEIJI manual is to get the official electronic version. Go to the official WEIJI website or the brand's official WeChat account and look under the "instruction downloads" or "manuals" section for the set you own. Usually you only need to enter the set number or set name to pull up the matching PDF download.

The digital version contains exactly the same content as the printed one: complete step-by-step diagrams and a full parts list. That is more than enough to finish the build. Once downloaded, you can view it on a phone, tablet or computer whenever you need it, which is often handier than a paper booklet anyway.

## 3. Search Brick Instruction Platforms

If the official channels don't have your set, try a dedicated brick instruction sharing platform. At **https://brickplan.cn** you will find a large library of user-uploaded instructions and MOC resources covering many brands and series. Search by keyword to see whether someone has posted the instructions for your set — plenty of helpful builders scan or tidy up their own booklets and share them with the community.

Social media and brick hobbyist forums also regularly host shared instruction files. When you search, always check the **set number** carefully so you know you have the correct version of the instructions and not a similar-looking set from the same line.

## 4. Rebuild by Taking Stock of the Parts

If no complete digital copy turns up, you can try rebuilding from the physical parts. Many sets are not structurally unique, so the following approach can work: first **sort and count every part** so you have a rough idea of what each section of the model does; then **study official photos of the finished set** to read its exterior and internal structure; finally, use the shapes and quantities of the parts you have to restore the build step by step.

Rebuilding is more of a challenge than following a manual, but it brings its own kind of satisfaction. For large, complex sets the difficulty is high, so it is worth prioritizing the search for the original instructions before you commit to this route.

## 5. Small Habits That Stop It Happening Again

A few good habits will save you from losing the booklet a second time:

- **Store the instructions separately** in a fixed spot, such as a folder or a shelf, rather than mixed in with other clutter.
- **Keep a digital backup** of the instructions on your phone or a cloud drive so you can check them anytime.
- **Don't move the booklet around mid-build** — put it back in its place when you are done with a session.

Build these habits and a missing WEIJI manual stops being a real problem, keeping the whole building process smooth.

## 6. Make Good Use of Instruction Libraries

Losing a manual is frustrating, but the solutions are plentiful. The one I recommend most is **making good use of an online instruction library**, which handles finding, checking and printing instructions in a single place. To sum it up: a missing WEIJI manual is not a disaster. Search carefully first, then look for a digital version through official channels or an instruction platform, and fall back on rebuilding from the parts if you have to.
