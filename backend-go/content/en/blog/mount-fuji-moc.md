## Shape and Realism

The hardest design problem in any Mount Fuji MOC is where the snow cap meets the mountain body. This design handles it with three brick colours — white, light grey and dark grey — to build a gradient of snow cover, with the snow line sitting about four-fifths of the way up the mountain. The visual proportion sits close to the real thing.

The mountain itself is stacked in layers using slope bricks and SNOT techniques, with the slope angle at roughly 35 degrees. Viewed from the side, the lines run continuously with no obvious stair-stepping. The base scene adds cherry blossom trees, a lake and a torii gate, and accounts for about 15 percent of the total part count — enough to make the finished model read like a three-dimensional landscape painting rather than a plain peak on a plate.
