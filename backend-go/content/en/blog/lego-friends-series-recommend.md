# LEGO Friends Buying Guide: Heartlake City Sets

The short answer first: newcomers to LEGO Friends should start with one of the small shop sets to test the style, the mid-range ¥400–¥800 tier offers the best balance, and the parts are so interchangeable that the theme works well as a parts library for MOC building.

For builders chasing a "detailed and cosy" brick experience, LEGO Friends is hard to avoid. Set in the fictional Heartlake City, the theme uses bright colours, carefully finished buildings and everyday street scenes to construct a warm small-town world — a favourite with detail-focused builders and parents building alongside children.

## Heartlake City: A Carefully Crafted Town

What defines LEGO Friends is how thoroughly it reproduces everyday life. From cafés, bakeries and hair salons to lighthouses, pet clinics and city-centre apartments, every Heartlake City building comes with a complete interior — tables and chairs, fridges, kitchenware, decorative plants. The finished level of detail stands out against sets at the same price. Minifigures use the theme's distinctive cute art style, and the accompanying pets, vehicles and accessories are unusually generous, giving the whole range a polished, comforting look.

## Classic Sets at a Glance

Sets fall into three rough tiers by size. Entry-level sets are mostly small shops or street-corner scenes at about ¥100–¥300, with roughly two hundred pieces — good for sampling the theme's style. Mid-range sets form the backbone of Heartlake City, including the café and the pet clinic: five to six hundred pieces at about ¥400–¥800, with strong interior detail and plenty of play features. Flagship sets are large landmarks and combined scenes such as the city-centre buildings or the amusement park — over a thousand pieces at about ¥900–¥1800, and the first choice for builders who want a complete street. Some sub-themes also release festive limited editions with transparent elements and lighting effects, which carry higher collectible value.

## Buying Advice: Match Playstyle and Budget

For a first purchase, start with a small shop: build time is short, the sense of achievement comes quickly, and you'll learn fast whether you like the theme's style. If scene building is your thing, prioritise buildings with multiple rooms — they carry the richest detail. For parent-and-child play, sets with pets and moving accessories offer the best value, since children can replay them again and again. One warning: some LEGO Friends sets risk retirement, and popular models can rise in price, so it's worth acting decisively when you see a good deal.

## LEGO Friends and MOC Building

Heartlake City's architectural parts are mainly plates and window and door elements, which are extremely versatile — ideal as a "materials warehouse" for MOCs. Many builders modify official buildings, combine them into street corners in their own style, or add original shops to the town. On the brickplan site you can search for player write-ups on LEGO Friends recommendations, and also find MOC instructions for street scenes, architecture and everyday-life settings to add a unique corner to your own Heartlake City.

## Final Thoughts

LEGO Friends builds a warm, comforting town life out of one carefully finished building after another. Whether you're building quietly on your own or sharing the table with a child, Heartlake City keeps giving — and every new build makes the town a little warmer.
