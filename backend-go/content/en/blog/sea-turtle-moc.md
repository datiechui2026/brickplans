# Brick Sea Turtle MOC Review: Shell Texture and Poseable Flippers

The short version first: this is one of the larger ocean-animal MOCs on the market. It uses roughly 890 pieces and finishes at about 28 cm long, with the instruction file at 28 yuan and loose parts running about 165 yuan. Expect around four hours of building. The shell recreates the hexagonal scute pattern at roughly 88% fidelity, all four flippers are mounted on ball joints so you can set a swimming pose, and the finished model has enough presence to anchor a display shelf.

## 1. Shell Accuracy: Texture and Layering

The shell is what makes a sea turtle instantly readable, and this design leans into it. Hexagonal and trapezoid pieces are layered together to build the scute pattern, with a deep green running through the centre and a lighter green handling the transition at the edges. The carapace arches slightly rather than sitting flat, which is what sells the silhouette from a side angle. Overall fidelity lands at roughly 88%.

The plastron underneath is built from beige pieces into a gently curved plate, and the head and flippers use light and dark green in distinct layers instead of a single flat colour. The palette stays clean and cool throughout, so the model reads as an ocean animal rather than a generic green creature.

## 2. Build Experience: Poseability and Stability

Each of the four flippers connects to the body through a ball joint, so they swing up and down and hold a paddling position instead of being frozen in place. The head also turns through a small range. Together that gives the model real life when you photograph it or rearrange it on a shelf.

The centre of gravity sits low, and once all four flippers are down the turtle settles and stays put without wobbling. The instructions are split into 12 steps, each held to 12 pieces or fewer, and the whole build takes about four hours. The one genuinely tricky stretch is the symmetrical assembly of the shell texture — work both halves side by side and compare as you go rather than finishing one side first.

## 3. Value: Instructions and Part Cost

For a large-scale ocean animal MOC the value is solid, and the finished turtle has real presence on a coffee table or inside a display cabinet.
