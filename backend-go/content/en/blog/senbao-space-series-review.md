# SEMBO Space Series Review: Worth Buying?

The short version: SEMBO's space line is solidly engineered, its printed parts are crisp, the subject matter is faithfully reproduced, and the value sits near the top of Chinese space bricks. Beginners are safest starting with an entry-level set.

Is SEMBO's space series worth it? That is the first question most builders ask when they consider getting into Chinese-made space bricks. SEMBO is one of the few domestic brands holding a space-themed license, and the range covers space stations, launch vehicles and lunar probes. This review looks at the real-world experience across four angles: structure, parts, printing and value.

## Build Structure and Accuracy

In practice, the structural design of SEMBO's space sets sits in the upper-middle tier of Chinese bricks. The space station sets use modular assembly: the core module, laboratory module and robotic arm are each built as separate sub-assemblies, the build order is clear, and the final docking holds firm without any loose, wobbly sections. On the launch vehicles, the joints between the core stage and the boosters get extra reinforcement, so the model stays stable on its centre of gravity when displayed upright. On accuracy, the module proportions and window positions reference the real vehicles, and lined up together the finished models have a real launch-site sense of occasion.

## Part Quality and Print Precision

Part quality is where the space series earns its best marks. Clutch is moderate across the board, the fit is smooth going together, and several rounds of disassembly and reassembly do not leave stress whitening; special parts such as hatches and solar-panel bases are moulded cleanly. The standout is the printed parts: module livery, national flag markings and window details are all printed rather than stickered, with sharp edges and clear graphics that will not peel off after long play. The one small drawback is that a few transparent parts carry fine scratches; if that bothers you, keep a polishing cloth on hand.

## Value and Who It Suits

Pricing across the space series is wide. The small lunar rovers and probes land at entry-level money and are ideal for a first try; the space station and launch vehicle sets sit in the mid range and are the value core of the line; large launch-scene sets need a budget of under 1,000 yuan, aimed at advanced builders and display collectors. Overall, the series suits space enthusiasts, parent-and-child builders and newcomers buying Chinese bricks for the first time: the subject matter is strong, the margin for error is high, and the cost of a failed experiment is low.

## Verdict

So, is SEMBO's space series worth it? My answer: faithful subject reproduction, excellent printed parts and durable structures make it one of the most sensible picks in Chinese space bricks right now.
