# Brick Moose MOC Review: 620-Piece Christmas Display Build

The short version: this brick moose MOC uses roughly 620 pieces and stands about 19 cm tall by 16 cm long. Curved bricks and slopes recreate the animal's signature palmate antlers, long muzzle and slim legs, landing at roughly 85% visual accuracy. As a display piece it sits in the top tier of Christmas animal MOCs. It is aimed at builders aged 14 and up, and sourcing the parts as loose bricks costs about CNY 140-180.

## Exterior Accuracy

The hardest feature to get right on a moose is the antler pair. The designer builds each antler from branched brown bars joined in several segments, then stacks smaller forks on top so the silhouette reads full and layered rather than flat. The long muzzle transitions through curved and sloped bricks, with the bridge of the nose slightly raised, and the proportions stay close to a real moose.

Overall accuracy is about 85%. The side profile is where it looks most convincing, and the red-and-green accents carry the Christmas mood without overwhelming the animal itself.

## Structural Design

Head and neck connect to the body through a ball joint, so the head can tilt up, down and slightly left or right for a more natural pose. The legs mount into the body on technic axles, and all four feet sit flat on the ground without the model tipping forward.

The body is left hollow to cut weight. Even so, about 620 pieces hold plenty of rigidity, and a knock on the shelf will not shake it apart.

## Building Experience

The instructions come from brickplan.cn. In testing the build took about 2.5 hours across 4 stages.

The antler branching is the difficult stretch: roughly 30 pieces have to be locked in at precise angles one after another. Leg-to-body connections are clearly keyed, so it is hard to seat them wrong. Steps are well sequenced throughout, and no stickers are involved. All the printed elements go on the antler bases and the bell on the chest.

## Value and Who It's For

Sourcing the parts costs about CNY 140-180, close to half the price of a Christmas set of comparable size. It suits builders putting together a Christmas-themed scene, collectors of animal display models, and anyone hunting for a holiday gift. Placed under the tree or beside the fireplace, the red and green palette lifts the room immediately.

## FAQ

**Q: What age is this brick moose MOC suitable for?**

A: 14 and up is recommended. The antler branching and leg connections call for fine motor precision, and the pieces run small, so younger children may lose parts. With a parent helping, the build takes about 2.5 hours, and the finished model is sturdy enough to stay on display long term.

**Q: How much does the brick moose MOC cost?**

A: Parts run about CNY 140-180. The instructions offer a free preview of some steps on brickplan.cn. If you already keep brown and beige bricks in stock, the real outlay can drop under CNY 100, which makes the value considerably better.

**Q: What is the difference between a moose MOC and a reindeer MOC?**

A: A moose has palmate, forked antlers, a larger body and a longer face. A reindeer's antlers are thinner and longer, and its neck is shorter. This moose MOC follows the palmate antler shape, so it reads clearly differently from the reindeer pulling Santa's sleigh, and it is easy to tell apart at a glance.

**Q: Is the brick moose MOC worth buying?**
