# Brick Set Missing a Piece Mid-Build? How to Fix It

Almost every builder knows the moment: you are moving fast through the instructions, then a part shown in the diagram simply cannot be found. You empty the bag, check the floor, check again, and it is still gone. This guide walks through the full process, from a careful search to getting the correct part delivered, so your build does not stall out.

## 1. Search Properly Before You Assume a Part Is Missing

Before you declare a part missing, do a complete check.

First, **tip every part out of the bag you are working from** and count them against the parts list in the instructions. Parts often hide at the bottom of the bag or underneath larger pieces.

Second, check whether the part slipped into the previous bag or fell somewhere around your workspace. Table edges, chair seats and carpet seams are classic hiding places for small tiles, plates and pins.

Third, check whether the part has been **installed in the wrong place**. A lot of "missing" parts are actually misplaced parts: a piece meant for step 40 got used in step 12, so it is no longer in the bag when you need it. Reread the steps you have already finished and compare them to the diagrams. Only after this full check should you move on to the next option.

## 2. Look for a Substitute Part

If you own other sets, **dig through your loose parts for something close in shape and size**. Brick parts are highly standardized, so a piece in a different colour but with the same function can usually stand in temporarily without affecting the structure. **Generic bricks and connectors** are the easiest to swap.

Also **check the instructions for extra or optional parts**. Some sets ship with a few spare small pieces, and one of them may be exactly what you need. Finishing the build with a stand-in and swapping in the correct part later is standard practice among experienced builders.

## 3. Request an Official Replacement Part

If no substitute works, the proper route is to **ask the brand for a replacement**. Most brick brands, including Chinese manufacturers, run a missing-part service. You can usually submit a request through the customer service page on the brand's website, its official social or messaging account, or the shop's customer service on the marketplace where you bought the set.

Expect to provide **the set number, the part number of the missing piece (it appears in the parts list in the instructions) and your shipping details**. A photo showing where the part appears in the instruction diagram helps the agent confirm the request and speeds things up. Turnaround varies by brand, typically anywhere from a few days to about two weeks.

## 4. Contact the Seller

If you bought the set from an online marketplace, **messaging the seller directly** is often the fastest option. Sellers will generally send the missing parts free of charge, and some will add a small compensation. Be friendly and describe the problem clearly, and it usually gets resolved without friction.

**Keep your order confirmation and the packaging** so the seller can verify the purchase. If the seller refuses to help, you can escalate to the platform's own customer service to protect your rights as a buyer.

## 5. What to Build While You Wait

While the replacement is in transit, **work on the sections that do not depend on the missing part** and leave that spot for last. Many sets are built as separate modules, so you can complete the main structure first and drop in the small missing piece at the end without losing progress.

If the part sits in a critical spot inside the internal structure and cannot be skipped, clear a dedicated space on your desk for the half-finished assembly and continue once the part arrives.

## 6. Small Habits That Prevent Missing Parts

To cut down on this problem in future, **sort and count the parts before you start**, grouping them by colour and checking them against the list. Keep your desk tidy while building so nothing disappears into clutter. And **open bags one at a time** as the instructions call for them, rather than emptying every bag at once.

The core approach is simple: verify first, substitute second, and use the official or seller replacement channels last.
