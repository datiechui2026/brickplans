# Brick Guitar MOC Review: 1,150-Part Playable Rock Display

The short version: this brick guitar MOC is one of the most popular instrument designs on brickplan, and it is a rock display piece you can actually play with. It uses about 1,150 parts, measures 38 cm long when finished, and its six strings really do move and make a sound when you pluck them. Design accuracy comes in at roughly 88%, the build takes around 5 hours, and sourcing the loose parts costs about 260–380 CNY. It earns its place on a desk or in a music room.

## 1. Structure and Accuracy

The designer used smooth tiles to shape the flowing body and headstock of the electric guitar, with a long, straight, slender neck. Frets, pickups and control knobs are all present and detailed. At the bridge, six strings are tensioned with flexible cord, and plucking them produces a light sound close to an acoustic guitar.

The standout feature is the headstock: the tuners rotate, so you can adjust how tight each string is. The sound is not meant to be in tune, but the playable feel is about 88% faithful to the real thing, which makes it far more engaging than a purely static display model.

## 2. Parts and Color Scheme

The model uses roughly 1,150 parts in a red-and-black contrast scheme, with a white pickguard and gold pickups at the center of the body for a strong rock look. Every pattern is done with printed parts, not stickers, and the wood-grain effect on the body comes from alternating light and dark red bricks that build up visible depth.

Part tolerances are well controlled, with clutch power sitting in a comfortable middle range. The only small complaint is that the red bricks pick up fingerprints easily, so you will want to wipe the body down after handling it.

## 3. Building Experience

Working from the PDF instructions available on brickplan.cn, our tester finished the model in about 5 hours. The steps are split into 5 major stages. The curved surfaces of the body come in stage 2, where you need to keep adjusting angles to find a smooth, even curve.

The instructions are clearly labeled, each step uses a sensible number of parts, and the overall logic flows well. The part that demands the most patience is installing the strings. Follow the order in the instructions and fix both ends one after the other; a pair of tweezers makes the job considerably easier.

## 4. Value for Money

Sourcing the loose parts costs about 260–380 CNY, and you can gather everything by working through the parts list on brickplan.cn. That works out roughly 35% cheaper than a comparable-size retail set. Given the pluckable strings, the high proportion of printed parts and the 38 cm display presence, the price is a clear win.

It suits rock music fans and collectors of desktop display pieces, and it also makes a good gift for men who love music. Anyone aged 12 and up can complete it independently.
