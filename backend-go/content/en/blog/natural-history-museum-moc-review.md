# Natural History Museum MOC Review: 10,500 Bricks

The short version first: this natural history museum MOC is a centerpiece build. Around 10,500 pieces produce an 88 x 56 cm exhibition building with three halls, assembly runs about 36 hours, the parts budget lands near 1,700 CNY, and the difficulty sits at medium-high. The dinosaur skeleton hall, the mineral hall and the animal specimen hall are all finished to a high standard, and once it is standing you have a miniature natural history museum on your shelf. Builders who chase big scenes should pay attention.

## Scene accuracy

The model's accuracy rests on its three exhibition zones.

The dinosaur skeleton hall is the undisputed lead. A Tyrannosaurus skeleton roughly 30 cm tall stands in the middle of the hall, built from white bone elements that follow real skeletal structure. The skull, spine and tail vertebrae read as clearly separated sections, and standing inside the open, full-height hall it has genuine presence. The mineral hall uses transparent elements in different colors to simulate crystals and ore on display inside glass cases; with the light elements switched on it genuinely feels like a gem hall. The animal specimen hall is a row of specimen plinths, with mammoths, deer and birds staggered across it, backed by ecological backdrop panels. The three halls are clearly layered against each other, and the fidelity is a solid 9 out of 10.

## Building experience

The roughly 10,500 pieces arrive split into 16 numbered bags, and the build advances in the order of hall framework, dinosaur skeleton, mineral hall, specimen hall, then the surrounding landscape. The skeleton is both the hardest and the most satisfying stretch: about 700 bone elements are joined section by section following the numbering, and the spine only gains its three-dimensional curve once the arc is aligned properly. A tip worth following is to build the skull and the tail vertebrae separately first and then join them together. Aligning the transparent elements in the mineral hall tests patience in much the same way. The whole thing takes about 36 hours, of which the dinosaur skeleton accounts for roughly 8. Clutch power is tight and nothing was missing from the bags, which makes it the classic large-scale build: tiring, but worth it.

## Value and verdict

At a budget of about 1,700 CNY, the cost works out to roughly 0.16 CNY per piece. Add the oversized 88 x 56 cm footprint and the completeness of all three halls, and the value stands out clearly among large MOCs. The drawbacks are real but predictable: it demands a lot of display space, and precision structures like the dinosaur skeleton are not something you want to take apart and rebuild often. Overall, dinosaur fans, nature-themed builders and anyone chasing a strong display presence can buy with confidence, and it works well as a parent-and-child project too.
