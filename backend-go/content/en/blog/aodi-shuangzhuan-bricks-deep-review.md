# Auldey Bricks Review: Value, Part Quality and MOC Potential

The short version: Auldey is one of the strongest value picks in the Chinese brick market. It suits MOC builders who need to stock up on bulk parts, its clutch and printing sit comfortably above average, and its precision trails Gobricks slightly. It is well worth trying if you are new to the brand.

Auldey built its name on slot cars and mini 4WD racers long before it entered the brick market. This review looks at the bricks from four angles — part quality, clutch power, printed elements and MOC potential — so you can decide whether a set deserves your money.

## 1. Part Quality and Clutch Power

Auldey uses a PC+ABS compound. The surface texture is grainier than LEGO and cleaner than what small workshops turn out. In testing, the clutch runs tight overall: the first build needs a firm press, and after repeated disassembly and reassembly the fit loosens a little, but parts do not slop around or fall off the way they do on budget brands. Taking a roughly 800-piece engineering truck as the test case, there were no obvious slipping parts across the whole build, and overall rigidity lands in the upper-middle range.

## 2. Printed Parts Quality

Printing is where Auldey earns extra points. In typical sets, printed bricks make up around 15%–20% of the parts, the graphics are sharp, the colors are saturated, and there is essentially no color misregistration. Compared with Gobricks UV printing, Auldey's wear resistance is a step behind, but at this price point the result is genuinely good.

## 3. Value for Money

This is Auldey's headline selling point. For a comparable roughly 1,000-piece sports car, LEGO runs 500–700 yuan while Auldey costs just 80–120 yuan — about one sixth of the price. Measured against loose Gobricks MOC parts, an Auldey set works out to roughly 0.1 yuan per piece, which makes it practical to stockpile in volume for diorama and city-scene builds.

## 4. MOC Potential and Compatibility

Compatibility is what MOC builders care about most. In testing, Auldey parts mixed with Gobricks and LEGO at over 95% compatibility: basic bricks, plates and Technic axle and pin dimensions all match the standard and combine without trouble. Two caveats. First, rack and gear parts have looser tolerances, so use Gobricks to reinforce any high-load drivetrain. Second, a few specialty pieces differ from LEGO color codes, so check a color chart before mixing them into an existing build.

## 5. Who Should Buy

Students on a tight budget, diorama builders who need large volumes of basic bricks, and newcomers trying MOC for the first time will all find Auldey a high-value choice. If you are chasing the tightest possible clutch and collector-grade quality, keep Gobricks or LEGO as your main supply and treat Auldey as the supplement.

## FAQ

**Q: Can Auldey bricks be mixed with LEGO?**
A: Yes. Basic bricks, plates, and Technic axle and pin dimensions meet the standard, and tested compatibility is above 95%, so they combine without issue. Rack and gear tolerances are looser, though, so reinforce high-load drivetrains with Gobricks.

**Q: How is Auldey brick quality, and is it worth buying?**
A: It is worth it. Clutch runs tight and printed parts are sharp, and a roughly 1,000-piece sports car costs only 80–120 yuan — about one sixth of LEGO's price. That makes it excellent value for budget-conscious builders and MOC beginners.

**Q: What is the difference between Auldey and Gobricks?**
A: Gobricks is more precise with a smoother feel, which suits fine-detail MOC work. Auldey comes in cheaper at about 0.1 yuan per piece with richer printed parts, which suits mass production and stockpiling basic bricks. The two complement each other.

**Q: Are Auldey bricks loose when assembled?**
A: Not really. The first build is on the tight side and needs a firm press; after several teardowns it loosens slightly but parts do not drop off. Overall rigidity is upper-middle and far sturdier than low-cost workshop brands.

**Q: How much does an Auldey set cost?**
A: Small sets run 20–50 yuan, mid-size sets 80–150 yuan, and 1,000-piece large sets 150–300 yuan depending on the series. That places the brand in the lower-to-middle price bracket of Chinese bricks, with strong value throughout.
