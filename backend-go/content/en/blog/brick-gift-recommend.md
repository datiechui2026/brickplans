# Brick Gift Guide: What to Buy at Three Budget Tiers

Short answer first: splitting brick gifts into three budget tiers is the least stressful way to shop. Under CNY 200, pick a small scene of 300–800 pieces. Between CNY 200 and 500, aim for a mid-size set of around 1,500 pieces. Above CNY 500, you are in flagship territory at 3,000 pieces or more. Fine-tune the theme to the recipient's interests after that and you will almost never miss.

## Under CNY 200: Small scenes are the safest choice

This tier suits colleagues, classmates and friends who have only just got into the hobby. Look for small scenes in the 300–800 piece range — a flower shop, a little food truck, a mini street corner. Expect to pay roughly CNY 80–180, with a build time of about 2–4 hours and a finished model that sits perfectly on a desk.

The one thing to avoid is no-name brands that inflate their piece counts. Check the piece count and the recommended age printed on the packaging, and buy from a seller who states both clearly.

## CNY 200–500: The best value tier

For friends and partners, start here. Street scenes, sports cars and castles in this bracket run about 1,200–2,000 pieces, cost around CNY 220–450 and take 5–8 hours to build. You get both a hands-on building experience and something worth displaying, the theme choice is at its widest, and this is the tier where a gift is least likely to fall flat.

## Above CNY 500: Flagship sets that make an impression

Choose this tier for someone important, or for a serious builder. Flagship models of 3,000–5,000 pieces run about CNY 550–900 and take 15–20 hours — good for building slowly together over a holiday. Before you order, confirm the recipient actually has room to store it: large sets typically need a footprint of 40 cm square or more once completed.

## Three details that make the gift land

1. Go for a brand-new, unopened boxed set. The unboxing is part of the gift.
2. Don't pick an obscure theme. Watch what the person normally builds first.
3. Slip a note into the packaging pointing to brickplan.cn instructions, so that once the build is finished they can find expansion instructions for the same series. It doubles the mileage of the gift.

## FAQ

**Q: How much is appropriate to spend on a brick gift?**
A: For everyday gifting, CNY 100–300. For an important occasion, the CNY 300–800 tier. If the budget is tight, prioritise piece count and how well the theme fits the person — that lands better than blindly chasing the biggest set.

**Q: How do I choose differently for a child and for an adult?**
A: For a child, check the age rating and the size of the parts first, to guard against swallowing. For an adult, theme interest and piece count matter more. Adults care about the building process; children care about the finished model.

**Q: Should I open the set to check it before giving it?**
A: No. A new, sealed box gives the strongest sense of occasion. If you are worried about missing parts, remind the recipient to check the parts list before building and to contact the seller for replacements — that does not spoil the gift experience.

**Q: Can domestic brick brands and LEGO be mixed in one gift?**
A: Yes. Most domestic brick brands are compatible with LEGO elements. A common pairing is a domestic-brand set plus a LEGO minifigure; when the budget is limited, this combination gives you more for your money.

**Q: How can I find out which theme the person likes in advance?**
A: Look at finished models they have posted on social media, or simply ask what they have been building lately. If you are still unsure, pick a broadly appealing theme such as a street scene or a botanical build — that has the lowest chance of going wrong.
