# Ninja Turtles Brick Build: Characters, Sewer Lair, Action Scenes

Few childhood cartoons stick like **Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles**. Four mutated turtles living under New York, fueled by pizza and a sense of justice. Building that world in brick has become a favourite project for MOC (My Own Creation) builders, and this guide walks you through the core techniques from a blank baseplate to a finished display piece.

## 1. Character Design: Making All Four Turtles Distinct

The first and most important step is shaping four turtles with clearly different personalities. They share the same body template, but each one's signature features have to read at a glance.

### 1.1 Body Foundation

A standard minifigure scale is a good reference point, but for better articulation go with a **brick-built joint structure**:

- **Head**: use a dome brick as the base of the shell-shaped head, with 1x1 round bricks for the eyes. Leave attachment points on both sides of the head for the mask ties.
- **Torso**: stack 2x3 slope bricks to create the raised curve of the shell. **Use dark green or earth brown for the shell** so it separates visually from the skin tone.
- **Limbs**: connect arms and legs with hinge bricks so the limbs can be posed freely in fighting stances.

### 1.2 Signature Colours for the Four Brothers

- **Leonardo**: blue mask, twin katana. Fix the sheaths to both hips with clip parts.
- **Raphael**: red mask, sai. He has the shortest fuse, so give him a printed head with a furious wide-eyed expression.
- **Donatello**: purple mask, bo staff. Add a pair of glasses to underline the brains of the team.
- **Michelangelo**: orange mask, nunchaku. His pose should be the most energetic, so build him mid-kick on one leg.

## 2. Scene Building: Recreating the Sewer Lair

With the four turtles done, the next job is the **sewer lair** they live in. This is where a Ninja Turtles build really shines.

### 2.1 The Pipe System

- **Main pipes**: use plenty of curved slopes and cylinder bricks to build the round cross-section of the sewer tunnels. Grey and dark grey are the dominant colours.
- **Ladder and manhole**: lay 1xN tiles for the floor, then fit an opening manhole cover in one corner. Below the cover, run a vertical shaft and use grille tiles to suggest ladder rungs.
- **Water flow**: transparent blue bricks or 1x1 transparent round plates stand in for the running water, dotted along the bottom of the pipes to bring the scene to life.

### 2.2 Living Area Details

- **Pizza box stack**: stack 2x2 tiles in red-and-white stripes, then add a round flat tile on top as the pizza.
- **TV and game console**: a 1x2 black tile makes the screen, framed with 1x1 bricks. A 2x2 turntable base works as an arcade joystick.
- **Training corner**: build a wooden dummy from 1x1 cylinder bricks locked together with Technic pins, and add dumbbells from 2x2 round bricks plus bar handle parts.

## 3. Advanced Techniques: Dynamic Scenes and Battle Effects

To push your Ninja Turtles build to the next level, try a **dynamic combat scene**.

### 3.1 Flying Props and Effect Pieces

- **Shuriken**: combine a 1x1 round plate with a 1x1 cheese slope to form a four-pointed star.
- **Smoke effects**: stack transparent grey and white loose parts such as plant leaves to mimic an explosion or smoke bomb.
- **Pose stands**: use transparent bars on a base to suspend a turtle mid-air for a flying kick or a jump.

### 3.2 The Villain: Shredder

No hero story works without a villain. When you build Shredder, the key is the **metallic armour**:

- Use chrome silver parts or metallic silver bricks.
- Mount 2x2 slopes upside down for the shoulder armour, giving it a three-dimensional look.
- For the mask, use a black visor part paired with red printed eyes to maximise the sense of menace.

### 3.3 Vehicle: The Party Wagon

If your parts bin is well stocked, take on the classic **Party Wagon**:

- Green and orange dominate the body, with a Technic frame underneath for strength.
- Large wheel hub parts give it the off-road stance.
- The roof opens up so all four turtles can be dropped inside.
- Add two round headlights at the front using transparent yellow dome bricks.

## 4. Building Notes and Advice

After several Ninja Turtles builds, here are a few lessons worth passing on:

1. **Sort colours first**: before you start, separate the main colour groups so the four turtles' palettes never get mixed up.
2. **Prioritise joint articulation**: the joints are the soul of a brick-built figure. Spend the extra time refining the hinge structure rather than leaving a character stuck standing straight.
3. **Treat scene and figures as equals**: four characters on their own are good, but paired with a well-designed sewer scene the display value doubles.
4. **Use third-party parts wisely**: special printed faces and weapon parts are available through third-party channels and can lift a character's expressiveness considerably.
5. **Study the original animation**: look at the art design of the 1987 and 2003 series and pull proportion and composition ideas from them.
