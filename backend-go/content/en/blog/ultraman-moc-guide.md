# How to Build a Brick Ultraman MOC: Chest, Timer, Colors

Short version: a convincing Ultraman MOC comes down to three things — layering slopes to build the chest armor, using transparent parts for the color timer, and controlling the red-to-silver ratio across the whole figure. Get those right and 400-600 loose parts will give you a 25 cm tall figure.

## 1. Color scheme: 70/30 red and silver

The classic Ultraman is a red-and-silver character, so aim for roughly 70% silver and 30% red. Mix light gray with white for the silver areas to add a metallic feel, and keep the red consistent — one bright red throughout. Use black only for joints and the area around the eyes, and keep it under 5% of the build, or the whole figure starts to look muddy.

## 2. Chest armor: the three-layer method

**Step one — build the chest frame.** Start with 2x4 plates as the base and lay out a chest outline about 8 cm wide.

**Step two — add slopes.** Working from the bottom up, use 1x2 slopes and step each layer inward by half a stud, creating the curve of the armor.

**Step three — add the red stripes.** Run two vertical columns of 1x1 red bricks down the center line of the chest, then cover both sides in silver. That gives you the classic red-center, silver-edge look.

## 3. Color timer: a pulsing light effect

The timer sits dead center on the chest. Set a 1x1 transparent round brick into the light opening. To recreate the flashing effect, stack two layers: transparent blue underneath and transparent yellow on top, so the color shifts as you rotate the assembly. Glow-in-the-dark parts also work — they'll keep glowing for 2-3 hours in the dark.

## 4. Head and limbs

**Head:** use 2x2 curved bricks for the back of the head, and angle 1x2 transparent yellow parts in for the eyes to get that signature wide oval eye look.

**Limbs:** connect the arms with ball joints so you can pose 30 or more fighting stances. Add two 1x2 hinge pieces to the legs to make kicking poses easier.

## 5. Instructions and parts

brickplan.cn has parts lists and building instructions for Ultraman-themed MOCs. Buying the loose parts from a list runs about 200-300 yuan. If you're swapping the color scheme yourself, stock about 10% extra red bricks so a lopsided ratio doesn't force you to rework the build.

## FAQ

**Q: How many parts does a brick Ultraman MOC need?**

A: A 25 cm tall figure takes roughly 400-600 parts, with slopes and curved bricks making up about 30% of that. Buying loose parts against the brickplan.cn parts list usually comes to 200-300 yuan total.

**Q: How do you make the Ultraman color timer glow?**

A: Stack a 1x1 transparent round brick with glow-in-the-dark parts and it will glow for 2-3 hours in the dark. For an always-on effect, fit a micro LED inside with the battery box hidden in the back and a 1x1 brick as the switch — that gives the most faithful result.

**Q: What's the best way to combine red and silver parts?**

A: Stick to 70/30 — 70% silver, 30% red — and keep black under 5%. Mix light gray with white for the silver, use one consistent bright red, and run vertical red bricks down the chest center line with silver wrapping the sides. The layering reads immediately.

**Q: Can I design an Ultraman MOC without instructions?**

A: Yes, but it's much harder. Start by downloading an existing plan from brickplan.cn to practice, get comfortable with how slopes close off edges and how ball joints work, then design your own figure — your success rate goes up by more than half.
