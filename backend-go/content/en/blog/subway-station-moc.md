# Subway Station MOC: Build a 32x32 Underground Stop

Here is the short version: a brick subway station MOC is not hard. Build it in three layers — platform, concourse, entrances — and a beginner can finish a standard 32x32 stud station (roughly 25.6 x 25.6 cm) in about 6 hours for around 700–800 CNY. That makes it one of the best value landmarks you can add to a brick city.

## Step 1: Set the scale and pick your baseplate

A 32x32 baseplate is the most common choice for a subway station. If you want to include platform screen doors along the full platform edge, size up to 48x32. Decide first whether this is a standalone display piece or a module that slots into an existing street layout — that decision fixes every dimension that follows.

## Step 2: Build the platform level

Lay dark gray plates for the platform surface and leave 8 bricks of height clear for the track zone, keeping 16 studs between the two tracks. Build the platform screen doors from transparent elements inside white frames, 4 studs wide per door, with 8 doors along the platform. The moment those panels go in, the whole build reads as a real underground station.

## Step 3: Build the concourse level

The concourse sits 6 bricks above the platform, with a 12-stud-wide passage down the middle. Line both sides with ticket gates, ticket machines and support columns. Build each ticket gate from three groups of 1x2 bricks with a rotating bar sandwiched between them — the small details are what sell the realism.

## Step 4: Add entrances and escalators

Build the stairway from 45-degree slope pieces and top it with a glass canopy. For the escalator, run slope bricks at an angle and bend a flexible hose to form the handrail curve. This is the section of the station that looks best when it is finished.

## Step 5: Detail work and lighting

Use printed pieces for the station name signs, stickers for advertising boards, and add 2–3 warm-colored light bricks to simulate interior lighting. Lit up at night, the atmosphere is completely different. Finish by sorting your parts list so the build can be reproduced later.

## FAQ

**Q: How many parts and how much budget does a brick subway station MOC need?**

A: A standard 32x32 station takes roughly 2,500–3,000 parts and costs about 700–800 CNY. Using domestic compatible parts can bring that down to around 500 CNY — the total depends mainly on how many screen doors and light bricks you use.

**Q: Can a beginner succeed on a first subway station MOC?**

A: Yes. Build in the order platform, concourse, entrances, and do the main structure before the details; a beginner can finish in 6–8 hours. For a first attempt, skip the motorized escalator and lighting to lower the risk of things going wrong.

**Q: What separates a subway station MOC from an ordinary street building?**

A: The underground feel. A subway station needs a sunken structure, platform screen doors and a track zone, it runs about 8 bricks taller than a normal street building, and you need to leave track connections so it can join the surrounding blocks into a complete neighborhood.

**Q: Is a subway station MOC worth building?**

A: Yes. It is one of the rare functional buildings in a city display — it links districts together and activates the street flow. It looks good on display, plays well, and holds its value on the secondhand market.
