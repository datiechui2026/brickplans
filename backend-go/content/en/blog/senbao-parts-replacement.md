# SEMBO Replacement Parts: How to Request Missing Bricks

Short version: replacing a missing SEMBO piece is not difficult. In the vast majority of cases the seller you bought from will sort it out free of charge, with the part landing in 3–15 days. SEMBO's official channels or the BrickPlan loose-parts marketplace cover the remaining situations. What decides whether the process takes three days or three weeks is preparation: the set number, the part number, and a clear photo of the gap.

## Step 1: Confirm what is missing and photograph it

Empty every bag onto the table and work through the parts list in the instruction booklet item by item before you conclude anything is absent. Small pieces hide in bag corners, cling to the inside of the box, or drop onto the floor during sorting, and a "missing" part that turns up later costs you a wasted request.

Once you are certain something is gone, take three photos: the place where the part appears in the instruction diagram, the part number printed in the parts list, and a close-up of the empty spot in the build so far. Those three images let a support agent understand the request at a glance, and they cut down the back-and-forth that slows most claims down.

## Step 2: Contact the marketplace seller first

If you bought the set on Taobao, JD.com or a similar platform, open the order page and message the store's customer service directly. Quote the set number, which is printed on the box, plus the part number of the missing piece. In most cases the seller handles SEMBO replacement parts free of charge, covers 1–3 pieces per request, and turns the claim around in roughly 3–7 days. This is the fastest of all the available routes, which is why it should always be your first move rather than a last resort.

## Step 3: Fall back on official channels

When the seller has gone silent, the order is long closed, or you bought the set across platforms and no single store will take responsibility, go to SEMBO's official channels: the brand's official WeChat account or the customer service on its official website. Submit the set number, the missing part number, the quantity needed and your delivery address, and attach the photos from step one.

Official replacements are free as well, but review and logistics are slower than a marketplace seller working from stock. Expect about 7–15 days, and plan accordingly rather than assuming the parcel is lost after the first week. If you are building a gift or a display model to a deadline, this is the channel to start early, not the one to rely on at the last minute.

## Step 4: Bridge the gap with the loose-parts market

If you cannot wait, search the BrickPlan loose-parts marketplace by part number for a domestic piece of the same specification; single pieces run about CNY 0.05–0.3. Gobricks and Double Eagle both sit close to SEMBO in clutch fit, so their standard bricks work as temporary stand-ins. Drop the substitute into the build now and swap the original back in when the factory part arrives — the finished model is unaffected either way.

Keep two caveats in mind. Printed elements and transparent pieces are where substitutes show, because a slightly different print registration or tint is visible in the finished build. And a substitute is a stopgap, not a cancellation: leave the original request open so you still receive the correct part.

## FAQ

**Does SEMBO charge for replacement parts?**

No. Replacement parts through marketplace sellers and through official channels are basically free, and shipping is covered by the manufacturer. The only money you spend is when you choose to buy a substitute yourself on the loose-parts market, where individual pieces typically cost a few cents to a few tenths of a yuan.

**How long does a SEMBO replacement take to arrive?**

Marketplace seller support is fast, generally 3–7 days. Official channels take about 7–15 days including review and shipping. If two weeks pass with no movement, contact the agent proactively and quote your request number so they can look up the status.

**What do I need to prepare before filing a missing-part request?**

Have the set number, the missing part number, the quantity required and your delivery address ready, plus a photo showing where the part belongs in the build. The more complete the information you supply, the higher the chance the claim is approved first time and the faster it is processed.

**Can SEMBO parts be mixed with other Chinese brick brands?**

Yes. SEMBO, Gobricks and Double Eagle share essentially the same clutch specification, so ordinary bricks and plates can be swapped directly. For special printed elements and transparent parts, wait for the original replacement instead — a colour difference will be noticeable in the finished model.
