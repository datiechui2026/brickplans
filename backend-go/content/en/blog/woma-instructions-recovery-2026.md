# Woma Instructions Lost? How to Get Replacements and PDFs

Start here: a missing Woma manual is not a disaster. You have two routes open to you — an official replacement request (paper, free or shipping-only) and digital instruction recovery (a PDF in as little as 10 minutes). The reliable approach is to confirm your set number first (step 1 below), then work step 2 and step 3 in parallel.

## Why manuals go missing so often

Woma sets regularly run to a thousand pieces or more, yet each box ships with a single manual. After a house move, a storage clear-out, or one enthusiastic play session with the kids, it easily disappears. Sets released from 2024 onward sometimes ship without a paper manual at all, which makes the digital copy an increasingly important fallback.

## Step 1: Confirm the set number and where you bought it

Everything depends on identifying the set number. Woma numbers are usually printed on the side of the box and molded into the studs of the baseplate, in either the `WM-XXXX` format or plain digits (8808, 6802 and similar). If the box is gone too, log into the Taobao or Pinduoduo account you ordered from — the product title or detail page normally contains the number. You can also search the set name on BrickPlan and verify the number on the set page. Confirming it up front saves a great deal of back-and-forth later.

## Step 2: Official replacement request (paper manual)

Ordering a replacement is the official way to get a paper manual, and the best fit for households where children flip through the pages again and again.

1. **Contact official support**: search for the "Woma Bricks" official WeChat account, open the "After-sales service" or "Replacement request" menu, and fill in the set number, the missing part or manual, and your shipping address as prompted.
2. **Attach an order screenshot**: support usually asks for a screenshot of your purchase order to confirm the set is genuine. Sets bought through unofficial channels are sometimes accepted as well, but the review takes longer.
3. **Wait for dispatch**: manual replacements are typically free — you cover shipping only, roughly 6-10 yuan — and are processed in about 3-7 working days. If the set has been discontinued, the official channel may not be able to ship one, in which case go straight to step 3.

## Step 3: Recover the digital instructions (fastest route)

Digital files are unaffected by discontinuation, which makes this the most dependable option of all.

1. **Official channel**: follow the "Woma Bricks" account and reply with the set number — some models get a direct link to the digital instructions. You can also request the electronic version from support, which usually replies within 1-2 working days.
2. **Third-party instruction sites**: if the official archive does not hold your set, search the set number on instruction communities such as BrickPlan, which hosts a large collection of scanned and PDF Woma instructions with online preview. You can be building within 10 minutes.
3. **Ask other builders**: as a last resort, post the set number plus a photo of the box in a builder group. Fellow fans often share scans, and the success rate is around 50%.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I replace a lost Woma manual?**
A: Two steps. Submit a replacement request through the "Woma Bricks" WeChat account (free, shipping around 6-10 yuan, dispatched in 3-7 working days). If the set is discontinued, search the set number on an instruction site such as BrickPlan and download the digital version — you can start building within 10 minutes.

**Q: Where can I find Woma digital instructions?**
A: Three places: reply to the official WeChat account with the set number, contact support for an electronic copy (1-2 working days), or download from a third-party instruction site such as BrickPlan by searching the number. Try the official route first, then the instruction sites, which hold broader coverage and are available immediately.

**Q: Does a Woma manual replacement cost anything?**
A: The manual itself is free; you pay shipping only, usually 6-10 yuan. Digital instructions are completely free. Note that discontinued sets cannot be replaced at all — the digital version is your only option.

**Q: Can I still recover a manual if I can't find the set number?**
A: Yes. Check your order history on the platform where you bought the set; product titles and detail pages generally include the number. You can also post photos of the box and of distinctive parts in a community such as BrickPlan and ask other builders to identify the exact model for you.

**Q: Are digital instructions from third-party sites reliable?**
A: On reputable sites such as BrickPlan, scanned copies are essentially as sharp as the official version, and online preview lets you check before you download. Prefer sites that offer preview so you don't end up with a blurry or mismatched file.

A lost manual is a minor interruption. Work through the steps above and you will end up with either a paper copy or a digital one.
