# Brick Snowman MOC: Step-by-Step Build and Parts List

Here is the short version first: this snowman MOC uses about 350 parts, takes roughly 1.5 hours from sorting to finished model, and stands around 20 cm tall. A white-brick snowball body, an orange carrot nose and a red scarf do all the work — park it on a hallway shelf or a desk and the winter mood is instant, with no puddle to mop up afterwards.

## Step 1: The Snowball Body

The body is three white spheres of decreasing size, roughly 200 parts in total. The bottom ball measures about 8 cm across, the middle one about 6 cm and the head about 5 cm; a ratio close to 2:1.5:1.2 looks the most natural. Each sphere is closed up with curved bricks and plates around the shell, then a technic cross axle runs through the interior to tie all three layers together. The finished stack is genuinely stable — shake it and nothing comes apart.

## Step 2: The Face and Details

The head is where the personality lives, and it costs about 80 parts. Use an orange 1x1 cone for the carrot nose and small black round pieces for the eyes and the wide grin; two red plates on the cheeks work as blush if you want a friendlier look. For the arms, push two long brown bars into the sides of the body at a slight angle, then cap the ends with small black pieces as fingers. The pose comes out playful rather than stiff.

## Step 3: Scarf, Hat and Scene

Wrap a red hose piece or a ring of red plates once around the neck, leaving a length of the tail hanging loose so the scarf reads as fabric. The hat is a black bowl-shaped piece with a white pom-pom on top — this section runs about 70 parts. Under the feet, add a snow base of white plates and finish it with a few small Christmas trees and gift boxes, and the snowman becomes a complete little winter scene instead of a standalone figure.

## Parts and Play Suggestions

- Main colors: about 220 white parts, 60 red, 40 black and 10 orange.
- Budget: around 60 to 100 yuan for 350 parts. White bricks dominate the count, so buying loose parts by weight works out cheaper.
- Holiday upgrade: mount the snowman on a base with a light brick — it glows at night and the Christmas feel goes up another notch.

## FAQ

**Q: How many parts does a brick snowman MOC need?**
A: About 350 for the basic version, of which roughly 200 are white parts for the snowball body, at a budget of 60 to 100 yuan. Add Christmas trees, gift boxes and other scene accessories and you are looking at around 500 parts and a cost of about 120 yuan.

**Q: How long does it take to build?**
A: Around 1.5 hours for a beginner; an experienced builder can finish in 40 minutes. You assemble the three snowballs in layers and join them at the end, so the sequence is simple and there is barely a tricky step anywhere — which makes it a good parent-and-child project.

**Q: How is a brick snowman different from a real one?**
A: No shoveling and no melting. It stays on display indoors all season, and you can swap the hat, scarf and expression whenever you like, plus it photographs well for sharing. The trade-off is that you miss out on the outdoor fun of rolling a real snowman.

**Q: What does the whole thing cost?**
A: Total parts come to about 60 to 100 yuan, which is better value than buying a finished Christmas set. To spend less, mix in spare white and colored bricks you already own — you can push the cost under 50 yuan and still get a good result.
