# Free MOC Plans: 5 Platforms Reviewed for Builders

For many new brick builders, the biggest worry is whether MOC building is too expensive. In practice, high-quality free brick plans are far more common than expected. This review compares five platforms worth bookmarking by plan quality, update frequency, and user experience.

## The Value of Free MOC Plans

Free brick plans do not automatically mean low quality. Many skilled MOC designers share plans for free because they want to build an audience and reputation that can lead to paid plans, because they simply enjoy contributing to the community, or because they enter plan-design contests whose entries are shared publicly.

On [BrickPlans.cn](https://brickplan.cn), you can find many free, high-quality MOC plans. If you are starting from zero, read the [MOC Plan Beginner Guide](/en/blog/moc-plans-beginner-guide) first, then use the free resources below for hands-on practice.

**Why it is recommended:** BrickPlans free plans cover architecture, vehicles, characters, mecha, and almost every mainstream MOC theme. Each plan includes a detailed parts list and can link directly to Taobao to buy parts packs, which is very convenient.

### 2. Rebrickable — Free Plan Treasure House

**Free plans:** 30000+ | **Update frequency:** daily | **Chinese support:** No

Rebrickable is the world's largest MOC plan platform, and most plans can be downloaded free.

**Pros:**
- The largest plan count globally
- A strong rating system that helps you quickly filter high-quality plans
- Parts inventory management that automatically detects the parts you are missing

**Cons:**
- All-English interface, which is not very friendly to users in China
- Some high-rated plans require payment
- Access from China can be slow

### 3. BrickLink Studio — Free Digital Models

**Free plans:** 10000+ | **Update frequency:** weekly | **Chinese support:** No

BrickLink Studio is not only plan-design software but also a plan-sharing platform. Users can browse and download free plans directly in the software.

**Recommended use:** Download BrickLink Studio, then search the Gallery section for free brick plans. The software supports 3D preview, so you can rotate a model 360 degrees to inspect the build steps.

### 4. Baidu Tieba — Domestic Free Plan Hub

**Recommended forums:** Brick Building, MOC, and LEGO forums on Baidu Tieba

Baidu Tieba is an old-school platform, but it has a rich supply of free plans. Many experienced builders post their own free designs there.

**Search tips:** Search for the Chinese equivalents of 'free plans,' 'MOC plan sharing,' and 'plan bundle,' then sort by time to see the newest resources.

### 5. Xiaohongshu — Social Media Free Plans

**Search terms:** free MOC plans, brick plan sharing, MOC tutorials

Many brick bloggers on Xiaohongshu regularly share free plans. The format combines images and text, shows steps clearly, and is convenient to view on a phone.

## Free Plan Download Tips

### Tip 1: Use Filters Well

On BrickPlans, you can filter free plans by difficulty and part count to quickly find resources that suit you.

### Tip 2: Follow Designers

Many designers who offer free plans also have paid work. Following them lets you hear first when new plans are released.

### Tip 3: Combine Free Plans

If you have a large enough parts collection, try combining elements from several free plans into your own MOC. For example, combine a free combat vehicle plan with a free architecture plan to form a complete battle scene.
