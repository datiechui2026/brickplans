# Brick Brontosaurus MOC Review: 1,250-Part Long-Neck Dinosaur

The short version: this brontosaurus MOC is the long-necked pick dinosaur fans should not skip. It uses roughly 1,250 parts, measures 48 cm long and 32 cm tall, and captures about 90% of the classic long-neck, long-tail silhouette. The PDF instructions sell for 35 yuan on brickplan.cn, sourcing the loose parts yourself runs about 300 yuan, and the actual build took around 6 hours.

## 1. Appearance and Accuracy

The long neck, trailing tail and heavy four-legged stance land at roughly 90% accuracy against the real animal. The neck is built over an internal skeleton of hinge pieces and extension rods, with bricks layered on the outside to sculpt a natural S-curve. The body is mostly grey-green, with light yellow parts easing the transition across the belly. Skin texture comes from curved slopes and wedge pieces, and from a distance the model has the look of a dinosaur documentary render.

## 2. Structural Design

The four-legged stance keeps the center of gravity low, so it stands very securely. The tail is chained together with hinge pieces into a long, swingable assembly whose curve you can adjust. The neck's internal skeleton runs all the way into the torso and locks onto the body's cross axles, so even a head-down grazing pose will not collapse. Among dinosaur MOCs at this scale, the structural reliability sits in the top tier.

## 3. Building Experience

Following the PDF instructions on brickplan.cn, the build took about 6 hours in testing, split into 12 stages. The neck curve is the hardest part, concentrated in stages 5 through 7. Each step covers 20-40 parts and the diagrams are clearly labeled. When you reach the neck, check every layer against the side view to confirm the curve — it saves you from tearing work apart later.

## 4. Value for Money

Instructions are 35 yuan and the loose parts come to about 300 yuan. Official dinosaur sets often run 700-800 yuan, so this comes in at less than half the price. Given the 48 cm size and the posable neck, the value is strong. It suits dinosaur enthusiasts, families building together, and intermediate builders who want to take on a large MOC.

All in all, this brontosaurus MOC backs up its 48 cm bulk with solid structure, and it is one of the more complete large dinosaur reproductions you can build.
