# DC Brick Building Guide: Batman, Superman & Wonder Woman MOCs

![Finished DC brick scene model](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/dc-brick-building-guide/dc-brick-building-guide-1.jpg)

DC heroes are instantly recognizable, and **DC brick building** is the most direct way to put that world into bricks. Batman in the dark of Gotham, Superman above Metropolis, Wonder Woman stepping off Paradise Island — each one can be rebuilt in brick form. This guide moves in three stages, from minifigures to vehicles to full scenes, so you can assemble your own Justice League.

## From Minifigures to Scenes: Three Steps into DC Brick Building

![DC brick building step-by-step diagram](https://brickplan2-1317217681.cos.ap-hongkong.myqcloud.com/blog/dc-brick-building-guide/dc-brick-building-guide-2.jpg)

**Step one is gathering minifigures.** Most **DC brick building** projects start with the core heroes: Batman's black-and-grey suit, Superman's red-and-blue color scheme, and Wonder Woman's gold armor with the red skirt are all high-recognition silhouettes. Step two is a small display scene — one baseplate plus a building or two, enough to give your figures somewhere to stand. Step three is where vehicles and large scenes come in, and that is also the step that tests your composition skills the most. Beginners should finish the first two steps and build up a wider range of parts before attempting a large MOC.

## Iconic Vehicles: the Batmobile and the Invisible Jet

Vehicles are the highlight of any DC build. The Batmobile has gone through many generations of design, from the long, retro version to the sharply angled tank-style version, and both can be recreated with black panels combined with dark grey angular pieces. Don't forget a pair of folding tail fins — the top-down silhouette is what makes it read as "bat." Wonder Woman's Invisible Jet has clean, flowing lines and works well with a large number of curved elements plus transparent pieces to suggest something that is half-there. Superman's ride is relatively rare in MOC circles; if you want to emphasize speed, give him a streamlined sports car in silver and blue.

When picking sets, **DC brick building** works best with combinations that offer high play value — a Batmobile paired with a Batman minifigure, or the Joker's car paired with the Joker. Each can be displayed on its own, and together they recreate a classic chase scene, which gives you more value for the money.

## Scene Building: Composition Ideas for the Hall of Justice and the Watchtower

The Hall of Justice is the Justice League's signature headquarters. Build it with white and light grey as the main colors, stand a row of Roman columns across the front, and place a round conference table inside with seven seats for the seven founding members — the sense of scale arrives immediately. The Watchtower space station suits Technic-style parts: a ring structure with solar panels, set against a dark blue or black baseplate, with transparent pieces dotted around to simulate stars. The display impact is striking.

## Color and Detail: Making a Build Feel "DC"

DC's color language is very clear — Batman's black and grey, Superman's red and blue, Wonder Woman's gold and red, Green Lantern's green. In **DC brick building**, keep each model's theme color consistent and don't let stray colors break the mood. For details, use printed pieces for chest emblems and belt textures; they look more refined than stickers.
