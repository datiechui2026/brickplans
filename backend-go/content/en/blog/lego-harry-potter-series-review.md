# LEGO Harry Potter Review: The 2020s Reboot, Set by Set

Verdict first: the 2020s reboot is the strongest batch the LEGO Harry Potter line has produced, with minifigure printing at an all-time high for the theme. If your budget is tight, start with one of the mid-sized castle or shop sets rather than a flagship.

The LEGO Harry Potter theme has been through two full cycles of being retired and then revived, and many builders consider the 2020s relaunch the best of them. In this review we work from the castle and Diagon Alley through to minifigure design, to pin down exactly where these sets improved.

## Hogwarts Castle: From 71043 to the New Flagship

The 2018 Hogwarts Castle (71043) set the benchmark with a massive 6020 pieces, still packing the Great Hall, the Chamber of Secrets, towers and stained-glass windows into a micro-scale build. The 2020s reboot took a more approachable route instead: 71043 remains the collector-grade centerpiece for the shelf, while 76405 Hogwarts Express Collectors' Edition (5129 pieces) pays tribute to the films' signature scene with three complete train carriages.

Across the newer sets, the design emphasis has shifted toward playability around famous scenes, which fits family building better than sheer brick volume. That trade-off is the clearest way to read the current lineup: the micro-scale castle is for display, the express is for the moment everyone remembers from the films.

## Diagon Alley and Street-Style Scenes

75978 Diagon Alley is the true peak of the line. At 5544 pieces, its four street-facing shopfronts can be lifted off individually or joined into one continuous street. Madam Malkin's Robes, Ollivanders Wand Shop and Weasley's Wizard Wheezes are extraordinarily dense with detail, and small easter eggs such as wand boxes and prophecy orbs keep turning up as you build.

In essence it is a wizarding-world version of a modular street: it captures Diagon Alley's crowded, fantastical feel exactly, which is why display-shelf builders rate it so highly. Few sets in the theme reward close inspection this well.

## The 2020s Minifigure Upgrades

The biggest minifigure advance in this reboot is print quality. Heads now carry finer facial printing, torsos have dual-sided patterns front and back, legs gained new printing, and hair and hat pieces use newly moulded elements. The likenesses of characters such as Draco, Snape and Dumbledore improved visibly.

One caveat worth flagging: some exclusive minifigures only appear inside expensive flagship sets, so completing the full cast is not a cheap project. If you collect characters rather than scenes, plan around that before you commit.

## Verdict and Buying Advice

The conclusion of this review: the sets released after the 2020s reboot beat the older versions on both accuracy and playability, and minifigure printing has reached a new high for the theme. They suit devoted fans building a deep collection, and they also work as an entry point for building together with kids.

When budget is limited, prioritize the mid-sized castle or shop sets. If you want to build a wizarding scene on a smaller budget, you can also look for Hogwarts-style brick MOC instructions on brickplan and recreate a corner of Diagon Alley yourself.
