# Jiaqi Brick Sets: Build Quality, Manuals and Sorting Tips

Jiaqi has built a strong reputation among Chinese brick brands over the past couple of years, with solid releases across street scenes, architecture and mech lines. I have built five of their sets of varying sizes, and this hands-on review covers brick quality, manual design, bag sorting, pressing techniques and the small practical details — then finishes with who should actually buy one.

## 1. Brick Quality: Consistent Precision, Tight Clutch

Start with the thing everyone asks about first. Jiaqi's ABS parts have accurate colours with very little batch-to-batch variation, and part precision sits in the top tier of domestic brands. The clutch is firm but not gritty, finished models feel solid, and moving them around doesn't shake anything loose. A few large baseplates and long beams occasionally run slightly tight on mould tolerances — push and pull vertically rather than at an angle, or you risk stressing the part. Overall the feel is close to the major international brands and is more than adequate for both beginners and experienced builders.

## 2. Manuals and Build Experience

Jiaqi's instructions are a genuine strength. Steps are broken down finely, arrows are clearly marked and the printing is crisp, so you rarely hit that "which brick goes here?" moment. Bags are grouped by floor or by module, which keeps the build flowing — a small set takes an hour or two, a large one three to four hours. Plenty of Jiaqi build guides online only cover the unboxing and the step count, but the techniques below are what actually shape the experience.

## 3. Bag Sorting and Finding Parts

Before you start, line up every numbered bag in order, then decant parts by colour and type into a few small trays. When you reach a step, you search the matching tray instead of digging through bags again — a big time saving. Keep transparent and specialised parts separate so they don't get lost among generic bricks. Building the habit of sorting by colour improves your speed more than any trick — if there is one lesson worth taking from any Jiaqi build guide, it is this one.

## 4. Pressing Parts Down and Handling Missing Pieces

Press every brick fully home as you go, especially long beams, plates and corner parts. Anything left proud will throw off the next level and cost you far more time in rework. If you think you have a missing or wrong part, don't message support straight away: most cases turn out to be a part you overlooked during sorting, or one you placed in the wrong position earlier in the build. Once you have double-checked, Jiaqi does run an official replacement-parts channel — send a photo and the set or part number and turnaround is reasonably quick.

## 5. Who Should Build Jiaqi

Three types of builder get the most out of these sets. First, anyone moving from the big international brands to a domestic option and wanting better value for money. Second, students on a budget who like street-scene and architecture themes. Third, MOC builders who need large volumes of generic parts — Jiaqi's standard bricks are compatible with mainstream brands, so breaking a set down and feeding the parts into your own build works smoothly.

## Final Thoughts

Jiaqi is a domestic brand you can buy without much hesitation, and the quality justifies the price. Save this guide and run through it before your next unboxing — it will save you a few wrong turns.
