# EVA LEGO Plans Review: Unit-01, Angels and Beast Mode MOCs

Neon Genesis Evangelion earned its place in animation history through its dense storytelling and its unmistakable machine design. EVA LEGO plans let fans rebuild those towering, half-organic humanoid weapons out of brick. This review covers several popular EVA-themed MOC plans in detail, and continues the coverage in our earlier [EVA LEGO roundup](/en/blog/moc-eva-lego-review).

## 1. Top EVA MOC Plans Worth Building

### 1. Unit-01 (EVA-01) Berserk Mode
- **Plan name**: EVA Unit-01 Berserk Mode Deluxe Edition
- **Piece count**: roughly 2,500
- **Difficulty**: High
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

This EVA LEGO plan is the most complete take on Unit-01's berserk state. Compared with the standard version, the Berserk build adds far more mechanical detail: the jaw hangs open to expose sharp teeth, the eye effect uses transparent red bricks, and torn armour plates reveal the internal mechanical structure underneath. The shattered shoulder pylons are achieved with a SNOT (studs-not-on-top) reverse build.

### 2. Unit-02 (EVA-02) Beast Mode
- **Plan name**: EVA Unit-02 Beast Mode
- **Piece count**: roughly 2,200
- **Difficulty**: High
- **Rating**: ★★★★★

Unit-02's beast form is one of the most popular subjects among EVA LEGO plans. Beneath the red colour scheme the limbs grow noticeably thicker and heavier, and the claws are built from claw-shaped pieces. The energy mass in the mouth uses transparent orange light bricks, so it glows in the dark.

### 3. Angel Ramiel
- **Plan name**: Angel Ramiel 1:100 Scale
- **Piece count**: roughly 1,500
- **Difficulty**: Medium-high
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆

The geometric transformation of Ramiel is one of the most iconic Angel designs in the whole series. Built from large quantities of transparent blue bricks and grey bricks, the model can switch between its octahedron form and its attack form. The AT Field is built as a ring of transparent blue tiles wrapping around the main body.

### 4. Unit-01 with Entry Plug Diorama
- **Plan name**: EVA-01 with Entry Plug Diorama
- **Piece count**: roughly 1,800
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Rating**: ★★★★☆

A Unit-01 set that includes an entry plug launch scene. The entry plug is built from cylinder bricks and slots into the Eva's back. The cockpit interior is well detailed, with a seat and control levers included.

## 2. Key Building Points

### 1. Recreating the Organic Texture

EVA units are not ordinary mecha — they read as biological. Mixing curved slopes with standard slope bricks is the usual approach, mimicking muscle lines and skeletal structure rather than flat armour panels.

### 2. AT Field Effects

Transparent blue and purple bricks, arranged in rings, form the AT Field. The barrier can be left floating around the model to strengthen the visual impact of the display.

### 3. Designing the Berserk Form

The berserk state calls for more damaged pieces and darker-coloured bricks, paired with the red eye effect parts, to create that out-of-control, oppressive presence.
