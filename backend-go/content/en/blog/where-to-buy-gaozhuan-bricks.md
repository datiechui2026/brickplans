## How Strong Is the Gaozhuan Brand?

### Why Do Builders Rate Gaozhuan So Highly?

Before hunting for a store, it helps to know why the parts are in demand:

**1. Mold precision leads the field**
Gaozhuan molds are held to roughly 0.005 mm, a match for LEGO. The clutch between parts lands where you want it — not so loose that pieces drop off, not so tight that your fingers hurt.

**2. Colour stability**
Imported pigments keep shades consistent between batches. For an MOC that needs a large run of one colour, that is critical.

**3. Good compatibility**
Gaozhuan is fully compatible with LEGO and mixes without modification. Among Chinese brick brands it has consistently ranked near the top for compatibility.

**4. A deep parts catalogue**
Over 2,000 part types, covering essentially everything a typical MOC requires.

### How to Tell Genuine Gaozhuan Bricks from Fakes

Counterfeits appeared once the brand became well known. A few checks:

**1. Look at the studs**
Genuine Gaozhuan studs usually carry a 'G' marking — an important identifying feature.

**2. Check the packaging**
Official packaging follows a consistent design and carries an anti-counterfeit code you can verify through official channels.

**3. Feel the clutch**
Genuine parts engage evenly, with no loose or over-tight pieces. If clutch force varies from part to part, it is probably a copy.

**4. Buy through proper channels**
The most reliable method is simply to buy official or authorised stock rather than chasing the lowest price.

### Value for Money

Which channel gives the best value? A quick breakdown:

- **Loose parts:** official store > Taobao shops > second-hand
- **Sets:** Pinduoduo > Taobao > JD > official store
- **Bulk buying:** wholesale > Pinduoduo > Taobao

Overall, if you build MOCs and buy parts often, build a stable mix of channels — the official store for unusual parts, Pinduoduo for basic restocks, and the BrickPlans community for parts lists tied to instructions.
