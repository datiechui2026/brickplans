# Brick Goat MOC Review: Curved Horns, Stability and Value

The short version: this brick goat MOC falls into the "great looks, easy build" category of animal models. It uses about 520 pieces, stands roughly 12 cm tall, costs 22 yuan for the instructions and about 95 yuan for the loose parts, and takes around 2.5 hours from first bag to finished model. The signature curved horns land at roughly 90% accuracy, the four legs hold the model steady, the beard is a nice extra touch, and the overall value is strong. Beginners can finish it on their own.

## Looks and Accuracy: Curved Horns and Beard Details

The first thing anyone notices on a goat is the pair of large horns sweeping backward. Here they are built from curved elements stacked layer by layer, then edged with dark brown pieces, and both the arc and the visible layering come through well — about 90% faithful to the real thing. Under the chin, a few small white pieces form the beard, the ears use slope pieces angled slightly outward, and round black studs serve as eyes. The overall expression reads clearly as a real goat. The color scheme is clean and simple: off-white body, brown horns, black hooves.

## Build Experience: Structure and Stability

The legs are the critical part. Each one is stacked from plates about four layers high and connects to the torso through ball joints, so once all four hooves are on the ground the center of gravity is stable — a gentle push won't knock it over. The instructions are split into 8 steps, with each step kept to no more than 12 pieces, and the diagrams are clear enough that the whole thing takes about 2.5 hours. The one thing to watch: the curved horn elements have to be aligned with the connection points layer by layer, so follow the step order in the manual and don't skip ahead.

## Value: Instructions and Part Costs

The instructions cost 22 yuan. Sourcing the loose parts yourself from the parts list on the brickplan platform runs about 95 yuan, putting the full set at roughly 120 yuan — about 0.23 yuan per piece, which is a budget-friendly tier among animal MOCs. If you already have off-white and brown parts in your stock, you can push the cost down to around 70 yuan. Given the accuracy and the build experience, it's worth the money.
