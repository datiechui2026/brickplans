# Brick Pet Shop Build: Step-by-Step Street Scene Guide

The short answer: a pet shop is the warmest subject you can build for a street scene. Pick a plan in the 1,200-1,800 piece range, build in four passes (storefront, cages, shelves, interactive details), and you will finish in 4-6 hours on a budget of 150-300 yuan. Wherever you place it, it draws the eye, and kids love it in particular.

## Step 1: Choosing Plans and Planning Parts

### How to choose a plan

Look for a two-storey design of 1,200-1,800 pieces on a 16x16 stud footprint: the ground floor is the pet supply shop, the upper floor is the grooming salon. You can search "pet shop" on brickplan and download a plan that comes with a parts list. Check first that the cat and dog figures are all available, because those little animals are the soul of the whole build.

### Parts list

Stick to warm tones for the main structure: about 600 beige bricks, about 200 white plates, about 80 transparent pieces (display windows and cage doors), about 40 green pieces (plants and storefront accents), plus cat, dog and bird figures and accessories such as food bowls and leashes. Keep a few extra animal figures on hand so the cages look busy.

## Step 2: Building the Storefront and Display Windows

### Storefront design

Use beige as the base and make the sign from a printed piece reading "Pet Shop", with a row of small paw-print printed pieces above the lintel as decoration. For the awning, top green plates with white edging: it sits naturally next to a flower shop or a café, and the colour transition stays smooth.

### Display windows

Put transparent windows on both sides of the door. Inside, place a cat tree (three tiers of brown plates) and a dog bed, with a cat minifigure "on duty" inside, so passing minifigures stop to look. Add 1x1 supports under the window base so the shelves do not sag under load.

## Step 3: Cages, Shelves and Interactive Details

### Cage area

Build two rows of cages against the wall: white plates for the frames, transparent panels for the doors, one animal figure per compartment. Add supports at the bottom of each cage to stop the base plate from collapsing, and put a bird cage (transparent dome plus perch) on top. The more species you include, the more it feels like a real pet shop.

### Shelves and checkout counter

Stock the shelves with pet food bags (printed pieces), toy balls and leashes, and put a small cash register on the counter. On the second floor, add a grooming table (white tile on supports) and a bathtub to the salon; the printed scissors and hair dryer on the wall are the highlight, packing a lot of detail into a small space.

### Interactive easter eggs

Stand a "pet adoption" sign by the door, drape a cat piece over the window sill and add a dog bowl on the ground. With warm lighting added, the shop becomes the most comforting corner of the street scene at night, and it photographs beautifully.

## FAQ

**Q: How much does a brick pet shop set cost?**
A: Sets of 1,200-1,800 pieces usually run 150-300 yuan, and versions with more cat and dog figures cost more. If you buy only the plan and source the parts yourself, you can keep the cost under 200 yuan.

**Q: How should I choose the animal figures?**
A: Favour cats and dogs with clean printing that can stand upright, and match the quantity to the number of cages. If you want more species, search "animal" plans on brickplan separately and add the parts yourself.

**Q: Is a brick pet shop a good gift for a child?**
A: Yes. The pet theme is a strong draw for children, the cage and grooming table structures are simple and safe, and it takes 4-6 hours to finish, which makes it suitable for a birthday or holiday gift.

**Q: Can it sit alongside other street scene buildings?**
A: Yes. The pet shop is compact and warmly coloured, so it works well between a café and a flower shop. Once baseplate size and floor heights match, the modules line up flush and the street feels busier.
