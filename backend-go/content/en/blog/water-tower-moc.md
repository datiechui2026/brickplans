# Water Tower MOC Review: 420-Part Industrial Street Scene

Bottom line first: this water tower MOC from brickplan is a polished piece of work — solid silhouette, sturdy structure, and an overall score of **8.8/10**. Roughly **420 parts** produce a **14 cm** storage tank for your street layout, and it goes together in about **1 hour**. If you want to add industrial flavour to a modular street without spending much, this is an easy recommendation, especially alongside an old-town block.

## Design and Silhouette

The main body is a cylindrical storage tank sitting on a four-legged cross-braced frame, and the proportions are genuinely pleasing. The tank measures about **8 studs in diameter**, topped with a ring of railing and a conical roof cap, so the lines stay clean and sharp. The frame mixes dark gray with rust red — enough weight to read as heavy industrial equipment, but never loud enough to steal attention from the main buildings on your street. On a corner plot or an empty lot, it photographs well from any angle.

## Detail Work

Details are where this MOC earns its score. The tank sides carry rivet texture and vertical reinforcing bands, mimicking the welded look of a real storage tank. At the bottom you get a water valve, pipework and a small water meter, with curved pipe elements routing the lines naturally between connections. The frame base also includes a two-step staircase and an access hatch, as if a worker might come by for an inspection at any moment — plenty of narrative for such a small footprint. The one minor gripe: the tank surface is fairly stud-heavy, which is fine from a distance but noticeable in close-up photos.

## Build Experience

Built from the brickplan instructions, the whole thing takes about **1 hour** at a moderate difficulty, suitable for builders **10 and up**. The step breakdown is sensible, with **8–12 parts per step**; the tank section repeats a lot but keeps a comfortable rhythm. The only place that demands care is the cross-braced frame, where the alignment has to be right — the instructions label it clearly, so rework is basically unnecessary. Clutch power is tight throughout, and the finished model shows no looseness when you pick it up and shake it.

## Value for Money

At roughly **420 parts** and a reference price of **around CNY 60**, that works out to **under CNY 0.15 per part** — excellent value. The **14 cm** height sits comfortably in scale with standard street modules, so it works equally well as a standalone display piece or tucked into a corner of a layout. Overall score: **8.8/10**, with strong marks on both silhouette and detail. If your street is missing an industrial anchor, this is a safe buy.
