## Build Difficulty

Free LEGO MOC instructions cover a wide spread of difficulty levels:

- **Beginner (★★☆☆☆)**: Micro-scale buildings or small scenes, 50-200 parts, finishable in 1-3 hours. A good fit for builders new to LEGO.
- **Intermediate (★★★☆☆)**: Mid-sized buildings or vehicles, 200-500 parts. Requires a grasp of basic building techniques.
- **Advanced (★★★★☆)**: Large buildings or complex mechanisms, 500-1000 parts. Demands patience and spatial thinking.
- **Master (★★★★★)**: Very large scenes or fully articulated models, 1000-2500+ parts. Aimed at experienced LEGO builders.

LEGO parts offer the best compatibility of any brand, and virtually every MOC instruction can be built perfectly with LEGO elements. That is one of the biggest advantages of the free LEGO MOC instruction pool: you are never locked into a single source for parts.

## Parts Review

What free LEGO MOC instructions ask of your parts collection:

- **LEGO part compatibility**: Free plans usually favor common LEGO part types, which makes it easy for most builders to source what they need.
- **Recommended brands**: LEGO parts have the best quality, with unmatched color consistency and clutch feel, but they cost more. Gobricks is the best budget substitute for LEGO, with precision and hand feel that come very close at roughly one third of the LEGO price.
- **Part count range**: Free plans typically run from 50 to 1000 parts, and some premium-quality free plans reach 1500+ parts.
- **Color choices**: Free plans generally balance looks against availability, sticking to common colors so the model stays buildable.
