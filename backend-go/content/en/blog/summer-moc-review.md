# Summer Brick MOC Review: Watermelon, Pool and Beach Chair

The short version first: the BrickPlan summer brick MOC is the most photogenic build in the seasonal-theme category. Around 750 pieces, a 220 yuan budget, and nothing missing from the brief: watermelon, pool, beach chair and parasol are all there. The cool-toned colour scheme is easy on the eye, fidelity scores 8.5/10, and it works well as a summer seasonal display. The one small drawback is that the individual elements are on the small side.

## Design and Accuracy

The watermelon slices are the standout. Red pieces form the base of each slice, black dot pieces stand in for the seeds, and an outer ring of green and white pieces builds up the rind texture, so the cut face reads convincingly at a glance. The pool is made from transparent light-blue water pieces laid flat inside the base, with white pieces running around the perimeter to form the pool edge, giving the scene a clear sense of depth and layering. The beach chair and parasol carry a lot of detail for their size, and the parasol canopy opens and closes.

Together the summer elements are complete as a set, and the composition has real picture quality: the slices, the water and the seating area all read instantly as a holiday scene rather than a pile of loose parts.

## Parts and Build Quality

The roughly 750 pieces arrive split across two bags, and the build takes about 2.5 hours at a relaxed pace. The transparent water pieces are genuinely clear rather than cloudy, and the watermelon red and green are vivid without being harsh, so the cool summer feel comes through strongly.

The base is 24x24 studs, the clutch between pieces is stable, and missing pieces are rarely reported. The instructions are clear step by step. Build quality comes out at 8.5/10 overall. One practical point that matters for display: the pool water pieces sit down in the base and stay put, so they do not scatter when you move the finished model.

## Display Setup and Value

At a 220 yuan budget for about 750 pieces, the cost works out to roughly 0.29 yuan per piece, which is good value for a scene this size. Displayed on its own, the watermelon, pool and beach chair zones are clearly separated, and adding minifigures turns it into a convincing "summer vacation" theme that photographs well.

The cool palette makes it a natural fit for a seasonal limited display; placed side by side with an island MOC, the summer feel doubles immediately. For builders who like to rotate a display through the year, this is a practical pick.
