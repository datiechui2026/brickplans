# EVA LEGO Blueprint Guide: Build the Unit-01 MOC Step by Step

Neon Genesis Evangelion's mecha designs sit right where organic texture meets mechanical precision, and they remain a milestone of mecha animation. Plenty of brick builders want to recreate Unit-01 in bricks but stall because they can't find a workable **EVA LEGO blueprint**. This guide walks you through parts prep, build order and the core techniques so you can go from zero to a finished EVA-01 of your own.

## What to Prepare Before You Start

Before you download any **EVA LEGO blueprint**, check what you already have in the part bins. Using a typical 1:35 scale Unit-01 as the reference:

| Part type | Main use | Suggested quantity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purple plates and bricks | Main armor | 400+ |
| Green curved slopes | Limb joints | 200+ |
| Technic bricks and pins | Internal frame | 300+ |
| Trans-blue bricks | AT Field effect | 100+ |
| Ball joints | Shoulder and hip articulation | 20+ |

If you come up short, don't force the exact original colors — substituting close shades is part of the fun of MOC building. Once the parts are collected, work in three passes: frame, armor, details. Start with a Technic frame that can stand on its own, then cover it with purple plates piece by piece, and finally add the eye elements and printed parts. Doing it in this order keeps disassembly and adjustment easy.

## Three Core Techniques for Building Unit-01

Most of the difficulty in an **EVA LEGO blueprint** comes from three places.

The first is the slender body proportion. Unlike Gundam's boxy torso, an EVA needs a lot of SNOT work — stacking bricks sideways — to produce long limbs and smooth, flowing muscle lines across the silhouette.

The second is the shoulder pylons. These are Unit-01's signature structure, and the smart move is to build them as detachable modules so you can switch between the normal form and the berserk form. For berserk mode, simply swap in red transparent eye elements.

The third is the organic feel. Alternate curved slopes with regular slopes to imitate the way muscle runs beneath skin, and the model stops looking like a stack of cubes.

## The AT Field and Scene Atmosphere

A big part of the EVA appeal is the AT Field. Build it from transparent blue and purple bricks arranged in rings that float around the body, and pair it with a dark base to create that pressurized, pre-battle tension. More advanced builders can add an entry plug bay for Unit-01: build the cockpit from cylindrical bricks so it can slot into the mech's back, and the accuracy jumps immediately. If you want more practice after that, Unit-02's red paint scheme and the geometric body structures of the Angels are both worthwhile challenges.
