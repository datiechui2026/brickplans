# Brick Seahorse MOC Review: 560 Pieces, Curled Tail

The short version: this seahorse MOC pattern is a strong piece of design work. It comes in at roughly 560 pieces and stands about 12 cm tall, the instructions cost around 25 yuan, and an experienced builder can finish it in about two hours — roughly 0.045 yuan per piece. For likeness to the real animal, I'd give it 9 out of 10. It's worth buying.

## Design Fidelity

The three features that make a seahorse instantly recognisable — the streamlined body, the fan-shaped dorsal fin and the tail that curls inward — are all present here. The torso is built from curved slopes stacked sideways, and the transition from snout to belly reads naturally, with no obvious stair-stepping along the profile. The dorsal fin is made from three small wing-shaped elements set at offset angles, so it holds a clear layered look even in a static pose.

The curled tail is the standout section of the whole build. Joint pieces draw it in segment by segment, and the tip can still swing through a small arc, which captures the way a real seahorse grips seaweed far better than a rigid sculpt would. Colour is an orange-to-red gradient, with dark pieces around the head to sharpen the silhouette. Placed in a bookcase, it holds its own.

## Building Experience

The pattern is split into 8 step bags, with parts sorted by colour, so finding pieces stays quick. The real difficulty sits in the curved torso: about 30% of the steps have to be repeated as mirrored left-right pairs, and beginners need to watch the mirror direction carefully. The instructions can be viewed online on the brickplan.cn platform with zoom support, and the tricky angles get local close-up views, so following along rarely leaves you stuck. There are no stickers anywhere — all the detail comes from colour separation between parts, which saves you the alignment work entirely. The one small drawback is that the tail joints are on the tight side and need a little force to turn; loosen them up before assembly rather than after.

## Value and Recommendation

With 560 pieces, a 25 yuan pattern and a two-hour build, this seahorse MOC sits in the upper-middle tier of animal patterns. At about 0.045 yuan per piece it undercuts comparable sets currently on sale by a fair margin. It suits builders who like marine-life subjects, and beginners who want to practise curved-surface building techniques. If posability matters to you, the curled tail and dorsal fin are display-only, which may be a slight disappointment. Overall, as a desk ornament or a first animal MOC, it's a safe pick.
