# Tower Bridge MOC Review: 3,800-Part London Landmark Build

The short version: this Tower Bridge MOC builds to roughly 52 cm long and 28 cm tall from about 3,800 parts. The instructions cost around 58 yuan, an experienced builder will need about 14 hours, and the cost works out to roughly 1.5 fen per part. Overall accuracy sits above 85 percent, the twin towers and the working bascule deck are the standout features, and among mid-to-large architecture MOCs it lands on the better side of the value curve. It suits builders with some experience behind them.

## 1. How Faithful Is the Model?

The soul of Tower Bridge is the pair of Gothic towers and the deck that splits and lifts. This design handles the towers very well: dark brown and cream bricks alternate across the facades to suggest stone coursing, the corner turrets, spires and arched windows are all reproduced with dedicated parts, and even the flagpoles and decorative trim on the tower roofs are accounted for. The bascule deck uses a segmented hinge assembly, so the two leaves genuinely rise to about 30 degrees, and with the chain detailing on both sides it photographs with real presence. The pedestrian walkways, railings and the curved arches of the piers are simplified to a sensible degree. From a distance the silhouette reads correctly; up close there is plenty of detail. Overall accuracy lands at around 85 percent.

## 2. Build Experience

The full run is about 3,800 parts, split into four booklets covering the pier bases, the twin towers, the deck and the walkways, which makes it easy to work through in sessions. The opening mechanism is the most interesting stretch of the middle of the build; the hinges and stop pieces need a little patience, but once assembled the deck swings smoothly without catching. The tower facades lean heavily on 1x1 bricks and tiles for surface texture, so repetition is high, though with the step diagrams most builders manage around 270 parts an hour. That puts the total at roughly 14 hours, and splitting it across three or four evenings keeps it comfortable rather than tiring.

## 3. Value and Who Should Buy It

The instructions run about 58 yuan. Estimating the full parts inventory at average market prices for loose bricks, the pieces come to roughly 550 yuan and the per-part cost to about 1.5 fen, which is more than 40 percent cheaper than a comparable LEGO set of the same size. This is positioned as a mid-to-large display model, the kind of thing that sits in a living room or study as an English-style accent piece. If you like Gothic architecture and want an interactive landmark whose deck actually opens, it is well worth picking up. If you only want something small for a shelf, look at the same designer's mini version instead.
