# Brick Castle MOC Tutorial: Plan, Build and Detail Your Fortress

A brick castle MOC is where many builders start, and it stays one of the best subjects for showing off both personal creativity and structural thinking. Whether you are going for a fairy-tale castle or a medieval defensive fortress, a good castle MOC depends on disciplined structural planning and careful detailing. This guide walks you from an empty baseplate to a finished castle, one layer at a time.

## 1. Planning First: Scale and Color

Before you pick up a single brick, the most important work is deciding what you are actually building. Whether a brick castle MOC looks right depends heavily on proportion. Start by fixing the overall footprint: will this be a compact tower on a 32×32 baseplate, or a full-scale imposing fortress on a 48×48 baseplate? Only once the ground area is settled can you sensibly divide space between towers, curtain walls and courtyard.

On color, classic grey-brown stone walls are the safe choice; pair them with a dark grey roof and a brown main gate and you get a strong medieval atmosphere straight away. If you want a brick castle MOC with more visual identity, add small accents of gold or red to pull the eye toward the focal point.

## 2. A Solid Foundation: The Base Everything Rests On

Every brick castle MOC has to solve the stability problem first. The taller the tower, the higher the center of gravity, so the foundation is no place to cut corners. Build the base from multiple layers of bricks laid in cross-bonded courses, and widen it slightly beneath the towers to create a stable trapezoidal profile. If your castle has curtain walls, bury baseplate clips in the foundation so the walls lock firmly to the ground.

If you have the parts, add support columns or stiffening ribs inside the walls. This noticeably increases overall strength and saves you the misery of a half-finished build collapsing into a pile of loose bricks.

## 3. Walls and Towers: Where Depth Comes From

The walls are the skin of a brick castle MOC and the best place to show detail. Do not leave the surface flat. Use slope bricks, inset bricks and protruding stone blocks to imitate the uneven texture of real masonry. Add a course of inset or protruding bricks every few layers and the wall immediately gains depth and light-and-shadow variation.

Towers are the soul of the castle. A classic round tower can be built by offset stacking: rotate each layer slightly and combine it with curved bricks to produce a smooth cylindrical body. Remember to leave observation windows and crenellations at the top — it looks better and it matches the functional logic of a military castle.

## 4. Gates, Drawbridges and Decorative Detail

A brick castle MOC people remember usually wins on detail. Add a drawbridge in front of the gate that can be raised and lowered, with door bars and chain elements on either side. Hang banners, shields and torches on the walls, place a well, stables and minifigures in the courtyard, and the whole castle suddenly comes alive.

If you want a higher level of finish, spend some effort on the moat, farmland or even a cliff backdrop, so the brick castle MOC is not a single lonely building but a complete miniature world.

## 5. Your Build, Your Story

The most appealing thing about building a brick castle MOC is that every brick tells your story. From foundation planning to tower detail, from color choice to scene dressing, each session at the table is a act of creation. Hopefully this tutorial helps you take the first step and build the castle you have been imagining.
