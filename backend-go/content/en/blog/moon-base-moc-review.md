# Moon Base MOC Review: 1,030 Pieces, 51cm Wide Research Station

The verdict first: this moon base MOC builds a 51cm-wide research station out of 1,030 pieces, complete with a dome, an airlock and a lunar rover. Accuracy lands around 90%, which makes it a first-choice project for builders who want a large display scene rather than a small desk model. The full instructions live on brickplan — search "moon base MOC" to pull up the 140-step PDF and the parts list.

### Instruction Structure: A Sensible Modular Breakdown

The manual is split into 6 modules: main habitat, dome, solar panels, airlock, lunar rover and the surface base. Each module is bound as its own booklet, so a group can divide the work and build in parallel instead of passing one book around the table. Across all six booklets there are 140 steps.

That split is more than a convenience. Every module is finished and stable before it meets the others, so a single evening session gets you a complete sub-assembly and the final join-up stays short. Building solo, work the habitat first so the surface base is ready when the heavier sections need to be seated.

### Parts List: White Hull Pieces and Trans-Clear Domes

Three groups dominate the inventory:

- 310 white hull pieces for the habitat walls
- 45 trans-clear dome pieces
- 60 gray surface plates for the lunar terrain

A full set of parts comes to roughly CNY 620. If you already own a decent stock of white and gray, filling only the gaps brings it down to about CNY 460. The 1,030-piece count includes 8% spares, which gives the transparent dome pieces — the ones most likely to scuff — a small buffer.

### Build Experience: 8 Hours and a Solid Structure

Budget about 1 hour per module, roughly 8 hours for the whole station. The moving highlights are the airlock's rotating door and the solar panel array, which folds out on a deployment mechanism. The finished station weighs 1.6 kg, and the structure is solid enough to be lifted and moved without flexing.

The trans-clear dome is the part that demands care: handle those pieces lightly to avoid scratches, since a scuffed canopy is the one flaw that shows on a finished model. Sorting the white hull pieces by size before you start pays off here, because 310 of them pass through your hands.

### Accuracy and Value for Money

Accuracy sits at about 90%. The dome curvature and the airtight-seal detailing on the hull follow real research-station concepts closely, and that is what sells the scale. At roughly CNY 0.60 per piece, the whole build undercuts comparable 1,000-piece official scene sets by around 30%.
