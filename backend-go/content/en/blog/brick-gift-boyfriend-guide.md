# Brick Gift Guide for Boyfriends: Mechs, Cars and Technic

Short answer first: sort him into one of three buckets and buy accordingly. Sci-fi fan, go mech. Car person, go vehicle. Someone who likes taking things apart, go Technic. A CNY 300-500 set gets you a solid entry build; above CNY 1,000, jump straight to a flagship Technic model. Either way it ends up on a shelf as a display piece, which is more personal than another gadget.

## 1. Work Out His Interest Type First

Before you buy, watch for three things. Does he scroll mech videos or racing videos? Are there die-cast cars or figures in his room? How confident are his hands? Mech sets suit collectors, car sets suit people who care about looks, and Technic suits the engineering-minded builder who wants to know how the drivetrain works. Pick the wrong category and even an expensive set just collects dust.

## 2. Mechs: The Sci-Fi Fan's First Choice

Mech sets are built around accuracy and articulated joints. Two good picks: a classic Gundam-style buildable mech, roughly 1,800 parts and about 35 cm tall when finished, with joints that hold a standing pose or a weapon-ready pose; and a transformable finished mech, which switches between humanoid and vehicle forms for maximum play value. Expect CNY 300-600, and plan on desk or display-cabinet space.

## 3. Cars: For the Looks-Obsessed

Supercars, classic cars and motorcycles are the three most popular directions. Start with a vintage classic car at around 1,200 parts, a 4-6 hour build, with a steering-wheel linkage and working front-wheel steering. Step up to an F1 car or a supercar, where the body lines follow the real model and some versions include suspension. Prices run CNY 200-800. Check that the model is officially licensed, because detail accuracy varies enormously between sets.

## 4. Technic: The Engineer's Happy Place

Technic is the top tier of gifting. Two recommendations: an entry off-roader at about 2,500 parts with a differential, a steering system and piston linkage, which actually rolls when you push it; and a flagship build of 4,000+ parts, either large construction machinery or a hypercar, with a working gearbox shift structure and a 15-20 hour build time. That one works well as an anniversary gift. Prices run CNY 800-2,000, so if the budget allows, go flagship.

## 5. Gifting Tips and Money-Saving Options

1. If you are unsure of the exact model, aim for the 1,500-2,500 part mid-range: moderate difficulty, strong sense of achievement.
2. A finished build needs somewhere to live. Add an acrylic display case to the gift and it lands twice as well.
3. On a tighter budget, search brickplan.cn for MOC instructions tagged mech, supercar or Technic, then order the loose parts from the parts list. A finished model of the same spec costs about 60% of an official set, and you can pick his favourite colour scheme.
4. Do not open the packaging in advance. Let him unbox it himself, because half the value of a brick gift is the ritual.

## FAQ

**Q: What budget is reasonable for a brick gift for my boyfriend?**

A: CNY 300-500 buys a quality mid-range set of around 1,500 parts with the full building experience. If you want a flagship Technic model, budget CNY 1,000 or more: the finished size and structural complexity are on a completely different level.

**Q: He has never built a brick set. What will not put him off?**

A: Choose something under 2,000 parts with clearly separated instruction booklets, such as a vintage classic car or an entry off-roader. Those take 4-6 hours and deliver a strong payoff. Do not start him on a 4,000+ part flagship.

**Q: What is the difference between bricks and LEGO, and which should I give?**

A: LEGO is a brand; brick is the category name. For a gift, any brand with good clutch power is fine. If you want value for money, find instructions on brickplan.cn and order the loose parts, which costs about 60% of an official set.

**Q: Can a finished brick car be remote controlled or motorised?**

A: Some Technic sets accept motors and remote-control modules. Check before buying whether the set has a power interface reserved. Purely static models cannot be converted, so if he likes remote control, buy a version that ships with a motor kit instead.
