# Keeppley for Beginners: Best Starter Sets for 2025

If you are new to Chinese brick brands and want a set that is good value, pleasant to build and officially licensed, this Keeppley beginner guide is for you. Keeppley has built a strong reputation among domestic brands thanks to tight mould tolerances, a deep roster of licensed IP and friendly pricing — which is why plenty of builders who started with LEGO now pick it up. Below is a look at what makes the brand beginner-friendly, plus five sets that make a sensible first purchase.

## Why should beginners choose Keeppley?

Before getting into specific sets, it is worth explaining why the brand suits newcomers.

### Solid part quality

Keeppley's mould precision sits in the top tier of Chinese brick brands. Clutch power is moderate — parts do not fall off when you touch the model, and they are not so tight that your fingers hurt. For a beginner, that comfortable resistance removes most of the frustration from building, so you can enjoy the process and still get a good feel for the parts.

### Official licences and nostalgia appeal

Keeppley holds official licences for **Doraemon, Pokemon, Hello Kitty, Crayon Shin-chan, Detective Conan** and more. That means the characters you build closely match the source material rather than being knock-off designs that skirt the law. For anyone who grew up watching these shows, building a favourite character is a pleasure in itself.

### Low prices, low risk

Where LEGO sets often run into the hundreds or thousands of yuan, Keeppley entry-level sets usually sit in the ¥30–100 range. For a beginner the **cost of trying something out is very low** — if a set turns out not to suit you, it is no great loss. That is why "good value" is the single most upvoted point in most Keeppley beginner recommendation threads.

## Best Keeppley sets for beginners in 2025

The Doraemon line is Keeppley's most classic licensed series and the first choice for almost every newcomer. This Time Machine model recreates the famous scene from the anime and includes two mini figures: Doraemon and Nobita.

If this is your first Keeppley set, starting here is a safe bet — it sits at the top of most Keeppley beginner rankings year after year.

## Beginner building tips for Keeppley

Once you have chosen a set, a few small habits will improve the experience.

### Sort your parts

When you open the box, sort the parts by colour or shape first. **Keeppley bags are usually numbered by building stage**, so open them in order and sort each bag separately — this speeds things up considerably.

### Use the right tools

Keeppley's clutch is moderate, but some steps still need a firm press. Keep a **brick separator** (the tool builders call a "de-bricker") on hand so you can pull a mistake apart easily instead of biting the part or digging at it with a fingernail.

### Start simple, then scale up

If you are a genuine beginner, start with a BrickHeadz or small scene of **under 200 parts**, get used to how Keeppley parts feel and how the instructions read, then work up to mid-size sets of 500 parts or more.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Keeppley compatible with LEGO?

**Fully compatible.** Keeppley uses standard brick dimensions and is compatible with LEGO and other major Chinese brick brands. You can mix Keeppley parts with LEGO in your own MOC (My Own Creation) builds.

### Are Keeppley instructions in Chinese?

Yes. Every Keeppley set includes **Chinese-language instructions**. The diagrams are clear, and the written notes are in Simplified Chinese — well suited to readers who can follow Chinese.

### Where is the best place to buy Keeppley?

Keeppley runs official flagship stores on Tmall and JD.com, and authorised shops also operate on platforms such as Pinduoduo. Watch the **major sales events such as 618 and Double 11**, when spend-and-save discounts often bring some sets down to as little as half price.
