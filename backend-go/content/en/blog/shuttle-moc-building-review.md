# MOC Space Shuttle Review: 2,800 Parts, 60 cm Build

Bottom line first: this space shuttle MOC on brickplan is one of the best-value space-themed builds you can get right now. Around 2,800 parts, roughly 12 hours of building, and a finished model 60 cm long that dominates a living room shelf. If you build space subjects, this one earns a strong recommendation.

## Appearance and Design

The model is scaled at 1:110, and the fuselage lines stay clean from nose to tail. The cargo bay doors open and close, and the remote manipulator arm and aft nozzles are all reproduced rather than simplified away. The white primary color is broken up by black thermal-tile pieces, which gives the surface real visual depth. The cockpit uses trans-clear parts, so the glazing reads well and photographs beautifully.

## Structural Stability

The fuselage uses an internal keel wrapped in a double-layer shell, and the wing-to-fuselage joints have extra reinforcement, so handling the model does not make it fall apart. The landing gear retracts and extends, and it holds the weight steadily. The one thing to watch: the parts around the tail nozzles sit a little loose. Follow the reinforcement steps marked in the brickplan instructions and the finished assembly is very solid.

## Value for Money

Part of the instructions can be previewed free on brickplan, and the full version costs less than a cup of coffee. Priced out by part count, the total build comes in about 40% cheaper than the official space shuttle set, and you can change the color scheme to whatever you like. The included bill of materials (BOM) can be ordered from directly, which saves you the usual trial-and-error of hunting individual parts.

## Building Experience

The instructions divide steps sensibly, adding only 3–5 parts per step, so even a first-time MOC builder can keep pace. The whole model takes about 12 hours, comfortably split across two evenings, and along the way you pick up useful MOC techniques such as layered fuselage construction and linked cargo door mechanisms.
