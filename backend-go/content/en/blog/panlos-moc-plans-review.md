# Panlos MOC Plans Review: Value and Part Quality Tested

## Product Overview

Panlos has climbed quickly among Chinese brick brands, and its MOC plans have built up a serious following among builders. Unlike official LEGO instructions, Panlos MOC plans are aimed squarely at players who want unusual shapes and strong value for money. The brand is at its best on mecha, military vehicles and sci-fi scenes; the design language leans hard-edged and detail-dense, and cost control is handled well enough that an average builder can take on a large-scale MOC without a large-scale budget.

This review is based on more than 10 mainstream MOC plans Panlos released across 2025 and 2026, spanning three categories — mecha, ships and buildings — and assesses them on build difficulty, part quality and the building experience itself.

## Build Difficulty

**Overall rating: ★★★☆☆**

Difficulty is tiered rather than uniform. Entry-level plans, such as the small mecha, run roughly 300 to 600 parts and sit at a moderate level — a good fit for builders with three months or more of experience behind them. Mid-size and large plans, such as sci-fi warships and big mecha, generally land between 1,500 and 3,000 parts. Structural complexity there is noticeably higher, and some of the joint designs demand careful, precise handling; these are best treated as intermediate-level challenges.

**Part count range:** 300 to 3,500 parts

That spread matters when you are choosing a first purchase. The low end is a weekend project; the top end is a multi-session build with subassemblies that need to be handled gently before they lock into the frame.

## Parts Review

The parts package is where Panlos MOC plans earn most of their credit:

- **Part precision:** ★★★★☆ — Clutch is good and sits at a medium tightness, with LEGO compatibility above 95%
- **Colour consistency:** ★★★★☆ — No shade variation within a single batch; rare colours are missing, but the common palette is complete
- **Material feel:** ★★★☆☆ — ABS, slightly behind LEGO in hand feel, but far ahead of knockoff brands
- **Bag numbering:** ★★★★☆ — Most plans ship parts pre-bagged by step, which cuts down searching time considerably

In practice, the compatibility figure is the one to keep in mind. Above 95% means most of a Panlos build will accept LEGO replacements without trouble, which opens up the option of upgrading specific sections later rather than committing to one brand throughout.

## Building Experience

The standout feature of Panlos MOC plans is **digital documentation**. High-resolution PDF instructions are available through official channels, steps are clear, and exploded views for corner assemblies are marked properly. Some of the popular plans go further and offer a 3D rotate view, which is a real help on complex structures where a flat diagram hides the order of assembly.

There is a caveat. Some of the earlier Panlos plans divide steps too coarsely, and you will occasionally hit a step that asks you to place ten parts at once — at that point you have to work out the installation order yourself. Recent plans have improved this significantly, so the issue is largely confined to older releases. If you are buying a plan that has been out for a while, it is worth checking which generation of instructions you are getting.

## Finished Model

The finished builds hold up well. On the large mecha, the articulated joints are sensibly designed and the model stands stably; on the building plans, the structure is tight and does not loosen over time. Exterior detail is rich throughout — rivets, piping and mechanical structure are all handled well, and the models photograph extremely well for display or sharing online.

## Verdict

**★★★★☆ (Highly recommended)**

For builders on a limited budget who still want a quality MOC experience, [Panlos MOC plans](https://brickplan.cn) are a very strong value proposition. Beginners are best off starting with a mid-small mecha plan under 800 parts and working up to the larger builds from there. If your standards for part quality are absolute, you can swap out key structural parts for LEGO equivalents.

**Recommended brand:** Panlos
**Best suited to:** Intermediate MOC builders, mecha fans, budget-conscious builders
