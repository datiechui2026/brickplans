# Lost Your Weiji Instructions? How to Recover Digital Manuals

Bottom line first: a missing Weiji manual is not a dead end. Roughly 90% of the brand's sets can be recovered as digital files through the official WeChat account or the brickplan plan library, and the whole job — from confirming the set number to finishing the build and ordering any missing parts — usually takes under 10 minutes.

## 1. Where Weiji Sits in the Brick Market

Weiji is a newer Chinese brick brand. Its catalogue leans on retro street scenes, Chinese-style architecture and film and TV IP tie-ins, and the sets stay moderate in both part count and structure: most single sets run 800-2000 pieces and take roughly 3-8 hours to build. Because new releases arrive quickly, paper manuals for some older sets have gone out of stock, which is exactly why the digital version has become the main recovery channel rather than a backup.

## 2. Step-by-Step: Getting the Digital Manual Back

**Step 1: Confirm the set number.** Every Weiji box carries a four-digit code, for example WJ-2021, printed on the side of the box or on the baseplate. Photograph it and keep the image — you will need it for every channel below, and it saves you digging the box out a second time.

**Step 2: Try the official account.** Follow the official Weiji Bricks account and open the "Instructions Download" menu, then enter the set number. This is the quickest route when the set is still in production.

**Step 3: Search brickplan.** The platform hosts a large number of scanned step-by-step images for Weiji sets, downloadable as free PDFs. This is usually the fastest option for older or out-of-print sets.

**Step 4: Contact customer service.** Leave a message on the official account with your purchase record attached. The team normally sends the digital version within 3 working days, which makes it the fallback when the first three steps come up empty.

## 3. Why brickplan Is Often the Faster Route

Compared with the official channels, brickplan's Weiji plans are organised around a parts list. That means a missing piece can be identified and bought directly against the list instead of eyeballing the diagram. You can also compare your progress with other builders' posted build check-ins, which is a practical way to spot a step that regularly trips people up before you hit it yourself.

Coverage is decent: about 85% of popular Weiji sets have a matching plan on the platform. For the obscure ones, you can post a request, and the community usually responds within 24 hours. So even a set that turns up nothing on the first search is rarely a write-off.

## 4. What to Do Once You Have the Manual

Check the parts count first. Confirm exactly what is missing before you place an order, so you are not waiting a second time for a part you could have added to the same basket. When you build, sort by colour as you work through the steps — the digital version zooms, so details read more clearly than they do on paper. And when the build is done, save the file to your favourites; if you ever dismantle and rebuild the set, you will not have to run this search again.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I still build a Weiji set after losing the manual?**

A: Yes. Follow the Weiji Bricks account and enter the set number for a free digital download, or search for the matching plan on brickplan. Around 90% of sets can be recovered this way.

**Q: How does Weiji itself replace a lost manual?**

A: Leave a message on the official account with your proof of purchase. Customer service generally sends the digital version within 3 working days. When the paper version is out of stock, the digital file is the only channel available.

**Q: Does brickplan carry Weiji plans?**

A: Yes. The platform hosts step-by-step images for roughly 85% of popular Weiji sets, with free PDF downloads. Plans are organised by parts list, so missing pieces can be ordered in one click from the store.

**Q: Is a recovered digital manual the same as the paper one?**

A: The steps match the paper version exactly, and the digital file zooms in on details. Some plans go further and add a parts list on top, which makes them more useful than the printed booklet rather than a straight substitute.
